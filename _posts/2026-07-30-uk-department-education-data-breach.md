Cybercriminals are attempting to extort the UK's Department for Education
after compromising what they claim is more than 600,000 records, including
names, email addresses, and phone numbers, according to The Record.

The department has not confirmed the full scope of the breach in the
reporting available. No ransom demand details or attacker group name were
included in the summary.
