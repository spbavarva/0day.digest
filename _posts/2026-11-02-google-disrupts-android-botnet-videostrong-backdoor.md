Google led a takedown of the XCPE botnet, spanning more than 1.3 million
Android TV streaming boxes, cutting the operators off from their
command infrastructure in late October. Google says it is highly
confident that device maker Videostrong intentionally built a backdoor
into the boxes and sold criminal access to it.

The access was used for ad fraud and to proxy other people's internet
traffic through victims' home networks. Google has banned Videostrong
as a result. Because the backdoor was built in at the manufacturer
level rather than added post-sale, this is effectively a hardware
supply chain compromise at scale.
