The Australian Federal Police charged two Western Australian men —
Louis Michael Gaebler, 23, and Ruben Ian Thomson, 21 — with a combined
14 offences over their alleged role in TeamPCP. The group is blamed for
the March 2026 compromise of the open-source security scanners Trivy
and Checkmarx KICS, along with the AI gateway LiteLLM. Both suspects
appeared in Perth Magistrates Court on August 27.

Krebs on Security separately described TeamPCP as responsible for the
longest-running software supply chain attack spree to date, targeting
thousands of global businesses through malicious open-source packages.
