Online fashion retailer Revolve disclosed a data breach affecting at
least 180,000 customers after hackers accessed its shopping platform
and stole personal and payment card information.

The company has not published technical details of the intrusion
vector in available reporting. Affected customers should monitor
statements for fraudulent card activity and watch for Revolve's
breach notification guidance.
