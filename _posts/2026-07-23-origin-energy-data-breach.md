Origin Energy has confirmed that an unauthorized party accessed and
subsequently leaked customer data online, including sensitive personally
identifiable information. A hacker separately claims to have stolen data
belonging to 2 million Origin Energy customers and is threatening a
further leak.

Origin is one of Australia's largest energy providers, and the scale of
the claimed exposure crosses the threshold for a major consumer breach.
Affected customers should watch for phishing attempts referencing their
Origin Energy account details.
