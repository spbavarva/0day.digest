France's national blood service confirmed that a cyberattack targeting a
refrigeration equipment provider altered temperature and defrost settings
at three of its facilities. The change in settings put stored blood
products at risk of spoiling.

The incident is a reminder that attacks on upstream equipment vendors can
have direct physical safety consequences, not just data loss, for the
organizations that depend on them.
