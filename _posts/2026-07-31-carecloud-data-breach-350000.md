CareCloud disclosed that hackers stole personal, financial, and medical
information belonging to more than 350,000 people. The company said the
intrusion occurred in March 2026 and that data was taken from its AWS
environment.

No further detail on the initial access method or specific data fields
exposed was included in the disclosure. Healthcare and financial data
exposure at this scale makes affected individuals a target for follow-on
fraud and phishing.
