Google launched a new bug bounty category offering a top payout of
$250,000 for research demonstrating full administrative ("root-in-cloud")
control through architectural vulnerabilities, rather than routine web
bugs.

Full program scope and submission details were not included in the
available summary. Cloud security researchers focused on GCP
architecture may find this program worth tracking.
