OpenAI said it disrupted a Cambodia-based operation that used ChatGPT to
support investment, romance, gambling, and impersonation scams. The takedown
is part of OpenAI's ongoing effort to identify and shut down malicious uses
of its models.

Details on the scale of the operation or number of victims were not
included in OpenAI's disclosure. The case is a reminder that scam operations
are integrating commercial LLMs into existing fraud workflows rather than
building novel AI tooling from scratch.
