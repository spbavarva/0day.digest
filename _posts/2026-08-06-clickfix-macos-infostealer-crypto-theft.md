A Go-based infostealer is being distributed to macOS users through ClickFix-
style social engineering lures. Once running, it harvests cryptocurrency
wallet data, browser-stored passwords, Apple Keychain contents, and cached
credentials. BleepingComputer reports the campaign is specifically targeting
crypto theft. ClickFix attacks trick victims into pasting and running a
malicious command themselves, typically via a fake verification prompt,
bypassing many download-based defenses.
