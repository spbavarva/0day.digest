Google and partners disrupted a botnet spanning roughly 1.3 million
infected devices, mostly set-top boxes and other IoT hardware, that was
being used to build criminal infrastructure. Google alleges Android device
manufacturer Videostrong operated the network via a backdoor built into its
devices.

The takedown is one of the larger IoT botnet disruptions reported this
year by device count.
