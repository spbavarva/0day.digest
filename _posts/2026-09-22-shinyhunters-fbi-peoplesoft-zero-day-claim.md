The ShinyHunters extortion gang claims it breached FBI systems using a
previously unknown zero-day in Oracle PeopleSoft, gaining access to internal
services and stealing data on employees and job applicants.

The FBI has not confirmed the breach. Organizations running PeopleSoft
should watch for an official advisory and patch guidance, since the
underlying flaw is not yet disclosed in detail.
