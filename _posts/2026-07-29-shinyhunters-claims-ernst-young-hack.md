The threat actor group ShinyHunters is claiming a breach of Ernst & Young.
EY has previously confirmed that personal and financial information was
stolen from a third-party management platform, though it isn't confirmed
whether that earlier disclosure and this new claim refer to the same
incident. No further technical detail on the claimed breach — such as data
volume or method — is available yet.
