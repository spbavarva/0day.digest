French bank Crédit Mutuel Arkéa confirmed hackers stole an estimated $133
million after compromising an unnamed supplier. The attackers used that
foothold to initiate transfers using the bank's own account, rather than
targeting customer accounts directly.

The incident is a reminder that a single compromised vendor can give
attackers a trusted path straight into a financial institution's own
transaction systems.
