Unlimited Technology Systems disclosed a breach affecting roughly 3.8 million
people. Attackers stole personal, medical, and health insurance information
from the company's data center. SecurityWeek's report does not specify the
attack vector, threat actor, or whether the stolen data has surfaced for sale.
Individuals affected by breaches involving medical and insurance data face
elevated risk of targeted phishing and identity theft.
