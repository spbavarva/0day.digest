CISA published an advisory for the Ebyte NA111-M (firmware 9013-2-17),
warning that successful exploitation of a set of vulnerabilities could
allow an attacker to fully compromise the device. The advisory carries a
CVSS v3 score of 9.8 and lists thirteen associated CVEs.

No mitigation guidance beyond the advisory itself was included in the
source summary. Operators of NA111-M devices should consult the full
CISA advisory for affected firmware versions and remediation steps.
