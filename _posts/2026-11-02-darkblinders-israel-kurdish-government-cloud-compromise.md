Researchers at Dream Research Labs disclosed an Iran state-aligned
cyber espionage campaign, tracked as DarkBlinders, active between
August and October 2026 against targets in Israel and the Kurdistan
Region of Iraq.

The campaign compromised a high-profile individual in Israel along
with a government cloud environment in Iraqi Kurdistan. The initial
access vector was not specified in the available summary.
