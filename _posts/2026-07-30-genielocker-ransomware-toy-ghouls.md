Kaspersky researchers dissected GenieLocker, a new custom ransomware family
with variants for Windows, Linux, and ESXi systems.

The malware is being used by Toy Ghouls, a financially-motivated extortion
group, according to Securelist. ESXi support means the ransomware can
directly target virtualization hosts, a common tactic for maximizing impact
in enterprise environments.
