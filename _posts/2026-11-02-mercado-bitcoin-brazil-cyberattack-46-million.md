Mercado Bitcoin, Brazil's largest cryptocurrency exchange, confirmed a
cyberattack after blockchain security firms tracked more than $46
million flowing out of the platform. Details on the attack vector
have not yet been disclosed.

The incident adds to a string of high-value crypto exchange
compromises in 2026. Users should watch for confirmation on affected
accounts and any recovery guidance from Mercado Bitcoin.
