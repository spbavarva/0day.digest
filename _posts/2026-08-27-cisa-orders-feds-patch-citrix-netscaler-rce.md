CISA has ordered U.S. federal agencies to patch Citrix NetScaler
appliances against a remote code execution vulnerability, tracked as
CVE-2026-8452, by Saturday. The flaw is under active exploitation,
according to CISA and confirmed separately by SecurityWeek.

Any organization running NetScaler ADC or Gateway should prioritize
patching regardless of the federal deadline, given confirmed in-the-wild
attacks.
