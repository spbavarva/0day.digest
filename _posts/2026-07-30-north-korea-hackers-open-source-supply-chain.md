Amazon researchers say a North Korea-linked hacking group was behind several
high-profile compromises of open-source software libraries used by
developers worldwide, per The Record.

The attribution links a string of prior supply-chain incidents to a single
state-sponsored actor rather than isolated financially-motivated campaigns.
Specific package names were not detailed in the available summary.
