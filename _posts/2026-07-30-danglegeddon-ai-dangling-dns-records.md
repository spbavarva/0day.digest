Security researchers cited by SecurityWeek warn that AI could be used to
automate discovery and takeover of forgotten, dangling DNS records at a scale
capable of disrupting governments, banks, and global supply chains.

The concern is framed as a nation-state-capable weaponization of an
already-known subdomain-takeover technique rather than a novel exploit. No
active campaign was reported at time of publication.
