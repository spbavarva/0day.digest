A campaign attributed by Acronis Threat Research is delivering the
open-source Spark RAT against targets in Cambodia, using lures including
fake government notices, public health materials, and real estate
content, suggesting an effort to appeal to a broad range of victims.

The malware abuses a vulnerable OPSWAT driver to disable security tools
on infected machines.
