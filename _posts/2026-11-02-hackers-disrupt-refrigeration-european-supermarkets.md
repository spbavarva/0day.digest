A cyberattack disrupted refrigeration systems at multiple supermarket
chains across several European countries after attackers remotely
altered temperature settings on freezers and cooling units. The
incident spans more than one country, pointing to a shared vendor or
platform compromise rather than isolated local intrusions.

No attribution or technical root cause has been published in the
available summary. The attack is a reminder that OT/ICS exposure in
retail refrigeration can have immediate physical and financial
consequences.
