Blockchain analytics firm Chainalysis disclosed a data breach in
which attackers used offensive agentic AI techniques to access
company systems and internal tools. The company did not detail the
specific agentic methods or scope of data accessed in the available
summary.

The breach of a firm that itself supplies crypto-crime intelligence to
law enforcement and exchanges underscores that AI-assisted offensive
tooling is now being used directly against security vendors.
