WorkOS, Northflank, and Trigger.dev have each disclosed breaches tied to
attackers harvesting AI access tokens and using AI-assisted offensive
tooling to automate parts of the attack. All three are developer
infrastructure platforms, meaning stolen AI tokens could provide downstream
access to customer applications built on top of them.

The pattern suggests AI credentials are becoming as valuable a target as
traditional API keys and should be rotated and scoped with the same rigor.
