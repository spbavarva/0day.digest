The Chinese cybercrime group Silver Fox was observed using a chain of three
vulnerable drivers in a bring-your-own-vulnerable-driver (BYOVD) attack
against a Japanese industrial manufacturing organization, per The Hacker
News.

The campaign combines new vulnerable-driver abuse with abuse of legitimate
tooling to ultimately deliver ValleyRAT (also known as Winos 4.0) for
persistent remote access. The attack targeted the industrial manufacturing
sector specifically.
