The Bureau of Alcohol, Tobacco, Firearms and Explosives (ATF) confirmed
a cyberattack on a system containing investigation-related information,
after the Qilin ransomware gang claimed responsibility for the breach.
ATF separately confirmed to BleepingComputer that the incident is a
"major incident."

The scope of compromised investigation data, including whether it
includes information on active cases or informants, was not disclosed.
