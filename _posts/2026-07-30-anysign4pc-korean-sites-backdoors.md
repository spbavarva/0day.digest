South Korean authorities and four security firms disclosed a state-sponsored
campaign that compromised trusted domestic websites to exploit a vulnerable,
locally-installed financial security tool called AnySign4PC.

A compromised page could infect visitors running a vulnerable AnySign4PC
version with SIGNBT or COPPERHEDGE backdoors without any user prompt, per
The Hacker News. Both backdoors have prior associations with North Korean
threat activity.
