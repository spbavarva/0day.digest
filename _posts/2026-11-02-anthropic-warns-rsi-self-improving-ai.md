Anthropic says it is approaching recursive self-improvement (RSI)
capability — the point where an AI system becomes proficient enough at
training itself to trigger a rapid acceleration in capabilities. Some
AI safety researchers view crossing this threshold as effectively
irreversible.

No specific capability demonstration or timeline was disclosed in the
available summary. The warning is notable mainly as a signal of how
close Anthropic believes the industry is to this threshold.
