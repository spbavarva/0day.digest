A state-sponsored group tracked as "Laundry Bear" is exploiting a Zimbra
zero-day against targets in the US and Ukraine. The group sends "half-click"
phishing emails that require a victim only to open or preview the message
to trigger exploitation, rather than click a link or open an attachment.

Organizations running Zimbra should watch for anomalous mail-preview
activity and apply vendor guidance as it becomes available. No patch
details were available at time of writing.
