The US government has banned imports of foreign-made humanoid robots,
citing cybersecurity and other national security risks, according to
SecurityWeek. The action is aimed primarily at Chinese-manufactured robots.
The agency's rationale groups these devices with other connected hardware
previously restricted over supply chain and remote-access concerns. Details
on enforcement scope and covered manufacturers were not included in the
available summary.
