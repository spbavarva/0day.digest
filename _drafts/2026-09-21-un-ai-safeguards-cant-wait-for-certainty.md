A United Nations scientific panel has published its first major
assessment of AI risk, warning that governments need to rein in
increasingly capable AI agents before their risks are fully understood.

The report applies a precautionary-principle framing and cites
OpenAI's earlier hack of Hugging Face as a reference incident.

The assessment coincides with world leaders gathering in New York this
week, further cementing AI governance on the global diplomatic agenda.
