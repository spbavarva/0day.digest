A prompt-injection vulnerability has been disclosed in Manus, the
agentic AI app valued at $4 billion.

The flaw stems from a common weakness in AI agents that interpret
external, untrusted data: without rigorous input filtering, that data
can be crafted to hijack the agent's behavior.

Specific technical details of the Manus bug were not included in
available reporting. The broader takeaway for teams deploying agentic
AI: treat all external content the agent reads as untrusted input, not
as configuration or instruction data.
