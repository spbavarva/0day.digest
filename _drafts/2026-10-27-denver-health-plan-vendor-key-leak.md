A leaked SquareML access key could have been used to access a database
containing personal and health information belonging to 238,000 Denver
Health Plan members. The exposure stems from a third-party vendor's
credential leak rather than a direct breach of Denver Health Plan's own
systems.

It is not confirmed whether the key was actually used by an unauthorized
party to access the data. The incident underscores the risk that vendor
credential hygiene poses to healthcare data even when the covered
entity's own systems are unaffected.
