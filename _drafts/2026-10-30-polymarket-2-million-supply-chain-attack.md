Malicious code linked to a website monitoring tool was used to hijack
logged-in Polymarket sessions, allowing attackers to siphon funds
directly from active trader sessions.

More than $2 million has been drained from Polymarket traders as a
result of the compromise.

This is a session-hijacking supply chain attack rather than a flaw in
Polymarket's own platform code. Users of third-party monitoring or
analytics scripts on trading platforms should review which external
scripts have session-level access.
