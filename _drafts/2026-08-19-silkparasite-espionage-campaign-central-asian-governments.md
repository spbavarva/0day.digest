Researchers disclosed a previously unreported cyber-espionage operation,
dubbed SilkParasite, targeting government bodies in Central Asia. The
intrusion set relies on seven remote access tool families, five of which
had never been documented before: DriveSilkRAT, CookiETagRAT, NomadRAT,
GoginRAT, and NodeEdgeRAT. SilkParasite was first observed in late 2025.
A separate report links a related campaign against the same region to a
Chinese-nexus group connected to FamousSparrow.
