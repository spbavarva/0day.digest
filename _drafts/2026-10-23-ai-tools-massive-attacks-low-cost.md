Dark Reading describes two campaigns illustrating how cheap AI-driven
attacks have become: one targeted 105 sites using open-weight models,
and another achieved full domain control using public models for $12.

The findings suggest the cost of running broad, automated attack
campaigns has dropped sharply as open-weight and low-cost commercial
models become capable enough to drive exploitation and reconnaissance
without custom tooling.
