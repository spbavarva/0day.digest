Researchers disclosed a human-operated phishing platform that impersonates
advertising products for AI chatbots including Google Gemini, Anthropic
Claude, OpenAI ChatGPT, Perplexity, Meta Muse, and Manus. The fake portals
claim to offer ad campaign optimization, spend audits, and business-account
connections. Victims who sign in are prompted for credentials and MFA codes,
which the platform captures in real time. The campaign targets marketers and
ad account managers rather than end users of the chatbots themselves.
