Google's Mandiant and Threat Intelligence Group say the Russia-linked
espionage group Cold Relic (formerly tracked as COLDRIVER and
UNC4057) turned a single cloud account compromise into a chain of
further intrusions, using stolen Google Workspace data to impersonate
trusted contacts and breach a second US-based organization.

The group leveraged information stolen from cloud accounts to craft
convincing impersonation attempts against people in their victims'
professional networks, extending the reach of an initial compromise
well beyond the original target.

The campaign shows how a single compromised cloud account can cascade
into third-party compromises when attackers weaponize stolen contact
and identity data for further social engineering.
