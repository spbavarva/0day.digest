CISA added 10 vulnerabilities to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities (KEV)
catalog based on evidence of active exploitation.

The batch includes several Danfoss AK-SM 800A Series flaws (path traversal,
improper input validation, improper authentication), an HPE/Juniper Session
Smart Conductor and Router OS command injection vulnerability, and a Mitel
MiCollab unrestricted file upload flaw.

Federal agencies face mandatory remediation deadlines under Binding
Operational Directive 22-01; other organizations running these products
should prioritize patching.
