Kiteworks (formerly Accellion) is urging customers to shut down their
systems for nine hours over the weekend as a precaution, after receiving
threat intelligence from federal authorities about a possible attack.

Chief Information Security Officer Frank Balonis said the company
received "credible threat intelligence" indicating a threat actor may
target Kiteworks systems. No confirmed compromise has been reported yet.
