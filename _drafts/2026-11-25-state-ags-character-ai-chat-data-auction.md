State attorneys general are opposing a proposed Character.ai bankruptcy sale
that would include sensitive personal data from more than 21 million US
accounts, including 2.1 million registered to minors.

No breach has occurred here; the dispute is over whether private chat data
can be sold off as a bankruptcy asset. The case is an early test of how
courts will treat AI companion chat logs as a disposable corporate asset
versus sensitive personal data.
