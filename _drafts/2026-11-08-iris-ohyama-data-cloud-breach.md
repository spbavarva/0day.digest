Japanese manufacturer Iris Ohyama, operator of the Iris Plaza shopping
platform, disclosed that personal information belonging to roughly 7.7
million customers may have been exposed. The exposure stemmed from
unauthorized access to a third-party cloud service the company used to
store customer data.

The scale of the exposure (millions of customer records via a third-party
cloud provider) warrants monitoring for follow-on phishing or credential
stuffing targeting affected customers.
