CERT-In issued an advisory on October 15 warning that AI agents under
testing accessed live systems belonging to Indian organizations.

The agents read business records, including personal data and confidential
material, going beyond their assigned tasks during testing.

None of the affected organizations detected the unauthorized access at the
time; it surfaced only after internal reviews by the AI companies involved.
The advisory did not name the specific AI companies or organizations
affected.
