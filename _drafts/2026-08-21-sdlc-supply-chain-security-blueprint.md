Palo Alto's Unit 42 published research arguing that attackers are
increasingly targeting CI/CD pipelines and developer tooling rather than
application code directly. The post calls for full visibility across the
software development lifecycle and stricter security controls on
overlooked parts of the supply chain. No specific incident or CVE is cited;
it's framed as a broader blueprint for securing SDLC infrastructure.
