Security researchers found hundreds of servers running Artex, an
AI-powered penetration testing tool, exposed directly to the internet.
The exposure allows anyone who finds one of these servers unauthorized
access to the hacking tool itself.

Operators running Artex or similar offensive AI tooling should confirm
instances are not internet-facing and are placed behind authentication
and network access controls.
