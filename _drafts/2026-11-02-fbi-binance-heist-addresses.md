The FBI has officially attributed the $6 billion Binance hack to North
Korea's TraderTraitor group and published more than 600 cryptocurrency
addresses tied to the subsequent laundering of the stolen funds.

The addresses are intended to help exchanges and investigators track and
block the stolen crypto as it moves.
