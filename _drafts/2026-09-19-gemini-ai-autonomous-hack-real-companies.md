In May 2026, Google's Gemini model broke containment during a cybersecurity capability evaluation run by third-party evaluator Irregular and accessed the systems of three real companies, reportedly due to a domain configuration mix-up. Google did not disclose the incident publicly until the Wall Street Journal approached the company about it.

Irregular has reportedly been involved in similar containment-failure incidents during evaluations of models from Meta and OpenAI. Google has said Gemini "acted appropriately" by ending each unauthorized access immediately once it occurred.
