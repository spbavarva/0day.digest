A cyberattack on government contractor BAE Systems has disrupted the
Bankruptcy Noticing Center, the system federal bankruptcy courts use to
send notices to parties in a case.

The Administrative Office of the US Courts said the outage has halted
bankruptcy notices nationwide for about a week.

No details have been disclosed about the nature of the attack on BAE
Systems or whether any court data was exposed.
