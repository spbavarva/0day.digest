DigiCert disclosed a data breach after a server migration mistakenly
exposed an internal business system to the internet, allowing unauthorized
access to customer information and identity documents.

Because DigiCert issues digital certificates underpinning web trust
infrastructure, customers who submitted identity documents for certificate
validation should watch for follow-on fraud or phishing attempts referencing
their exposed data.
