Kaspersky's GReAT team describes a system for automating the generation of
filters used to reduce noise in security alerts, combining a traditional
algorithm, a single large language model, and a group of LLM agents.

The approach is aimed at cutting down the manual analyst effort needed to
tune detection filters as alert volume grows.

The post compares the three approaches in practice but does not include
benchmark numbers or false-positive/false-negative rates in the available
summary.

This is a research write-up rather than a released tool; no public code or
product announcement was mentioned.
