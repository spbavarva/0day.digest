Kaspersky's GReAT team describes an approach to automating filter
generation for security alerts, combining a traditional algorithm, a
single large language model, and a group of LLM agents.

The goal is reducing the manual effort of writing and tuning detection
filters, a task that's traditionally slow and heavily dependent on
analyst expertise.

Detection engineering teams exploring AI-assisted alert tuning may find
this a useful reference point for where LLMs can realistically help
versus where traditional algorithms still outperform them.
