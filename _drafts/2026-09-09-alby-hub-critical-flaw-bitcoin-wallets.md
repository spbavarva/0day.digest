Bitcoin wallet company Alby warned of a critical flaw in Alby Hub, a
self-hosted Lightning wallet, that could let an attacker take over a wallet
and drain its funds — but only where the owner had exposed the Hub to the
internet. The flaw affects versions v1.7.0 through the version preceding the
fix. Self-hosted Alby Hub operators should patch and avoid exposing the
service directly to the internet.
