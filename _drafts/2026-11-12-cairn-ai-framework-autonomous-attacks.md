Unit 42 analyzed how attackers used the open-source Cairn AI agent
framework to autonomously breach targets in the semiconductor, defense,
and civil society sectors.

The research illustrates how agentic AI frameworks — originally built for
legitimate automation — are being repurposed to run unattended intrusion
operations against sensitive industries.
