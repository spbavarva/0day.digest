Attackers remotely defrosted refrigerators at multiple French supermarket
chains overnight in a cyberattack reportedly targeting Danfoss
refrigeration controllers.

The incident caused physical disruption rather than data theft, leaving
affected stores to deal with spoiled inventory.

The report did not specify the entry point into the Danfoss controller
network or whether other operational technology systems were affected.
