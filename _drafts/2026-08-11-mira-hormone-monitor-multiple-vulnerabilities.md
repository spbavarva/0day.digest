CISA issued an ICS medical advisory covering the Mira Hormone Monitor
firmware and its companion Android app, disclosing eight CVEs. Successful
exploitation could let an attacker access unauthorized health profile
information, alter health data, cause denial of service, disclose session
tokens, or take over user accounts. Affected: Mira Monitor Firmware
1.7.1.47 and Mira Android App 4.5.15.4.
