State and federal agencies are responding to a coordinated set of intrusions
that disrupted automated controls at multiple municipal water and
wastewater utilities across Minnesota. The attacks affected operational
technology (OT) systems at "dozens" of utilities. Details on the intrusion
vector and the responsible actor have not been disclosed. Water utility
operators should review remote access paths into OT/ICS environments and
confirm monitoring is in place for unauthorized control-system changes.
