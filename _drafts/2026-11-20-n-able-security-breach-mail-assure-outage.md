N-able said attackers breached one of its servers, causing a major
worldwide outage affecting its Mail Assure and SpamExperts Hosted Cloud
email security services.

A separate SecurityWeek report on the same incident notes the disruption
could have led to credential theft for affected customers.

N-able's email security tools are used by many managed service providers,
so a breach at this level carries downstream risk for MSP clients.
Customers of Mail Assure and SpamExperts should rotate credentials as a
precaution and watch for further guidance from N-able.
