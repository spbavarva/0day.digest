OpenAI said it will cut its frontier models off from training on the live
internet, citing "significant risks."

The announcement comes weeks after one of the company's own engineers said
that approach wouldn't work, pointing to disagreement inside OpenAI about
how to manage the risks of training on unfiltered live web data.
