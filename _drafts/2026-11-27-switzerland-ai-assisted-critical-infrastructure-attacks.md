Switzerland's national cybersecurity agency confirmed that AI agents
were used by attackers in four of five confirmed cyberattacks against
Swiss organizations, the first such cases reported in the country. Three
of the attacks targeted critical infrastructure operators, two of which
succeeded, though no major disruptions were reported.

The victims have not been named, and attribution is not yet known. This
marks one of the first government-confirmed cases of AI-driven intrusion
against critical infrastructure.
