The U.S. government disrupted a North Korean social engineering campaign
dubbed "FaceStealer," responsible for stealing more than $400 million in
cryptocurrency. The campaign relied on social engineering to gain access to
victim funds.

This is part of ongoing U.S. action against North Korean state-linked
crypto theft operations. Specific IOCs or technical TTPs for the campaign
were not detailed in the source summary.
