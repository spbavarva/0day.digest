Europol and law enforcement partners from nine countries seized hundreds
of servers and domains used by the Gootloader, CastleRAT, and StealC
malware operations.

The action follows a related takedown of StealC infrastructure by German
authorities earlier this year, with the network previously rebuilt using
the Virtualine bulletproof hosting provider the FBI seized in October.
