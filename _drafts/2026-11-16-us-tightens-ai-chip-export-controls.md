The US government issued an interim rule tightening export controls on AI
chips. Under the new rule, firms must obtain a license before exporting AI
chips if the hardware may end up providing compute to China or other
restricted countries.

The move targets loopholes that let Chinese companies access advanced compute
remotely rather than through direct chip imports.
