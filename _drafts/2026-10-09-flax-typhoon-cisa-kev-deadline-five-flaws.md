CISA added five security flaws to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog
after they were abused by Flax Typhoon, a China-linked threat actor. One of
the flaws is CVE-2015-3306, an improper access control vulnerability in
ProFTPD with a CVSS score of 10.0.

Federal agencies have until October 11 to remediate the listed flaws.
Organizations running affected software should prioritize patching given
confirmed exploitation by a nation-state actor.
