Pro-Russian hackers published the personal information of Italian
Foreign Minister Antonio Tajani and other senior European officials on
the dark web.

The group is offering cryptocurrency rewards for additional personal
details about the officials and their families, a crowdsourced doxxing
tactic aimed at current and former government figures.

Italian Prime Minister Giorgio Meloni's office confirmed the leak
involving Tajani over the weekend.
