CISA added two vulnerabilities to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities
catalog, citing active exploitation: a critical flaw (CVSS 9.2) in
Collabora Online's Built-in CODE Server, and a critical flaw in Arista
VeloCloud Orchestrator (VCO), tracked as CVE-2026-110447.

Federal agencies are required to remediate KEV-listed flaws within
CISA's mandated timeline. Organizations running either product should
patch immediately given confirmed in-the-wild exploitation.
