OpenAI has parted ways with three members of its safety team after they
leaked private company information in violation of internal policy, per
a Wall Street Journal report cited by The Hacker News.

A company spokesperson said an investigation confirmed the individuals
accessed and handled sensitive company information improperly. No further
detail on what was leaked or to whom has been disclosed.
