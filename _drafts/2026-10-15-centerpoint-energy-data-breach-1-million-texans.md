CenterPoint Energy disclosed a data breach affecting approximately 1 million
customers in Texas. Compromised data includes names, addresses, Social
Security numbers, and driver's license numbers.

The scale and sensitivity of the exposed data — SSNs and government ID
numbers for roughly a million people — puts affected individuals at elevated
risk of identity theft and targeted phishing.
