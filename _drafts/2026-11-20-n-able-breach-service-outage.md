Attackers who breached one of N-able's servers caused a major outage
affecting Mail Assure and SpamExperts Hosted Cloud services worldwide.
N-able provides remote monitoring and management (RMM) software widely
used by managed service providers (MSPs).

Organizations relying on N-able's hosted email security services should
watch for official guidance on the scope of the breach and any exposed
data.
