A new Mirai variant adds capabilities beyond the notorious botnet's usual
functions, including encrypted communications with command-and-control
servers and a "sniffer" that scans for default access credentials on
targeted devices.

The added stealth features make detection and takedown harder for
defenders tracking Mirai-derived botnet infrastructure.
