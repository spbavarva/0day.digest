Researchers dubbed a newly identified cloud attack chain "Greeter"
after discovering that attackers deliberately left "hello" messages in
logs to cover their tracks. The report frames this as a shift toward a
different scale of cloud attack, though the available summary doesn't
detail the specific entry vector or affected cloud provider.

Defenders monitoring cloud audit logs should treat unexplained
greeting-style log entries as a potential indicator of this kind of
anti-forensic behavior.
