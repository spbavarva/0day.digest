CISA added three Linux kernel vulnerabilities to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities (KEV) catalog, citing evidence of active exploitation. One of the flaws, CVE-2025-39682 (CVSS 9.8), is an improper check for unusual or exceptional conditions in the TLS receive path.

Federal civilian agencies are required to remediate KEV-listed flaws under mandatory patching timelines; organizations running affected Linux kernel versions should prioritize patching regardless.
