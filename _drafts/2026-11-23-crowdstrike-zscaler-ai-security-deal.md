CrowdStrike is acquiring Zscaler in a deal reported at $44 billion,
combining CrowdStrike's endpoint protection with Zscaler's zero-trust
network security.

CrowdStrike CEO George Kurtz framed the deal around AI security, saying
the combined company aims to gain greater visibility and control over AI
agents operating across customer environments.
