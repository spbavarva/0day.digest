Gen's H1 2026 Threat Report details two separate attack chains. One combined
compromised business email inboxes with browser manipulation to deliver
banking malware. The other used clipboard hijacking to silently redirect
cryptocurrency payments to attacker-controlled wallets. The report presents
both as representative of active fraud patterns observed during the first
half of 2026.
