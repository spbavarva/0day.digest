CISA reported that more than 100 internet-exposed water systems were
targeted in cyberattacks in July, in the wake of recent Iran-linked
attacks on water infrastructure. The agency released new guidance for
reducing internet exposure of these systems.

Water utilities and other operational-technology operators should use
the guidance to audit which control systems are reachable from the
public internet and remove unnecessary exposure.
