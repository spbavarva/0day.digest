Researchers and partners disrupted BadBox 3.0, an ad-fraud botnet
running backdoors on low-cost consumer devices that was generating an
estimated 40 billion fraudulent ad-bid requests per day.

The malware modules served both ad fraud and residential-proxy
functionality, turning infected devices into relay points for other
malicious traffic. The scale shows how cheap, poorly secured consumer
hardware remains a durable source of botnet capacity years after
BadBox was first identified.
