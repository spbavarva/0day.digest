Four flaws in EPICS Base, control software used at particle accelerators and
fusion research labs, have been patched after being found by an autonomous
research system. The bugs could let an attacker crash control processes at
affected facilities. No exploitation in the wild has been reported.
Facilities running EPICS Base should apply the patched release.
