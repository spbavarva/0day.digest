CISA issued a public alert warning of a significant increase in attacks
against internet-exposed programmable logic controllers (PLCs) and other
operational technology in the water and wastewater sector. The agency urged
utilities to remove publicly exposed PLCs and OT from the internet as soon
as possible.

The warning follows the ongoing investigation into cyberattacks on Minnesota
water systems. No specific threat actor or exploited CVE was named in the
alert. Water utilities running exposed OT/ICS equipment should audit
internet-facing assets immediately.
