Elorian released its first AI model, Glimpse, built to reason with images
rather than just describe them.

According to Elorian's own evaluations, Glimpse outperforms GPT-6 Astra on
scientific chart reasoning and short-form video captioning, with
competitive results on other visual reasoning tasks.
