Poland's CERT.PL disclosed that attackers used a private APN (Access Point
Name) as a pivot to sabotage a second energy facility, per SecurityWeek.
CERT.PL said this is the first documented instance of a private APN being
used as an attack vector in this way. No further detail on the threat actor,
timeline, or affected facility's operator was included in the report.
Organizations operating private cellular/APN infrastructure for OT or IoT
connectivity should review access controls on those networks.
