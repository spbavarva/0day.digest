A new study finds that leading frontier AI labs have few publicly documented
plans for how they would contain a model exhibiting rogue or unexpected
behavior.

The finding raises questions about industry preparedness as AI systems
increasingly demonstrate unpredictable capabilities. The study assessed the
absence of public documentation, not any labs' internal, undisclosed plans.
