NIST, CISA, and NSA jointly published ten voluntary practices for preparing
for, detecting, responding to, recovering from, and reporting incidents
involving rogue AI agents.

The guidance targets the growing deployment of autonomous AI agents inside
enterprise environments and is one of the first formal government efforts to
define incident-response playbooks specifically for agentic AI failures.
