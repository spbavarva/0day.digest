CERT-UA is warning of a campaign that disguises a malicious program as a
Notepad++ plugin to compromise Windows systems. The activity, tracked as
UAC-0099, delivers a payload identified as MATCHBOIL.V2.

CERT-UA attributes the cluster to a Russia-aligned group that has
previously weaponized flaws in WinRAR software. Users should avoid
installing Notepad++ plugins from unofficial sources and verify plugin
authenticity before installation.
