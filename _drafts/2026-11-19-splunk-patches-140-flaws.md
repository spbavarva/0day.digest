Splunk released security updates resolving close to 140 vulnerabilities
across its three core products, including critical and high-severity
issues that could lead to authentication bypass, remote code execution,
and privilege escalation. Splunk deployments typically sit at the center
of an organization's logging and detection pipeline, so a compromise there
can blind defenders as well as grant direct access. No active exploitation
was reported, but the scale of the patch set warrants prioritizing the
update given Splunk's role in incident detection.
