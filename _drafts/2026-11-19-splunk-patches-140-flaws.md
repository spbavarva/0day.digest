Splunk released security updates resolving close to 140 vulnerabilities
across three of its core products. The fixes address critical and
high-severity issues that could lead to authentication bypass, remote
code execution, and privilege escalation. No active exploitation has
been reported. Organizations running Splunk should review the advisory
and prioritize patching based on exposure.
