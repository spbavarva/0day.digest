ServiceNow AI published AutoSynthData on Hugging Face, a method for
generating synthetic training data targeted at enterprise agent use
cases.

The release is aimed at teams building and fine-tuning LLM-based agents
for enterprise workflows who need training data beyond what's publicly
available.
