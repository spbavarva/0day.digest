The US, Japan, Germany, and Australia published a joint report detailing
the scope of North Korea's WaterPlum campaign, in which IT workers pose as
legitimate remote employees to generate revenue for the regime.

Japan dismantled its first known laptop farm supporting the scheme, marking
an expansion of enforcement against the campaign beyond the US.
