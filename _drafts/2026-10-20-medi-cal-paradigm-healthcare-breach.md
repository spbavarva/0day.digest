Paradigm Healthcare Services disclosed a data breach affecting 970,000
Medi-Cal members. The company detected the intrusion in October 2025 but
took nearly a year to begin notifying affected individuals.

The long gap between detection and notification is itself drawing scrutiny
from regulators and affected patients.
