OpenAI fired researchers Jasmine Wang, Tomek Korbak, and Mikita Balesni,
saying they violated "clear policies on handling sensitive information." The
company said an internal investigation found the researchers committed "a
significant breach of trust."

OpenAI is standing firm on the decision following pushback, posting its
defense on X. The dispute has been framed by some as a disagreement over how
the company handles internal AI risk concerns, though OpenAI maintains the
firings were about policy violations, not the substance of any risk
disclosure.
