Microsoft's November 2026 Patch Tuesday addressed 1,259 vulnerabilities —
the second consecutive month with more than 1,000 flaws — spanning 726 in
Windows, 174 in Office, 61 in Developer Tools, 59 in SQL Server, and 27 in
SharePoint Server, plus 165 non-Microsoft CVEs.

Among them is an actively exploited Outlook bug that may expose NTLMv2
hashes, which attackers can use for relay or offline cracking attacks.

Given the exploited Outlook flaw, prioritize that patch first even if the
rest of the rollout takes longer to test and deploy.
