Kaspersky's GReAT team documented a new version of the MacSync macOS
stealer that now includes a backdoor module, alongside new delivery
methods.

The malware specifically targets crypto enthusiasts and developers.

The addition of a backdoor component marks an escalation from pure
credential and data theft toward persistent access on infected Macs.
