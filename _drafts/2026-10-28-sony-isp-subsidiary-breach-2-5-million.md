Sony Network Communications, an ISP subsidiary of Sony, confirmed a data
breach affecting approximately 2.5 million records belonging to current and
former customers. The exposed data includes personal information tied to
customer accounts. The company has not disclosed the intrusion vector or
whether the data has surfaced for sale. Affected customers should watch for
phishing attempts referencing their account details.
