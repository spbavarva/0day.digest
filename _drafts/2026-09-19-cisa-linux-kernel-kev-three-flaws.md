CISA added three Linux kernel vulnerabilities to its Known Exploited
Vulnerabilities (KEV) catalog, citing evidence of active exploitation.
One of the flaws, CVE-2025-39682 (CVSS 9.8), is an improper
condition-check bug in the TLS receive path.

Federal agencies must remediate KEV entries within CISA's mandated
timelines; other organizations should prioritize patching given
confirmed in-the-wild exploitation.
