The extortion group Luna Moth is retargeting law firms that refused to
pay its first ransom demand, using a social-engineering con aimed at
stealing Microsoft access tokens.

Law firms previously targeted by Luna Moth should treat renewed contact
attempts as a known tactic and review access-token hygiene for Microsoft
365 accounts.
