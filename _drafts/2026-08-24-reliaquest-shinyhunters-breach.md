Security vendor ReliaQuest confirmed that an employee fell victim to a
phishing attack, giving the ShinyHunters threat group access to an
internal dashboard.

The company says the impact of the intrusion was limited, though it did
not disclose further detail on what data or systems the dashboard exposed.
ShinyHunters has been linked to a string of high-profile breaches this
year.
