An attacker used stolen passwords belonging to staff at France's tax
administration to exfiltrate tax data on hundreds of thousands of taxpayers
and businesses during June and July. Neither the tax administration nor
France's national cybersecurity agency ANSSI detected the data leaving during
that window. ANSSI's report characterizes the attack as unsophisticated,
attributing its success to weak access controls rather than novel technique.
Organizations should audit for reused/shared staff credentials and ensure
egress monitoring can catch bulk data exports.
