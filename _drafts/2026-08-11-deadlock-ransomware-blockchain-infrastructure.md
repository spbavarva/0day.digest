The DeadLock ransomware operation is running decentralized infrastructure
backed by blockchain services to protect its victim communications and
data-leak site.

By relying on blockchain-backed services rather than conventional hosting,
the group aims to resist the infrastructure seizures and takedowns that
have disrupted other ransomware operations.

This shift signals ransomware operators adapting their infrastructure
resilience in response to law enforcement takedown efforts.
