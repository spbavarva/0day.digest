GitGuardian's 2026 State of Secrets Sprawl Report found that commits
identified as AI-assisted leak credentials at roughly twice the rate of
human-written commits.

The report notes that most of the fastest-growing categories of leaked
credentials are now tied to AI-driven development workflows.
