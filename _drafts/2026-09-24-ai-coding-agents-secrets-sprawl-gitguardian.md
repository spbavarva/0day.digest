GitGuardian's 2026 State of Secrets Sprawl Report finds that commits
identified as AI-assisted leak secrets at roughly twice the rate of
human-written commits.

The report identifies most of the fastest-growing categories of leaked
credentials as now connected to AI tooling and workflows.

The findings suggest secret-scanning and credential-rotation practices
need to account for the pace of AI-assisted development, not just
traditional human commit patterns.
