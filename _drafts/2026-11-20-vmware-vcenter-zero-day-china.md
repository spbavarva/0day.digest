A suspected China-nexus espionage group known as Armory Castle (formerly
UNC5221) exploited a zero-day in VMware vCenter Server, tracked as
CVE-2026-123890 (CVSS 9.8), to gain admin access and steal data starting
in early September 2026.

The flaw is an authentication bypass in the VMware Directory Service.
Organizations running vCenter Server should apply available patches and
check for indicators of compromise dating back to September.
