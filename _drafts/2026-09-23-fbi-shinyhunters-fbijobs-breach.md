The ShinyHunters cybercriminal group defaced the FBIjobs.gov site,
replacing agency imagery with an image of the Pokémon character that
has become the group's informal mascot. The FBI is investigating the
alleged breach.

Details on the underlying access vector and scope of any data
exposure were not included in the source summary.
