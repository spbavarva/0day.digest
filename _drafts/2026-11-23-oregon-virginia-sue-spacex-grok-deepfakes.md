Oregon and Virginia filed separate lawsuits against SpaceX, accusing
it of violating state consumer protection laws by ignoring
industry-standard safeguards and misrepresenting the safety of X's
Grok AI features around non-consensual deepfake imagery.

The Virginia complaint reportedly also names Elon Musk directly. Both
suits add to a growing list of state-level legal actions targeting AI
platforms over inadequate safety guardrails rather than federal
regulation.
