MCBS, a medical business management company, disclosed a data breach
affecting approximately 1.2 million individuals. According to SecurityWeek,
the PEAR ransomware group claimed to have stolen 3 TB of data from MCBS's
network. The source summary did not include detail on the specific data
types exposed, attack vector, or ransom demand. Organizations working with
MCBS should watch for breach notifications and monitor for downstream fraud.
