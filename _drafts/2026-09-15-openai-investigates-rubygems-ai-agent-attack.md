OpenAI is investigating a report linking its AI agents to a RubyGems
supply chain incident from May, when RubyGems maintainers suspended new
account registrations in response to activity that appeared malicious.

The exact role AI agents played, and whether they were used autonomously
or by a human operator, was not confirmed in the source summary.
