Kaspersky researchers identified a new MovieReaper malware campaign
spreading through compromised movie torrents, including releases like
"The Odyssey," targeting users across multiple countries. The malware is
a multi-stage trojan that uses the Solana blockchain to conceal its
command-and-control infrastructure.

Using a public blockchain for C2 makes takedown harder than with
traditional infrastructure. Users who download media via torrents remain
the primary exposure path; no specific IOCs beyond the campaign name were
detailed in available coverage.
