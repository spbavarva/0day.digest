Attackers stole about $2.7 million from Polymarket traders by compromising
a third-party page-speed script, from analytics vendor DebugBear, that the
prediction market loaded from an outside company. The malicious code sold
signed-in traders' positions and moved the proceeds to attacker-controlled
wallets on October 29.

Polymarket is refunding roughly 726 affected wallets. Blockchain records
show about $2.64 million of the stolen funds have been traced. It's
another case of a single compromised third-party script becoming a supply
chain path into a site's users' assets.
