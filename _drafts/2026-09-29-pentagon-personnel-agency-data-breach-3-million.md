A data breach at the Defense Manpower Data Center (DMDC), which maintains
personnel records for the U.S. Department of Defense, has impacted 3 million
people.

Given the agency's role holding sensitive personnel data for DoD staff and
contractors, this ranks among the more consequential breaches disclosed this
year. Further details on the intrusion method and exposed data fields were
not available at time of writing.
