Researchers showed that attackers can embed simple HTML invisible to human
readers inside emails to manipulate AI-powered email summarizers. The
hidden text is picked up by the summarization model and can cause it to
produce misleading or attacker-controlled summary content.

The technique is a variant of prompt injection aimed at AI features that
process untrusted email content on a user's behalf, rather than a
traditional email client vulnerability.
