The Financial Times reports that OpenAI disbanded its preparedness team at
the end of last month. The team's role was to assess whether frontier
models posed serious risks, including misuse for cyberattacks, and to
develop mitigations before those risks materialized. According to FT,
responsibility for that work has reportedly moved elsewhere inside OpenAI,
though the new structure has not been detailed. The change raises questions
about how frontier risk assessment is staffed at OpenAI going forward.
