Russian threat actors previously linked to exploitation of a patched Zimbra
vulnerability are now exploiting a separate flaw in Microsoft Outlook Web
Access (OWA) to maintain mailbox access even after victims rotate
credentials, per The Hacker News.

The activity, which began July 22, 2026, targets US and European government
entities along with the telecommunications, financial, hospitality, and
aerospace sectors. Organizations in these sectors should treat credential
rotation alone as insufficient remediation and check for OWA-specific
persistence.
