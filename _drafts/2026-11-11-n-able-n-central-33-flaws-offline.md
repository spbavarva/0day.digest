N-able took its N-central RMM platform offline after discovering 33
flaws, including alleged zero-days with public exploits that could let
an attacker take over managed endpoints. The company released a hotfix
as one of its recommended mitigations. BleepingComputer confirms the
underlying zero-days were publicly disclosed over the weekend, which
forced N-able to take vulnerable hosted environments offline.

Administrators running N-central should apply the hotfix and review
their environments for signs of compromise given that exploit code is
reportedly public.
