Ledger disclosed that hardware wallets sold through a Southeast Asian
reseller contained a hidden circuit board implanted under the signer's
screen. The implant read user data, including recovery phrases, and
transmitted it to attackers.

The compromise has been linked to an $86 million cryptocurrency theft.
Ledger has not disclosed the reseller's name or how many units were
affected. Anyone who bought a Ledger device through a third-party reseller
in Southeast Asia should verify device authenticity and consider moving
funds to a wallet sourced directly from Ledger.
