Kaspersky's GReAT team published a breakdown of misconfigurations in
virtual environment security, drawing on real-world ransomware cases
affecting ESXi and Proxmox deployments. The research details the
attack layers involved in these virtualization-targeted incidents.

Operators running ESXi or Proxmox should review hypervisor-level access
controls and configuration hardening in light of these cases.
