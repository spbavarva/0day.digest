OpenAI reaffirmed its Zero Data Retention (ZDR) commitment for eligible
API customers, meaning covered request/response data is not retained
beyond processing. The company also previewed "Private Safety
Processing," a mechanism intended to let it apply AI safety review
without compromising the data privacy guarantees ZDR provides. Further
technical details on how Private Safety Processing works were not
disclosed in the announcement.
