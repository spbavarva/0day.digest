CISA has published an advisory for Siemens Reyrolle 7SR5 protection
relays running firmware before V2.70, covering 14 CVEs, several rated up
to CVSS 9.8.

Siemens has released a fixed firmware version and recommends all affected
customers update immediately. Per-CVE exploitation impact was not broken
out in the source summary.
