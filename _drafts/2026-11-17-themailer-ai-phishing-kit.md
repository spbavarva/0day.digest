A commercial phishing kit called "TheMailer" lets buyers plug in their own
AI models to automatically personalize phishing lures, evade spam filters,
and scale campaigns to tens of thousands of targets. Putting model choice
in the attacker's hands means the kit's output quality improves as
off-the-shelf AI models improve, independent of the kit author's own work.

The approach mirrors a broader trend of commodity attack tooling absorbing
general-purpose LLMs to boost conversion rates on social-engineering
campaigns. Defenders should treat AI-personalized phishing as a baseline
capability of commercial kits going forward, not a novelty.
