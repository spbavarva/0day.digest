New York has banned AI chatbot toys for five years.

The ban follows new research from Common Sense Media that found safety
failures in these toys, including exposure to inappropriate content and
risks to children's emotional development.

This is a concrete regulatory action with direct impact on AI product
deployment aimed at minors, ahead of this year's holiday shopping season.
