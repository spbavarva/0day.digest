An attacker operated inside the network of 3BB, one of Thailand's
largest broadband providers, maintaining remote root-level control of
internal machines using the legitimate management tool MeshCentral,
according to threat intelligence firm Hunt.io.

The intrusion targeted subscriber credentials and was discovered after
the attacker left a server exposed on the internet containing their
own tooling. Using a legitimate remote-management tool as a backdoor
made the activity harder to distinguish from normal administrative
traffic.
