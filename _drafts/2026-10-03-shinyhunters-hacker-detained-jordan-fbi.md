A suspected member of the ShinyHunters extortion group, known online as
"Rey," has reportedly been detained in Jordan and is cooperating with the
FBI to help identify other members of the group.

ShinyHunters has been linked to a string of high-profile data theft and
extortion campaigns. No further details on charges or the scope of the
cooperation have been confirmed yet.
