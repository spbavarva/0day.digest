Anthropic said it identified and disrupted industrial-scale attempts to
distill Claude's outputs into training data for competing models,
attributing the activity to seven China-based AI labs including Alibaba,
Moonshot, DeepSeek, Z.ai, and MiniMax. Knowledge distillation is a
legitimate ML technique on its own, but Anthropic characterized this
activity as illicit extraction at scale rather than ordinary research use.
No customer data exposure was reported.
