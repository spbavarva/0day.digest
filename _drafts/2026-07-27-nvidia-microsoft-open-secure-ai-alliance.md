Nvidia, Microsoft, SpaceX, IBM, and other companies have formed the Open
Secure AI Alliance to build and share open-source tools for testing,
auditing, and protecting AI models and agents. The initiative is framed as a
response to mounting concerns over defending against attacks from frontier
models. Notably, OpenAI, Google, and Anthropic are not members of the new
alliance. No specific tools or timelines were detailed in available
reporting.
