Perplexity has launched "Teammates," always-on AI agents aimed at workplace
tasks, positioning the company against OpenAI and Anthropic in the enterprise
AI agent market. Teammates can take on multiple roles within a workspace and
pull from existing projects, skills, and memory already used inside
Perplexity's tools.

The agents integrate with Perplexity Computer, a system that can deploy
agents directly on a user's device. Teams evaluating the tool should review
what data access and device-level permissions Teammates and Perplexity
Computer require before rollout.
