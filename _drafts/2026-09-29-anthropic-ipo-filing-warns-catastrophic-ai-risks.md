A preview of Anthropic's IPO prospectus reportedly discloses mounting
losses, leadership proposals to retain control after going public, and a
warning that the company's AI development plans could "further increase the
risk that our models cause harm."

The disclosures come as Anthropic prepares for a public debut at a valuation
reportedly approaching $2 trillion.
