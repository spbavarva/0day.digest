Uber is notifying roughly 150,000 people after hackers compromised a
third-party services provider and stole names, addresses, and driver's
license numbers.

The breach originated outside Uber's own infrastructure, through the
compromised vendor. No details were given on whether financial or login
credentials were also exposed.
