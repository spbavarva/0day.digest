Uber disclosed a data breach affecting 150,000 people after hackers
compromised a third-party services provider. Stolen data includes names,
addresses, and driver's license numbers.

Because the breach originated at a third-party provider rather than
Uber's own systems directly, affected individuals and any other Uber
partners that share data with the same provider should watch for
follow-on phishing or identity-theft attempts using the leaked driver's
license numbers.
