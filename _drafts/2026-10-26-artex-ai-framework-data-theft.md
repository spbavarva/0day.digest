South Korean police identified seven suspects linked to a cybercrime
operation that breached more than 90 organizations in the country, stealing
8.2 million data records in total.

The group used the ARTEX AI pentesting framework in most of the attacks,
according to investigators. A former penetration tester is among the
suspects. Details on the specific organizations affected and the exploited
entry points were not disclosed.
