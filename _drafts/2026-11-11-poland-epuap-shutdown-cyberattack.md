Poland shut down its nationwide ePUAP government services platform
following a cyberattack. The platform allows citizens to submit
official documents, communicate with government agencies, and complete
administrative procedures online, so the shutdown disrupts a wide range
of government services.

The available reporting does not specify the attack vector or whether
any citizen data was accessed.
