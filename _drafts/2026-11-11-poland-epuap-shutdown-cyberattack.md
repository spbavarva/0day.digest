Poland took its ePUAP government services platform offline following a
cyberattack. ePUAP is a nationwide system used by citizens to submit
official documents, communicate with government agencies, and complete
administrative procedures online.

No technical details on the attack vector or scope of compromised data
have been disclosed yet. The shutdown affects a national-scale digital
government service, underscoring the operational risk such attacks pose to
public administration.
