Palo Alto Networks Unit 42 is tracking numerous waves of vishing (voice
phishing) attacks by Com-affiliated threat actors since August 2026 that
have led to cloud account hijacking, data theft, and extortion.

Targets include financial services, healthcare, legal services, and
construction firms in the US, UK, and Australia. Organizations should
reinforce help-desk and account-recovery verification procedures, since
vishing against support staff is the entry point for these hijackings.
