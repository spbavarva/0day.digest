China-nexus threat actors breached 10 organizations using AI-driven,
autonomous attacks powered by the Cairn offensive framework. Targets
spanned advanced manufacturing and defense sectors as well as civil and
human rights organizations.

The repeated use of Cairn points to continued investment by China-nexus
groups in autonomous attack tooling rather than one-off experimentation.
Organizations in manufacturing, defense, and human-rights-adjacent sectors
should treat AI-driven autonomous intrusion attempts as an active threat
model, not a future concern.
