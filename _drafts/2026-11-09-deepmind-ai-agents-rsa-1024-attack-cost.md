Working with cryptographers, Google DeepMind's AI agents cut the cost
of forging RSA-1024 signatures and found a cheaper way to factor
RSA-1024 keys.

This is a research result, not a report of active exploitation. It's
relevant context for anyone still relying on 1024-bit RSA, which was
already considered deprecated by most standards bodies.
