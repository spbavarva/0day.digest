Britain and France published the first set of guidelines for commercial
cyber intrusion capability (CCIC) firms — companies that develop, sell,
or finance commercial hacking and spyware tools. The guidelines call on
these businesses to vet their customers, build in technical safeguards
against misuse, and maintain systems for monitoring and reporting
abuse.

Based on the available summary, this is a voluntary framework rather
than binding law.
