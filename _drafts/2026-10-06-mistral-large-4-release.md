French AI lab Mistral AI released a preview of Mistral Large 4, a
1-trillion-parameter model with 49 billion active parameters, trained on a
cluster of 3,800 Nvidia Grace Blackwell GPUs. The preview is available via
Mistral's API now; the company says it plans to release open weights by the
end of the month. The model supports two reasoning levels — "none" and
"high" — through the API. Mistral positions the release as an attempt to
compete with both US and Chinese frontier labs.
