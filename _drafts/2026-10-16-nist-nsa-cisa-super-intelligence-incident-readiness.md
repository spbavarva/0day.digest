NIST, the NSA's Super Intelligence Security Center (NSA SISC), and CISA
jointly published Key Practices for Incident Readiness and Response in
Super Intelligence Training and Evaluation.

The guidance outlines ten voluntary core practices to help organizations
that train and evaluate highly capable super intelligence (SI) agents
prepare for and manage SI control incidents.

It is aimed particularly at pre-deployment, cutting-edge SI agents with
reduced safeguards or other high-risk, non-public systems.
