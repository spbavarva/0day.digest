Google Project Zero is replacing its "90+30" vulnerability disclosure
policy with shorter, fixed deadlines, citing AI-accelerated vulnerability
discovery and exploitation as the reason for the change. The move shortens
the runway vendors have to patch before Project Zero publishes technical
details of a flaw.

Security teams that track Project Zero disclosures for patch prioritization
should expect less lead time between private disclosure and public detail
going forward.
