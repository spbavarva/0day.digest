CISA published an advisory (CVE-2026-22755) affecting multiple VIVOTEK
camera firmware models, including the V, C, and FE series. Successful
exploitation could allow attackers to achieve remote command execution,
potentially with root privileges, leading to full device compromise.
VIVOTEK cameras are widely used in commercial surveillance deployments.
Operators should check CISA's advisory for the full list of affected models
and apply vendor firmware updates.
