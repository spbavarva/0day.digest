Google Cloud has made quantum-safe digital signatures (ML-DSA, SLH-DSA) and
post-quantum key encapsulation (ML-KEM) generally available in Cloud KMS.
The launch responds to the approaching threat of cryptographically relevant
quantum computers and follows updated U.S. government timelines for
migrating to quantum-safe signatures. Organizations relying on Cloud KMS for
long-term data integrity can begin transitioning signing workflows to the
new algorithms.
