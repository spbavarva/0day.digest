Nvidia's board announced an independent investigation into CEO Jensen
Huang's relationship with Megaspeed executive Alice Huang, who was
reportedly recruited by China's Ministry of State Security to spy on the
company.

No findings have been disclosed yet. The investigation itself signals
nation-state interest in Nvidia given its central role in the AI hardware
supply chain, though details remain unconfirmed at this stage.
