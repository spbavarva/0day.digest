Signal announced new protections to stop system-level AI assistants from
capturing conversation content on mobile. The update blocks screenshots on
iOS 27 and prevents Siri and Gemini from reading or screenshotting Signal
chats on supported Android devices.

The change responds to OS-level AI assistants gaining increasing access to
on-screen content, closing an exfiltration path that didn't exist until
assistants started indexing what's on a user's screen.
