Attackers compromised a third-party page-speed script that Polymarket
loaded from DebugBear and used it to drain funds from signed-in traders on
October 29, 2026.

The malicious code sold affected traders' positions and redirected the
proceeds -- about $2.64 million in crypto -- to attacker-controlled wallets.

Polymarket is refunding roughly $2.7 million to the 726 wallets affected.
