The scope of a data breach at healthcare SaaS provider CareCloud has grown
sharply, from an initial estimate of roughly 350,000 affected individuals to
3.7 million, according to the HHS breach tracker.

CareCloud provides practice management and EHR services, so exposed data
likely includes patient health information. Organizations that use CareCloud
should watch for updated breach notifications and confirm what data
categories were involved once CareCloud provides further detail.
