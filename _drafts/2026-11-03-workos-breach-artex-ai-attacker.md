Developer platform WorkOS disclosed a breach of an internal sales
application after an attacker used an AI tool called ARTEX AI to gain
access. The attacker accessed business contact records and summaries
of customer communications.

The incident stands out for the attacker's use of an AI tool during
the intrusion rather than for the volume of data exposed. Further
technical detail on how ARTEX AI was used was not included in the
available summary.
