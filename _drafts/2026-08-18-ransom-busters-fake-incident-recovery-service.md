A ransomware affiliate has been observed posing as an incident-recovery
service, offering victims apparent help while actually working to divert
ransom payments intended for legitimate recovery efforts. The technique
lets the actor extract additional value from victims already dealing with
an active incident.

Organizations responding to ransomware incidents should independently
verify the identity of any "recovery" vendor that contacts them
unsolicited.
