A flaw dubbed GhostMap lets a malicious Android app force a device into a
persistent boot loop. The app requires no special permissions and only
needs to be installed to trigger the condition.

Because no permissions are needed, standard app-permission review won't
catch this. Android users should be cautious about installing apps from
outside trusted stores until a fix is confirmed available.
