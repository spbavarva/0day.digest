Researchers report that confused deputy vulnerabilities continue to appear
in Google Cloud and Microsoft Azure. This class of flaw lets an attacker
trick a privileged service into acting on their behalf, allowing them to
acquire administrative-level permissions and bypass the cloud providers'
normal access controls.
