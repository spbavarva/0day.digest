ANY.RUN has identified Wazza, a new phishing kit targeting banking,
government, and manufacturing organizations across the US, Europe, and
Australia.

Unlike simple credential-harvesting pages, Wazza builds traffic
filtering, session management, and access controls directly into the
phishing infrastructure, making the kit harder to detect and take down.
