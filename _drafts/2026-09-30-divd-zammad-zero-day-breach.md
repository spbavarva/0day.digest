The Dutch Institute for Vulnerability Disclosure (DIVD) says its own network
was breached after attackers chained two zero-day vulnerabilities in the
open-source Zammad ticketing system. DIVD says the intrusion showed signs of
AI-assisted exploitation.

Zammad is used by other organizations for support and ticketing, so the same
exploit chain may be viable elsewhere until patches are applied. Specific CVE
identifiers and a patched version were not included in this report.
