The Clop ransomware gang, also tracked as Cl0p, is targeting internet-
exposed PTC Windchill and FlexPLM instances in a new data theft
extortion campaign. Both are enterprise product-lifecycle-management
platforms widely used in manufacturing.

The campaign follows Clop's established pattern of stealing data from
exposed enterprise software rather than encrypting it, then extorting
victims with the threat of a leak. Organizations running internet-facing
Windchill or FlexPLM deployments should check for exposure and
unauthorized access.
