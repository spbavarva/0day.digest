Treasury Secretary Bessent said the US has proposed an AI incident alert
system as part of talks with China.

The administration has resisted calls to slow AI development
domestically, arguing that would let China close the gap with US AI
companies. Details on the proposed alert system's scope or reporting
mechanics were not specified.
