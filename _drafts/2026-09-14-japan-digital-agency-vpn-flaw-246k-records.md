Japan's Digital Agency has disclosed a data breach traced to a VPN
flaw, which may have exposed around 246,000 record rows containing
personal information belonging to government employees.

The agency has not yet detailed the specific VPN product or
vulnerability involved. Affected personnel should watch for follow-up
notices from the agency regarding what data was exposed and any
recommended protective steps.
