The FBI said threat actors tied to North Korea (DPRK) stole more than $400
million in cryptocurrency over the past 18 months through a social
engineering campaign codenamed FaceStealer, which used deepfake video calls
to deceive victims. The announcement was part of a coordinated set of
actions by the U.S. government.

Crypto platforms and individuals handling high-value transactions should
treat video-call identity verification as spoofable and require out-of-band
confirmation for fund transfers.
