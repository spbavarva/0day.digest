Meta disclosed details of its top bug bounty findings for Muse, including
a $300,000 award for a pre-launch flaw that exposed conversations and
authentication tokens on internal test VMs used by Meta staff. Total
payouts for the program have reached $1.2 million.

The highlighted bug was caught before Muse's public launch, underscoring
the value of pre-release bounty programs for AI products that handle
sensitive conversational data.
