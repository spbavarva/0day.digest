Ledger has confirmed that hardware wallets distributed through Southeast
Asian reseller CryptoBilis were compromised with an unauthorized hardware
implant.

The implant was used to drain users' cryptocurrency wallets, with reported
losses exceeding $86 million from victims in Southeast Asia.

Ledger has paused sales through CryptoBilis while it investigates. Anyone
who purchased a device through that reseller should treat it as compromised
and move funds using a wallet generated on verified hardware.
