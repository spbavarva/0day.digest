OpenAI said it identified and disrupted a coordinated campaign designed to
illicitly extract protected reasoning from its AI models.

A core cluster of the activity, dating back to early July, has been
attributed to individuals associated with Moonshot AI, a Chinese AI company
based in Beijing.

OpenAI did not cite evidence that Moonshot AI itself directed the campaign,
only that individuals associated with the company were involved.
