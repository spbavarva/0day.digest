Shipping giant Maersk confirmed data was stolen from its systems after the
WhiteArmor ransomware group claimed to have taken 100GB of data and
demanded $5 million.

Maersk is a major operator in global shipping and logistics, making it a
high-value extortion target; the incident is still developing and further
details on the scope of the stolen data have not been confirmed.
