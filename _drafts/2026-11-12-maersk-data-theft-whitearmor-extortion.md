Shipping giant Maersk confirmed data theft tied to a $5 million extortion
attempt. The WhiteArmor ransomware group claims to have stolen 100GB of
data from Maersk's systems, affecting a legacy system.

The incident is the latest in a string of attacks on ports and shipping
organizations worldwide, following a ransomware attack on Brazil's largest
port last week.
