Early testers of Instinct, an AI assistant with broad access to users'
devices and accounts, are raising privacy and security concerns about its
permissions model.

Reported issues include sweeping data access, broad terms of service, and
the assistant's ability to take action on a user's behalf — a combination
that creates meaningful trade-offs for users who adopt it.

No specific technical vulnerability or incident was cited in the available
reporting.
