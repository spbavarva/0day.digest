The FBI says the open source Artex AI-powered hacking tool was used to
breach networks in under three hours, with attacks hitting a U.S. IT giant
and South Korea's financial industry.

Artex is an open-source, AI-driven offensive security framework that
automates reconnaissance and exploitation, and its availability lowers the
skill bar for fast, multi-stage intrusions. Organizations in IT and
financial services should treat rapid, automated intrusion timelines as
the new baseline when sizing detection and response SLAs.
