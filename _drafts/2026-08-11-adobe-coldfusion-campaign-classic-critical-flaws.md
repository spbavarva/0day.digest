Adobe is urging customers to immediately patch critical security defects
in ColdFusion and Campaign Classic. The flaws could be exploited for
arbitrary code execution and denial-of-service attacks. No confirmed
active exploitation has been reported, but organizations running affected
Adobe products should apply the available patches without delay.
