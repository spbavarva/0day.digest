J.P. Morgan Chase warned customers of a data breach after a cyberattack on
a third-party service provider exposed personal information and marketing
data.

Visa and Mastercard customer information was also affected, pointing to a
shared vendor used across multiple major financial brands rather than a
breach of the card networks or banks themselves.

Neither the vendor's name nor the number of affected customers has been
disclosed. Affected customers should watch for phishing attempts
referencing their bank or card accounts.
