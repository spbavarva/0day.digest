A report from InfraTrust warns that attackers are increasingly
targeting the management systems used to control enterprise
infrastructure, citing several critical vulnerabilities that were
actively exploited either before or shortly after vendors disclosed
them.

The source summary did not name specific products or CVEs. The
broader trend — infrastructure management platforms exploited at or
before disclosure — echoes several other items in this digest.
