BTCPay Server, a self-hosted, open-source cryptocurrency payment
processor, urged users to upgrade after reports of Bitcoin and
Litecoin being stolen from hot wallets on instances running BTCPay
Server 2.4.1 (released July 23, 2026) or earlier.

Investigators found that the attacks specifically targeted older,
unpatched versions. Operators running self-hosted BTCPay Server
instances should upgrade immediately and review hot wallet balances
for unauthorized withdrawals.
