Google shipped an emergency Chrome update fixing CVE-2026-125350, a
high-severity use-after-free bug in Chrome's XML component that is
already being exploited in the wild. It's the eighth Chrome zero-day
patched so far in 2026.

The flaw was reported anonymously on November 6. Google has not disclosed
exploitation details, consistent with its usual practice while the patch
rolls out broadly. Update Chrome to the latest version to close the hole.
