Starting Friday, organizations operating in the EU will have just 24 hours
to notify the EU government whenever they discover a serious product
security incident, under the Cyber Resilience Act's new reporting
requirements.

Security and compliance teams with EU operations should confirm incident
response playbooks account for the accelerated reporting window.
