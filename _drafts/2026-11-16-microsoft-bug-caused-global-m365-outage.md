Microsoft says a software bug introduced in July caused last week's
worldwide Microsoft 365 outage. The bug triggered a cascading failure that
left more than 80% of Microsoft's networking gateway servers unresponsive,
knocking out access to M365 services globally.

The multi-month gap between the bug's introduction and the outage
highlights how latent defects in networking infrastructure can go
unnoticed until a triggering condition surfaces. Organizations dependent
on M365 availability should review their own failover and offline-access
contingency plans given the scale of this outage.
