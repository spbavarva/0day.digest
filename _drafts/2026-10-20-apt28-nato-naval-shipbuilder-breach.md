Russia-linked APT28 (aka BlueDelta, Fancy Bear, Forest Blizzard) has been
attributed to attacks against two NATO member states, including a breach of
a NATO-country naval shipbuilder.

Symantec's Threat Hunter Team, tracking the activity cluster as Swallowtail,
found the group using a backdoor called HOOKEDGE alongside a previously
undocumented reflective loader. Defense-sector targets in NATO countries
should treat this as an active, evolving threat cluster.
