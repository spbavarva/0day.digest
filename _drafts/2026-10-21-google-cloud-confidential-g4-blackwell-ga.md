Google Cloud announced general availability of Confidential Computing on
its G4 instances, powered by NVIDIA RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell Server edition
GPUs. The feature integrates NVIDIA's Confidential Computing capabilities
into Google Cloud's AI Hypercomputer stack, extending hardware-backed
protection to data processed on those GPUs.

Google positions this as protection for sensitive data in use during AI
workloads, including proprietary model weights, user prompts, and
generated outputs, aimed at enterprises running agents or other
sensitive workloads on GPU instances.
