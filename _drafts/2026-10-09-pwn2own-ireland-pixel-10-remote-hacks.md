Three research teams broke into a fully patched Google Pixel 10 on October 8
at Pwn2Own Ireland, a hacking contest in Cork whose rules require every
target to be fully patched. One of the three Pixel exploits earned Ikotas
Labs $300,000, the contest's top prize, making the team the overall winner.

As is standard for Pwn2Own, the disclosed flaws will be passed to Google for
patching rather than published immediately.
