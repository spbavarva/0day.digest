The Pentagon's Defense Manpower Data Center (DMDC) is notifying nearly 3
million current and former military service members that attackers stole
their personal data after breaching its human resources management system.
The breach occurred in October 2025 but is only now being disclosed to
affected individuals. The summary does not specify which data fields were
exposed or identify the attacker. Affected service members should watch for
phishing attempts referencing their military service history.
