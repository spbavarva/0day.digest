Okta's Threat Intelligence team linked a vishing-focused threat cluster,
tracked as O-UNC-053 and previously assessed as affiliated with the
ShinyHunters extortion crew, to a new data leak site called INCEPTION.

The cluster uses voice phishing to trick targets into entering credentials
on fake login pages and approving fraudulent access requests. The link
follows a series of recent FBI arrests targeting alleged ShinyHunters
members.
