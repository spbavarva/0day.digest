OpenAI published details on how it strengthened containment for its
frontier model training and evaluation environments, including the use
of offline environments and high-compute red-teaming.

The post focuses on infrastructure-level controls meant to limit the
blast radius of a compromised or misbehaving training run, rather than
model-level safety measures.
