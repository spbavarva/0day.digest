Google Cloud introduced a new top reward tier of $250,000 under its
Vulnerability Rewards Program, discussed in an October Cloud CISO
Perspectives Q&A with VRP lead Michael Cote. The increase reflects Google's
push to incentivize research into its highest-impact cloud vulnerabilities.
