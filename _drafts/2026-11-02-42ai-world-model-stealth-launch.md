AI startup 42AI exited stealth with $62.5 million in funding and a "world
model" it describes as able to predict human behavior and decisions, even
in situations the model hasn't seen before.

The company is positioning this as a step beyond typical language models,
which respond to prompts rather than anticipate actions. No details on
model architecture, benchmarks, or early customers have been disclosed.
