Russian state-linked hackers ran a zero-click espionage campaign against
iPhone and Android devices belonging to targets in Ukraine, the United
States, and Europe, researchers found. The attackers compromised legitimate
websites as watering holes, fingerprinted visiting devices, then selectively
harvested information from devices of interest using exploit chains
delivered through the compromised sites.

Organizations in the targeted regions, especially those connected to
Ukraine-related work, should treat mobile compromise via third-party
watering-hole sites as an active threat from this campaign.
