OpenAI published guidance on how it plans to comply with EU text
watermarking and provenance requirements. The post covers where
watermarks will apply and how detection will work, and confirms that
detection access will start with researchers rather than the general
public. No specific rollout timeline was given. This matters for
organizations deploying OpenAI text models in the EU that need to
track AI-generated content provenance for compliance purposes.
