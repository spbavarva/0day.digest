pCloud has confirmed that its Pass password manager suffered a cyberattack.
Users reported unknown devices connecting to their accounts despite having
two-factor authentication enabled, suggesting attackers found a way around
the 2FA protection. pCloud has not yet detailed the full scope of the breach
or how the bypass occurred.

Pass users should review connected devices and active sessions, rotate
stored credentials, and watch for unauthorized logins.
