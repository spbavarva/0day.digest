Researchers found exposed infrastructure revealing an AI-driven
intrusion ecosystem used by affiliates of the INC and The Gentlemen
ransomware groups.

The operation centers on an "Autonomous Red Team Kill Chain Framework"
called Phantom, used to run both intrusion and data-extortion
operations. The threat actor is assessed to be Russian-speaking and
operates as both an attack operator and an access broker for other
groups.
