Latvia's road traffic agency confirmed that hackers stole data connected
to roughly two-thirds of the country's population — about 1.2 million
people. The scale of the breach, affecting a national government agency,
has prompted public calls for senior officials to resign. Details on the
attack vector and what specific data categories were exposed were not
confirmed in initial reporting.
