U.S. Coast Guard and FBI personnel boarded an oil tanker in the Gulf of
Mexico following an apparent cyberattack, according to the Coast Guard.
The boarding was intended to "ensure integrity of the vessel's
operational and information technology systems."

No details on the attacker, method, or extent of compromise have been
disclosed. The incident highlights continued interest from actors
described as "foreign cyber actors" in maritime OT/IT systems.
