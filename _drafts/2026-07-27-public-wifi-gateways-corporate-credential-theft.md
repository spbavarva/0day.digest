A threat actor has been using compromised public Wi-Fi gateway appliances to
harvest Microsoft 365 credentials from traveling corporate employees. The
attack targets accounts as employees connect through hacked gateway
infrastructure at public hotspots. Compromised M365 credentials give
attackers a foothold into corporate email and cloud resources. Employees who
frequently connect through public Wi-Fi should use a VPN and enable
phishing-resistant MFA on M365 accounts.
