Researchers found that over the course of a month, 28 residential
proxy services used 6 IP addresses on the White House network, mainly
between 5 p.m. and midnight Eastern Time.

The finding raises questions about traffic anonymization or policy
violations on a highly sensitive government network; no confirmed
compromise has been disclosed.
