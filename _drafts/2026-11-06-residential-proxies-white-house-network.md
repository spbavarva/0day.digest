Researchers identified 28 residential proxy services operating from just
6 IP addresses on the White House network over the course of a month,
with activity concentrated mainly between 5 p.m. and midnight Eastern
Time.

Residential proxy traffic on a sensitive government network is notable
because it can mask the true origin of connections, whether used for
legitimate remote work tooling or something more concerning. No
attribution or further technical detail was available at publication.
