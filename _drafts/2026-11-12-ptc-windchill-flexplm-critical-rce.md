PTC released patches for a critical vulnerability in Windchill and
FlexPLM, tracked as CVE-2026-126325 (CVSS v4 9.2), described as improper
input validation and deserialization of untrusted data that could lead to
remote code execution.

Organizations running Windchill or FlexPLM for product lifecycle
management should apply the patch promptly given the severity score.
