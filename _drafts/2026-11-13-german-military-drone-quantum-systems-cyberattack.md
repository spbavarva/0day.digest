German defense technology company Quantum Systems confirmed it detected a
cyberattack by "hostile actors" in late October that disrupted its military
drone operations. The company has not disclosed the attack vector, scope of
access gained, or attribution.

Given Quantum Systems' role supplying drone systems to European militaries,
this is worth tracking for further technical disclosure.
