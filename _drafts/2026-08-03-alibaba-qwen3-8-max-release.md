Alibaba released Qwen3.8-Max, which it describes as its largest and most
capable model to date, claiming performance rivaling frontier systems from
Anthropic and OpenAI as well as domestic rival Moonshot AI's Kimi K3. The
model is being made widely available to users. This continues the trend of
Chinese labs closing the capability gap with US frontier labs on flagship
benchmark claims.
