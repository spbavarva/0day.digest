New research from Akamai argues that enterprise security teams are
focused on the wrong AI risk. While most attention goes to policing
casual employee use of tools like ChatGPT and Claude for drafting tasks,
the bigger threat comes from a small group of power users.

Akamai found the top 5% of enterprise AI adopters are quietly hardcoding
unvetted AI tools directly into critical business operations — a much
higher-stakes exposure than routine chatbot use.
