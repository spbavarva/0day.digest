The U.S. Department of Justice announced the disruption of two hacking
platforms, QScan and QTRouter, operated by a Chinese state-sponsored
group tracked as QTFY. The tools were used to scan, infect, and exploit
IoT devices, reportedly targeting the Federal Reserve, DOJ, and Senate
along with other critical infrastructure and sensitive networks.

The action also took down associated proxy and routing infrastructure
that provided reconnaissance and operational cover for the group's
espionage activity. QTFY has been attributed to Nanjing Xinjiuwei
Network Technology Company.
