Hundreds of European grocery stores lost millions of dollars after outdated
OT firmware running their cooling systems failed.

The affected chains had outsourced management of the refrigeration firmware
to third parties who did not keep it patched.

The incident highlights the operational risk of neglected OT/ICS patch
management even outside traditional IT environments.
