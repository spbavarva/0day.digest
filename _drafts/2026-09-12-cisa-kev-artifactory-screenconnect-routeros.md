CISA has added five security flaws affecting JFrog Artifactory,
ConnectWise ScreenConnect, and MikroTik RouterOS to its Known Exploited
Vulnerabilities catalog, following reports of active exploitation in the
wild.

One of the flaws, CVE-2026-42016 (CVSS 8.1) in Artifactory, is an
incorrect authorization issue. Organizations running any of these
products should prioritize patching under CISA's KEV remediation
timelines.
