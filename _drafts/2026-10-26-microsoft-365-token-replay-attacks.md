Huntress disclosed a Microsoft 365 campaign using an adversary-in-the-middle
relay to capture sign-in sessions and replay the stolen tokens to break into
email accounts. The activity was observed between July 14 and September 22,
2026.

Huntress detected 497 successful Microsoft 365 sign-in events tied to the
campaign, resulting in 97 compromised accounts across 79 organizations.
