Huntress disclosed a Microsoft 365 campaign using an adversary-in-the-middle
relay to capture sign-in sessions and replay the stolen tokens to break into
email accounts.

Between July 14 and September 22, 2026, Huntress observed 497 successful
Microsoft 365 sign-in events of this type, compromising 97 accounts across
79 organizations. Organizations should watch for anomalous token-replay
indicators and enforce conditional access policies.
