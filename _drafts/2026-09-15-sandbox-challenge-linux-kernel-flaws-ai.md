A $1 million bug bounty sandbox challenge run against Vercel's
infrastructure surfaced real Linux kernel vulnerabilities, with
AI-assisted researchers submitting a high volume of reports. The influx
forced Vercel to automate parts of its vulnerability triage process to
keep up.

The source summary did not name specific CVEs or confirm whether any of
the flaws are being actively exploited.
