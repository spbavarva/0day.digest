The U.S. Senate passed the Health Care Cybersecurity and Resiliency Act of
2026 by unanimous consent, a response to the Change Healthcare breach that
affected roughly 190 million people.

The bill would expand federal cybersecurity requirements for healthcare
organizations; the available summary does not detail specific technical
mandates.

The bill still needs further action before becoming law.
