ESET researchers report that the China-aligned threat actor FamousSparrow
has been deploying a previously unreported backdoor called SparroWocky
against government targets across multiple Latin American countries
since at least August 2025. SparroWocky is described as a modular C++
backdoor.

The campaign focuses on espionage against government institutions in the
region. No specific IOCs beyond the malware family name were included in
initial coverage; organizations with government ties in the region
should watch for follow-up technical reporting from ESET.
