OpenAI is rolling out an invisible, machine-readable watermark called
textGrain for text generated by ChatGPT and Codex, starting with users in
the European Union to comply with the EU AI Act.

OpenAI says the approach "matched or exceeded" other text-watermarking
methods, including Google DeepMind's SynthID, which also underlies the
watermarking Anthropic announced in August. Editing watermarked text can
make the marks harder to detect.
