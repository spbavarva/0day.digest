Microsoft announced Maia 300, its third-generation in-house AI chip,
positioned as the primary AI inference system for Microsoft's own products
and cloud services.

The company says Maia 300 improves cost and efficiency for cloud AI
workloads and is better suited to handle reasoning models and agentic
tasks, reducing Microsoft's reliance on Nvidia GPUs for inference.
