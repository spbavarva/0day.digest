Microsoft announced Maia 300, its third-generation in-house AI chip,
intended to be the "primary AI inference system" for Microsoft's products
and services.

The chip is designed to improve the cost and efficiency of running AI
workloads in the cloud and to better handle reasoning models and agentic
tasks.

The announcement reduces Microsoft's reliance on Nvidia GPUs for inference
workloads.
