Citizen Lab confirmed that the iPhone of Vladan Đokić, rector of the
University of Belgrade and a prominent supporter of Serbia's student
protest movement, was infected with NSO Group's Pegasus spyware.

The infection used a zero-click exploit requiring no interaction from the
target, and was discovered six days before Serbia's October 25 snap
parliamentary election — continuing a pattern of Pegasus deployment against
civil society figures around sensitive political events.
