Threat actors accessed databases containing customer information tied to
SBC Medical Group's clinics in Japan, exposing data on more than 2 million
people.

Details on the specific data types taken and the attacker's initial access
method have not been disclosed.

Given the scale, affected individuals should watch for phishing or
identity-theft attempts referencing SBC Medical Group or its affiliated
clinics.
