OpenAI reaffirmed Zero Data Retention (ZDR) for eligible API customers,
meaning prompts and outputs aren't stored after processing.

The company also previewed "Private Safety Processing," described as a
way to run advanced AI safety monitoring without compromising customer
data privacy.

Enterprises with data-residency or retention requirements should review
eligibility for ZDR and watch for further detail on how Private Safety
Processing handles monitoring data.
