Researchers flagged three active banking trojan campaigns: Manic, a
spyware-equipped trojan; an ongoing Grandoreiro campaign targeting Latin
America and Europe; and an expanded ToxicPanda 2.0.

The campaigns are aimed at harvesting banking credentials and enabling
financial fraud. No specific IOCs or technical breakdown were included in
the source summary.
