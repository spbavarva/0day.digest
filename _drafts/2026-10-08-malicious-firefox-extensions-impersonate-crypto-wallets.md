Researchers have discovered 16 malicious Firefox extensions masquerading
as Rabby and OKX wallet portals, desktop utilities, and browser tools.

The extensions intercept cryptocurrency wallet recovery phrases and
private keys during wallet import flows and attempt to exfiltrate those
secrets. Users should remove unrecognized wallet-related Firefox
extensions and move funds from any wallet imported through a suspect
extension.
