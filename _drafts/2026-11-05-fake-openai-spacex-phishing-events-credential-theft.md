Attackers are sending fake event invitations impersonating OpenAI, SpaceX,
and Anthropic to lure employees into phishing attacks. The campaign uses two
separate phishing-as-a-service (PhaaS) platforms to steal Microsoft and
Google account credentials along with active authentication sessions.

Session-token theft of this kind can bypass MFA. Security teams should flag
unsolicited "exclusive event" invitations referencing major AI or space
companies and treat login pages mimicking Microsoft or Google as a current
threat vector.
