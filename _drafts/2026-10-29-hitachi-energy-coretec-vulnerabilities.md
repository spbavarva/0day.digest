CISA published an ICS advisory covering Hitachi Energy CoreTec5 (versions
≤3.4.0 and 5.0.0) and CoreTec4 (≤3.4.0), affected by OS command injection and
"Dirty Frag" vulnerabilities (CVE-2026-13162, CVE-2026-43284). Successful
exploitation could impact confidentiality, integrity, and availability of
affected devices. Operators should follow Hitachi Energy's recommended
mitigations.
