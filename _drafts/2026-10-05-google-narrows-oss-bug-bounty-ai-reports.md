Google has temporarily stopped accepting new vulnerability reports through
its Open Source Software Vulnerability Reward Program (OSS VRP), citing a
wave of invalid, automated submissions.

The move reflects a broader trend of low-quality, AI-generated bug bounty
reports straining triage capacity at vendor programs.
