D-Link disclosed multiple unpatched zero-day vulnerabilities affecting
its DGS-3130 series managed Gigabit stackable switches and shared
mitigation measures in lieu of a fix.

Admins running DGS-3130 switches should apply D-Link's recommended
mitigations and restrict management interface access until patches are
available.
