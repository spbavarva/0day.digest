D-Link has disclosed multiple unpatched zero-day vulnerabilities affecting
its DGS-3130 series managed Gigabit stackable switches. No fix is
currently available; D-Link has published mitigation measures in the
interim.

Network administrators running DGS-3130 switches should review D-Link's
mitigation guidance and restrict management-plane access until patches
are released.
