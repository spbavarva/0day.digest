CISA added four vulnerabilities to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities
catalog based on evidence of active exploitation: SAP NetWeaver improper
neutralization in data query logic (CVE-2025-42884), Cisco Expressway
hard-coded credentials (CVE-2026-76466), VMware vCenter authentication
bypass (CVE-2026-123890), and Apache Roller deserialization of untrusted
data (CVE-2026-129448).

The Cisco Expressway and VMware vCenter flaws already have dedicated
coverage elsewhere in this digest given the available active-exploitation
detail. The SAP NetWeaver and Apache Roller bugs are newly flagged here as
actively exploited.

Federal agencies must remediate KEV entries by CISA's deadline; other
organizations running any of these products should treat them as a
patching priority.
