Norway's Data Protection Authority fined Xandr, a Microsoft-owned
advertising unit, €8.6 million ($9.62 million) for illegally relaying
Grindr users' data — including information revealing sexual orientation —
to advertisers.

The DPA called it the largest fine it has issued, citing the "very
serious" nature of relaying this category of sensitive data without
adequate safeguards.
