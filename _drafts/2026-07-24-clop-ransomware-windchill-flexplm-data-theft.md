The Clop ransomware gang, also tracked as Cl0p, is targeting
internet-exposed PTC Windchill and FlexPLM instances in a new data theft
extortion campaign. Windchill and FlexPLM are widely used product
lifecycle management platforms in manufacturing and engineering.
Organizations running either product should check for internet exposure
and apply available vendor mitigations.
