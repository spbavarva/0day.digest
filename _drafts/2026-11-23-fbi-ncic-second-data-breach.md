The FBI is reportedly investigating a second data breach involving the
National Crime Information Center (NCIC), a database used by U.S. law
enforcement agencies containing records on criminals, fugitives, and
missing persons.

Details on the scope of the breach, how access was obtained, or what
records were exposed have not yet been confirmed publicly.
