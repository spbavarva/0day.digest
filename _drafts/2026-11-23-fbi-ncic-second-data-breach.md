The FBI is reportedly investigating a new data breach affecting the
National Crime Information Center (NCIC), the database U.S. law
enforcement agencies use to look up criminal records, fugitives, and
missing persons. This is described as the second such breach of the
system.

No details on scope, cause, or timeline have been disclosed. Given
NCIC's role as a core law-enforcement lookup tool, confirmation of
scope and any technical findings are worth watching for in follow-up
reporting.
