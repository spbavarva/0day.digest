CISA added three actively exploited vulnerabilities affecting Cisco, Citrix,
and Fortinet products to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog,
including CVE-2026-20079 (CVSS 10.0), an authentication bypass flaw. Federal
Civilian Executive Branch agencies must apply patches by September 12, 2026.

Organizations running any of the affected Cisco, Citrix, or Fortinet
products should treat the KEV deadline as a floor, not a ceiling, and patch
immediately given confirmed in-the-wild exploitation.
