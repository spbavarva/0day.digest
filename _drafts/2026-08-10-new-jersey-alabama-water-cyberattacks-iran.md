New Jersey and Alabama have joined a growing list of U.S. states with water
facilities targeted in attacks linked to Iran-affiliated hackers, according
to SecurityWeek. The attacks have targeted industrial control systems (ICS)
at water facilities in at least a dozen states. No specific ICS vendor,
vulnerability, or operational impact was detailed in the available summary.
Water utilities should review remote access exposure on ICS/OT networks.
