Flaws in Zoom's screen-annotation feature could let any meeting
participant hijack another attendee's computer. The bug required no
interaction from the victim — no click, no download, no visible prompt.

The flaw worked both ways: a screen-sharing presenter could take over
viewers' machines, and a viewer could take over the presenter's.

Zoom has issued fixes. Users should update to the latest Zoom client.
