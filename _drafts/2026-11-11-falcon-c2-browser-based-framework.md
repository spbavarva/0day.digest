Unit 42 uncovered Falcon C2, a browser-based threat framework targeting
governments through malicious scripts, browser extensions, and an
implant called Antino. The framework is designed to blend in with
normal browser activity, evading traditional network-based detection.

Government and other high-value targets should extend browser-focused
threat monitoring to cover extension-based and in-browser C2 channels.
