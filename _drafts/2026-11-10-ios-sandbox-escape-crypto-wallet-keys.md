A researcher known as forcequit publicly released "worse_query," an iOS
sandbox escape that lets one app read another app's files on iOS 26 and
27. Binance warned this could expose crypto wallet seed phrases, private
keys, and login credentials to malicious apps.

Apple's iOS/iPadOS updates released the day before the disclosure do not
list a fix for this issue. Users running crypto wallet apps on iOS should
be cautious about installing untrusted apps until Apple patches.
