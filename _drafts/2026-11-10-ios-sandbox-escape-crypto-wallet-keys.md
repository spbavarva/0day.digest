A researcher known as forcequit, working with 0xjohnny, published an iOS
sandbox escape that lets one app read other apps' files on iPhones
running iOS 26 and 27. Binance warned that the flaw puts seed phrases,
private keys, and login credentials at risk of exposure to malicious
apps. Apple's iOS updates released this week do not list a fix for the
issue. Crypto wallet users on affected iOS versions should avoid
installing untrusted apps until a patch is available.
