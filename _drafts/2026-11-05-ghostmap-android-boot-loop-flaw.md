A flaw dubbed GhostMap lets a malicious Android app force a device into a
continuous boot loop simply by being installed, without requesting any
special permissions.

Affected devices would need manual recovery or a factory reset to restore
normal operation, making this primarily a disruption/denial-of-service
risk rather than a data-theft one.
