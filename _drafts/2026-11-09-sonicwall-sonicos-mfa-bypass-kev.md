CISA added a high-severity SonicWall SonicOS vulnerability, tracked as
CVE-2026-19868 (CVSS 7.1–7.5), to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities
catalog following reports that it may have been exploited in
real-world attacks.

The flaw is described as an authentication reinforcement weakness that
bypasses MFA enforcement. SonicWall SonicOS admins should patch and
confirm MFA is being enforced as expected.
