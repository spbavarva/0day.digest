Kaspersky detailed the continued evolution of the Cavern (Cav3rn)
command-and-control framework used by Iranian nation-state hackers
targeting entities in Israel. The updated framework uses DNS and Google
Apps Script to blend C2 traffic into legitimate-looking network activity,
complicating detection. Kaspersky's monitoring since December 2025 turned
up previously unreported components that expand the toolset's capabilities.
