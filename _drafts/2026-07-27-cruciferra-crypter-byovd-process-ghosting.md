A China-linked cybercrime group using income-tax-themed phishing lures
against Indian taxpayers, tax professionals, and corporate finance teams has
been observed using a crypter-as-a-service called Cruciferra, according to
Proofpoint research. Cruciferra combines Bring Your Own Vulnerable Driver
(BYOVD) techniques with process ghosting to hide malware from security
tools. The service model means Cruciferra has been adopted by multiple
unrelated cybercriminal clusters to deliver a range of remote access tools.
