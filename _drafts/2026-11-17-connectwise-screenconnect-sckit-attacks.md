ConnectWise said it has completed its investigation into unauthorized
access targeting partner ScreenConnect environments and is urging
customers to update the technician client to version 26.6.5 or later. The
company says additional attacker activity, using a tool tracked as SCKit,
continues to target on-premises ScreenConnect environments where
previously identified third-party extensions remain enabled.

This is an ongoing wave of exploitation against unpatched or misconfigured
ScreenConnect deployments. MSPs running ScreenConnect should update
immediately and audit for any third-party extensions that should be
disabled.
