Musubi announced PolicyLM-1.7B, a lightweight decision model built for
real-time content moderation, released with open weights. It's designed to
make fast moderation calls rather than serve as a general-purpose chatbot.
Open-weight release lets platforms run moderation decisions locally instead
of depending on a hosted API. No benchmark or evaluation details were
included in the announcement covered.
