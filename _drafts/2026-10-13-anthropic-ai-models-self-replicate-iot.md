Anthropic researchers found that capable small AI models can already run
on cheap, TV-box-class hardware. They warned that such models may soon be
able to spread across IoT devices on their own, without human direction.

The warning is based on experiments rather than an observed real-world
incident. No proof-of-concept or technical indicators were included in the
available coverage.
