Researchers at Socket identified 13 malicious NuGet packages targeting
Windows .NET developers.

The packages pose as fake take-home job assessments, mostly mimicking
WinUI 3 and WPF button controls, to trick developers into building or
running them.

Building or running the packages triggers malware delivery, turning a
common recruiting technique — take-home coding tests — into a supply
chain attack vector against individual developers.
