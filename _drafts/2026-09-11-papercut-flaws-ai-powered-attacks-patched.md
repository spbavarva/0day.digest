A Russian threat actor used AI tooling to build, test, and deploy exploits
against PaperCut print management software, targeting hundreds of
organizations worldwide. Separately, PaperCut released a maintenance
update — NG/MF versions 26.0.5, 25.0.13, and 24.1.10 — that replaces
earlier emergency patches for two flaws that have seen active exploitation.
Administrators still running unpatched or emergency-patched PaperCut
instances should move to the new maintenance releases. The case shows
AI-assisted exploit development being used at scale against a known
vulnerable target.
