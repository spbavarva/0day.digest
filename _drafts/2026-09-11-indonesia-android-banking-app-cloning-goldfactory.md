The GoldFactory threat group is running an Android banking app-cloning
campaign in Indonesia, abusing the Android Work Profile feature to deliver
the Gigabud banking trojan. A separate group, Mantax Otax, is spreading
cloned banking apps through its own campaign. Both rely on tricking victims
into installing malicious clones of legitimate banking apps outside
official app stores. Users should avoid sideloading banking apps and verify
installs come from official app store listings.
