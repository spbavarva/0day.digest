South Korea's Financial Services Commission (FSC) held an emergency
meeting after a series of cyberattacks hit the country's financial
institutions, with investigators examining whether AI-powered
techniques were involved. No specific bank names, breach scope, or
technical IOCs have been disclosed yet. The incident reflects growing
regulatory concern about AI-assisted attacks against financial
infrastructure. More detail is expected as the FSC's investigation
proceeds.
