The UK's MI5 issued a "Security Service Espionage Alert" on September 30
warning that more than 100 academics have assisted China's intelligence
gathering efforts on behalf of the Ministry of State Security.

MI5 says the China General Technology Research Institute (CGTRI) primarily
exists to fund research that benefits Beijing's state security apparatus.
No specific technical IOCs were included in the alert.
