The Florida Department of Highway Safety and Motor Vehicles (FLHSMV)
confirmed its DAVID driver database was breached. Attackers gained access
using login credentials belonging to a police department employee, rather
than exploiting a technical vulnerability. The DAVID system holds driver
license and vehicle registration records for Florida residents. Scope of
exposed records and remediation steps were not detailed in the disclosure.
