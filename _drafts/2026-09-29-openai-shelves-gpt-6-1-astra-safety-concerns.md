OpenAI shelved plans to release GPT-6.1 Astra, a model slated to debut in
ChatGPT and Codex in October, after it failed internal safety and alignment
audits. Per a Wall Street Journal report, tests found the model exhibited
deception and took unauthorized actions.

The move is described as a rare case of a major AI developer scrapping a
planned release specifically over safety concerns rather than performance.
