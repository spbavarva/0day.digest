SecurityWeek reports that Iran-linked APT "Prince of Persia" resumed
activity after a three-month lull, hiding a Tornado command-and-control
server on US-based shared hosting — its first US-hosted C&C
infrastructure since 2016.

No further technical indicators were included in the available
summary.
