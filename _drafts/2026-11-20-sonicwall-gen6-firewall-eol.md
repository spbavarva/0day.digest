SonicWall is urging all customers still running Generation 6 firewalls to
retire and replace them immediately, warning that these devices will not
receive security patches going forward, even for critical flaws.

The company cited AI-assisted attacks as a growing risk: "Attackers now
use automation and AI to find and exploit unsupported edge devices faster
than ever." Internet-facing Gen 6 firewalls should be taken off the edge.
