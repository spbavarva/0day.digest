Dell has patched two maximum-severity vulnerabilities in its Container
Storage Modules (CSM), which connect Dell enterprise storage arrays to
Kubernetes environments.

The flaws could give an attacker admin-level privileges. Dell is urging
admins running CSM-connected storage in Kubernetes clusters to patch
immediately.
