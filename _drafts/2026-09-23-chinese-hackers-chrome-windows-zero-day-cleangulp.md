A Chinese threat actor tracked as UTA0565 has been observed
exploiting a recently disclosed Google Chrome-Microsoft Windows
exploit chain as zero-days via fake websites, deploying malware
dubbed CLEANGULP. The attacks, detected September 3-4, 2026, chained
two vulnerabilities in Chrome (CVE-2026-85046, CVE-2026-87491) with
one affecting Windows Advanced Local Procedure Call (CVE-2026-85880).

Organizations should ensure Chrome and Windows patches addressing
these CVEs are fully deployed and watch for indicators associated
with CLEANGULP.
