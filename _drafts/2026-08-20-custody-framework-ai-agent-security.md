Security researcher Jake Williams released a new open framework called
CUSTODY designed to constrain what agentic AI systems can do once deployed
inside a network. Williams discussed the framework on the Dark Reading News
Desk, saying the release was motivated in part by recent agentic AI
incidents referenced in the interview. The source summary did not include
further technical detail on CUSTODY's enforcement mechanisms.
