Google used an AI agent called PageBreak to find 500 flaws across its own
web applications, combining AI-driven analysis with deterministic validation
to confirm exploitability and assess risk. The approach reflects a broader
industry trend of pairing AI agents with traditional validation methods to
cut down on the false positives typical of pure LLM-based vulnerability
scanning. Details on the types of flaws found or remediation timelines were
not disclosed in the coverage.
