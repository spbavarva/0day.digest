Chinese state-linked hackers chained the PHP2Shell and Fragnesia exploits to
gain root access on firewall appliances, SecurityWeek reported.

The campaign compromised two servers belonging to a US government-affiliated
organization.

Details on the affected firewall vendor and patch availability were not
specified in the initial report. Organizations should watch for follow-up
advisories referencing PHP2Shell or Fragnesia.
