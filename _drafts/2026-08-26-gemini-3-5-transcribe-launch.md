Google DeepMind launched Gemini 3.5 Transcribe, a new speech-to-text
model built for more intelligent transcription. It automatically detects
specialized jargon, supports more than 85 languages, and edits out
filler words like "um" and "ah".

The release follows Gemini 3.5 Live Translate and arrives ahead of the
still-unreleased Gemini 3.5 Pro model.
