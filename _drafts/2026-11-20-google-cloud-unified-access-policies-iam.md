Google Cloud launched Unified Access Policies (UAPs), a new IAM
access-control engine now in preview at no additional cost.

UAPs consolidate allow and deny rules into a single schema and decouple
policy definition from resource enforcement, aimed at organizations
managing access across thousands of autonomous agents and microservices.

The goal is to let administrators enforce least privilege without the
operational bottleneck of managing strict, resource-by-resource IAM rules
as cloud footprints scale.
