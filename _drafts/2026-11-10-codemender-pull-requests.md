Google published guidance on running CodeMender, its agentic
vulnerability-fixing tool, directly in pull requests rather than only
flagging issues after the fact. The post cites Mandiant's M-Trends 2026
data showing average developer fix time for software flaws has grown
from roughly 59 days a decade ago to more than 170 days in 2025, as
development velocity has outpaced manual remediation. Integrating
automated fixes into the PR workflow is Google's proposed way to close
that growing gap.
