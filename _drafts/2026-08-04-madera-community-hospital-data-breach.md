An extortion group breached Madera Community Hospital's network and stole
personal, financial, and medical information belonging to roughly 150,000
individuals.

The incident is another example of extortion actors targeting healthcare
providers to monetize sensitive patient data. Affected individuals should
watch for phishing attempts and monitor for fraudulent use of their medical
and financial records.
