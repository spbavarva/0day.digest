Cisco patched CVE-2026-76418 (CVSS 8.6), a flaw in the Remote Access SSL
VPN service of its Secure Firewall ASA and FTD software.

Unauthenticated remote attackers have been exploiting the flaw in the
wild to force vulnerable devices to reload or crash. It's the second
ASA/FTD zero-day exploited since August. Organizations running ASA or
FTD should patch immediately.
