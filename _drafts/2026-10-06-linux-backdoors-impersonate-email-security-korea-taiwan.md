Linux backdoors targeting telecom and network appliances in South Korea and
Taiwan are disguising their traffic as email security services to blend in
with legitimate processes. The technique follows a known defense-evasion
pattern of naming malicious binaries and processes after legitimate
operating system components. By mimicking recognizable software names, the
backdoors aim to avoid scrutiny from administrators and security tooling
reviewing running processes.
