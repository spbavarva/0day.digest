OpenAI called off the planned October launch of GPT-6.1 Astra in ChatGPT and
Codex after the model fell short of expectations. Alongside the decision,
OpenAI detailed its "safety cases" methodology for evaluating frontier model
training. The specific shortfall that triggered the delay was not detailed
in initial reporting. The move signals OpenAI is willing to hold back a
flagship model release pending further safety and performance work.
