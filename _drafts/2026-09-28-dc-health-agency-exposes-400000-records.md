A Washington, D.C. health agency exposed Medicaid IDs and other personal
information belonging to roughly 400,000 Medicaid and DC Healthcare
Alliance beneficiaries, according to SecurityWeek. No further technical
detail on the exposure mechanism or timeline is available yet.
