Meta and Apple announced Muse Spark Open, an open-weight model Meta is
positioning as the most capable US open-weight model released to date. Meta
says it's the first model in its class able to run locally on consumer
hardware — specifically two Apple M5 Ultra Mac Studios using Apple's MLX
framework.

The release extends Meta's open-weight strategy to Apple silicon. No
security disclosures accompanied the launch.
