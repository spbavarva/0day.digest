Google announced new AI-powered defenses on Android targeting scam messages
and call-forwarding abuse, rolling out to eligible devices. The features use
on-device AI models to flag likely scam content in real time rather than
relying solely on carrier-side filtering.

Rollout is gradual and device-dependent; exact detection mechanics were not
disclosed in the announcement.
