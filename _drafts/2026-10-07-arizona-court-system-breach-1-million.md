The Arizona Supreme Court disclosed that a cyberattack on its court system
resulted in the theft of personal information belonging to more than 1
million people.

The exposed data reportedly includes records dating back as far as 30
years. Specific data types and the attack vector were not detailed in the
source summary.

Affected individuals should watch for identity-theft and phishing attempts
referencing court case details.
