A researcher/threat actor going by "Nightmare-Eclipse" has published a new
zero-day exploit dubbed ShieldCrash against Windows Defender, continuing an
ongoing campaign against Microsoft. The exploit reportedly grants full
SYSTEM privileges on Windows machines running the current September 2026
patches, with no fix yet available.

No confirmed in-the-wild exploitation by other threat actors has been
reported, but the public exploit raises risk for organizations relying on
Defender as primary endpoint protection.
