CISA published an ICS advisory (CVSS v3 8.1) for Acrisure's KARR BT and
DR-100 devices, covering firmware versions prior to July 20, 2026. The
flaw is a use of hard-coded cryptographic key that could allow an
attacker to perform unauthorized vehicle control operations.

Affected devices are deployed worldwide in the Transportation Systems
critical infrastructure sector. Operators should apply the vendor's
firmware update once available.
