Japan dismantled its first identified North Korean "laptop farm," an
operation used to let North Korean IT workers pose as remote employees at
Western companies.

The US, Japan, Germany, and Australia jointly published a report
detailing the scope of North Korea's WaterPlum campaign, signaling
expanding international law-enforcement coordination against DPRK IT
worker fraud schemes.
