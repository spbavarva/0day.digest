Base Labs, a research group spun out of Baseten earlier this year,
announced an open-weight AI safety partnership with Hugging Face and
Goodfire. The partnership will develop and publish methods for training
and monitoring open models.

No specific technical deliverables, timelines, or benchmark commitments
were disclosed alongside the announcement. Watch for published methods
from this partnership as a potential resource for teams evaluating
open-weight model safety.
