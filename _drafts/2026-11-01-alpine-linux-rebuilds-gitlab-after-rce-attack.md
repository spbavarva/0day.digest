Hackers attempted to exploit a critical GitLab vulnerability against Alpine
Linux's development infrastructure on October 20-21, 2026.

The Alpine Linux project rebuilt its GitLab server from scratch and reset
all passwords as a precaution against a possible compromise of its
development pipeline.

No further technical detail on the exploited GitLab flaw, or evidence of a
successful breach, has been disclosed.
