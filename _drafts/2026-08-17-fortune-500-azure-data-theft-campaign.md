A threat actor is claiming to have exfiltrated millions of records from a
list of Fortune 500 organizations, including McDonald's, TCS, and Vodafone,
in a campaign targeting Azure environments. SecurityWeek's report did not
specify the initial access vector, and the claims have not been
independently confirmed by the named companies. Organizations running
workloads in Azure should review access logs and storage configurations for
signs of unauthorized data access. Treat the scope of the claimed breach as
unconfirmed until affected companies validate it.
