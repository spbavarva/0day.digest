Ubiquiti released patches for three new maximum-severity vulnerabilities
that can reportedly be exploited remotely without any privileges. No
public exploitation has been reported yet.

Given the remote, unauthenticated exploitability, administrators running
affected Ubiquiti equipment should prioritize patching promptly rather
than waiting for a routine maintenance window.
