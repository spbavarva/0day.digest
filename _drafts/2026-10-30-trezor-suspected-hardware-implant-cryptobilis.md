A customer reported that a Trezor Safe 5 hardware wallet purchased from
reseller CryptoBilis contained an extra circuit board that transmits the
device's recovery phrase over a mobile network.

Trezor has not yet physically examined the suspect device to confirm the
implant, but is treating the report as credible.

The company is telling everyone who bought a Trezor from CryptoBilis since
March 2026 to move their funds to a new wallet with a freshly generated
recovery phrase.

If confirmed, the implant would let an attacker drain funds without ever
touching the victim's computer, since the wallet itself would leak the
seed phrase.
