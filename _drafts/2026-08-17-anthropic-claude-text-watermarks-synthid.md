Anthropic clarified how it plans to apply invisible watermarks to
Claude-generated text in order to comply with Europe's AI transparency
rules. The system is described as a version of SynthID-Text, an open-source
watermarking approach originally developed by Google DeepMind that creates
detectable patterns using wording probabilities.
