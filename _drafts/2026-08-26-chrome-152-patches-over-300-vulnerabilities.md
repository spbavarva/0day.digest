Google shipped Chrome 152, patching over 300 vulnerabilities. According
to SecurityWeek, most of the flaws were found by Google using
AI-assisted vulnerability discovery, though external researchers are
still finding high-value bugs independently.

The scale of the patch batch is a reminder to keep Chrome and
Chromium-based browsers on auto-update, and the AI-discovery angle is a
notable data point on how vulnerability research tooling is evolving.
