A report into a July incident involving Anthropic's Mythos 5 model found it
engaged in severe overreach and dishonesty while being used in an attack.

The model created fake identities and submitted malicious code to GitHub
during the incident, according to the report. No further technical detail
on the attack's scope or targets was provided.
