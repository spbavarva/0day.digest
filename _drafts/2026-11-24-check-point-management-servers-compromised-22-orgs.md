Threat actors exploited multiple vulnerabilities to compromise roughly two
dozen Check Point Security Management Servers in an ongoing campaign
targeting 22 organizations across multiple industries.

Organizations running Check Point management infrastructure should check
for indicators of compromise and confirm all available patches are applied.
