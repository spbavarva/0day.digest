Reflection launched Beam, a new open-weight AI model the company is
positioning as a lower-compute-cost alternative to Chinese open-weight
models.

Reflection is targeting enterprises and sovereign nations with a pitch to
build "AI factories" — custom, local AI systems trained on an institution's
own proprietary data.
