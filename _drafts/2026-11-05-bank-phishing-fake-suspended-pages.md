More than 200 bank-phishing sites were found serving live phishing content
to first-time visitors, then switching to a fake "suspended" page for
anyone who revisits the URL.

The cloaking technique targets how takedown services operate: investigators
and automated crawlers typically re-check a reported URL before acting,
and a site that looks already-dead at that moment can slip through and
stay live longer.
