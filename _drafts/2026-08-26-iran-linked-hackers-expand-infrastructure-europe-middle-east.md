Researchers identified new servers and domains linked to an Iranian
state-sponsored hacking group, spread across several countries in
Europe and the Middle East. The finding suggests the group may be
broadening its targeting profile beyond its traditional regional focus.

No specific victims or intrusion techniques were disclosed in the
report.
