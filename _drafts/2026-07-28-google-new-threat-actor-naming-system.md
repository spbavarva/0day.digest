Google introduced a new two-word naming convention for threat actors,
combining a memorable term used in public reporting with a
cluster-categorization word. The change standardizes how Google's threat
intelligence teams refer to tracked groups across reports. Practitioners
relying on Google/Mandiant threat intel should expect a transition period
as old and new naming schemes coexist.
