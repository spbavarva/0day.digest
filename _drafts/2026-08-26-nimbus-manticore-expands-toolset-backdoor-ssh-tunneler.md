Group-IB researchers discovered previously undocumented malware and
infrastructure tied to Nimbus Manticore, an Iranian state-sponsored
group affiliated with the IRGC. The new toolset includes a backdoor
similar to the group's known TWOSTROKE malware, plus an SSH tunneler.

Group-IB describes Nimbus Manticore as among the most active Iranian
APT groups in 2026, indicating continued investment in its cyber
espionage capability.
