Google built an AI agent harness to search Chrome's codebase for
vulnerabilities, and it surfaced a flaw that had gone undetected for 13
years. The discovery comes amid a record pace of Chrome patching this year.

No CVE number, exploitability details, or confirmation of active
exploitation were included in the available summary. The finding is
notable primarily as a demonstration of AI-assisted vulnerability research
at scale in a large, mature codebase.
