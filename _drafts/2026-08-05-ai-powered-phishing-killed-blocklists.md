AI is helping attackers stand up disposable phishing infrastructure and
rapidly evolving toolkits faster than domain and signature blocklists can
track them.

Push Security argues that browser-level, technique-based detection offers a
more durable defense than relying on known-bad domains or indicators, since
those indicators now churn faster than blocklists can be updated.
