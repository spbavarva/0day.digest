Google and industry partners dismantled a botnet built from more than 1.3
million Android TV set-top boxes that shipped with malicious software
secretly renting out users' internet connections and conducting ad
fraud, according to Krebs on Security. The takedown is expected to sever
the botnet's access to more than 10 million additional compromised
systems, including other IoT devices such as Android-based healthcare
equipment and karaoke machines.
