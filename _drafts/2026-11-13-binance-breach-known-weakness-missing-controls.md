Analysis of the Binance breach found that attackers exploited a known
weakness combined with missing security controls to gain access, complicated
by blockchain-specific factors such as attacker-deployed "sweepers" that
automatically drain funds from compromised accounts. Dark Reading notes
these factors hamper the controls exchanges normally rely on to limit damage
after a compromise.

Crypto exchanges should treat automated fund-sweeping as a near-certain
follow-on action after any account compromise and prioritize detection and
response speed accordingly.
