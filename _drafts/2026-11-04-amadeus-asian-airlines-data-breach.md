Japan Airlines, Cathay Pacific, Korean Air, Asiana Airlines, Singapore Airlines,
and Malaysia Airlines have all disclosed data breaches over the past week,
tracing back to a single root cause: an incident at Amadeus IT Group, which
builds and operates flight reservation, ticketing, and check-in platforms
widely used across the airline industry.

Because the exposure stems from a shared third-party provider rather than
each airline's own systems, the breach fanned out across six major carriers
at once. No technical details on the nature of the Amadeus compromise were
available in the source reporting.
