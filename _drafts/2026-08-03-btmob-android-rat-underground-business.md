Flare researchers analyzed thousands of underground forum posts to map how
the BTMOB Android RAT operation evolved from a single malware family into a
fragmented ecosystem of resellers, source-code vendors, and custom build
providers competing across multiple sales channels. The findings illustrate
how mobile malware-as-a-service markets are maturing and diversifying
distribution, making a single takedown less effective against the broader
operation.
