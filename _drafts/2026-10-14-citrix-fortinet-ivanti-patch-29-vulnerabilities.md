Citrix, Fortinet, and Ivanti shipped patches for 29 vulnerabilities across
their product lines, including critical-severity flaws that could lead to
authentication bypass, remote code execution, and leaks of sensitive
authentication information.

Given the history of these vendors' edge devices being targeted shortly
after disclosure, organizations running affected Citrix, Fortinet, or
Ivanti products should prioritize patching.
