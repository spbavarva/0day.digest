Employees are increasingly running personal AI agents inside corporate
environments without IT's knowledge, creating a shadow-AI governance gap.

Amazon has begun blocking Meta's Muse agent because it doesn't identify
itself as a bot while browsing, and other personal AI agents have raised
ongoing privacy concerns.

The piece frames this as an emerging identity problem: agents acting on
employees' behalf are effectively already logged into corporate systems.
