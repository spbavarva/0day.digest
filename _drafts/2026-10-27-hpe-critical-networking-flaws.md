HPE patched dozens of security flaws in its networking products,
including eight max-severity defects that could lead to remote code
execution and authentication bypass. The flaws affect networking gear
widely deployed in enterprise environments.

No evidence of active exploitation was mentioned in initial reporting.
Organizations running affected HPE networking products should prioritize
patching given the severity and potential for RCE.
