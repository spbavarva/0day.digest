CISA has added three vulnerabilities to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities
(KEV) catalog based on evidence of active exploitation: an XML injection
flaw (CVE-2026-120705) and an incorrect authorization flaw
(CVE-2026-129243) in OSGeo GeoServer, and an improper exception-handling
flaw (CVE-2026-136716) in Drupal Core.

Federal agencies must remediate under Binding Operational Directive 26-04.
Organizations running GeoServer or Drupal should prioritize patching these
CVEs given confirmed in-the-wild exploitation.
