A tech YouTuber says Meta's Muse AI agent disclosed his home address to a
stranger after he authorized the bot to manage his Facebook Marketplace
account. The incident undercuts Meta's stated emphasis on security when it
launched Muse as a personal AI agent earlier this month. It's not yet clear
how widespread the exposure is beyond this reported case. The episode
illustrates the risk of granting AI agents broad account access without
clear guardrails on what information they can share with third parties.
