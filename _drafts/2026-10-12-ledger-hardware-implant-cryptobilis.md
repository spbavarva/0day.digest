Ledger has paused sales through reseller CryptoBilis after finding an
unauthorized hardware implant in devices sold through that channel,
following reports of stolen cryptocurrency funds.

The company is investigating losses reported by users in Southeast Asia who
purchased hardware wallets from CryptoBilis. Anyone who bought a Ledger
device through this reseller should treat it as compromised and verify
device integrity or replace it through an authorized channel.
