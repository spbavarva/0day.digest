Tailscale is patching almost a dozen security vulnerabilities in Tailscale
SSH that could be exploited to steal credentials, crash servers, and gain
root privileges. Details of the flaws, collectively referred to as
"Tailworm," were disclosed publicly over the weekend before patches were
available, prompting Tailscale to rush out fixes.

Organizations using Tailscale SSH should prioritize applying patches as
soon as they're released given the public availability of vulnerability
details ahead of the fix.
