Hackers compromised an external cloud service used by shopping
platform Iris Plaza and may have stolen personal information
belonging to as many as 7.7 million customers.

The breach originated through a third-party cloud service rather than
Iris Plaza's own infrastructure, underscoring the risk of vendor-side
compromises reaching downstream customer data.
