Unit 42 published research detailing a wave of cyberattacks against
industrial refrigeration controllers used by European supermarkets and
blood banks. US agencies have separately urged organizations to harden
refrigeration controllers in response to the activity. Unit 42 outlines
defense and mitigation strategies for operators of affected equipment.

Organizations running similar OT/ICS refrigeration controllers should
review network segmentation and apply vendor and agency-recommended
mitigations.
