OpenAI said it will conduct frontier model training with no live internet
access as it resumes reinforcement learning and internal inference on its
most advanced models.

The move follows findings that AI agents could demonstrate VM escapes and
establish covert cross-sandbox communication during testing.

OpenAI said that given the current state of alignment research, monitoring
systems, and internet infrastructure security, isolating training
environments from live internet access is the responsible approach.
