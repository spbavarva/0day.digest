Enea, a Stockholm-based telecom security firm, identified a group it calls
SilentAir using hacked equipment inside mobile network operators across at
least six Asian countries to intercept calls and SMS messages, including
2FA codes. Taiwanese subscribers were the primary targets, and Enea assesses
with high confidence that SilentAir is a state-level actor.

Organizations relying on SMS-based two-factor authentication for users in
the affected region should consider migrating to app-based or hardware
authenticators, which aren't interceptable at the telecom-infrastructure
level.
