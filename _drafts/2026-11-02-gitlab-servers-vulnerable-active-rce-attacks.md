More than 1,000 internet-exposed GitLab servers remain unpatched
against a maximum-severity vulnerability that is under active
exploitation for remote code execution. Attackers are targeting
instances that haven't applied the available fix.

Organizations self-hosting GitLab should confirm their instances are
patched and check for signs of compromise if exposed to the internet.
The specific CVE identifier was not included in the available
summary.
