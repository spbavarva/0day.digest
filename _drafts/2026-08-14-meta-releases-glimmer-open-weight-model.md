Meta released Glimmer this week, an open-weight AI model that anyone can
download and run on their own hardware. The release contrasts with Muse
Spark, Meta's more capable model that remains accessible only through
Meta's own APIs.

The launch coincided with a letter from Mark Zuckerberg arguing that AI
should be "for everyone" rather than concentrated among a handful of labs.
