A developer account tied to OVHcloud's command-line tool was compromised
in March using a GlassWorm-linked loader, according to researchers. The
hijacked account was used to plant an infostealer in the OVHcloud CLI,
putting users who installed the affected version at risk of credential
theft.

Anyone who installed OVHcloud CLI updates during the affected window
should audit for stealer activity and rotate any credentials that may
have been exposed.
