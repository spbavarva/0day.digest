Fireworks AI disclosed that its investigation into a security incident
first reported in early October has uncovered further malicious activity.
The expanded scope now includes account and billing information, secrets,
datasets, and incomplete model weights for a limited set of accounts,
along with Notion AI chat data. The company said unauthorized access to
internal Fireworks systems was detected and affected customers have been
notified. Organizations using Fireworks AI should review the vendor's
notice and rotate any shared secrets or API keys that may be affected.
