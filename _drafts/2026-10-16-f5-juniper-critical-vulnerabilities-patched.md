F5 and Juniper Networks each shipped patches this week for critical
vulnerabilities in their products.

Successful exploitation of some of the flaws could lead to remote code
execution and full device takeover.

No active exploitation has been reported. Administrators running F5 or
Juniper Networks products should apply the available patches as soon as
possible.
