The FBI seized the domains behind NightmareStresser, a DDoS-for-hire
("booter") service linked to thousands of prior distributed
denial-of-service attacks. The platform was reportedly one of the
longest-running services of its kind.

No arrests or additional operational details were included in the
initial reporting. The takedown removes one option from the DDoS-for-hire
market but is unlikely to meaningfully reduce overall booter-service
availability given how many similar platforms exist.
