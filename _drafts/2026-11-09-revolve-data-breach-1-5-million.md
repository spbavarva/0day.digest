Online fashion retailer Revolve has told regulators that a September
cyberattack affected more than 1.5 million individuals.

The company has not disclosed specific detail on what data types were
exposed. Affected customers should watch for breach notification
letters and be alert to follow-on phishing attempts referencing the
incident.
