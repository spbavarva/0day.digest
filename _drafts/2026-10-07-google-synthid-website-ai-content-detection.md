Google launched a public website for its SynthID technology that lets
anyone check whether an image, video, or audio clip was generated with AI.

The tool extends SynthID's invisible-watermark detection beyond Google's
own products to a standalone public-facing service.

No details were given on detection accuracy against content without a
SynthID watermark, or on rate limits for the public tool.
