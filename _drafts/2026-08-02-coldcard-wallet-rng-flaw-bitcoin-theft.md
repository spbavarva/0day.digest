A flaw in COLDCARD hardware wallet firmware's random number generator is
likely linked to the theft of an estimated $88.6 million in Bitcoin.
Thousands of wallets whose seed values were generated using the flawed RNG
are believed to be affected, giving attackers a way to predict or reconstruct
private keys. The incident underscores the risk of weak entropy sources in
cryptocurrency key generation, and the difficulty of detecting such a flaw
before funds are already at risk.
