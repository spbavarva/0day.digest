A Google Firebase misconfiguration in tl;dv, an AI meeting notetaker
tool, allowed users to query other users' meeting information and
potentially join their calls.

The flaw affects both government and corporate users of the tool.
Organizations using AI meeting or notetaking tools should verify vendor
cloud backend configurations and review meeting data access logs for
unauthorized queries.
