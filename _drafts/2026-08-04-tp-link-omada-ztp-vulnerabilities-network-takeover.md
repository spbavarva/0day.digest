Forescout researchers identified 15 new vulnerabilities in the TP-Link
Omada networking ecosystem's Zero-Touch Provisioning (ZTP) components.

Chained together, the flaws can lead to full takeover of affected
networks. Organizations running TP-Link Omada infrastructure should
check for vendor patches and review ZTP-related exposure on their
networks.
