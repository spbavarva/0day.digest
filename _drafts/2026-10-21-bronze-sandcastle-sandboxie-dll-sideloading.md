Sophos disclosed a previously undocumented China-aligned threat cluster,
tracked as Bronze Sandcastle, that has targeted organizations in Asia and
Africa since mid-2025. Researcher Gabor Szappanos said the cluster's most
distinctive technical marker is its consistent use of Sandboxie DLL
sideloading to deploy backdoors.

The use of a legitimate sandboxing tool's DLL loading mechanism lets the
group's backdoors execute under the guise of trusted software.
