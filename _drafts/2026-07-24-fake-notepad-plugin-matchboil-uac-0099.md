CERT-UA has warned of a campaign using a malicious program disguised as a
Notepad++ plugin to compromise Windows systems, delivering malware
tracked as MATCHBOIL.V2. The activity is attributed to UAC-0099, a
Russia-aligned threat cluster previously observed weaponizing
vulnerabilities in WinRAR software.
