A new Mirai-based modular Linux botnet dubbed Evooo1Bot is targeting
internet-facing gateway and router devices, converting infected hosts into
SOCKS5 traffic relay nodes. This lets operators route other malicious traffic
through compromised consumer/small-office hardware, complicating attribution
and blocking. Admins should ensure router/gateway firmware is current and
disable unnecessary internet-facing management services.
