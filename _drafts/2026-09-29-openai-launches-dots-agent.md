OpenAI unveiled Dots at its DevDay keynote, an always-on AI agent designed to
pursue user-defined goals across connected apps in the background with
minimal oversight. It's positioned as OpenAI's answer to Meta's recently
launched Muse agent. Dots is powered by OpenAI's GPT-6 Astra model and is
meant to operate independent of any specific hardware or interface. Unlike
Muse, Dots reportedly will not be free to use.
