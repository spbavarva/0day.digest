Researchers introduced a new tool aimed at tracing AI-generated videos back
to their source, intended to promote industry collaboration on detection and
provenance for synthetic media.

Technical details on the tool's methodology were not included in initial
coverage. It adds to a growing set of provenance and detection efforts as
AI-generated video becomes harder to distinguish from real footage.
