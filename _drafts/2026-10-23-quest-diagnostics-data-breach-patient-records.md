Quest Diagnostics confirmed that patient records were exposed after a
threat actor gained unauthorized access to its Quanum Lab Services
Manager (LSM) system, a platform used by some clients to place test
orders.

The disclosure follows an earlier regulatory filing warning of
potential data theft from the same system. The exact scope of affected
records was not specified.
