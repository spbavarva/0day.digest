A ClickFix campaign is abusing malicious Google search results related to
Amazon, leveraging the decades-old Windows "finger" utility to launch a fake
CAPTCHA that installs malware, including the Lumma stealer.

The technique repurposes a legitimate, rarely-monitored built-in Windows
utility to execute attacker commands, helping it evade defenses tuned for
more common ClickFix delivery mechanisms.
