Ahold Delhaize, the Dutch grocery conglomerate, disclosed a cybersecurity
incident and took precautionary measures including disconnecting some
systems. Store operations in the US, Netherlands, and Belgium were
reportedly affected.

The company has not disclosed the nature of the incident, whether customer
data was accessed, or who is responsible.
