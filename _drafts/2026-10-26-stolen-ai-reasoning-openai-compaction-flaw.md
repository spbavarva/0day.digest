Researchers at Xint say they found a flaw in OpenAI's compaction process
that allowed reasoning traces to be moved between models.

The technique could make it cheaper for a rival to distill a competing
model's reasoning capabilities from OpenAI's outputs, according to the
researchers. No detail was given on whether OpenAI has patched the issue.
