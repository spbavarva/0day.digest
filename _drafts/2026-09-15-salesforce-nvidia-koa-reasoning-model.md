Salesforce and Nvidia released Koa, a new reasoning model built on
Nvidia's open-weight Nemotron model. Koa is trained specifically for
sales, marketing, and customer-support tasks rather than
general-purpose use.

The release signals continued movement toward open-weight foundation
models being fine-tuned by enterprise vendors for domain-specific
applications. No additional technical or benchmark detail was
included in the source summary.
