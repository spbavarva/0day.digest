Google DeepMind announced Gemini Robotics ER 2, a model update focused on
video understanding, task orchestration, and multi-robot collaboration.
DeepMind says the model extends prior humanoid control work to whole-body
motions, from feet to fingertips, rather than upper-body movement only.

The company frames it as a step toward robots that can reason about and
coordinate real-world tasks across multiple machines. No pricing or
availability details were included beyond the announcement post.
