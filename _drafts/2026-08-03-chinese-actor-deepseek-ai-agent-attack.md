Researchers at Jesta intercepted and analyzed an AI agent built on DeepSeek
that a Chinese threat actor was using to attempt compromise of a security
firm. The agent was observed attempting to compromise more than 1,200 hosts
for proxyjacking and to launch further attacks, illustrating how agentic AI
models can be weaponized to drive offensive operations with reduced operator
involvement. Limited technical detail is available on the specific
exploitation techniques used.
