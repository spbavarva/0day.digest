Rust core team members and maintainers of popular crates.io packages
have been targeted through social engineering lures delivered over
video calls.

The techniques used match those previously associated with North
Korea-linked threat actors, though it's unclear whether this activity
is part of earlier campaigns against the Rust ecosystem.

The goal appears to be compromising maintainer accounts as a foothold
into the Rust package supply chain. No confirmed compromises were
mentioned in the source summary.
