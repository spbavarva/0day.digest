The Bundestag passed a law giving German intelligence agencies expanded
hacking and sabotage powers, approved 308-190 with 71 abstentions. It marks
the biggest overhaul of the country's intelligence legislation since World
War II, following approval by Chancellor Friedrich Merz's cabinet earlier
this year.

The law's specific operational scope and oversight mechanisms were not
detailed in available coverage.
