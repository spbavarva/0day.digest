The US Department of Justice charged 17 alleged members of Mabna
Institute, an Iranian hacking-for-hire company, over a years-long
operation that breached email accounts at government agencies and stole
roughly $3.4 billion in intellectual property from hundreds of
universities and organizations in the US and abroad. The State Department
is offering $10 million rewards for information on five of the accused.
