CISA issued an advisory on an active threat targeting Siemens S7 series
programmable logic controllers, while noting the broader PLC-targeting
activity extends beyond Siemens equipment. Top mitigations listed by CISA
include inventorying all S7 series PLCs, applying critical security
patches, and ensuring PLCs are not directly accessible from the internet.
ICS/OT operators should treat this as an active threat, not a theoretical
risk assessment.
