Eclypsium's November InfraTrust report found that most infrastructure
security advisories addressing exploited vulnerabilities over the past month
involve flaws in third-party software bundled into network appliances,
rather than code the vendors wrote themselves.

The finding points to ongoing supply chain risk sitting one layer removed
from the vendors defenders usually scrutinize, since the vulnerable code
often isn't the product vendor's own.
