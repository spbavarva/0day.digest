Signal is restricting the ability to take screenshots of its app on
Android and iOS to prevent AI agents like Gemini and Siri from reading
users' messages and other on-screen data.

Signal's president said agentic AI features built into mobile operating
systems that analyze on-screen content are undermining the app's ability
to independently guarantee user privacy.

This is one of the more direct pushbacks yet from a privacy-focused app
against OS-level AI agents that read screen content, and may prompt
similar restrictions from other security-conscious apps.
