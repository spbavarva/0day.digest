French authorities are investigating a breach at the Directorate General of
Public Finances (DGFiP), the country's tax authority, after a hacker claimed
to have obtained data on 600,000 people. Officials confirmed unauthorized
access to DGFiP systems occurred in late June, reportedly through misuse of
a stolen identity.

The investigation is ongoing and the full scope of exposed data has not yet
been confirmed.
