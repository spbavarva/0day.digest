Check Point Research disclosed a jailbreak bundle for OpenAI Codex,
codenamed Haiou, that modifies the coding agent's trusted configuration to
inject advertisements for a third-party AI gateway. Researchers are calling
this category of attack "prompt adware" — malicious modification of an AI
agent's instructions for monetization rather than data theft.

The technique illustrates how jailbreak toolkits distributed to developers
can carry a hidden payload beyond just unlocking restricted behavior.
