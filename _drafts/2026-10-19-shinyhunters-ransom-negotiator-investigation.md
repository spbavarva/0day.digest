U.S. federal investigators probing the ShinyHunters hacking group — which
recently stole sensitive data from the FBI — have identified executives at
two ransomware negotiation firms with unusually close ties to the group,
according to KrebsOnSecurity.

One U.S.-based executive is suspected of helping ShinyHunters operate a
website used to pressure breach victims into paying ransoms, raising
questions about conflicts of interest in the ransomware negotiation
industry.
