Researchers identified a previously undocumented Iran-aligned espionage
group, dubbed Volant Manticore, targeting telecommunications companies,
airlines, and critical infrastructure in Cyprus.

The group's custom backdoor, KuboShell, uses the InterPlanetary File System
(IPFS) for command-and-control, making its traffic harder to block with
conventional network defenses. Volant Manticore also deploys a
post-compromise framework called Homa.
