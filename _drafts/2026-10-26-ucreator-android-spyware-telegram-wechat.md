A rentable Android spyware kit called uCreator hides inside tampered copies
of Telegram and WeChat to take over victims' accounts, stealing Telegram
2FA codes and cloning WeChat sessions.

The kit only reaches people who install a tampered app copy spread outside
official app stores, or who run WeChat inside the kit maker's own container
app. Its Telegram component matches "Baohuo," a previously documented
backdoor.
