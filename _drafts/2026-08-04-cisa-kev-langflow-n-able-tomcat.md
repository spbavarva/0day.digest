CISA added three vulnerabilities to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities
catalog based on evidence of active exploitation: CVE-2026-9198 (IBM
Langflow code injection), CVE-2026-18556 (N-able N-central
authentication bypass via an alternate path/channel), and
CVE-2026-34486 (Apache Tomcat missing encryption of sensitive data).

Federal agencies must remediate under Binding Operational Directive
26-04. The Langflow flaw is notable given the framework's use in
building LLM-powered applications; organizations running any of the
three products should prioritize patching.
