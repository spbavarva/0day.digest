Attackers are altering DNS settings on Wi-Fi devices at hotels and
conference centers, redirecting connected guests to fake Microsoft 365
login pages. Because the tampering happens at the network infrastructure
level rather than through individual phishing emails, anyone who joins the
affected Wi-Fi can be funneled to the spoofed login page regardless of
which device or account they use. Travelers and conference attendees
should verify TLS certificates on login pages and avoid signing into
corporate accounts over unfamiliar hotel or venue Wi-Fi without a VPN.
