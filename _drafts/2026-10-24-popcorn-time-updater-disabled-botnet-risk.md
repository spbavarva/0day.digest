Dutch security firm Eye Security pushed more than 2 million updates,
between November 2025 and March 2026, to computers it did not own, in
order to switch off the background updater bundled with Time4Popcorn —
an abandoned fork of Popcorn Time.

The firm said the updater could have allowed an outside party to take
over affected machines, potentially fueling a large botnet. No breach
was reported; this was a preemptive takedown of exposed infrastructure.
