Kaspersky researchers detailed a new campaign by the Armored Likho threat
actor, which poses as fundraising efforts to deliver a new "Still Toolkit"
malware.

The toolkit is aimed at stealing Telegram data and eavesdropping on
victims, extending the group's existing cyber-espionage capabilities.
