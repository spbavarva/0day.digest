Anthropic expanded access to Mythos 5 for defenders and announced a $35M
fund supporting open source security projects.

Claude Security, currently in public beta for Claude Enterprise
customers, now runs codebase scans on Mythos 5 — extending Anthropic's
push to put its models directly into defenders' security tooling.
