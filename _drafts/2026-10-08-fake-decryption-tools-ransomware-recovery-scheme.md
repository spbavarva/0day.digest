Zohar Pinhasi, owner of a ransomware recovery firm, has been charged
after allegedly paying ransoms to obtain decryption keys and then
charging victims a substantially marked-up fee — reported at an $11
million markup — for remediation.

The case is a reminder for breach victims to vet incident response
vendors' payment and billing practices.
