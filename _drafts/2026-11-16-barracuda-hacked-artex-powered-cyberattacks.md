Security vendor Barracuda disclosed it was hacked amid a broader surge in
attacks powered by Artex, an agentic AI penetration-testing framework.
SecurityWeek reports Chinese threat actors are increasingly adopting Artex to
accelerate reconnaissance and exploitation.

The incident illustrates how agentic AI tooling built for offensive security
testing is being repurposed by threat actors to speed up real-world attacks.
