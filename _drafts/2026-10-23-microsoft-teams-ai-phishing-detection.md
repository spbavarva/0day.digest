Microsoft announced plans to add AI-powered, context-aware phishing
detection to Teams messages, with rollout starting in early November
2026.

Details on the detection methodology were not disclosed in the
announcement. The feature targets phishing delivered through Teams
chat, a channel increasingly used by attackers to bypass email-focused
defenses.
