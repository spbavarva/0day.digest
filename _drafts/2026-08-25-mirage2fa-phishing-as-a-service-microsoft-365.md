The Mirage2FA phishing-as-a-service toolkit has affected thousands of
companies since 2024, targeting Microsoft 365 accounts by abusing legitimate
login flows to bypass two-factor authentication. According to ANY.RUN
research, 48% of targeted email addresses across roughly 4,500 impacted US
and EU companies were potentially compromised, with most victims based in
the US. As a commercial PhaaS kit, Mirage2FA lowers the skill bar for
attackers running 2FA-bypass campaigns against M365 tenants. Security teams
should review sign-in logs for anomalous session activity tied to M365
accounts and reinforce conditional access policies.
