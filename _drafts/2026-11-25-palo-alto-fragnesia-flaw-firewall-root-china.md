A China-linked hacking group chained the PHP2Shell technique with a flaw
Palo Alto Networks calls Fragnesia to gain root access on firewalls, in
attacks described as AI-assisted. The campaign hit 25 servers before Palo
Alto Networks shipped a fix.

Organizations running Palo Alto Networks firewalls should prioritize
patching, since the chain gives attackers full root control rather than
limited access. The AI-assisted framing also points to growing use of
automated tooling in nation-state exploitation chains.
