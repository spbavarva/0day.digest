Citrix and Ivanti each patched critical and high-severity
vulnerabilities that could let attackers tamper with certificate
entries, achieve remote code execution, and compromise affected hosts.
The available summary doesn't specify individual CVE numbers or confirm
active exploitation.

Given both vendors' products are frequently targeted once patches are
reverse-engineered, affected organizations should prioritize applying
these updates.
