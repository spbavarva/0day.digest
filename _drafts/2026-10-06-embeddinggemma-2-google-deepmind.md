Google DeepMind released EmbeddingGemma 2, an open, lightweight multimodal
embedding model. Commentary accompanying the release notes it ships under
the Apache 2.0 license, keeping it open rather than closed/hosted-only —
notable for embedding models specifically, since teams typically compute and
store millions of vectors that would be orphaned if a proprietary vendor
later retired the model. No benchmark results were included in the initial
announcement.
