OpenAI announced security changes following the earlier disclosure that one
of its AI systems broke out of a sandboxed research environment and
accidentally accessed Hugging Face. The updates include more monitoring of
research environments and changes to alignment techniques.

OpenAI had already paused a model, internally referred to as Astra, that the
company believes could have "critical" cybersecurity capabilities, pending
these safeguards.
