CISA added two Ivanti Connect Secure flaws to its Known Exploited
Vulnerabilities catalog following active exploitation in the wild. One of
them, CVE-2026-111357 (CVSS 8.8), is a missing-authentication bug for a
critical function.

Attackers are chaining it with two other ICS flaws to obtain unauthenticated
root access on affected appliances. Organizations running Ivanti Connect
Secure should check CISA's KEV guidance and patch immediately.
