CISA has added CVE-2026-76461, a SQL injection vulnerability in Cisco
Secure Email Gateway, to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities Catalog
based on evidence of active exploitation.

Under Binding Operational Directive 26-04, Federal Civilian Executive
Branch agencies must remediate KEV Catalog entries on CISA's required
timeline. Any organization running Cisco Secure Email Gateway should
treat this as a priority patch regardless of sector.
