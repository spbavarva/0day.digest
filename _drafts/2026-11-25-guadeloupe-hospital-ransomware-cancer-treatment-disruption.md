A suspected ransomware attack impaired computer systems at the University
Hospital of Guadeloupe, forcing doctors to postpone surgeries, suspend some
cancer treatments, and revert to pen and paper. France's national
cybersecurity agency is investigating.

The incident underscores the continued real-world patient-safety impact of
ransomware against healthcare providers, independent of the ransom amount
or data exposed.
