Burger King Japan, the Japanese subsidiary of the global fast-food chain,
disclosed a data breach after attackers accessed a server containing 11.18
million customer records.

The company has not yet detailed what categories of customer data were
exposed or how attackers gained access to the server.

Customers should be cautious of unsolicited communications claiming to be
from Burger King Japan.
