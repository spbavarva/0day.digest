The U.S. Coast Guard confirmed evidence of malicious cyber activity
aboard the tanker VL Prosperity, prompting the Coast Guard and FBI to
board the affected vessels. A second tanker was also reportedly targeted.

The Coast Guard has not attributed the activity to Iran or any other
actor. No technical details, IOCs, or confirmation of operational impact
have been released; this is an early-stage incident and further
attribution may follow.
