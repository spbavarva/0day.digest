Researchers identified vulnerabilities, dubbed "God Mode" flaws, in online
visa and immigration systems used by Nigeria, Ethiopia, Zambia, Sri Lanka,
Fiji, and Ghana. The flaws could have allowed attackers to disrupt
traveler entry processing in affected countries.

The research highlights the risk of relying on poorly secured systems for
critical border control infrastructure. No evidence of active exploitation
was reported at the time of disclosure.
