Anthropic is warning Claude users whose devices have been compromised by
infostealer malware, which can harvest session tokens and account
credentials. The company is proactively logging affected customers out of
their accounts and removing stored payment data to prevent unauthorized
use of compromised Claude accounts. No specific infostealer family or
number of affected accounts was disclosed. Users should run anti-malware
scans and rotate credentials if they suspect infection.
