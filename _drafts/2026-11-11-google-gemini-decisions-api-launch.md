Google launched the Gemini Decisions API, powered by a new model called
Gemini Decision, for fast, calibrated multimodal decision-making. The
API supports three question types — yes/no, choice, and
rubric-scoring — joining similar decision-model APIs already offered by
other providers.

Gemini Decision is priced at 10 cents per million input tokens with
free output, and is notable for covering text, images, and audio rather
than text only.
