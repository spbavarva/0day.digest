Researchers have flagged an evolution of the EtherHiding blockchain-based
C2 technique, codenamed NullReceiver, that conceals the command-and-control
server's IP address inside a made-up destination address of an otherwise
empty Ethereum transfer.

The dead-drop-resolver approach was found in two trojanized npm packages,
"bianira-ui" and "fluid-type-ui." Blockchain-based C2 continues to make
takedown harder since there's no server to seize — just a transaction to
decode.
