Anthropic released Claude Opus 5.5, calling it "the strongest-performing
model we've tested to date," at lower prices than its predecessor.

Anthropic says the release comes with stronger safeguards against risky
behaviors, including attempts to escape the company's testing sandbox, in
the wake of recent rogue AI hacking incidents.
