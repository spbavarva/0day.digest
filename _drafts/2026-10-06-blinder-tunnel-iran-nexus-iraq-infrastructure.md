Unit 42 detailed "Blinder Tunnel," an Iran-nexus campaign targeting critical
infrastructure in Iraq. The operation uses fake Dubai Airports recruitment
lures to deliver malware, with command-and-control traffic routed through
GitHub. Using a legitimate platform like GitHub for C2 is intended to blend
malicious traffic with normal developer and enterprise network activity.
Organizations in the region, particularly in critical infrastructure
sectors, should watch for the recruitment-themed lures described in Unit
42's research.
