Researchers disclosed details of UAT-10147, a Chinese-speaking cybercrime
group targeting Windows and Linux web servers globally across education,
media, technology, and gaming sectors. Most targets are located in
Brazil, Bolivia, China, Canada, and Vietnam.

The group uses AI to help scale server attacks and deploys a toolset
called SPECTRE, which includes EDR bypass capability alongside a Linux
rootkit — giving it both detection evasion and persistence on
compromised hosts.
