The Dutch Institute for Vulnerability Disclosure (DIVD) confirmed it was
breached in an attack driven by an automated AI agent. DIVD described the
intrusion as "loud and very, very messy," suggesting the agent's actions were
noisy rather than stealthy. Technical details on the agent's tooling and
entry point were not disclosed. The incident adds to a growing pattern of
AI-agent-driven attacks against security organizations themselves.
