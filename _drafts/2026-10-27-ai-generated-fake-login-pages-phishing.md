Generative AI is lowering the barrier to creating convincing fake login
pages that impersonate trusted brands and target specific employees or
organizations. Beyond harvesting passwords, some of these AI-generated
pages are built to capture MFA codes or hijack authenticated sessions.

The trend doesn't introduce a fundamentally new technique but speeds up
and scales phishing page creation and customization. Phishing-resistant
authentication, such as FIDO2 passkeys, becomes more valuable as AI
lowers the cost of convincing lookalike pages.
