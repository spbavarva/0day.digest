Kaspersky researchers have identified GenieLocker, a new ransomware family with variants built for Windows, Linux, and VMware ESXi environments. The malware is being deployed by Toy Ghouls, a financially motivated extortion group. Cross-platform ransomware that specifically targets ESXi hypervisors is notable because a single successful encryption event can take down many virtual machines at once. Further technical detail and IOCs are available in Kaspersky's Securelist writeup.
