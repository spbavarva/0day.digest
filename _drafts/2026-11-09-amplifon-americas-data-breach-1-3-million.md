Hearing aid retailer Amplifon Americas disclosed that hackers stole
personal, health, and insurance information — including Social
Security numbers — from the company's internal systems.

The breach affects 1.3 million people. The combination of SSNs and
health records raises the risk of targeted identity theft and
insurance fraud for affected individuals.
