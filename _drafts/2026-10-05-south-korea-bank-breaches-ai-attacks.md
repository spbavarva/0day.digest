South Korea's Financial Services Commission (FSC) held an emergency meeting
following a series of cyberattacks targeting financial institutions in the
country, amid suspicion that some of the attacks were AI-powered.

The investigation is ongoing. Specifics on the attack technique or scope of
impact were not detailed in the available summary.
