Microsoft's November 2026 Patch Tuesday addressed 1,094 flaws. One of them,
an Outlook vulnerability tracked as CVE-2026-124194, is confirmed under
active exploitation, according to Microsoft. This continues Microsoft's
recent trend of four-digit monthly patch counts. Organizations running
Outlook should prioritize this update given the confirmed in-the-wild
exploitation.
