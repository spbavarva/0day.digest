CISA disclosed CVE-2026-3014, a remote code execution vulnerability rated
9.1 on CVSSv3, affecting Siemens Siveillance Video Management Servers
versions V2023 R3, V2024 R1, and V2025.

Siemens has released patched versions for all affected product lines.
Operators running Siveillance Video should update immediately given the
severity and the sensitivity of video management infrastructure.
