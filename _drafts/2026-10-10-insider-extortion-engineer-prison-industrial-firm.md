A former core infrastructure engineer at an industrial firm has been
sentenced to prison for an insider extortion scheme.

The engineer deleted administrative accounts and reset hundreds of
passwords, then demanded 20 bitcoin to restore access to the company's
servers. No external attacker was involved — a reminder of the damage a
single insider with privileged access can inflict.
