An attacker targeting a US retailer switched to AI-generated scripts after
hand-typed commands failed to achieve a breach, and reportedly used the
AI-assisted approach to move laterally across the network within minutes.

The case is another concrete example of attackers using generative AI
tools operationally mid-intrusion, not just for reconnaissance or
phishing content, which shortens the time defenders have to detect and
contain lateral movement.
