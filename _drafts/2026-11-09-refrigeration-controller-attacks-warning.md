U.S. government agencies warned that threat actors are actively
targeting industrial refrigeration and cold-chain monitoring systems
globally, aiming to cause service disruptions, fire hazards, and
inventory loss.

The actors are exploiting vulnerabilities or weaknesses in unpatched or
internet-accessible controllers to remotely alter settings and force
units into unsafe operating states.

Operators of cold-chain infrastructure should audit controller internet
exposure and patch status.
