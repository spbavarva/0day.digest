OpenAI has teased Astra, an unreleased model built to tackle complex,
long-running tasks, saying an internal version produced ten notable advances
in mathematics and theoretical computer science. No release date or public
access details have been announced. This is a preview rather than a launch,
so capabilities and availability may change before Astra ships.
