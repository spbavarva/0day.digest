The crates.io security team and rust-lang members are warning of an
ongoing campaign targeting rust-lang members and owners of popular
crates. Attackers set up video calls under the pretext of a job,
project, or contract opportunity, then use the call to get the target to
install malware (e.g. a purportedly missing audio codec) or run other
commands. The goal is to compromise developer devices and accounts to
publish malware into the crates.io ecosystem.
