A study of more than 6,000 AI-generated patches found that even patches
which appear to work can introduce new bugs, break unrelated functionality,
or remain open to bypass. The findings point to reliability gaps in
AI-assisted vulnerability remediation as more teams adopt automated patching
tools. The available summary does not specify which AI systems or patch
categories were tested.
