Google Cloud published a roadmap for reaching full post-quantum cryptography
readiness, with a target completion date of 2029. The plan includes
intermediate milestones set for 2027 and 2028.

The roadmap gives enterprise customers a timeline for planning their own
migration to quantum-resistant cryptography on workloads running on Google
Cloud.
