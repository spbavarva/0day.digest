Attackers remotely defrosted refrigeration units at multiple French
supermarket chains overnight, in an attack that reportedly targeted Danfoss
refrigeration controllers.

The incident highlights the exposure of internet-connected OT/ICS hardware
in retail and food-supply environments. Operators running Danfoss or
similar refrigeration controllers should verify network segmentation and
restrict remote access to these devices.
