U.S. authorities disrupted a hacking platform operated by a group named
QTFY, which offered hacking services to the Chinese government and other
clients. The platform had reportedly been used in attacks against
military and critical infrastructure targets.

Further operational and technical detail on the disruption operation
was not included in the source summary.
