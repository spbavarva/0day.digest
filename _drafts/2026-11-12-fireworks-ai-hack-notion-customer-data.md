A cyberattack on Fireworks AI allowed hackers to access chat transcripts
belonging to Notion customers. The attackers accessed transcripts for
fewer than 1% of Notion workspaces, and most of the exposed transcripts
were unattributable.

This is Fireworks' second disclosed data breach in as many months.
