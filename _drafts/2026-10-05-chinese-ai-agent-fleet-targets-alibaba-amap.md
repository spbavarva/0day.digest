Independent researchers discovered an autonomous AI agent swarm,
apparently running on Tencent's infrastructure, targeting Alibaba's
Amap mapping service. The finding is notable as an early example of AI
agents operating at scale against a commercial service rather than a
single automated script. The agents' specific objective — scraping,
fraud, or something else — was not specified in initial reporting. This
is a developing story worth watching for AI-driven automated attack
tooling.
