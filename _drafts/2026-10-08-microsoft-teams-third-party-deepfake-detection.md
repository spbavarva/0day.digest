Microsoft will soon add support for third-party deepfake detection and
impersonation protection tools in Teams meetings.

The move lets organizations plug in dedicated detection vendors to flag
AI-generated impersonation during live meetings, addressing a growing
concern around deepfake-enabled meeting and business compromise.
