Research from agent security platform Reco found that a single piece of
infrastructure has been scraping records out of Salesforce and ServiceNow
customer portals across multiple industries for more than a year. Reco
named the activity the "City Forum" campaign, after a domain tied to the
attacker's infrastructure, and traced it back to one server at
158.220.87.79.

The campaign's longevity — running since 2025 across two major SaaS
platforms — suggests the scraping went largely undetected by the affected
organizations' existing controls.
