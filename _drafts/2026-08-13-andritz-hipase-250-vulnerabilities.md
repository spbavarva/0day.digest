CISA disclosed multiple vulnerabilities (CVSS 8.1) in ANDRITZ HIPASE-250
and 250 SCALA devices, versions 7.20 and earlier, including passwords
stored in a recoverable format and missing authentication for a critical
function.

Successful exploitation could let an attacker read data from the device or
gain unauthorized access to affected workstations.
