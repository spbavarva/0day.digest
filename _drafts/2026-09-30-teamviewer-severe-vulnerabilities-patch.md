TeamViewer is urging customers to immediately patch a set of high-severity
vulnerabilities affecting both its client and host software.

The company did not specify exploitation in the wild, but given TeamViewer's
widespread use as remote-access software, unpatched flaws are an attractive
target for attackers seeking initial access or lateral movement. Admins
should apply the update without delay.
