Unit 42 disclosed a cyber-espionage campaign, tracked as CL-UNK-1211,
targeting Linux infrastructure tied to government entities, observed
between October 2025 and April 2026. The campaign deployed a backdoor
called SysGoRAT alongside scripts researchers suspect were
AI-generated.

The likely use of AI-assisted tooling in the attack chain reflects the
broader trend of offensive actors incorporating AI into operational
tradecraft. No attribution to a specific nation-state was confirmed in
the available summary.
