Ahold Delhaize, one of the world's largest food retail chains, is restoring
services after a cyberattack disrupted websites and apps across its
European and US brands last week.

The company has restored many affected European sites and apps, and expects
US e-commerce brands to be back online within days. No details on the
attack vector, ransom demand, or data impact have been disclosed publicly.
