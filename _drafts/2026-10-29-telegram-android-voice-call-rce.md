A flaw in Telegram for Android, discovered by security startup NebuSec,
could be chained with two other bugs to give an attacker full control of a
victim's phone once they answered a voice call. The attacker needed only an
ordinary Telegram account to initiate the attack.

Telegram fixed the issue in app version 12.10.4, released in late September;
NebuSec disclosed technical details on October 29. Android users should
confirm they're running a current Telegram version.
