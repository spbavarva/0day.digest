Researchers have flagged a previously undocumented Linux botnet, Evooo1Bot,
that reuses the DDoS engine from the leaked Mirai source code and extends it
with credential theft and reverse SOCKS5 proxy capabilities.

The added capabilities turn compromised internet-facing devices into
persistent attacker infrastructure rather than just DDoS nodes.
