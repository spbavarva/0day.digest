A campaign dubbed PHP2Shell has been targeting critical PHP flaws for more
than a week, with over 22.6 million exposed instances potentially
affected.

The attacks successfully achieve root-level compromise against Palo Alto
firewalls but fail when attempted against WordPress targets, according to
SecurityWeek.

The report doesn't specify which PHP CVE(s) are being targeted or why the
attack succeeds on one platform and not the other.

Palo Alto firewall admins should check for indicators of compromise and
apply vendor guidance as it becomes available.
