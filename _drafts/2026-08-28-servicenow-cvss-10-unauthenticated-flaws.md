ServiceNow has patched four security flaws in its AI Platform, three of
which are rated CVSS 10.0 and exploitable in certain circumstances by an
unauthenticated attacker to execute code or SQL. ServiceNow has already
deployed the fix to its hosted instances and provided the update to
partners and self-hosted customers.

Organizations running self-hosted ServiceNow instances are responsible
for applying the update themselves and should treat this as urgent given
the unauthenticated, maximum-severity nature of the flaws.
