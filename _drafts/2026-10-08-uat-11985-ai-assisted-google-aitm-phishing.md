Cisco Talos has identified an APT spear-phishing campaign, tracked as
UAT-11985, targeting individuals affiliated with Taiwan research
organizations.

The operation uses AI-assisted lures built around legitimate public
event themes, impersonating reputable academic and policy institutions
to deliver real-time adversary-in-the-middle phishing against Google
accounts.
