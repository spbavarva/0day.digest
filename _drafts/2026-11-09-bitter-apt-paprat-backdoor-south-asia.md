The South Asian cyber espionage group known as Bitter (aka APT-C-08,
APT-Q-37, Hazy Tiger, Orange Yali, T-APT-17, TA397) has been observed
targeting South Asian organizations with a previously unreported
remote access trojan called PapRAT.

Fortinet FortiGuard Labs researcher Cara Lin said PapRAT has both C++
and Rust implementations, with capabilities including command
execution and file operations.

The dual-language tooling suggests ongoing investment by the group in
evading detection and diversifying its malware codebase.
