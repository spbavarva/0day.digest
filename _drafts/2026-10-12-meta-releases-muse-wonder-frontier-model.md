Meta released Muse Wonder and Muse Wonder Full, its first frontier AI
models from the revamped Superintelligence Labs division.

The models are positioned as larger and more capable than the earlier
lightweight Muse Spark, with higher benchmark scores and better results on
complex tasks including coding.

Meta is pitching Wonder as competitive with the industry's leading frontier
models.
