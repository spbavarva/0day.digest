CISA has added a critical ownCloud vulnerability, CVE-2023-49105 (CVSS
9.8), to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog after reports that a
Chinese-speaking threat actor exploited it to steal nuclear research
records from a Philippine government research body.

Federal agencies are required to remediate KEV-listed flaws on CISA's
deadline, and any organization still running vulnerable ownCloud
instances should patch immediately given confirmed exploitation against a
sensitive government target.
