Texas utility CenterPoint Energy has confirmed that customer data was
stolen after a hacker claimed to have breached the company's systems and
leaked data on the dark web. The attacker claims to have stolen roughly 7.5
million customer records, and CenterPoint has notified federal regulators
about the incident.

The source summary did not specify the initial access vector or what
categories of personal data were exposed.
