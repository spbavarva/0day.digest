Resecurity reports that the INC Ransomware operation has become the dominant
threat actor exploiting recently disclosed flaws in SonicWall Secure Mobile
Access (SMA) 1000 series VPN appliances, gaining root access and enabling
lateral movement into victim networks. INC has accelerated its activity since
early August 2026 and is listing multiple new victims on its data leak site.
Organizations running SMA 1000 appliances should confirm they're on a patched
build and check for signs of unauthorized VPN access.
