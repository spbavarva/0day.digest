Google's latest AI Threat Tracker report says a Chinese state-linked group
is developing automated, AI-driven attack tooling for cyber espionage.
Google says threat actors are increasingly relying on autonomous
multi-agent offensive frameworks — naming Artex, CAIRN, CyberStrikeAI, and
PentAGI — to run multi-stage intrusions at higher operational velocity than
manually operated campaigns.

The report frames this as part of a broader trend of threat actors rapidly
evolving their use of AI in offensive operations, rather than describing a
single disclosed incident. No specific victims or IOCs were named in the
available summary.
