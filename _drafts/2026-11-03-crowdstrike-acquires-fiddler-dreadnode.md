CrowdStrike is reportedly paying $495 million to acquire AI security
firms Fiddler and Dreadnode. The deals would add AI agent
observability and automated AI security testing capabilities to the
CrowdStrike Falcon platform.

Terms have not been officially confirmed by CrowdStrike at the time of
the report.
