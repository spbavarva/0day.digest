Nearly 130 technology and cybersecurity companies have signed onto an
OpenAI-led pledge to strengthen collective cyber defenses in response to
increasingly sophisticated AI-enabled attacks. The pledge represents a
broad industry commitment rather than a specific technical standard or
product, with signatories spanning both AI vendors and traditional security
firms. Concrete deliverables or timelines were not specified in initial
coverage.
