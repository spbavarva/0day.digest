Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei said it's time to slow the pace of frontier
AI improvements so that security and risk-prevention efforts can catch
up, according to Dark Reading.

China's Ministry of Foreign Affairs responded to a question about
Amodei's essay by saying all parties should work together on AI,
SecurityWeek reported. The exchange underscores that calls to slow AI
development are becoming a geopolitical talking point, not just an
industry debate.
