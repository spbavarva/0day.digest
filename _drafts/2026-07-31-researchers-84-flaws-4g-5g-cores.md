Academic researchers from Nanyang Technological University in Singapore
disclosed a widespread class of vulnerabilities affecting 4G and 5G core
networks — 84 flaws in total. Successful exploitation could trigger
denial-of-service conditions or, in the more severe case, allow an attacker
to hijack a user's network session.

The findings point to systemic weaknesses in telecom core implementations
rather than a single vendor issue. No confirmed in-the-wild exploitation was
reported.
