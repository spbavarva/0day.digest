Attackers accessed the OT network at a Polish combined heat and power plant
through the private cellular network the grid operator uses to reach remote
equipment, shutting down a steam turbine and the process-water treatment
system.

The plant supplies heat to roughly 50,000 residents. Recovery began around
7:30 a.m. local time while the intruders were still active inside the
network; customers did not lose heat service during the incident.
