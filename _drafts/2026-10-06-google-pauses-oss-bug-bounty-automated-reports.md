Google has stopped accepting new vulnerability reports through its bug
bounty program for open-source projects it maintains, such as Go, Angular,
and Protocol Buffers, effective since October 1. The company cited a surge in
invalid, apparently automated submissions as the reason for the pause.
Reports about supply chain compromises are still being accepted, and reports
filed before October 1 continue to be honored. The pause illustrates a
growing operational burden on bug bounty programs from low-quality,
likely AI-generated vulnerability report submissions.
