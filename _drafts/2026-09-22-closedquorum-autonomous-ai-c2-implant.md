Cisco Talos, via its CAIRN project, discovered CLOSEDQUORUM, a malware
binary that exhibits fully autonomous command-and-control behavior. Talos
describes it as the first reported implant able to execute expanding
portions of an attack chain without operator involvement.

This marks a shift from AI-assisted human operations toward malware where
the AI system itself drives significant parts of the attack chain.
