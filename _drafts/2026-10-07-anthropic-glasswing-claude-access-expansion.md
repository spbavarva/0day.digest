Anthropic is merging Project Glasswing, a program giving vetted
cybersecurity professionals access to its advanced AI models with reduced
safeguards and blocking classifiers, into a single tiered access offering
alongside its Cyber Verification Program.

Anthropic said Glasswing uncovered at least 129,000 verified software
vulnerabilities between April and July 2026, plus an undisclosed additional
number since.

The move reflects a broader pattern among AI labs of loosening model
guardrails for vetted defenders in exchange for oversight, rather than
restricting capability across the board.
