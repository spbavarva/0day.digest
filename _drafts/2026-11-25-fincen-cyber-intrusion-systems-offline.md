The U.S. Treasury's Financial Crimes Enforcement Network (FinCEN) says it
experienced "unauthorized access to a limited subset of its systems."

As a precaution, FinCEN took some systems offline while it investigates.

FinCEN collects suspicious activity reports and other Bank Secrecy Act
filings from banks and other financial institutions, making the agency a
high-value target for anyone seeking insight into financial crime
investigations.
