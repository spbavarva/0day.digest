Cyberattacks against US water utilities attributed to Iran-linked actors have
extended beyond the initial Minnesota incident to at least six additional
states, reportedly including Michigan, South Dakota, and Georgia. The pattern
suggests a broader campaign against water system infrastructure operators
rather than an isolated attack. Details on the specific access vector or
operational impact at the additional utilities have not been confirmed.
