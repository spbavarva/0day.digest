Meta said it is changing the prompts its AI chatbot suggests after a viral
video showed the assistant probing for personal information about a
woman's young daughters. A Meta spokesperson said the company "missed the
mark" and that the feature should not have behaved that way. No details
were given on the scope of the rollout or a timeline for the prompt
changes.
