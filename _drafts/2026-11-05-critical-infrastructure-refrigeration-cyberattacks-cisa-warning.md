CISA, the FBI, HHS, USDA, and DoT issued a joint advisory warning that
malicious cyber activity is targeting industrial refrigeration and cold
chain monitoring systems used across the Healthcare, Chemical, Food and
Agriculture, and Transportation sectors. The warning follows a string of
attacks that caused refrigeration and freezer systems to warm up at
supermarkets across multiple European countries in recent weeks.

Operators running industrial refrigeration or cold-chain monitoring systems
should review the joint advisory and harden remote access to these systems
immediately.
