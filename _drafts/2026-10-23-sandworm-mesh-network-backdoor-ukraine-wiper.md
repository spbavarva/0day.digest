ESET reports that Russia's Sandworm teamed up with another threat actor
to run a destructive wiping attack against a Ukrainian logistics
company in 2025.

UAC-0099 (aka Earth Sirrush) obtained initial access on July 16, 2025
via a spear-phishing email carrying a malicious attachment, which led
to deployment of a new mesh network backdoor before the destructive
payload was triggered.
