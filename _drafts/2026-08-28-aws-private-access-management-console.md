AWS has extended its data perimeter controls to the AWS Management
Console with a new Private Access capability, aimed at organizations in
regulated industries that keep sensitive workloads isolated from the
public internet. Previously, customers could restrict console access to
authorized accounts and corporate networks, but the console itself still
required general internet connectivity.

Private Access closes that gap, letting regulated customers reach the
console without exposing the connection to the public internet. This is
relevant to teams building network-isolated AWS environments for
compliance reasons.
