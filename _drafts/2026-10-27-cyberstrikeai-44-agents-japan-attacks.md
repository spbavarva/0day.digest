Researchers at TrendAI disclosed three cyberattacks against organizations
in Japan where AI played a central role, either driving the operation or
assisting a human operator. The campaign, dubbed CyberStrikeAI, reportedly
used 44 AI agents across exposed threat-actor server environments to
automate hacking operations.

Researchers describe the operations as distinct from previously
documented AI-assisted attacks, suggesting a shift toward more autonomous
offensive tooling. Further technical detail on the specific techniques
used was not disclosed in initial reporting.
