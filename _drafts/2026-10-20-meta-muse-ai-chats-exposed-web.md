Meta paused a sharing feature on its Muse AI assistant after security
researchers found that shared conversation links were publicly discoverable
on the web. Researchers reportedly found more than 6,800 Muse conversation
links indexed through public DNS records.

The exposure means private AI conversations shared by users could be
accessed by anyone who found the links, continuing a recurring pattern of
AI chat-sharing features inadvertently leaking private conversations.
