Hackers stole 2.6 million records belonging to customers of Sony's
Japanese ISP So-net and its Plala service.

The exposed data includes 54,401 email account passwords and 16 bank
account records. Affected customers should change their So-net/Plala
passwords and watch for follow-on phishing or account takeover attempts.
