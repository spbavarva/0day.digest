Researchers are tracking a recurring social engineering pattern across
hundreds of incidents in which threat actors use Microsoft Teams' external
chat creation feature to impersonate IT support and deliver malware or
ransomware.

The campaigns reuse the same throwaway Microsoft 365 tenants and the same
"IT support" persona across incidents, a consistent fingerprint researchers
say helps identify the activity.
