Salesforce and Nvidia have launched Koa, a reasoning model built on
Nvidia's open-weight Nemotron model and trained specifically for sales,
marketing, and customer-support tasks.

The source summary framed it as a notable competitive move against
general-purpose AI labs but did not include benchmark comparisons.
