The Trump administration has named National Intelligence Director Jay
Clayton to lead a newly created federal AI task force. The announcement
follows a White House meeting last week with top executives from major AI
companies.

Details on the task force's scope, authority, and relationship to existing
AI regulatory efforts have not yet been published.
