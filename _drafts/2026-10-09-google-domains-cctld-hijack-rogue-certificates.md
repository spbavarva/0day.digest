Hackers hijacked the .gh (Ghana), .sl (Sierra Leone), and .as (American
Samoa) country-code top-level domains and used the access to obtain valid
HTTPS certificates for several Google domains.

Domain hijacks at the ccTLD level can undermine the certificate issuance
process for any domain registered under the affected TLDs, not just Google's.
