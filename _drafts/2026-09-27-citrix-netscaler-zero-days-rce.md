Security firm watchTowr disclosed on September 26 that two unpatched
zero-day vulnerabilities in Citrix NetScaler ADC and NetScaler Gateway
allow remote code execution and are being actively exploited.

Citrix has not confirmed the flaws or released a fix. Some administrators
have taken affected appliances offline rather than wait for a patch. No
CVE identifiers or further technical exploitation details have been
published yet.
