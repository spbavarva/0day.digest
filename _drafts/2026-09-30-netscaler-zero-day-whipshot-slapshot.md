Mandiant Consulting and Google Threat Intelligence Group observed threat
actors exploiting a patched Citrix NetScaler ADC/Gateway flaw to gain root
access and deploy two new malware families, tracked as WHIPSHOT and
SLAPSHOT. Government and financial-sector organizations in North America and
Europe have been targeted over several weeks, tied to CVE-2026-88771 and
CVE-2026-88772.

Multiple security firms have independently confirmed exploitation. NetScaler
operators should patch immediately and hunt for the named malware if
patching was delayed.
