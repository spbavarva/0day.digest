The European Commission confirmed it took certain services offline to
contain a cyberattack it describes as AI-assisted.

The Commission said it has no evidence of a data breach so far, but did
not detail how AI was used in the attack or which services were
disrupted.

This is one of the first instances of a major government body explicitly
attributing an attack's effectiveness to AI assistance rather than just
automation.
