NATO's CCDCOE Crossed Swords exercise in Estonia has introduced AI
agents, including OpenAI Daybreak, into joint cyber and kinetic
operations training, according to SecurityWeek. The exercise reflects
growing interest from military cyber units in integrating AI agents into
operational training alongside traditional offensive and defensive
drills.
