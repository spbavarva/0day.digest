Blockchain infrastructure provider Chainstack disclosed that an
external attacker gained unauthorized access to part of its production
infrastructure, exposing customer data and password hashes.

Chainstack assesses the intrusion as part of a large-scale,
AI-powered attack campaign that came to light the prior week.
Customers should rotate any credentials associated with their
Chainstack accounts given the exposure of password hashes.
