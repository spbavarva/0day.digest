Surfshark disclosed that a misconfigured test server exposed internal
engineering material, including internal configuration data, and that the
server was accessed by threat actors. The company did not indicate the
exposure involved production customer data or credentials. As a VPN
provider, exposure of internal engineering material carries elevated risk
even without direct customer data loss.
