Nvidia released new open-weight models aimed at letting enterprises run AI
agents in their own data centers, while also pushing toward private,
on-device AI agents on PCs. The release continues Nvidia's support for
self-hosted deployment as companies look to avoid sending data to
third-party model APIs.
