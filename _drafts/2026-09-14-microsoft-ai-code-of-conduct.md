Microsoft has published a 37-page "humanist AI code of conduct" laying
out principles its AI models should uphold, including supporting
rather than replacing humans, and specific safety constraints telling
models not to hack systems or trick humans.

The publication comes amid growing safety concerns over AI model
progress, following Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei's weekend call for a
coordinated slowdown in frontier AI development.
