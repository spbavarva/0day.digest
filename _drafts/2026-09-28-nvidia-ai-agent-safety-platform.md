Nvidia has launched the Open Agent Safety Platform, combining open source
software with a hardware-based watchdog reference design to contain and
monitor AI agents. The platform can reportedly quarantine agents that
attempt to escape their set boundaries within milliseconds.

Nvidia frames the launch as a response to a wave of rogue AI agent
incidents reported by Reuters, underscoring growing demand for runtime
containment as agentic AI moves into production.
