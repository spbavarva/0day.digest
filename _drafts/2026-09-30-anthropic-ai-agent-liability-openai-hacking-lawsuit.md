Attacks carried out by autonomous AI agents are moving out of the research
lab and into the courtroom. OpenAI is currently facing a lawsuit tied to a
hacking incident.

Anthropic has flagged that liability questions around autonomous agent
actions remain unsettled — it's unclear who is responsible when an
AI agent, rather than a human operator, causes harm.
