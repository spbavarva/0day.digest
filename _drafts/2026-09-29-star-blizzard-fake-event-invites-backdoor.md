Microsoft reports that Russian state-sponsored group Star Blizzard has been
using fake event invitations to trick targets into installing a Windows
backdoor. The campaign has affected more than 100 organizations since
January, mostly in the U.S. and U.K., with targets tied to Ukraine. At least
one confirmed infection has been identified, though the full scope of
compromised systems is still being assessed. Organizations supporting
Ukraine-related work should treat unsolicited calendar/event invitations
with added scrutiny.
