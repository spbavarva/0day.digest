Security company Claroc found 37,571 Grok-built apps online, of which
35,578 were accessible without any sign-in. About 1 in 10 of the open
apps contained internal business data or passwords, and 24 of the
exposed apps were built by staff at X and SpaceX, the company that
makes the Grok AI assistant.

Most exposed apps were games, landing pages, and calculators, but the
exposure illustrates how easily AI-generated apps can leak secrets when
deployed without authentication by default.
