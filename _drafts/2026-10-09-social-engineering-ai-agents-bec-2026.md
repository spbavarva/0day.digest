As AI agents gain authority over business systems like email, finance, and
approvals, security researchers warn that attackers can manipulate the
agents themselves the same way they manipulate human employees in business
email compromise (BEC) schemes — treating the agent as the social-engineering
target rather than a person.

Organizations deploying AI agents with transactional authority should apply
the same scrutiny to agent inputs that they apply to human approval workflows
susceptible to BEC.
