The French government formally attributed an October 11 cyberattack on
refrigeration controllers at roughly 700 retail sites to a cyber unit of
Iran's IRGC. Attackers altered defrost settings to force units to high
temperatures, causing food losses and at least 15 fires.

France summoned Iran's ambassador and said it will push for EU sanctions
against those responsible. The incident is a rare confirmed case of
nation-state cyber-sabotage causing physical fire damage via ICS
manipulation.
