Google launched "Personal Intelligence," a Search feature that draws
on data from Gmail, Google Drive, Google Photos, Google Calendar, and
Google Wallet to personalize search results.

This ties AI-driven search personalization directly to a user's
cross-product data footprint rather than search history alone. Google
has not detailed what access controls or opt-out mechanisms govern
this cross-service data use.
