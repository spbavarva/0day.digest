Flipper Devices, maker of the popular Flipper Zero pentesting tool, has
launched a crowdfunding campaign for the Flipper One — a $399 portable
Linux computer and networking multi-tool. The device extends the Flipper
lineup from its original pocket-sized gadget toward a more general-purpose
testing platform.

No further technical specifications were disclosed in the announcement.
Practitioners who rely on the Flipper Zero for pentesting workflows may
want to track the campaign as hardware details emerge.
