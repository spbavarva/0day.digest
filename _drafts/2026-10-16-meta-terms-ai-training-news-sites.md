According to a report from Nieman Lab, Meta's new business terms —
effective October 30 — appear to give the company permission to crawl and
scrape websites "associated" with businesses that use its platforms,
including online publishers.

The terms state Meta can use this outside content to "develop, improve,
research," and "train" its AI systems.
