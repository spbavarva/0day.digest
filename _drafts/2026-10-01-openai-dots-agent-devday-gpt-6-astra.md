OpenAI announced an AI agent called Dots at its annual DevDay conference,
powered by a model called GPT-6 Astra.

The launch is widely seen as a direct response to Meta's Muse agent
platform, which has seen early traction.

CEO Sam Altman described Dots as inspired by community-built agents,
positioning it as OpenAI's entry into the increasingly competitive consumer
AI agent market.
