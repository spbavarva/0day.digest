Cloudflare has introduced Kitesurf, a cloud-hosted browser designed for AI
agents rather than human users. The company says the browser uses less
compute than Chromium for common automation tasks, aimed at developers
building browser-based AI agents. No security-specific hardening details
were included in the available summary.
