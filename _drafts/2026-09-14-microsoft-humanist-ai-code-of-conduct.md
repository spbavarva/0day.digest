Microsoft has published a 37-page "humanist AI" code of conduct, saying
"people matter more than AI," amid growing concern over the pace of AI
model progress.

The publication follows Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei's call over the
weekend for a coordinated slowdown in AI development, after researchers
warned that model capability could outpace the industry's ability to
safely deploy and verify increasingly complex systems.
