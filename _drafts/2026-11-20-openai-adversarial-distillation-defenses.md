OpenAI outlined a layered approach to defending against adversarial
distillation, where outside parties try to extract a model's capabilities
by systematically querying it.

The approach combines training-time techniques, runtime safeguards,
detection systems, and enforcement action against accounts found
extracting capability at scale.

OpenAI frames this as protecting against capability extraction generally,
without naming specific actors or quantifying how much extraction has
been observed.
