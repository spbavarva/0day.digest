Four suspects were arrested in Brazil and three others charged in Europe
after allegedly exploiting a vulnerability at a service provider to withdraw
funds from Commerzbank customer accounts. Total losses are estimated at
roughly €30M.

Germany's BKA and Brazilian federal police both confirmed the arrests. The
specific service provider and vulnerability were not named in available
reporting. No indication the flaw remains unpatched or exploitable.
