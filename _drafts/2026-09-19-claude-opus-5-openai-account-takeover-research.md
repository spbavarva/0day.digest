Three researchers at security firm Hacktron used Anthropic's Claude Opus 5 to chain two vulnerabilities and take over the ChatGPT and Codex accounts of several OpenAI employees, ultimately reaching an internal OpenAI code repository.

The attack chain began with a bug in the software running OpenAI's public help forum and moved through a weakness in OpenAI's login system. The work was conducted as authorized security research, not a malicious breach.
