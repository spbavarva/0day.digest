Anthropic has acquired Opaque Systems, a startup that develops technology
allowing AI systems to use sensitive data while keeping it protected at the
hardware level, according to SecurityWeek.

The acquisition signals continued investment in confidential computing
approaches for AI workloads that handle sensitive or regulated data.
