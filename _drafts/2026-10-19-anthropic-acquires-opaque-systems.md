Anthropic acquired Opaque Systems, which builds confidential-computing
technology that lets AI systems process sensitive data while keeping it
protected using hardware-based confidentiality.

The acquisition signals continued investment in letting enterprises run AI
over sensitive data without exposing it in plaintext to the model provider
or underlying infrastructure.
