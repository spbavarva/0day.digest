Pretxt has emerged from stealth with $5 million in seed funding for a tool
designed to prevent AI agents from escaping their guardrails and causing
unintended harm. The company frames its product as a supervision layer
sitting between agents and the systems they act on.

Few technical details of the supervision mechanism were disclosed at
launch. As AI agents are given more autonomous access to tools and data,
guardrail-enforcement tooling like this is becoming its own security
category.
