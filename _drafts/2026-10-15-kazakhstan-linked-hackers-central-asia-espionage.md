A roughly two-year-old cyber-espionage campaign targeting Central Asian
government organizations appears to originate from Kazakhstan, according to
researchers. The campaign has focused on former Soviet states, consistent
with regional intelligence-gathering motives rather than financial gain.

Specific malware families and initial-access techniques used in the
campaign were not detailed in available reporting.
