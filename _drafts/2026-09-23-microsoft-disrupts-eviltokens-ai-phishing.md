Microsoft disrupted EvilTokens, a phishing-as-a-service platform that
used AI at every step of its attack chain, including writing social
engineering messages and deciding which targets to pursue.

The takedown highlights the growing use of AI to automate phishing
operations end-to-end. Microsoft did not disclose details on
remaining infrastructure or affiliated actors.
