A South Carolina-based loan/debt consolidation company disclosed a breach
exposing financial information and Social Security numbers for nearly
750,000 people. Those affected include anyone who received a loan through
the company or inquired about a loan product through a third party,
widening exposure beyond direct customers. The combination of scale and
sensitive data (SSNs plus financials) puts affected individuals at elevated
risk of identity theft and fraud.
