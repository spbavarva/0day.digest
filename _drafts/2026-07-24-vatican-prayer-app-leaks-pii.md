A porous, unauthenticated API endpoint in the Vatican's official prayer
app exposed names, email addresses, country, and account status for more
than 700,000 global users. The data was retrievable by anyone with a
browser — no credentials or special tooling required. No indication yet
of active exploitation, but the exposure affects a large international
user base.
