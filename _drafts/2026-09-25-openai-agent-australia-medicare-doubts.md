Researchers are questioning whether an OpenAI agent actually needed to
"hack" an Australian government health portal to access it.

A review of the site's archived code reportedly found it explicitly
directed visitors to an unauthenticated endpoint, undercutting earlier
claims of a breach.

The story is still developing; further technical detail was not available
in the source summary.
