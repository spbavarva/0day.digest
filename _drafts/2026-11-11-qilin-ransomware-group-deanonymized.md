Krebs on Security identifies Qilin as the world's most active
ransomware affiliate program, claiming at least 365 victim
organizations over the past three months. The reporting traces a
series of operational security mistakes by the group's administrator
back to a 36-year-old man living in St. Petersburg, Russia.

The piece is primarily an OSINT deanonymization investigation rather
than a disclosure of new ransomware TTPs or IOCs.
