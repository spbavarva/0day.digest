Medical device maker iRhythm disclosed a data breach affecting at least
360,000 people.

Stolen data includes patients' names, addresses, email addresses, dates of
birth, and phone numbers.

The disclosure summary did not specify the attack vector or whether
financial or clinical record data was also exposed.
