The tally of patients affected by the Oracle Health data breach has
climbed to nearly 20 million, far higher than counts that surfaced in
earlier filings and patient notifications.

The scale places this among the largest healthcare data breaches
disclosed to date; organizations using Oracle Health should watch for
updated notification requirements as the count grows.
