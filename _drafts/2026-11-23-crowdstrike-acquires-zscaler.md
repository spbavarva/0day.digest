CrowdStrike agreed to acquire Zscaler for $43.9 billion, one of the
largest acquisitions in cybersecurity industry history. CrowdStrike
said it expects the combined company to generate more than $10
billion in annual recurring revenue.

The deal merges CrowdStrike's endpoint and XDR platform with
Zscaler's SASE and zero-trust network offerings, continuing a trend
of consolidation among vendors competing to cover endpoint, network,
and cloud security from a single stack.
