An ongoing phishing campaign dubbed "GoodGuys" is impersonating major
brands — including Google, Nvidia, Accenture, and SpaceX — to steal
corporate Google and Okta credentials.

The lures are fake invitations to paid expert interviews and webinars, a
social-engineering angle distinct from typical credential-phishing
templates.

Targets include employees whose Okta and Google Workspace logins would
give attackers a foothold into corporate identity infrastructure. No
victim count or technical IOCs have been disclosed so far.
