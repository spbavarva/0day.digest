A new malware strain is infecting Android-based car infotainment systems
and enrolling the devices into a proxy botnet.

This extends botnet recruitment beyond phones and IoT boxes into
in-vehicle Android systems, giving operators a fresh pool of always-on,
network-connected devices to route traffic through.
