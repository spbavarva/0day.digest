The FBI on Thursday arrested the co-founder of a Canadian cybersecurity firm
that provides ransomware negotiation services, in connection with an
investigation into the ShinyHunters hacking group. Krebs on Security reports
that ShinyHunters recently stole sensitive data on thousands of FBI agents.
The arrest ties a figure from the ransomware-negotiation industry directly to
an active extortion group under federal investigation. Charges and further
case details have not yet been disclosed.
