Google DeepMind introduced SynthID Bio, a proof-of-concept watermarking
technique for AI-generated protein sequences that aims to preserve the
protein's biological function while embedding a detectable signal.

The goal is traceability for AI-designed biological sequences, a step toward
provenance tooling as generative models are increasingly applied to biology —
an area with clear dual-use biosecurity implications.
