Google DeepMind described using teams of AI agents paired with formal
verification to discover faster quantum algorithms, targeting
applications in fusion and chemistry simulation.

The approach combines generative search by agents with formal proof
checking to confirm proposed algorithms are actually correct, rather
than relying on benchmark testing alone — a pairing worth watching as
more teams apply agents to code and algorithm generation.
