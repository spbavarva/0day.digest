Citrix and Ivanti each shipped patches this week for critical and
high-severity vulnerabilities in their products. The flaws could let
attackers tamper with certificate entries, achieve remote code execution,
or compromise affected hosts.

Both vendors are frequent targets for post-patch exploitation, so
organizations running Citrix or Ivanti infrastructure should prioritize
testing and deploying these updates promptly rather than waiting for a
standard patch cycle.
