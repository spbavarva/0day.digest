A large-scale campaign targeting exposed network edge devices, including
Fortinet FortiGate and Palo Alto Networks GlobalProtect firewalls, has
exposed more than 70,000 credentials.

Investigators say the operators have gained access to numerous
organizations worldwide and have attempted further compromise at some
victims. Organizations running affected edge devices should treat
exposed VPN and firewall credentials as compromised and rotate them.
