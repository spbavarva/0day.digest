Canadian cybersecurity executive Edward Dubrovsky has been arrested in
Pennsylvania in connection with alleged extortion activity that multiple
reports link to the FBI's ongoing crackdown on the ShinyHunters hacking
group.

Specific charges and Dubrovsky's alleged role have not yet been disclosed.
The arrest is part of a broader law enforcement push against ShinyHunters,
which has been tied to a string of high-profile data breaches and
extortion campaigns.
