Researchers at Hunt.io disclosed "Operation CameraSwarm," a campaign that
compromised more than 14,530 Dahua devices between June 17 and July 22,
2026. The operation combined credential attacks, two authentication-bypass
flaws, and a peer-to-peer relay technique to reach devices. The findings
were reconstructed from a 407 MB exposed working directory containing
2,616 files left behind by the operators. Owners of Dahua devices should
verify firmware is current and default/weak credentials are rotated.
