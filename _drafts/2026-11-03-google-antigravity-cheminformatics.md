Google DeepMind detailed new Cheminformatics Skills for Google
Antigravity that can search chemical compounds and propose novel
rigidified macrocycles to accelerate drug discovery workflows.

The feature targets agentic research use cases rather than general
consumers. No security-specific details were included in the
available summary.
