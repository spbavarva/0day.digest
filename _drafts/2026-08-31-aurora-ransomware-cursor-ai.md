Threat actors linked to the Aurora (Aur0ra) ransomware operation have been
observed using the AI-powered coding assistant Cursor to help breach target
networks, according to separate analyses from CloudSEK and Gambit Security.
Both findings stem from exposed infrastructure tied to the Russian-speaking
cybercrime group, spanning at least 10 targets. No details on the specific
malicious use of Cursor within the intrusion chain were included in initial
reporting.
