Cisco Talos reports ransomware incidents in Japan rose 4.7% year over
year in the first half of 2026. The Gentlemen was the most active group,
with leak-site listings more than doubling between January and July,
while Qilin ranked second and showed evidence of using AI in its
operations.

Small and midsize businesses with capital under JPY 1 billion accounted
for 80% of victims. The report did not detail specifically how Qilin is
using AI in its tooling or operations.
