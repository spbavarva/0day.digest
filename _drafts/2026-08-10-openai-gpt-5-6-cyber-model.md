OpenAI released "GPT 5.6 Cyber," a model tuned for vulnerability research,
penetration testing, incident response, and remediation.

Access is restricted to approved users rather than a general release,
reflecting the dual-use risk of a model purpose-built for offensive and
defensive security work.
