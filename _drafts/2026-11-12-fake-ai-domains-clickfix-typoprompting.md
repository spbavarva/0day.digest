Researchers found that 186 lookalike domains masquerading as AI tools are
being used to serve ClickFix-style lures, browser-hijacking malware, and
extensions that hijack user searches. Island codenamed the delivery
pattern "TypoPrompting."

The campaign turns an attempt to reach a familiar AI tool into a prompt to
"verify" via a ClickFix-style instruction, tricking users into running
malicious commands themselves.
