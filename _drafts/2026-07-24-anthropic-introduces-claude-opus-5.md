Anthropic released Claude Opus 5, described as a "thoughtful and
proactive" model approaching the frontier intelligence of Claude Fable 5
at half the price. It is currently leading the Artificial Analysis
leaderboard, ahead of Fable 5. Pricing matches Opus 4.8, and a "fast
mode" remains available at twice the base cost. Early commentary also
notes Opus 5 shows notably improved resistance to prompt injection
according to its system card.
