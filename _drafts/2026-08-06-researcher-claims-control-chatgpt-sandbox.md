A researcher presented a proof-of-concept attack chain at Black Hat USA 2026
demonstrating C2-style influence over ChatGPT's isolated code execution
sandbox. The report frames this as gaining a degree of control over an
environment meant to be isolated from the host and other sessions. No detail
was given on patch status or the specific technique used. Sandbox escapes in
AI coding/execution environments are a growing research area as these
sandboxes see wider use in agentic workflows.
