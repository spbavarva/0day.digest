Hardware wallet manufacturer Trezor disclosed a data breach affecting
nearly 14,000 of its customers, after its shipping and logistics provider,
ShipMonk, was hacked.

The breach originated at a third-party vendor rather than Trezor's own
systems, underscoring supply-chain risk for hardware wallet buyers whose
shipping data can be used to target them with follow-on phishing or
physical-security scams.
