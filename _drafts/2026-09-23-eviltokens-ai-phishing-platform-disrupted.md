Microsoft disrupted EvilTokens, an AI-powered phishing-as-a-service
platform that used AI at multiple stages of its attack chain,
including generating social-engineering messages and selecting
targets.

The disruption highlights the growing use of AI to automate and
personalize phishing campaigns at scale, a pattern security teams
should account for when evaluating detection coverage against
AI-generated lures.
