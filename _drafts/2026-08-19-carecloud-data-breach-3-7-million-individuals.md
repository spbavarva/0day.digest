The confirmed scope of the CareCloud data breach has grown substantially.
The incident was initially believed to affect roughly 350,000 people, but
the HHS breach tracker now shows the impact reaching about 3.7 million
individuals. CareCloud provides healthcare technology services, so exposed
data likely includes patient information; affected individuals should
watch for phishing and identity-theft attempts referencing healthcare
providers.
