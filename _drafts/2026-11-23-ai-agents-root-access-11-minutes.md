A China-nexus threat actor used the Cairn AI-driven penetration testing
tool to go from flaw discovery to root access in just 11 minutes, Dark
Reading reports. The attacks targeted East Asian universities and
exploited a zero-day vulnerability in a medical imaging application.

The compressed timeline illustrates how AI-assisted offensive tooling is
shrinking the window defenders have to detect and respond to exploitation,
turning what used to take hours or days of manual work into a near-instant
attack chain.
