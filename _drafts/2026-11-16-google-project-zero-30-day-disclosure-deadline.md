Google Project Zero announced a phased tightening of its vulnerability
disclosure timeline. The team will move to a 60-day disclosure window
starting in February 2027, then to a 30-day window in January 2028, down
from its current 90-day standard.

The shorter windows will put more pressure on vendors to ship patches faster
once Project Zero privately reports a flaw.
