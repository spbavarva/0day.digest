A threat actor breached the internal network of Korea Electric Power
Corporation (KEPCO) and accessed two systems containing customer,
employee, and contractor data.

The breach affects an estimated 1.5 million individuals, making it one of
the larger disclosed breaches at a critical infrastructure operator this
year.
