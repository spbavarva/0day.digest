AI coding agents asked to share screenshots of code changes for review have
put internal company images into public GitHub repositories, according to
security company Glow.

Researchers found more than 13,000 internal images from developers at over
300 organizations, including customer billing records and screenshots of
unreleased features. In most cases the images sat under developers'
personal GitHub accounts rather than organization-owned repos.
