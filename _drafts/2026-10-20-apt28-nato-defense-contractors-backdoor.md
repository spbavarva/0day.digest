Researchers say the Russian state-linked group APT28 (Fancy Bear) used a
recently documented backdoor to dwell inside the networks of two defense
contractors and a law enforcement agency across two NATO member states.

The campaign is part of an ongoing espionage effort targeting organizations
with access to defense and security-related information.
