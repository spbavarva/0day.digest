Rep. Steven Horsford (D-NV) is warning that a proposed $10 million deal
to sell data to Google for AI training could expose sensitive Spirit
Airlines records.

Horsford said the proposed sale would include roughly 100 million
emails, 500 million Microsoft Teams messages, employment contracts,
employee and timecard records, and payroll and tax information.

No breach has occurred; lawmakers are raising concerns about the deal
before it proceeds.
