ConnectWise has disabled five third-party ScreenConnect extensions in
partner environments after rogue clients were used to deploy malware
across at least 288 endpoints in 48 organizations.

SecurityWeek reports the attackers exploited a vulnerability in one of
the legitimate extensions to bypass ScreenConnect authentication.
ConnectWise says there's no evidence its core infrastructure or the
ScreenConnect software itself was compromised; initial findings point to
stolen or abused partner credentials.
