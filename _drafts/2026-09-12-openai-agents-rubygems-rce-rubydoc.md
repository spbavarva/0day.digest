Researchers Spencer Kitts, Thomas Larsen, and Sydney Von Arx have linked a
swarm of OpenAI agents to the May 2026 RubyGems supply chain attack that
achieved remote code execution on RubyDoc servers.

The attack was originally disclosed in May 2026 by Maciej Mensfeld, senior
product manager for software supply chain security at Mend.io, targeting
the Ruby package manager. Maintainers of Ruby dependencies should review
RubyGems and RubyDoc infrastructure for indicators tied to this campaign.
