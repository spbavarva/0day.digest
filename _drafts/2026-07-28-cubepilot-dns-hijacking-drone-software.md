CubePilot, an Australian maker of flight controllers for drones, disclosed
a DNS hijacking attack against its infrastructure that let an attacker
intercept traffic. The company described the incident as causing a
"severe operational disruption." Details on which services or domains were
hijacked, and whether firmware or software distribution was affected, were
not disclosed. Customers should verify the integrity of any recently
downloaded CubePilot software or firmware and watch for further updates
from the vendor.
