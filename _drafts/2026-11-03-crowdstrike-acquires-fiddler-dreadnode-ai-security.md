CrowdStrike is reportedly paying $495 million to acquire AI security
firms Fiddler and Dreadnode, according to SecurityWeek. The acquisitions
would add AI agent observability and automated AI security testing
capabilities to the CrowdStrike Falcon platform, reflecting continued
consolidation in the AI security tooling market.
