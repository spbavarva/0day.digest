Cisco Talos published research tracing how JavaScript obfuscation
techniques have evolved from novelty tricks into a core component of
modern phishing kits. The research covers what obfuscation is, why a
researcher would try to reverse it, and several approaches to
deobfuscation.

Aimed at practitioners analyzing phishing infrastructure and building
detections for obfuscated kit code.
