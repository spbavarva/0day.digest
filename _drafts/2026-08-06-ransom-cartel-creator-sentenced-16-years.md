A federal judge in Alexandria, Virginia sentenced Maksim Silnikau, the
Belarusian creator and administrator of the Ransom Cartel
ransomware-as-a-service operation, to 16 years in prison.

Between 2021 and 2023, Ransom Cartel affiliates attacked at least 18
companies across the US and abroad; Silnikau was also involved in
distributing the Angler exploit kit.

No new technical detail or IOCs accompany the sentencing — it closes out a
long-running DOJ case against one of the more prolific RaaS operators of
the early 2020s.
