Nitori, a major Japanese furniture and home goods retailer, disclosed that
an attacker accessed a system on its network and may have stolen
approximately 37.62 million customer records. The company has not yet
detailed the attack vector or specified which categories of personal data
were exposed. Given the scale, affected customers should watch for
phishing attempts referencing Nitori.
