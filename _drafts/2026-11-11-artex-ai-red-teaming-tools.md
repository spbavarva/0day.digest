Unit 42 published research on ARTEX and other AI-powered red-teaming
tools, examining how attackers are adopting agentic offensive-security
tooling that was originally built for defenders. The research frames
guidance for defenders around this emerging category of tooling rather
than a single confirmed incident.

Security teams evaluating or deploying AI-driven red-teaming platforms
should be aware that the same tooling can be repurposed by attackers,
and should apply the same access controls and monitoring they'd use for
any other offensive-security capability.
