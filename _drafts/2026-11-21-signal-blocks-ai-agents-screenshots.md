Signal will restrict screenshots by default to stop AI agents built into
mobile operating systems from reading private chats on Android and iOS.
The feature targets agentic systems in iOS 27 and Android versions where
the encrypted messaging app can detect an AI assistant attempting to read
its content via screen capture.

Signal acknowledged the protection is "incomplete," meaning some
screen-reading agent access may still get through. The move reflects a
broader concern about OS-level AI agents accessing sensitive app content
without explicit user consent.
