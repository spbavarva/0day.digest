CISA added six vulnerabilities to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities
catalog based on evidence of active exploitation, including CVE-2026-20320
(Cisco BroadWorks XXE), CVE-2026-111560 (Barracuda Networks OS command
injection), CVE-2026-111793 (Openwave Messaging Webtop Media Transcoder
resource-transfer flaw), and CVE-2026-116606 (Barracuda Email Security
Gateway authentication bypass). Federal civilian agencies must remediate
KEV-listed flaws within CISA's mandated timelines. Organizations running
any of the affected Cisco, Barracuda, or Openwave products should check
for available patches.
