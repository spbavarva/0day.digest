A malware-as-a-service platform called Lunex is distributing the Psychedelic
Stealer through compromised Ukrainian websites, using ClickFix-style fake
CAPTCHA pages to trick victims, according to research from Ontinue.

The four-stage attack chain targets Ukrainian-speaking users and abuses a
legitimate AMD driver to disable security monitoring on infected hosts
before harvesting browser-stored credentials.
