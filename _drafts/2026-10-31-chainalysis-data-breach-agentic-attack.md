Blockchain analytics firm Chainalysis disclosed a data breach after
attackers used "offensive agentic techniques" to gain access to internal
systems and tools. The exposure includes customer names, email addresses,
user IDs, and organizational roles.

The incident is notable for the attacker's use of AI agents offensively
rather than as a side tool, an emerging pattern worth tracking. Chainalysis
customers should watch for targeted phishing using the exposed identity data.
