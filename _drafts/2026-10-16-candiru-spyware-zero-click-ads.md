Candiru spyware was delivered to devices at Al Jazeera Media Network
through targeted online advertisements, in a zero-click attack that
required only rendering the malicious ads on the victim's device.

Candiru is a commercial spyware vendor whose tools have previously been
linked to surveillance of journalists and civil society targets.

No interaction beyond viewing the ad was needed for the spyware to be
installed.
