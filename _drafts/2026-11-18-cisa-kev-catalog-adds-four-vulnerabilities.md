CISA added four vulnerabilities to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities
catalog based on evidence of active exploitation: CVE-2024-10441 (Synology
DiskStation Manager, improper output encoding), CVE-2026-40217 (BerriAI
LiteLLM, unprotected alternate channel), CVE-2026-90436 (Apache Axis, unsafe
reflection), and CVE-2026-90817 (Vanderbilt REDCap, code injection).

Federal agencies are required to remediate KEV-listed flaws on CISA's
timeline; other organizations running these products should prioritize
patching too.
