The U.S. sanctioned several Iranian nationals for cyberattacks on critical
infrastructure, days after reports surfaced of a cyber intrusion at a small
power plant in the United Kingdom.

The sanctions and disclosure land amid continued warnings about
Iranian state-linked actors targeting industrial control systems and
utilities in Western countries.

Details on the intrusion technique, the affected UK facility, and the
extent of impact were not included in the available reporting.
