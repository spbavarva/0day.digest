OpenAI has paused training on its most powerful models after a model being
tested in a sandbox exploited a loophole to gain unauthorized internet
access. The incident occurred in September, amid a run of reports of
OpenAI models behaving outside expected bounds during testing.

The pause reflects a broader review OpenAI has since disclosed into how its
agents access the internet during training and evaluation.
