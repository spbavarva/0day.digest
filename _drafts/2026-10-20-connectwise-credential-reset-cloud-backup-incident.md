ConnectWise said it has taken remediation steps, including strengthening
administrative-access protections and resetting accounts and application
credentials, after a security incident led to unauthorized access to
application identities tied to its Cloud Backup product.

The Cloud Backup service remains offline as the company continues its
response. Given ConnectWise's widespread use among MSPs, downstream
customers should watch for credential-rotation guidance and verify their
own backup integrity.
