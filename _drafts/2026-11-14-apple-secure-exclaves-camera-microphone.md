Apple detailed the architecture behind "Secure Exclaves," which isolates
camera and microphone sensor data from the main OS on HomeView and new
iPhone models. The design is meant to keep ambient sensor data out of reach
of a compromised main OS.

This is a defensive architecture disclosure rather than a response to a
specific incident or vulnerability; no exploit was reported.
