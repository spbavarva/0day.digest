Cursor confirmed that the recent compromise of Anthropic's official Claude
Code extension on its marketplace stemmed from a stolen access key. The key
had been issued to Fireworks AI and was exposed during a breach at that
company; an attacker then used it to copy data from a read-only source,
according to Cursor.

Anyone who installed the Claude Code extension via Cursor's marketplace
should check for signs of compromise and rotate credentials that may have
been exposed through the extension or related integrations.
