Medical device maker iRhythm disclosed that hackers stole personal
information belonging to at least 360,000 people, including names,
addresses, email addresses, dates of birth, and phone numbers.

No financial or medical record exposure was disclosed. Affected individuals
should watch for phishing attempts that leverage the exposed personal
details.
