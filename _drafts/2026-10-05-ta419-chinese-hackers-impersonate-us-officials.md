A threat group tracked as TA419 established seemingly legitimate
professional relationships with AI policy experts at US think tanks,
universities, and legal organizations by impersonating US officials.

The campaign appears aimed at cyber espionage against individuals working
on AI policy. Further technical detail on delivery methods or IOCs was not
included in the available summary.
