Healthcare and pharmaceutical distribution giant McKesson has disclosed a
cybersecurity incident involving unauthorized access to third-party
applications and data theft. The extortion group ShinyHunters claims to
have stolen 284 million patient data records in the attack. McKesson has
not independently confirmed the scale of the claimed theft.

Given the sensitivity of patient health data and the size of the alleged
exposure, organizations that work with McKesson or its affected
third-party systems should watch for official notifications and monitor
for downstream phishing or fraud attempts tied to the incident.
