A data-extortion group tracked as UNC6671 is running a vishing campaign
against employees at financial services, private equity, and professional
services firms, posing as IT help desk staff running mandatory security
migrations. The group contacts targets on personal phone numbers rather
than corporate lines, a tactic aimed at bypassing corporate call-monitoring.

The goal is theft of data from SaaS platforms rather than ransomware
deployment. No specific IOCs or affected companies were named in initial
reporting.
