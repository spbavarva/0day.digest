The Florida Department of Education disclosed a breach of its student
financial aid website that may have exposed Social Security numbers
and other personal data belonging to 4.9 million people. The
department has not detailed the attack vector in public statements so
far.

Given the scale and sensitivity of the data involved, affected
individuals should watch for identity-theft and credit-monitoring
guidance from the department. This is among the largest education
sector breaches disclosed this year.
