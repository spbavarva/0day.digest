Anthropic released Claude Fable 5.2 and Claude Mythos 5.2, which it says
show improvements in coding, reasoning, and vision over prior versions.
The company said that during pre-release testing, the models helped
engineers complete otherwise-infeasible software migrations, design
working electronic devices, and make progress on open physics problems.
Anthropic also claims Fable 5.2 outperformed GPT-6 Cyber in a multi-stage
cyberattack benchmark — a notable data point for security teams
evaluating AI capability in offensive and defensive cyber tasks.
