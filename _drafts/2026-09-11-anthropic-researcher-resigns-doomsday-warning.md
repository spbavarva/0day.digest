An Anthropic researcher resigned this week and posted a warning on X
stating the company is "racing straight to self-improving superintelligence
and gambling with our lives." Anthropic's own alignment lead co-signed the
message rather than distancing the company from it. The warning comes as
Anthropic is reportedly preparing for an IPO, adding scrutiny to the timing.
No technical details of a specific incident were disclosed alongside the
warning.
