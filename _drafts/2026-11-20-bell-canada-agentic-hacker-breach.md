Bell Canada confirmed that a threat actor it describes as "agentic"
breached its systems on November 17, 2026, and stole enterprise customer
data.

The stolen data includes information from a system used to order and
provision telecommunications circuits, which could expose details about
enterprise customers' network infrastructure.

Bell has not detailed what it means by "agentic" — whether the attacker
used AI agents to conduct the intrusion — or disclosed how many customers
were affected. Enterprise customers should watch for follow-on social
engineering referencing their provisioning details.
