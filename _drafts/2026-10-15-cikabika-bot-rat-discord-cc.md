A newly documented RAT dubbed CikaBika is delivered via phishing and uses
Discord as its command-and-control channel.

Once installed, it provides reconnaissance, remote command execution, and
data exfiltration capabilities.

Discord-based C2 remains attractive to malware operators because the
traffic blends in with legitimate collaboration-tool usage, complicating
network-based detection.
