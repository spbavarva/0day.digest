Abdulrahman Alfozan, a 35-year-old Saudi national and OpenAI engineer, was
arrested and charged with using a device to intercept oral communications
after allegedly planting a hidden recording device in an executive
conference room at OpenAI's San Francisco offices.

OpenAI terminated Alfozan following the arrest. Authorities have not
disclosed what, if any, information was captured by the device.
