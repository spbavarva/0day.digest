An advanced persistent threat group dubbed "FrostyNeighbor" launched a
destructive attack in Ukraine, according to Dark Reading.

The activity is raising concerns about deeper alignment between
Belarusian threat actors and Russian cyber objectives as the broader
Russia-linked cyberwar against Ukraine continues to evolve.

Organizations with exposure to Ukrainian or regional infrastructure
should treat Belarus-linked activity as an extension of the existing
Russian-aligned threat landscape rather than a separate, lower-priority
actor.
