Attackers are abusing legitimate Bing search-result redirects as click URLs
inside Google search ads to direct victims to fake Claude desktop installers.
The fake installers deliver ClickFix-style attacks, which typically trick
users into running malicious commands themselves via a copy-paste "fix"
prompt.

Using a trusted redirect domain inside the ad click URL helps the malicious
ad slip past Google's ad-review process. Users should download AI tools only
from official vendor sites and be wary of any installer that asks them to
paste commands into a terminal or Run dialog.
