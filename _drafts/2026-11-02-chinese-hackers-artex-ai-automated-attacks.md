Chinese state-linked threat actors used ARTEX, an open-source
multi-agent AI penetration-testing framework, to automate attacks
against more than 200 organizations across 14 countries. The campaign
shows legitimate offensive-security AI tooling being repurposed for
real intrusion at scale rather than authorized testing.

No specific CVEs or IOCs were included in the available summary.
Organizations should review detection coverage for automated,
multi-agent attack patterns rather than single-tool signatures.
