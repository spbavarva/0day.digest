Anthropic has launched OSS Scanner, a program that sends unreviewed,
model-generated vulnerability reports directly to open source maintainers
who opt in, according to SecurityWeek.

Separately, Anthropic said it has brought on 11 firms to work on
operational technology (OT) security, though further detail on the scope
of that effort wasn't given.

Because the OSS Scanner reports are explicitly unreviewed by humans before
reaching maintainers, projects that opt in should expect a mix of real
findings and false positives, and triage accordingly rather than assume
AI-generated reports carry the same signal quality as human-reported ones.
