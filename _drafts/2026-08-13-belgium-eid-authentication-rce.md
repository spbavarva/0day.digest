Severe vulnerabilities in a browser extension underlying Belgium's
electronic ID (eID) trust framework fully compromised the authentication
system protecting citizen accounts.

The findings highlight broader risks in relying on browser extensions as a
trust anchor for national identity systems, a pattern that may extend to
other countries' eID implementations.
