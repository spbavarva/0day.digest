Kaspersky researchers discovered malicious TrueConf software installers
being used by the Head Mare APT group to deliver the PhantomCore and
PhantomGraph backdoors to video conference participants. The group
exploits vulnerabilities in unpatched, self-hosted TrueConf servers to
distribute the trojanized installers to targets.
