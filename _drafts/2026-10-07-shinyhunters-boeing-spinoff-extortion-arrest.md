A teenager from Amman, Jordan, suspected of leading the ShinyHunters data
theft and extortion group has been detained and is reportedly cooperating
with the FBI to identify other group members.

The suspect, who uses the handle "Rey," was detained while ShinyHunters was
in the process of extorting a business unit recently divested by Boeing.

The arrest is a significant development against one of the more prolific
extortion groups currently operating, though the extent of data taken from
the Boeing spin-off has not been detailed.
