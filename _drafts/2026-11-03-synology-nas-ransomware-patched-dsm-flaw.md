Synology is urging customers to update DiskStation Manager (DSM) after
observing ransomware attacks exploiting a vulnerability that was patched
in December 2024, per The Hacker News. Threat actors are targeting
devices still running outdated DSM versions to encrypt data and extort
victims. Synology NAS admins on older DSM builds should patch
immediately.
