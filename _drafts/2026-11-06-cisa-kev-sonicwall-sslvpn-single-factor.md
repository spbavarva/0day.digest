CISA added CVE-2026-19868, a SonicWall SonicOS SSLVPN vulnerability
involving use of single-factor authentication, to its Known Exploited
Vulnerabilities catalog based on evidence of active exploitation.
Under Binding Operational Directive 26-04, FCEB agencies must
prioritize patching based on this risk.

SonicWall SSLVPN access has historically been a common entry point for
intrusions; affected organizations should enforce MFA and patch
promptly.
