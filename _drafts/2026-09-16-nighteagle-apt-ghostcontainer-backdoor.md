Kaspersky's GReAT researchers uncovered a new campaign by the
NightEagle APT group targeting Russian companies, using a backdoor
dubbed GhostContainer along with tooling hosted on GitHub. The group is
also exploiting vulnerabilities in Active Directory and RDP as part of
the intrusion chain.

No specific victim count was given in the source summary.
