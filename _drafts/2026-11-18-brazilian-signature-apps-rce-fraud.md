Government-sanctioned authentication middleware used for legally binding
digital signatures in Brazil is reportedly affected by remote code execution
bugs, according to Dark Reading.

Because this middleware underpins legally important documents, flaws in it
carry both a technical RCE risk and a fraud risk for signed records. The feed
summary did not include a CVE identifier or vendor name.
