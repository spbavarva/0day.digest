A report highlights the rise of "Bring Your Own Agent" (BYOA), where
employees' personal AI assistants are already operating inside corporate
environments, often without security team visibility.

Cited examples include Amazon blocking Meta's Muse agent for not
identifying itself as a bot while browsing, alongside ongoing privacy
concerns tied to other personal AI agents. The piece frames unmanaged agent
adoption as an emerging shadow-IT-style risk for enterprises.
