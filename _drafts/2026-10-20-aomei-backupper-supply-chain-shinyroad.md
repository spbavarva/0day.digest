Attackers compromised AOMEI's software update infrastructure and used it to
serve the ShinyRoad modular backdoor framework during multiple short,
separate compromise windows between April and July. AOMEI has reportedly
not yet formally notified customers of the incident.

Organizations running AOMEI Backupper should check for signs of ShinyRoad
compromise and verify update integrity going forward, since the brief
compromise windows make this easy to miss in standard patch logs.
