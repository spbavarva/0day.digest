Check Point patched two critical vulnerabilities in its VPN products,
tracked as CVE-2026-85102 and CVE-2026-85103, that could be exploited for
remote code execution. The disclosure did not detail the specific affected
product lines or attack vector. Administrators running Check Point VPN
gateways should apply the vendor's patches promptly given the RCE impact.
