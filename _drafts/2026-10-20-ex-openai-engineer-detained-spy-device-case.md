A U.S. court granted the government's request to detain former OpenAI
engineer Abdulrahman Alfozan, a 35-year-old Saudi national, over allegations
he planted a recording device in an executive conference room at OpenAI's
San Francisco office to steal sensitive secrets.

Prosecutors said a court filing revealed frequent WhatsApp communications
tied to Saudi royal contacts.
