Google unveiled Gemini 4 Carbon, a frontier model it says leads on SEC-bench
Pro (88.2%) for finding vulnerabilities and ExploitGym (54.8%) for
exploitation, along with strong results on patching tasks. The model is
rolling out first to vetted cyber defenders through Google's Fairwind
Program before wider availability.
