A proxyjacking campaign has compromised D-Link and Ubiquiti devices across 34
countries, enrolling them into a commercial residential proxy network
without owner consent. Compromised devices' internet connections are
monetized by reselling bandwidth, a lower-visibility model than traditional
botnets since affected users mostly just notice reduced throughput.

Owners of D-Link and Ubiquiti gear should check for unfamiliar outbound
connections and apply available firmware updates.
