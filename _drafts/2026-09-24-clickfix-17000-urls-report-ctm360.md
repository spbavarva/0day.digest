A new global threat report from CTM360, analyzing 17,000 URLs, finds
ClickFix has become the most common way attackers gain initial access to
enterprise networks — without an exploit, attachment, or file on disk.

The report traces ClickFix's evolution from a novelty technique in late
2023 to a commercialized subscription product with on-chain
infrastructure and a state-sponsored user base.

CTM360 argues that blocking malicious domains is no longer an effective
defense against the technique, given how quickly infrastructure rotates.
Organizations should focus on user training against paste-and-run
prompts instead.
