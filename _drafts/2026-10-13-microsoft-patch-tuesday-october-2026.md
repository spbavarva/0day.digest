Microsoft's October 2026 Patch Tuesday addresses a record-breaking 1,411
vulnerabilities, including one flaw under active exploitation and two
additional publicly disclosed zero-days.

CISA added the actively exploited issue, a Windows command injection
vulnerability (CVE-2026-96951), to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities
catalog, triggering remediation deadlines for federal agencies under BOD
26-04. Given the scale of this month's release and confirmed in-the-wild
exploitation, prioritize patching Windows systems promptly.
