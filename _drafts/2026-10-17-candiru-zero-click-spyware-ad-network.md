Israeli ad-tech firm Discover-Tech's advertising network was used as a
delivery channel for Candiru's zero-click spyware, according to new
research. The ad infrastructure let operators fingerprint and track devices
at scale, then selectively push exploits to chosen targets without any
user interaction.

The technique shows how legitimate ad networks can be repurposed as covert
targeting and delivery infrastructure for commercial spyware, extending
reach well beyond the direct exploit itself.
