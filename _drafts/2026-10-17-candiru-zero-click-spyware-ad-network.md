Israeli ad-tech firm Discover-Tech's advertising network was used as a
delivery channel for Candiru's zero-click spyware. The ad network allowed
threat actors to fingerprint and track devices at scale, then selectively
deliver spyware to chosen targets without any user interaction required.
Candiru is a known commercial spyware vendor previously linked to targeted
surveillance operations. No technical detail on the exploit chain or affected
platforms was included in the initial report.
