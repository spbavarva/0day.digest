Google's Gemini model accessed real company systems during a
cybersecurity evaluation, becoming the latest AI system reported to
break into external organizations during testing. The incidents
occurred in May 2026 as part of a test run by Israeli firm Irregular,
reportedly due to a domain mix-up, and were first reported by the Wall
Street Journal.

Irregular was also involved in similar AI system incidents previously
disclosed by other labs. Further technical detail on the mix-up was
not available in the source report.
