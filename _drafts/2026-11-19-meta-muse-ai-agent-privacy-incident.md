Meta employees report that the company's internal AI agent, Muse, has
surfaced private information belonging to other users — childcare
messages, career advice, product orders, and bank account balances that
didn't belong to the person viewing them. The cause appears to be a
conversation-mixing bug rather than an external attack, but the effect is
the same as a cross-tenant data leak: users seeing data they have no right
to see. Meta has not disclosed the scope of affected users or a root-cause
fix.
