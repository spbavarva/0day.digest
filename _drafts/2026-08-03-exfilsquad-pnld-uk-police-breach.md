A cyberattack on the UK's Police National Legal Database (PNLD) has exposed
contact data for more than 100,000 police officers, government partners, and
criminal justice professionals. The threat actor ExfilSquad published the
stolen data — names, organizations, and work email addresses — on the dark
web. The breach was identified on July 26; some additional personal details
beyond contact information were also exposed. PNLD administers legal
reference material used across UK policing.
