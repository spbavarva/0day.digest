In March 2026, attackers stole personal, financial, and medical information
belonging to more than 350,000 individuals from CareCloud's AWS environment.
CareCloud provides healthcare technology and billing services, making the
exposed data particularly sensitive.

No further technical detail on the intrusion vector has been disclosed.
Affected individuals should watch for identity theft and phishing attempts
referencing their medical or financial history.
