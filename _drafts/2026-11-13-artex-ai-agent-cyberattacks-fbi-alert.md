The FBI issued a flash alert warning that ARTEX, an open-source agentic AI
penetration-testing framework, is being used by threat actors to automate
and chain attack techniques "at machine speed," with some intrusions
progressing from initial targeting to privileged access in under three
hours.

Cisco Talos separately detailed how ARTEX and similar AI-enabled pentesting
platforms lower the skill threshold needed to chain reconnaissance,
vulnerability testing, and follow-on actions. AWS said its MadPot honeypot
network captured requests from ARTEX agents running real offensive campaigns
between October 3 and November 5, 2026, confirming the tool is already being
used against live targets, not just in research settings.
