North Korean threat actor BlueNoroff, previously linked to ClickFix-style
campaigns using typosquatted Zoom and Microsoft Teams domains, has been
found operating an active phishing kit that impersonates videoconferencing
platforms. The kit profiles a victim's cryptocurrency wallets before
delivering malware, and the group combines compromised industry contacts
with social engineering to build trust ahead of the attack. Organizations
in crypto and fintech should treat unsolicited Zoom/Teams meeting links
from new contacts with added scrutiny.
