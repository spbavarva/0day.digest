Vishing extortion group UNC6671, which initially operated under the name
BlackFile, has expanded operations under additional brand names: Redact,
Pink, Helix, and Falcon. The group is reported to have made significant
money from its extortion campaigns before rebranding. No new technical
indicators or victim details were included in the available summary.
