Liquid AI released LFM2.5-24B-A2B, a model built to run AI agents
on-device rather than through cloud inference. Running inference locally
keeps prompts and outputs off external servers, which practitioners
evaluating privacy and data-residency requirements for AI agents may want
to note.

No further technical detail was available in the announcement.
