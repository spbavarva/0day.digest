The Jewelbug hacker group has been running espionage operations against
government and military targets while simultaneously conducting
cryptocurrency fraud, including breaching government webmail systems.

The dual-purpose activity blurs the line between state-linked espionage and
financially motivated cybercrime, complicating attribution for defenders
tracking the group.
