A threat actor distributed the Vinci remote access trojan through a
Microsoft Store application, according to research from eSentire.
Victims were redirected to a fake site impersonating a sniping tool,
where a Microsoft-signed installer named "Snipping-Tool.exe" delivered
the RAT.

The Microsoft-signed nature of the installer may have helped the malware
evade default security warnings. Vinci RAT gives attackers remote
control over infected systems.
