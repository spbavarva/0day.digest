Surfshark disclosed that a misconfigured test server was accessed by
unauthorized parties, exposing internal engineering material including
configuration data. The company said the exposed server was used for
testing rather than production, limiting the scope of the exposure. No
customer credential or VPN traffic data was reported affected. Surfshark
has since secured the server.
