OpenAI announced it will honor a shared robots.txt-based signal that lets
website operators communicate their AI training preferences to
participating AI providers.

The goal is to give site owners a standardized, machine-readable way to
opt in or out of having their content used for AI training, beyond ad hoc
per-crawler rules.

OpenAI says other providers can adopt the same signal, though the post
doesn't name which other companies have committed to honoring it.

No enforcement mechanism was described for sites whose preferences are
ignored by non-participating crawlers.
