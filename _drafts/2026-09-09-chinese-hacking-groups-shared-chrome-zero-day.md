A Google Chrome bug identified in August was exploited by at least four
separate China-linked cyber-espionage groups, according to researchers. The
shared use of an identical exploit across multiple distinct threat actors
suggests either a common exploit supplier or shared tooling within
China-nexus espionage operations.
