Google announced Gemini 4 Argon, its latest frontier AI model, and is
rolling it out to a set of trusted cyber defenders through its Fairwind
Program.

Google says the model delivers frontier performance across software
engineering, enterprise knowledge work, and cybersecurity defense
workflows.

Google also plans to release a guardrail-free version of the model, though
further detail on scope and safeguards for that release was not disclosed.
