Google announced Gemini 4 Argon, a new frontier AI model, and began rolling
it out to a set of vetted cyber defenders through its new Fairwind Program.
A fully "guardrail-free" access tier is planned for trusted defenders but
has not yet shipped broadly. Google said the model has already found a
critical vulnerability in software used by hospitals worldwide, though no
further technical detail on that finding was disclosed. Google describes the
model as delivering frontier performance on software engineering, enterprise
knowledge work, and cybersecurity defense tasks.
