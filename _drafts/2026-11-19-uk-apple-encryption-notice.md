A UK tribunal confirmed that the British government served Apple with a
secret legal notice targeting the cryptographic protections on iCloud
accounts, ending nearly two years of official refusal to acknowledge what
had been widely reported. The notice is understood to relate to Apple's
Advanced Data Protection encryption feature. The confirmation doesn't
reveal the notice's current status or whether Apple has complied, but it
sets a legal precedent other governments may point to when seeking similar
access to encrypted cloud data.
