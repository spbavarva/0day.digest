Attackers exploiting what's being called "FortiBleed" are locking
legitimate administrators out of compromised Fortinet devices by
creating new accounts and deleting existing accounts and passwords.

Organizations running Fortinet devices should check for unauthorized
account changes and review access logs for signs of this lockout
behavior.
