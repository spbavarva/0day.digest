SecurityWeek reports that the Russian state-linked threat group Star
Blizzard weaponized data and identities stolen from a compromised firm
in late 2025 to phish and compromise a separate US organization.

By posing as an employee of the already-breached company, the group
added credibility to its phishing attempts against the new target.

The campaign is a reminder that stolen identity data from one breach
routinely gets reused to enable follow-on attacks against unrelated
organizations, particularly by nation-state-aligned phishing
operations.
