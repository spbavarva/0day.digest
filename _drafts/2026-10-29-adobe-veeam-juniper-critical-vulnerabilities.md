Adobe, Veeam, and Juniper Networks each shipped patches this week for
critical vulnerabilities in their products.

The flaws could lead to arbitrary code execution, authorization bypasses,
and privilege escalation. No active exploitation has been reported.
Affected organizations should apply the vendor updates.
