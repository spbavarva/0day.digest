The Pwn2Own Ireland 2026 hacking contest concluded with organizers paying
out $1,262,000 across 98 zero-day vulnerabilities disclosed by competing
research teams, BleepingComputer reports.

Among the results, three separate teams demonstrated remote exploit chains
against a fully patched Google Pixel 10 — one, from Ikotas Labs, earned
$300,000 and the contest's top overall prize.

All disclosed flaws are passed to the affected vendors for patching under
Pwn2Own's responsible-disclosure rules rather than released publicly, so
none of the 98 zero-days are exploitable in the wild outside the contest.
