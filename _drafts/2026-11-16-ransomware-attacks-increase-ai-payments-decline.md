A growing number of ransomware groups are using large language models and
other AI systems to craft more convincing phishing emails and to quickly
identify sensitive data worth stealing during intrusions.

Despite the rise in AI-assisted ransomware activity, overall ransom
payments are declining, suggesting victim organizations are increasingly
refusing to pay even as attacks scale. The trend reinforces the value of
phishing-resistant authentication and data classification to blunt
AI-accelerated reconnaissance.
