Anthropic's Opus 5 is nearing the bug-finding performance of Mythos 5 but
falls short on generating working exploits. Binary-based vulnerability
scanning, penetration testing, and exploit generation are blocked features
in Opus 5. The comparison suggests frontier models are closing the gap on
defensive vulnerability discovery faster than on offensive exploit
development. The distinction matters for organizations evaluating
AI-assisted vulnerability research tools for defensive versus offensive use
cases.
