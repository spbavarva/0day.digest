NVIDIA released Nemotron 3.5 Transcribe and Granary v2, open multilingual
transcription models that can be prompted and adapted, supporting up to 40
languages. The models work on recorded audio rather than live/real-time
streams, positioning them for meeting and medical transcription workflows
rather than real-time use cases.
