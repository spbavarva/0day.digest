Anthropic is expanding its program that gives vetted cybersecurity
professionals access to its most capable Claude models with reduced
safeguards and blocking classifiers.

The company said its Project Glasswing initiative, run under this program,
found at least 129,000 verified software vulnerabilities between April and
July 2026.

Anthropic is folding this program together with its existing Cyber
Verification Program (CVP) into a single offering with tiered access
levels.
