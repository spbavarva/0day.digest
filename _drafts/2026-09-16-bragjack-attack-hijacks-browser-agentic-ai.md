Researchers disclosed a new attack, dubbed BragJack, that hijacks the
agentic AI assistants built directly into web browsers. Once hijacked,
the assistant can be used to access sensitive information, execute
malicious actions, and exfiltrate data on the attacker's behalf.

The technique targets the growing category of in-browser agentic AI
features, an attack surface expected to expand as more browsers ship
built-in AI agents.
