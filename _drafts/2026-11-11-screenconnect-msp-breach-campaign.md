ConnectWise is investigating an active malware campaign targeting
managed service providers that uses compromised ScreenConnect
environments to deploy rogue remote-access clients onto customer
devices. The campaign has already impacted dozens of organizations
downstream of affected MSPs.

Because ScreenConnect is widely used by MSPs for remote support, a
single compromised MSP instance can give attackers a path into many
client environments at once. Organizations using MSP-managed
ScreenConnect deployments should review remote-access client
inventories for unauthorized entries while ConnectWise's investigation
continues.
