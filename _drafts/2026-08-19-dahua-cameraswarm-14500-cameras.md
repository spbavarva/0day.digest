Researchers dubbed a 35-day campaign "CameraSwarm" after tracking the
compromise of more than 14,500 Dahua IP cameras, concentrated mostly in
Ukraine and Russia.

The campaign's scale suggests automated, opportunistic exploitation
across exposed camera deployments rather than a single targeted intrusion.

Organizations running Dahua camera fleets should verify firmware is
current, confirm cameras aren't exposed directly to the internet, and
rotate default or weak credentials.
