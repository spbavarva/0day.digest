An account tied to the ShinyHunters extortion group announced the group's
return. Okta researchers separately spotted possible links between a newer
group calling itself "Inception" and ShinyHunters, which the Inception group
rejects.

No new victim data has been confirmed tied to either group yet, but the
possible resurgence of a group with ShinyHunters' track record is worth
tracking closely.
