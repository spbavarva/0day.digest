The Department of Justice and FBI announced two separate actions against
North Korea-linked cryptocurrency theft operations: the seizure of $1.2
billion in funds stolen from Binance, and a takedown of the "FaceStealer"
toolset.

The technical scope of the FaceStealer takedown wasn't detailed in initial
reporting. The actions are part of a broader US law enforcement push
against DPRK-linked crypto theft infrastructure.
