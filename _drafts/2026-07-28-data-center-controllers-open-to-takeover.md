A large number of internet-exposed remote hardware management processors
used to administer data center servers are vulnerable to offline
password-cracking attacks, according to Dark Reading. Researchers say
adversaries have already taken note of the exposure. These controllers
typically have privileged, out-of-band access to the hardware they manage,
so compromise can bypass OS-level security controls entirely. Operators
should confirm management interfaces for server controllers are not
exposed to the public internet and enforce strong credentials.
