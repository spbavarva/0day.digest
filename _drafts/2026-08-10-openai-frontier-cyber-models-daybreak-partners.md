OpenAI said "Daybreak" partners approved through a governance process can
now use its frontier cyber models to deliver authorized cybersecurity
services to customers. The program is framed as a way to give vetted
organizations governed access to higher-capability cyber models rather than
releasing them broadly. No list of current Daybreak partners or eligibility
criteria was included in OpenAI's post.
