SonicWall urged customers still running Generation 6 firewalls to retire
and replace them immediately, warning the devices won't receive security
patches even for critical flaws going forward.

SonicWall specifically cited AI-assisted attack automation as a reason the
risk is accelerating, saying attackers now use AI to find and exploit
unsupported edge devices faster than before.

Organizations with internet-facing Gen 6 SonicWall firewalls should
prioritize replacement, since any newly discovered flaw in these devices
will go unpatched.
