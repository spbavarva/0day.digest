The European Union has designated ChatGPT a "Very Large Online Search
Engine" under the Digital Services Act. The classification puts OpenAI on
the hook for new obligations around mitigating risks to minors, user mental
health, and the spread of illegal content within the EU. The tougher
regulatory regime applies as part of the DSA's ongoing expansion to major
online platforms.
