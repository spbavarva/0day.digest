SAP released patches for critical vulnerabilities in NetWeaver and
S/4HANA that could allow attackers to invoke internal operations and
execute code, fully compromising affected systems.

No detail was given on active exploitation, but given the business-critical
role these systems play, affected organizations should prioritize patching.
