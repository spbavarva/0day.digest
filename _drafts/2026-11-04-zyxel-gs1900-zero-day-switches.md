Zyxel has patched a zero-day vulnerability in its GS1900 series network
switches after attackers exploited it to compromise close to 1,000 devices
worldwide. The attackers used the flaw to steal device configuration files
and credentials from affected switches.

Admins running GS1900 switches should apply the patch immediately and
rotate any credentials that may have been exposed on compromised devices.
