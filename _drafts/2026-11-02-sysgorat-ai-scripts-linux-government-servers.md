Palo Alto Networks' Unit 42 disclosed a cyber espionage campaign, tracked
as CL-UNK-1211, targeting Linux infrastructure tied to government and
state-affiliated entities between October 2025 and April 2026.

The campaign deploys malware dubbed SysGoRAT alongside scripts researchers
suspect were AI-generated. The cluster's origin remains unattributed.
