Microsoft says organizations should treat AI agents the same way they treat
insiders, and is rolling out controls that grant agents access on a
per-task basis rather than standing permissions.

Access tightens automatically as an agent's risk score rises, aiming to
limit the blast radius if an agent is compromised or goes off-script.
