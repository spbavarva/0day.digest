Dodo Pizza, a chain with roughly 1,500 locations, confirmed a data breach
following hacker claims. Potentially compromised data includes customers'
names, addresses, email addresses, phone numbers, dates of birth, and order
details. The company has not disclosed the number of affected customers or
the initial attack vector. No ransomware or extortion demand was mentioned
in initial reporting.
