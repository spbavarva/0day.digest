Researchers are tracking an active, widespread email-driven phishing
campaign using adversary-in-the-middle techniques to hijack Microsoft 365
accounts. The campaign specifically targets personnel involved in financial
workflows to collect payroll and finance-related email. Attackers use
residential proxies to disguise malicious sign-ins as ordinary consumer
traffic, complicating detection for defenders relying on IP reputation.
