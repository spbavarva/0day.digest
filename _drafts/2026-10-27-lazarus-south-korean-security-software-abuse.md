Kaspersky attributes a cyber espionage campaign, dubbed Operation
FlashHole, to North Korea's Lazarus Group, targeting at least three South
Korean organizations in construction, software, and
wire-communication equipment manufacturing. The campaign abuses
legitimate South Korean security software to deploy malware and has been
active since at least July 2025.

Notably, the deployed malware uses post-quantum encryption, an unusual
choice for malware communications. The abuse of trusted local security
software as a delivery or execution vector is the key technique for
defenders in the affected sectors to watch for.
