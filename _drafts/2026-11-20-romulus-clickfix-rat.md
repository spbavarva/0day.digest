Threat actors are using the ROMULUS ClickFix malware delivery platform to
distribute a previously undocumented remote access trojan that shares
similarities with CNCMachineRMS, giving attackers the ability to spy on
victims and control infected PCs.

ROMULUS operates as a delivery service, distributing both CNCMachineRMS
and Legion Loader through separate chains while relying on ClickFix
social-engineering lures and signed binary sideloading.
