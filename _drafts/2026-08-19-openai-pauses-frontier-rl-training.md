OpenAI paused reinforcement learning (RL) training for its latest AI
models for two weeks to shore up defenses and expand monitoring.

The company said it wants to avert "another Hugging Face-like incident"
as model capability grows, adding that risks associated with developing
and testing frontier models internally grow alongside their capability.

OpenAI did not disclose specifics of the incident being referenced or the
technical defenses being added. The pause signals a lab voluntarily
slowing frontier training over internal safety concerns.
