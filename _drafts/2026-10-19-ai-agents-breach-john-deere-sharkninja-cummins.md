A hacker compromised more than 100 websites using a largely autonomous army
of AI agents, according to findings from AI security firm Norforge. Reuters
reports the victims included John Deere, SharkNinja, and Cummins, though
Norforge itself did not name targets.

The agents reportedly operated with minimal human direction, reviving
concerns that AI-powered offensive tooling is lowering the bar for
large-scale compromise. Specific exploitation techniques used have not been
disclosed.
