An attacker exploited a security flaw in Web3Auth, the provider that
powers Google and Apple sign-in for the Keplr crypto wallet, to steal
funds from users who set up their wallet via those sign-in options.

Both companies confirmed the incident late last week. Web3Auth said it
used the attacker's own access route to empty other at-risk wallets
before the attacker could reach them, limiting further losses.
