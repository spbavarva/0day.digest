Cyberattacks against water sector systems have reportedly affected at
least 12 U.S. states, according to SecurityWeek.

Georgia has been confirmed as one of the affected states after Clayton
County reported a disruption at a pump station caused by the attacks.

Details on the specific attack vector, threat actor, or scope of
impact in the other states have not yet been disclosed.
