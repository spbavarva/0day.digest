Water utilities in at least 12 U.S. states have now reported cyberattacks
against their operational technology, with South Dakota and Georgia the
latest to announce incidents.

The scope of the campaign, allegedly linked to Iranian hackers, continues
to grow. Water utilities should treat OT network segmentation and default
credential rotation as immediate priorities given the campaign's spread.
