CISA added two vulnerabilities to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities (KEV)
catalog based on evidence of active exploitation: CVE-2025-61624, a path
traversal flaw in multiple Fortinet products, and CVE-2026-102764, an
authentication bypass by spoofing vulnerability in BeyondTrust Remote
Support.

Under Binding Operational Directive (BOD) 26-04, federal civilian agencies
must prioritize patching KEV-listed vulnerabilities based on risk.
