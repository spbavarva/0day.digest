Anthropic researcher Jacob Coxon resigned over fears that AI development is
heading toward uncontrollable self-improving systems, calling for pacing
agreements between labs. Hours later, a senior Anthropic safety researcher
said there is more than a 10 percent chance AI "could kill all humans" by
the end of the decade, citing the risk of building superhuman systems labs
cannot reliably control.
