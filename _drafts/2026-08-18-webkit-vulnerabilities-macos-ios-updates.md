Apple shipped fresh macOS and iOS security updates patching dozens of
WebKit vulnerabilities. The bugs could be exploited to crash Safari,
corrupt memory, leak sensitive data, escape the browser sandbox, and
exfiltrate data.

Users should update to the latest macOS and iOS releases to receive the
fixes.
