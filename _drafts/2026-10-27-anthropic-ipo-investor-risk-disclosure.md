Anthropic's IPO disclosures to investors reportedly spell out risks
including potential harms from runaway AI systems. The company frames its
public offering as a step toward mitigating the risks it believes AI
poses, rather than purely a capital-raising event.

Details on specific risk language or financial terms were not included in
initial reporting. The disclosure is notable as one of the first
instances of an AI lab formally stating catastrophic AI risk as an
investor-facing risk factor ahead of a public listing.
