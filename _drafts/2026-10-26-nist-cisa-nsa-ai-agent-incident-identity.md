A new joint guide from NIST, CISA, and NSA frames runaway AI agent behavior
as an incident type organizations should rehearse for, not just a theoretical
risk.

The hardest part of responding, per the guide, is establishing whose
authority an agent acted under — an answer that has to exist before an
incident happens, not be reconstructed afterward.
