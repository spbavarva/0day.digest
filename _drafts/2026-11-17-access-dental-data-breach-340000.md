Access Dental Management disclosed that hackers breached its network and
stole personal, health, and insurance information belonging to more than
340,000 people. The exposed data includes health and insurance details,
which carries elevated fraud and identity-theft risk beyond typical PII
breaches.

No further technical details on the intrusion method were disclosed.
Affected individuals should watch for notification letters and consider
credit or identity monitoring given the health-data exposure.
