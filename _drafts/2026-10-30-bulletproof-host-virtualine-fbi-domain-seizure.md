The FBI has seized the domain belonging to Virtualine, a bulletproof
hosting provider, according to SecurityWeek.

Virtualine allowed customers to host hack-for-hire services and supported
ransomware and infostealer operators, and it accepted cryptocurrency
payments to help obscure transactions.

The seizure removes infrastructure used by multiple cybercrime operations,
though affected actors will likely migrate to other bulletproof hosts.

No details on arrests or indictments tied to the seizure were included in
current reporting.
