TSMC confirmed that personal employee data was exposed after a threat actor
put 6.9GB of information allegedly stolen from the company up for auction
online. TSMC says the data originated from a third-party cloud provider
rather than its own internal systems.

The identity of the cloud provider and the number of employees affected were
not disclosed in the available reporting.
