Security researchers found that several White House staffers are using
computers or mobile devices equipped with residential proxy services,
which make their internet address available for others to rent.

One of the proxy services involved is run by a Russian company whose
founder's web hosting business was tied to 2016 cyberattacks against
election systems in Arizona and Illinois. The presence of this software on
government devices raises questions about how it got there and who else
may be routing traffic through them.
