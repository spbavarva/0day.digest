Attackers used compromised IAM credentials belonging to data platform
Qubole to access customer environments, stealing files, secrets, API
keys, environment variables, and other credentials. SecurityWeek reports
more than a dozen organizations were affected.

The incident is a reminder that third-party data platform credentials
carry downstream blast radius — any secrets stored or referenced through
Qubole integrations should be rotated.
