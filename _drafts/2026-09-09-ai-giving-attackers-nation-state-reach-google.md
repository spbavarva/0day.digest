Criminal and state-sponsored adversaries are increasingly using AI to
automate and scale their attacks, according to Google's Threat Intelligence
Group (GTIG). The report warns that AI tooling is lowering the resource bar
needed to conduct operations that previously required nation-state-level
capability.
