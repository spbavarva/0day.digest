CISA added a new Arista VeloCloud Orchestrator SQL injection flaw,
CVE-2026-114625, to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog, citing
evidence of active exploitation.

The critical-severity flaw lets attackers reach privileged internal
functionality and gain operator privileges on VCO instances — the third
VCO zero-day exploited in four months. Federal agencies are bound by
BOD 26-04 to remediate; all VCO operators should prioritize this patch
and check for signs of compromise.
