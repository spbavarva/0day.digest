Unit 42 published an analysis of vishing (voice phishing) attacks carried
out by affiliates of The Com, a loosely organized cybercriminal community,
targeting healthcare and finance organizations.

The campaigns combine social-engineering phone calls with cloud data theft
and extortion, rather than traditional ransomware encryption.

Unit 42 includes defense guidance for organizations in the targeted
sectors; the research is a reminder that voice-based social engineering
remains an effective initial-access vector against well-defended cloud
environments.
