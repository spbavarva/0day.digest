Two Democratic senators introduced legislation that would allocate $300
million annually to fund cybersecurity improvements for the water and
wastewater sector, according to The Record. The bill follows a string of
reported attacks against U.S. water utilities. No further detail on bill
sponsors, committee status, or passage timeline was included in the source
report.
