A new category of AI model, described as a "decision model," went from
nonexistent to widespread within about a month. TypeSafe AI released its
decision model Jev on September 15, calling it a "System One" model after
Kahneman's fast, intuitive thinking.

OpenAI opened a public beta for its own Decisions API on October 6, after
announcing it at DevDay. Cribl published cribl-decision, an open LoRA
adapter that can be run locally, and other vendors are building similar
offerings for AI SOC platforms.
