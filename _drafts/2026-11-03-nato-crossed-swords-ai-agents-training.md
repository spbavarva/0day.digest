NATO's CCDCOE Crossed Swords exercise in Estonia introduced AI agents
and OpenAI Daybreak into joint cyber and kinetic operations training
for the first time.

The exercise is intended to prepare cyber and military personnel for
operating alongside AI agents in live-fire training scenarios. No
further technical detail on the agents' role was included in the
available summary.
