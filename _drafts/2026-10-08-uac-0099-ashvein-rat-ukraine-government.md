Researchers have attributed a previously undocumented .NET infostealer
and RAT codenamed ASHVEIN to the Russia-aligned threat actor UAC-0099.

TrendAI says the malware has been used in attacks targeting Ukrainian
government personnel, and tracks the activity cluster under the name
Earth Sirrush (formerly SHADOW-EARTH-065).
