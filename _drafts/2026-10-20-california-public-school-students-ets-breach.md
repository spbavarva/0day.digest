California's Department of Education has warned the state's public school
students that assessment contractor Educational Testing Service (ETS)
suffered a months-long data breach. The CDE issued multiple statements over
the past week alerting affected students and families.

Exact details on the data types exposed were not available at time of
writing. Given the statewide scope, the breach potentially touches a large
share of California's student population.
