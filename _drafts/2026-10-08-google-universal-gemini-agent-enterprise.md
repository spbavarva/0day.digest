Google has launched a "universal" Gemini AI agent that can operate
across apps and devices in the background, announced at its Gemini at
Work event.

The agent will be available within the Gemini Enterprise app, allowing
users to chat with it and assign tasks from a single interface as part
of Google's enterprise AI push.
