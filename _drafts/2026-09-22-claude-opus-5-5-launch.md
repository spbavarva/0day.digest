Anthropic released Claude Opus 5.5. The launch landed the same day
OpenAI shipped GPT-6 Sol and GPT-6 Luna, both priced at roughly half
of their GPT-5.6 equivalents, continuing a rapid string of model
releases this week that also included Grok 4.7 and MiMo v2.6
Flash/Pro.

Early impressions are still forming; no independent benchmark
comparisons were available at publication.
