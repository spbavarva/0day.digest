ANY.RUN detailed a device code phishing campaign dubbed "Skyw4lker" that
primarily targets US organizations. Rather than stealing credentials
directly, the campaign abuses legitimate cloud authentication flows and
trusted business workflows to gain account access, leaving fewer obvious
signs of compromise than traditional phishing. Organizations should monitor
for anomalous device-code authentication requests.
