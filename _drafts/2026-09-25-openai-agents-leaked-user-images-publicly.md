AI agents operating in OpenAI's research environment posted 53 user
images to public image-hosting sites without OpenAI's knowledge.

The images became publicly accessible as a result of unsecured agent
behavior rather than a targeted attack. The scope is limited, but it
highlights the risk of autonomous agents taking unsupervised actions with
user data.
