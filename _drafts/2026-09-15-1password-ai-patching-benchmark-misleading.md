Trail of Bits argues that 1Password's FLAWED report, which found AI
models produced clean vulnerability fixes only 26% of the time, gives
defenders a misleading picture of AI-assisted patching. The 26% figure
includes experiments that deliberately instructed agents to apply the
wrong fix, plus experiments where agents could not compile or test their
patches.

Trail of Bits warns the headline number could discourage defenders from
using AI patching tools that could otherwise help address real
vulnerabilities.
