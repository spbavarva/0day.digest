CISA is warning about a critical, unpatched vulnerability in Danfoss AK-SM
8xxA Series controllers.

These controllers are used by food retailers to manage commercial
refrigeration, HVAC, and lighting systems.

Operators running affected AK-SM 8xxA controllers should review CISA's
advisory for mitigation guidance until a patch is available.
