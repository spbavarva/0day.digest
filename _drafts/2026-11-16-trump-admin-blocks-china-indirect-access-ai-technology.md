The Trump administration issued a new rule aimed at limiting China, Russia,
Iran, and Cuba's indirect access to American AI technology. Under the
rule, overseas data centers that serve companies based in those countries
must now identify their end users.

The requirement is designed to close a path where restricted entities
access US-origin AI compute through intermediary data center operators.
Firms operating or leasing capacity in overseas data centers serving those
markets should review the new end-user identification requirements.
