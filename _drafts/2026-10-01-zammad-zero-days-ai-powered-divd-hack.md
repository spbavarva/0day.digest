Zero-day vulnerabilities in Zammad were chained and exploited in an
AI-powered attack against DIVD.

The flaws were used together to hijack sessions, achieve remote code
execution, and elevate privileges to root.

The incident is notable both for the zero-day chain itself and for the use
of AI tooling in carrying out the attack. Zammad users should watch for
vendor patches and indicators of compromise.
