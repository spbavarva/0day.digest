United Airlines will no longer allow customers to use third-party AI
agents to book flights or manage reservations. The airline said sharing
account credentials with personal AI assistants can expose sensitive
information, and actions taken by these tools may result in unintended
purchases.

The policy reflects a growing concern among enterprises about credential
sharing with autonomous AI agents acting on a user's behalf.
