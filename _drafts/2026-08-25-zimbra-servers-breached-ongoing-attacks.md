Threat actors have compromised more than 270 Zimbra Collaboration Suite
(ZCS) instances in ongoing remote code execution attacks against a
high-severity vulnerability in the platform. Zimbra is widely used as a
self-hosted email and collaboration platform, making compromised instances
valuable both for direct mail data access and as pivot points into victim
networks. The campaign remains active. Organizations running Zimbra should
confirm they are on a patched version and check internet-facing instances
for signs of compromise.
