An active campaign tracked as UNK_CondorFiltration used the TeamFiltration
tool to target over 5,700 Microsoft 365 accounts across 28 tenants,
according to Proofpoint. The activity primarily focused on Chilean retail
and financial institutions and originated from 1,487 unique AWS EC2 IPs.

Seven accounts were confirmed compromised, reportedly via default
passwords.
