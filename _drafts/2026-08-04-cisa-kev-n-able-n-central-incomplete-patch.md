CISA added CVE-2026-18577 (CVSS score: 8.2) to its Known Exploited
Vulnerabilities catalog following reports of active exploitation
against N-able N-central customers.

The flaw is a case of incomplete patching for the previously disclosed
CVE-2026-18556 (also CVSS 8.2), meaning the original fix did not fully
close the authentication bypass. N-able N-central operators should
apply the newest patch rather than assume the prior fix was sufficient.
