Cisco patched dozens of vulnerabilities across Firepower Management
Center (FMC), Identity Services Engine (ISE), and Nexus Dashboard. The
flaws could lead to root access, command execution, authentication
bypasses, SQL injection, and remote code execution depending on the
product and configuration.

The feed summary did not break down exploitation status per CVE.
Administrators running these platforms should review Cisco's advisories
and prioritize patching given the number and severity of issues bundled
in this release.
