OpenAI is now urging California lawmakers to strengthen SB 53, the state's
AI safety bill, after previously opposing the legislation.

The reversal comes amid continued debate over how much transparency and
safety testing frontier AI labs should be required to disclose. No specific
new provisions or technical requirements were detailed in the coverage.
