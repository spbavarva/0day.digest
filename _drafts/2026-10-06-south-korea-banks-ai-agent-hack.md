South Korean officials believe AI agents were used to breach at least seven
financial institutions, exposing the personal data of at least 68,000
people. Officials say a Chinese cybersecurity tool was used to carry out the
intrusions. The incident is among the first publicly attributed bank
breaches where investigators specifically point to AI agents as the attack
mechanism, rather than AI-assisted reconnaissance or phishing content
generation. Further technical detail on how the agents were deployed was not
disclosed.
