GitHub and PyPI have introduced a time-based mechanism in Dependabot to
protect against supply chain attacks and limit their blast radius. The
feature targets dependency-confusion and typosquatting-style attacks by
adding time constraints around package/dependency changes. No specific
CVE or active exploitation was cited; this is a proactive defensive
capability rollout for maintainers relying on Dependabot.
