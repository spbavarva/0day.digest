Researchers at the University of Birmingham and security firm Fuzzware
found that a malicious SIM card can order the cellular modem it's inserted
into to run attacker-chosen commands. On the cellular modules built into
EV chargers, industrial routers, and car telematics units, that's enough
to take over the whole device. The team tested 26 phones and cellular
modules for the capability.
