Hackers reportedly breached the US Air Force's Cloud One computing
platform, according to BleepingComputer. The incident adds to a recent
string of breaches affecting US federal agencies, including the FBI,
CIA, and Treasury Department.

Details on the attackers, access gained, or data exposed have not been
disclosed. The Air Force has not yet issued a public statement
confirming the scope of the intrusion.
