Hackers have reportedly breached the US Air Force's Cloud One computing
platform. The incident adds to a recent wave of cyberattacks affecting US
government agencies, including the FBI, CIA, and Treasury Department.

Details on the scope of access gained and data potentially exposed have
not yet been confirmed. The incident underscores ongoing targeting of
federal cloud infrastructure amid a broader pattern of government breaches
this year.
