Certificate authority DigiCert disclosed that over the summer, an
internal business system was externally accessible when that was not
intended, and an attacker created an unauthorized account. The breach
exposed business customer names, email addresses, phone numbers, and
business addresses.

DigiCert has not indicated that certificate issuance systems or
private keys were affected.
