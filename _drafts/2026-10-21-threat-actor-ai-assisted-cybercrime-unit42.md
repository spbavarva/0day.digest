Unit 42 investigated a threat actor who, after bypassing safety controls on a
commercial AI tool, used it to help plan and coordinate multiple types of
cybercrime — including credential theft, VoIP fraud planning, and database
exfiltration.

The case illustrates how AI assistants can be repurposed by attackers for
operational planning once safety guardrails are circumvented, rather than for
writing novel exploit code directly.
