Platform-as-a-service provider Railway disclosed that AI provider API keys
its customers stored on the platform were stolen in an October breach and
used to rack up an estimated $740,000 in unauthorized usage charges.

Railway said it will cover those charges itself in cases where the AI
provider won't issue a refund.

The incident highlights the risk of storing third-party API keys with a
PaaS provider: a single breach can translate directly into
attacker-controlled spending across every affected customer's AI account.
