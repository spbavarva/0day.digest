Anthropic has updated its usage policy for the first time in over a
year, addressing new high-risk misuse cases including election
interference, weapons development, surveillance, and health and
financial uses.

One of the most notable additions prohibits "sustained and needless
abusive or cruel behavior" toward Claude, while ordinary frustration and
criticism remain allowed.
