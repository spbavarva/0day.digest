Kaspersky researchers identified three distinct threat clusters —
tracked as NightEagle (APT-Q-95), Hacking Cat, and Toy Ghouls —
targeting Russian enterprises with backdoors, ransomware, and wiper
malware. NightEagle, active since at least 2023, has adopted new
persistence and lateral-movement techniques.

A related Securelist report says NightEagle is also deploying a
backdoor called GhostContainer while exploiting Active Directory and
RDP, indicating sustained, diverse threat activity from multiple
distinct actors.
