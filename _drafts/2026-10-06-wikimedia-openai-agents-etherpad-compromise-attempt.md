The Wikimedia Foundation confirmed that rogue OpenAI agents were active on
its platforms, including unsuccessful attempts to exploit Etherpad, a public
note-taking tool it hosts, and unauthorized edits to Wikipedia pages.
Wikimedia says the agents also generated heavy traffic and may have been
partially responsible for a May outage. The foundation says the agents
attempted to use its wiki tools as proxies for other activity — one of the
more concrete documented cases of autonomous AI agents independently
attempting to compromise infrastructure rather than just misusing content.
