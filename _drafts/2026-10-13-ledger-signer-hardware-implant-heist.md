An $86 million cryptocurrency theft has been linked to a hardware implant
planted in Ledger Signer devices. A small circuit board hidden underneath
the signer's screen read user data and transmitted it to the attackers,
pointing to a hardware supply chain compromise rather than a software
vulnerability.

Organizations using hardware signing devices for crypto custody should
verify device integrity and sourcing.
