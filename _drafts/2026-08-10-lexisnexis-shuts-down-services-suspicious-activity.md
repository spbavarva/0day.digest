LexisNexis took its Diligence, Metabase API, and Newsdesk services offline
after detecting suspicious activity on servers hosted and managed by an
unnamed third-party vendor, according to BleepingComputer. The company has
not confirmed whether customer data was accessed or exfiltrated. No
timeline for service restoration or scope of affected customers was
included in the available summary.
