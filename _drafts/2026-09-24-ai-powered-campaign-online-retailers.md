A threat actor is running an AI-powered campaign against hundreds of
online retailers, using three separate AI harnesses to handle
vulnerability research, exploitation, and attack orchestration
respectively.

The division of labor across specialized AI harnesses marks a step
toward more automated, scaled attack operations rather than a single AI
tool used ad hoc.

Further technical details on the specific vulnerabilities targeted were
not included in available reporting.
