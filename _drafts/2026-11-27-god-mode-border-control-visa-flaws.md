Researchers identified a set of vulnerabilities, dubbed "God Mode," in
online visa and immigration systems used by Nigeria, Ethiopia, Zambia,
Sri Lanka, Fiji, and Ghana. If exploited, the flaws could have let
attackers disrupt traveler entry processing in those countries.

The report does not indicate the vulnerabilities were exploited in the
wild. No CVE identifiers or technical exploitation details were included
in the initial coverage.
