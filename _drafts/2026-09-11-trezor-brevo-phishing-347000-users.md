Hackers compromised the Brevo marketing platform and used that access to
send phishing emails to 347,000 users of hardware wallet and crypto-tracking
services Trezor, BitBox, and CoinTracking. Trezor said about 2,500 users
clicked the embedded malicious link in the campaign. Because the affected
users are cryptocurrency hardware wallet customers, successful phishing
carries direct fund-theft risk. Anyone who received the email should treat
related login or recovery-phrase prompts as malicious.
