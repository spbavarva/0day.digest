CISA published an advisory for Subnet Solutions PowerSYSTEM Center,
used in electric utility operations, covering vulnerabilities
(CVE-2026-63017, CVE-2026-63774) rated up to CVSS 9 that could let an
attacker execute unauthorized SQL commands and access unauthorized
resources.

Affected versions span PowerSYSTEM Center 2020 through 2026 releases.
No confirmed in-the-wild exploitation was reported in the available
summary; utility operators running the product should apply available
patches.
