A suspected China-nexus threat actor combined the PHP2Shell vulnerabilities
with a kernel privilege escalation exploit to root 25 servers, breaching a
US government-affiliated network. The activity was observed on October 25,
2026, and researchers describe it as AI-assisted. PHP2Shell affects roughly
70% of websites running the PHP language and was disclosed and patched the
week prior; organizations still running unpatched instances remain exposed.
