Kaspersky identified a cyber-espionage campaign, codenamed Operation
ShadowCloud, that has been targeting government networks in Asia and
Africa since early 2026. The operation uses two previously undocumented
backdoors, GitDoor and GraphDoor, which abuse legitimate services — GitHub
and Microsoft SharePoint — as command-and-control channels.

Using trusted platforms for C2 traffic makes the activity harder to
distinguish from normal developer and collaboration traffic. Defenders
monitoring government or enterprise networks should watch for anomalous
automated activity against GitHub repos and SharePoint sites tied to
unexpected service accounts.
