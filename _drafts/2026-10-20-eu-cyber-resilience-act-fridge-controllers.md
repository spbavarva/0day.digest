The EU's Cyber Resilience Act, which took effect last month, only requires
vendors to patch vulnerabilities and issue free updates during a product's
support period — a window manufacturers define themselves.

The law's limits came into focus after an attack affecting fridge
controllers in France, where the vendor's support window had already
lapsed. Practitioners relying on CRA coverage for long-lived IoT/embedded
devices should account for this gap.
