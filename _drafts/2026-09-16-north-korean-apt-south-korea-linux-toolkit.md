A likely North Korean APT group used a previously undocumented Linux
espionage toolkit to compromise load balancers at South Korean media
and automotive organizations, according to Dark Reading. The access was
used to gain visibility into communications and expand further into
victim networks.

No specific malware family name or IOCs were included in the source
summary.
