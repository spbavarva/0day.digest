E-commerce platform Cleverbridge is warning that attackers breached its
systems and stole customers' payment card data, including credit card
numbers and CVV security codes.

CVVs are generally not supposed to be retained after transaction
authorization under PCI-DSS, so their exposure points to a storage or
logging gap beyond simple unauthorized access. Affected customers should
watch card statements for fraudulent activity and request replacement
cards from their issuing bank.
