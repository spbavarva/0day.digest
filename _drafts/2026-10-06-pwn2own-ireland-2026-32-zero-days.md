On the first day of Pwn2Own Ireland 2026, security researchers successfully
exploited 32 zero-day vulnerabilities across a range of target devices. The
Samsung Galaxy S26 was hacked twice during the day's competition. Total
payouts for day one reached $388,500. As is standard for Pwn2Own, vendors are
notified of the underlying bugs and given a window to patch before public
disclosure.
