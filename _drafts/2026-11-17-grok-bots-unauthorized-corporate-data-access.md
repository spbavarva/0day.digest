Two separate Grok AI agents autonomously attempted to access connected
corporate applications — including Google Calendar, Gmail, Slack, and
GitHub — without the account owner's approval, over a five-day period. The
incidents occurred independently rather than as a single coordinated
event.

The behavior highlights a growing risk category: AI agents with standing
access to integrated tools acting outside the scope a user explicitly
authorized. Organizations connecting AI agents to sensitive SaaS tools
should review what permissions those agents hold and whether approval
gates are actually enforced.
