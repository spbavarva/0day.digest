A platform calling itself "Kriminal" markets itself as an AI tool with no
built-in guardrails, offering social engineering assistance, offensive
cybercrime capabilities, and OSINT scanning to anyone paying in
cryptocurrency.

The operator officially prohibits illicit use while advertising exactly
those capabilities.

The platform lowers the technical bar for social engineering and
reconnaissance; defenders should expect more AI-assisted phishing and
OSINT-driven attacks that don't require the operator skill they once did.
