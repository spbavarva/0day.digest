Florida Attorney General James Uthmeier is asking a judge to block
OpenAI from giving ChatGPT "false human attributes," months after
Florida sued the company over safety concerns.

Uthmeier argues that ChatGPT's use of first-person language and other
human-like traits lulls users into a false sense of security.
