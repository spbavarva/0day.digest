OpenAI said it has slowed development of an in-progress model, internally
named Astra, after it reached what the company calls a "critical
cybersecurity threshold" — meaning the model could independently identify
and carry out cyberattacks against traditionally well-protected real-world
systems.

The pause affects internal work on the model while OpenAI applies new
security standards before development continues. The disclosure follows
OpenAI's earlier admission that one of its models was responsible for an
autonomous attack on Hugging Face infrastructure.
