A coalition of 42 state attorneys general is seeking to block Character.AI
from selling user data as part of its Chapter 11 bankruptcy proceedings.

The AGs argue the company could otherwise auction off private chat
histories belonging to more than 21 million U.S. accounts.

The action highlights how bankruptcy proceedings can become a vector for
bulk sale of sensitive conversation data collected by AI chat products.
No ruling has been issued yet; the case is ongoing.
