Researchers demonstrated how attackers could abuse built-in AI email
assistants to evade detection, impersonate trusted employees, compromise
executive accounts, and facilitate financial fraud.

The technique targets chatbot features increasingly embedded in
enterprise email platforms rather than a single vendor flaw. Security
teams should treat AI email assistant actions as an additional trust
boundary and watch for assistant-initiated behavior tied to account
takeover or fraud patterns.
