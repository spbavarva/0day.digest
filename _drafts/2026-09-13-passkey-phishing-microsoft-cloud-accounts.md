Microsoft disclosed two active campaigns abusing third-party email delivery
infrastructure. One sent over a million scam emails between August 3-5,
2026, impersonating CEOs to run financial fraud. The other uses
passkey-themed social engineering to trick users into handing over access to
Microsoft cloud accounts.

The passkey campaign lets attackers breach cloud environments and exfiltrate
data without needing a traditional password. Admins should review
conditional access policies and educate users on passkey-enrollment
phishing lures.
