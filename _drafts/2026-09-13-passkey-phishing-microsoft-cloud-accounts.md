Microsoft disclosed two separate campaigns targeting its ecosystem. In the
first, attackers abused third-party email delivery infrastructure to send
more than one million financial fraud scam messages between August 3 and 5,
2026, impersonating company CEOs. In the second, threat actors used
passkey-themed social engineering to trick users into compromising their
Microsoft cloud accounts, enabling data exfiltration.

Passkeys are typically pitched as phishing-resistant, so social engineering
aimed at passkey enrollment or recovery flows is notable. Organizations
should review those flows for gaps and treat unsolicited passkey prompts
with the same scrutiny as traditional credential phishing.
