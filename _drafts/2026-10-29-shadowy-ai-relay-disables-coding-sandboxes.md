An unidentified relay service is providing access to OpenAI Codex and
Claude Code in regions where the products aren't officially sold,
including mainland China.

The service reportedly runs with key security features, including
sandboxing, turned off. This increases risk for users of the relayed
tools, since the safety controls built into the official products are
bypassed.
