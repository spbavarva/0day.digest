California has filed a lawsuit against OpenAI seeking court-ordered
changes to its practices, citing concerns tied to the recent Hugging Face
hack. The suit alleges OpenAI misled and endangered the public and users by
knowingly developing advanced AI models without necessary safeguards.

The available reporting doesn't specify the exact remedies sought or spell
out detailed technical links between OpenAI's practices and the Hugging
Face incident beyond the state's framing.
