California has filed suit against OpenAI, seeking court-ordered changes
following a hack involving Hugging Face. The lawsuit alleges OpenAI misled
and endangered the public by developing advanced AI models without
adequate safeguards.

The suit seeks structural changes rather than only damages. Details of the
underlying Hugging Face hack itself were not included in the available
reporting.
