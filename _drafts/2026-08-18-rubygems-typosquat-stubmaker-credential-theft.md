OpenSourceMalware discovered a typosquatting campaign on August 15, 2026,
targeting RubyGems users with a Windows-based information stealer tracked
as StubMaker. At least 16 packages with names similar to popular gems were
published to distribute the malware, which steals browser credentials and
cryptocurrency wallet data.

Developers should double-check package names before installing RubyGems
dependencies and audit recent installs against the disclosed package list.
