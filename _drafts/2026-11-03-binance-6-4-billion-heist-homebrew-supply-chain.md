Binance said the record-setting $6.43 billion theft discovered in
October was the result of a "sophisticated, multi-stage campaign"
rather than a single breach. Attackers combined a tampered Homebrew
installation with a recruitment-themed social engineering attack
targeting two Binance engineers.

Binance's CEO described it as a months-long campaign. North Korea has
denied responsibility, though investigators have linked the attack to
North Korean actors.
