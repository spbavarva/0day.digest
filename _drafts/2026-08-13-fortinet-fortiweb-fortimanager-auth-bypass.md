Fortinet patched authentication vulnerabilities in FortiWeb and
FortiManager that could allow attackers to log in with arbitrary usernames
and passwords, or impersonate any FortiGate appliance.

Given Fortinet products' history of being targeted shortly after
disclosure, administrators should prioritize patching FortiWeb and
FortiManager deployments.
