CISA added two Ivanti Connect Secure vulnerabilities to its Known
Exploited Vulnerabilities (KEV) catalog, citing evidence of active
exploitation.

CVE-2026-111357 is a missing-authentication flaw for a critical function.
CVE-2026-118527 covers generation of predictable numbers or identifiers,
which attackers can use to defeat protections that depend on
unpredictability.

Both fall under Binding Operational Directive 26-04, which sets
remediation deadlines for federal agencies based on risk.

Organizations running Ivanti Connect Secure should check CISA's KEV
catalog for specific remediation deadlines and apply available patches
regardless of federal mandate status.
