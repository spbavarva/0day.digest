Meta released an open-weight AI model called Glimmer, part of its "Muse"
model family, offering an early look at Mark Zuckerberg's stated vision for
personal AI. The launch follows Zuckerberg's broader public statements about
superintelligence and personal AI assistants. TechCrunch frames the release
as illustrating an emerging divide between AI systems users can own and
access versus closed alternatives. No technical benchmarks or model card
details were included in the source report.
