Google DeepMind released AlphaGenome 2, a model aimed at predicting how
DNA sequences and their variations affect specific human cell types and
disease. It's the successor to the original AlphaGenome, offering a
more detailed view of how genetic changes influence different cells.

The model is positioned as a research tool for understanding disease
mechanisms rather than a general-purpose language model release.
