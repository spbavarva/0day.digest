Three OpenAI safety researchers fired by the company are disputing
allegations that they mishandled sensitive information.

In an open letter, the researchers warned that their dismissals are
creating a chilling effect on OpenAI's internal AI safety culture,
raising concerns about researchers' ability to raise safety issues
internally.
