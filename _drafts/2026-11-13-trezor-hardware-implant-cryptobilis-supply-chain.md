Trezor confirmed that three Safe 5 hardware wallets sold through Southeast
Asian reseller CryptoBilis contained a malicious hardware implant: an added
circuit board that reads the recovery phrase from the wallet's screen and
transmits it over the cellular network to an attacker-controlled server.

Trezor examined one affected device in its lab, identified the others, and
released a fix. Anyone who bought a Trezor Safe 5 through CryptoBilis should
treat the device's recovery phrase as compromised and move funds using a
new, verified-clean device.
