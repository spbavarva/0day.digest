The FBI and Department of Justice announced the disruption of malicious
infrastructure used by Flax Typhoon, a China-linked advanced persistent
threat group, seizing seven domains and blocking access to platforms used
to scan and, in some cases, infiltrate U.S. critical infrastructure.

The action targeted tooling the group used for reconnaissance and
intrusion against critical infrastructure targets, though the full list of
seized domains and affected platforms wasn't detailed in available
reporting.

This disruption lands the same week CISA added five Flax Typhoon-exploited
flaws to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog, suggesting a
coordinated government response to the group's activity.
