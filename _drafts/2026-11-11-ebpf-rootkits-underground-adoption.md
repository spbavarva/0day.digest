Linux eBPF rootkits are gaining traction among underground threat
actors because they can hide malicious activity and evade traditional
detection without loading kernel modules. Threat actors are sharing
rootkit source code, tutorials, and exploits targeting Linux systems on
underground forums.

Linux defenders should ensure detection coverage extends to eBPF-based
evasion techniques, not just traditional kernel-module rootkits.
