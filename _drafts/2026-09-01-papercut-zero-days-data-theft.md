Two vulnerabilities in PaperCut NG and MF print management software,
tracked as CVE-2026-82078 and CVE-2026-81578, were patched last week
after being exploited as zero-days. They are now being abused in
follow-on data theft attacks. CISA has added both to its Known
Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog.

Because exploitation began before the patch was available,
organizations running PaperCut should apply the update immediately
and review logs for signs of compromise predating the patch.
