OpenAI launched GPT-6.1 Sol, positioned as delivering near-GPT-6 Astra
intelligence for coding, computer use, and professional work at roughly
one-fifth of Astra's standard API token pricing. The company says it targets
complex professional tasks including code writing/debugging, document
understanding, and multi-step business workflows. Sol is available now via
OpenAI's API and ChatGPT.
