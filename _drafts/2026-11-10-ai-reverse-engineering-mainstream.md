Researchers used AI tooling to unpack, map, and recreate complex
software, including the Turla malware family, according to Dark Reading.
The work illustrates how AI is lowering the skill and time barrier for
reverse engineering both legitimate software and malware — helping
defenders analyze threats faster, but also lowering the bar for
attackers studying or cloning existing tools.
