Hackers breached Access Dental Management's network and stole personal,
health, and insurance information belonging to more than 340,000 people.
No further technical detail on the attack vector was available in the
disclosure.

Affected individuals should watch for identity-theft and insurance-fraud
attempts using the exposed health and insurance data.
