Microsoft disclosed that attackers exploited a vulnerability in its Entra
Connect Health service to gain unauthorized access to administrator email
addresses and tenant configuration information.

Entra Connect Health is used to monitor the sync status of hybrid
environments connecting on-premises Active Directory to Entra ID (formerly
Azure AD).

Microsoft has not detailed the scope of affected tenants or whether the
flaw has been fully remediated.
