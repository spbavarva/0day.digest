Apple announced plans to tighten controls around macOS Full Disk Access
(FDA) due to security risks posed by AI agents. The company said some
developers use FDA in ways that could expose everything on a user's
system — including files, mail, messages, and browsing history — without
the user's full knowledge.

Details of the specific new controls were not included in the available
summary.
