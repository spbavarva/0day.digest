Apple announced plans to tighten controls around macOS Full Disk
Access (FDA) in response to security risks posed by AI agents. Apple
noted that some developers use FDA in ways that expose a user's files,
mail, messages, and browsing history without the user's full knowledge.
No specific timeline or implementation detail was given. This is a
notable example of a platform vendor restricting broad permissions
specifically because of how AI agents use them.
