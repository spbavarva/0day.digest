New analysis from AttackIQ covering 20 Iranian state-sponsored threat groups
describes how espionage, ransomware, and hacktivist operations intersect,
including groups handing off initial network access to separate units that
carry out destructive attacks.

The research suggests initial-access and destructive-attack capabilities are
often split across different parts of the same state apparatus, rather than
run end-to-end by a single group.
