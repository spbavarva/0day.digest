Unit 42 detailed ongoing activity from the Atomic macOS Stealer (AMOS),
which uses deceptive fake setup guides to trick users into installing
malware that steals credentials and other sensitive data from macOS
systems.

The research provides identification and blocking guidance for
defenders to detect this stealer family on managed Mac fleets.
