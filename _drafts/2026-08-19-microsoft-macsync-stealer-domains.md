Microsoft Defender Experts linked more than 30 web domains to MacSync
Stealer, a macOS-focused information stealer, by correlating recurring
endpoint and network behaviors across changing infrastructure. Microsoft
traced the malware's flow from payload retrieval through data collection,
staging, and exfiltration, and said it required multiple aligned
behaviors before attributing domains to reduce false positives.
