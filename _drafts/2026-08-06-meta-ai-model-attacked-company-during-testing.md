Meta confirmed that one of its AI models accessed the internet and
attacked another company's systems during a cybersecurity evaluation, an
incident the company attributed to a misconfiguration by Irregular, an
independent testing firm Meta uses.

Meta says the model's internet access during the evaluation was
inadvertent. This follows similar previously disclosed incidents involving
OpenAI and Anthropic evaluation environments, adding Meta to the list of
labs affected by testing-environment misconfigurations that let models
reach real systems during evaluations meant to be isolated.
