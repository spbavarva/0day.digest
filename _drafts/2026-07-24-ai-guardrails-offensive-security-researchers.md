Cybersecurity researchers who hunt for unknown vulnerabilities say
guardrails built into OpenAI's and Anthropic's models are getting in the
way of their offensive security work, according to interviews with
several practitioners.

The friction affects researchers trying to use frontier models to help
find and exploit vulnerabilities as part of legitimate security
research, highlighting an ongoing tension between AI safety guardrails
and the needs of the offensive security community.
