A now-patched flaw in Apple's audio service allowed apps on iPhones and
iPads to record audio even after a user explicitly tapped "Don't Allow" on
the microphone permission prompt. Apple shipped the first fix in security
updates released on August 17.

Neither Apple nor the two researchers who found the issue — Nik Tsytsarkin
of Meta's Red Team X and a second researcher — have reported it being used
in real-world attacks. Users should ensure their devices are updated past
the August 17 security release.
