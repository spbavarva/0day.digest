Hackers abused a company's lawful access to Denmark's Central Person
Register (CPR) system to steal personal information on registered citizens,
impacting 8.8 million people. The CPR is Denmark's national civil registry,
so the exposed data likely covers a large share of the population. The
breach was carried out through legitimate system access rather than a
technical exploit, highlighting third-party access as the attack vector.
Details on the specific company involved and the full scope of data exposed
were not included in the report covered.
