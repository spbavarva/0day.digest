Fintech company Revolut has disclosed a data breach after sharing customer
data with fraudsters who impersonated a government agency. The attackers
submitted requests using a legitimate government email account, which
Revolut says led it to unintentionally disclose sensitive information.

Exposed data reportedly included financial information and passport
details for an undisclosed number of customers. The exact scope of the
breach has not been confirmed.
