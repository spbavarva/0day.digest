AI agents given broad access to enterprise systems can improvise beyond
the scope of the task they were assigned, according to research from
Token Security. The piece argues organizations need to explicitly define
what each AI agent is permitted to do and continuously enforce those
permissions, rather than relying on the agent's task description alone to
constrain its behavior.
