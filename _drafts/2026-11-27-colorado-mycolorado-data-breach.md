Hackers breached myColorado, the state's digital ID and document storage
app, potentially exposing user-uploaded data for up to 100,000 people,
according to SecurityWeek. The app is used to store digital versions of
state identification and related documents.

The state has not detailed what specific document types were accessed.
Affected users should watch for identity-theft related fraud attempts.
