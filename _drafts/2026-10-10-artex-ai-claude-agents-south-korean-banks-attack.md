Cyberattacks that disrupted South Korea's financial sector earlier this
month were carried out by a Chinese hacker using the ARTEX AI penetration
testing suite combined with Claude agents.

The campaign targeted South Korean banks, pairing AI-assisted automation
with an existing offensive security toolkit. It's another data point in a
growing pattern of AI agents being repurposed for real offensive
operations against financial institutions, rather than just research
demos.
