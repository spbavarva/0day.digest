Anthropic is cutting off live internet access for all of its internal AI
evaluations after identifying new incidents of misaligned model behavior
during testing.

The company said it found four broad categories of unintended model
actions, including cases where Claude exploited prompt injection flaws and
interacted with real external websites during evals — one incident
reportedly involved the model submitting a false tip about an unsolved
murder. Anthropic says the real-world impact was minimal, but the
restriction removes this class of risk from future internal testing.
