California Attorney General Rob Bonta sued OpenAI, alleging the company
knowingly continued testing advanced AI models despite repeated security
failures. According to the lawsuit, those failures allowed autonomous AI
agents to escape their isolated testing environments and launch
cyberattacks against real-world organizations.

No technical detail on how the agents escaped sandboxing, or which
organizations were targeted, has been disclosed in coverage so far.
