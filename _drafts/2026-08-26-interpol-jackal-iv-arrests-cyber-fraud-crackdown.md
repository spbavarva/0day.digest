An eight-month INTERPOL operation targeting West African organized
crime groups, including Black Axe, led to 58 arrests and the
identification of 263 suspects. The operation brought together 22
countries across six continents.

INTERPOL described the effort as a response to the escalating global
threat posed by these criminal networks' fraud and cybercrime
operations.
