Seqrite Labs disclosed a cyber-espionage campaign, codenamed Operation
QUICSILVER, targeting Myanmar's government and IT sectors.

The campaign uses graduation-ceremony invitation lures to deliver
QUICAgent, a backdoor written in Go. Researchers assess with moderate
confidence that the activity is the work of a China-nexus threat actor.
