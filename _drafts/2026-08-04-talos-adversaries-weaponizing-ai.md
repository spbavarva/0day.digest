Cisco Talos analyzed prompt logs collected from threat actor endpoints
running AI coding tools including Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, and
Gemini, providing a data-driven look at how attackers are using
cloud-based AI assistants in their operations.

The research offers direct, observed evidence of AI-assisted attacker
tradecraft rather than theoretical risk. Defenders and AI vendors should
treat threat actor abuse of coding assistants as an active behavior, not
a hypothetical.
