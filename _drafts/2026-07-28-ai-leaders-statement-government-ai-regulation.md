Employees from OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Meta, Thinking Machines,
Microsoft, Mistral, and other frontier AI labs signed a joint statement
calling on the US government to act on the risks of increasingly automated
AI systems. The statement asks for a form of coordinated slowdown, or at
minimum faster progress on global AI governance efforts. No specific policy
proposal or enforcement mechanism was detailed in the reporting. It signals
continued cross-lab employee concern about the pace of autonomous AI
capability deployment relative to safety and governance work.
