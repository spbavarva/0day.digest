The Android banking trojan ToxicPanda has shipped a new version with
features that expand its global reach.

The updated malware now puts more than victims' financial apps at risk,
extending its impact toward broader enterprise exposure on infected
devices. Mobile device management and endpoint teams should treat
ToxicPanda as an evolving threat rather than a fixed known quantity.
