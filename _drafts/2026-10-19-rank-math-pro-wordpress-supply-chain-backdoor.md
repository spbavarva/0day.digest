Copies of Rank Math PRO, the paid version of the popular Rank Math WordPress
SEO plugin, contained injected malware if downloaded from rankmath.com or
delivered as an update between 20:02 UTC on October 15 and 07:12 UTC on
October 16. The compromised build is version 3.0.123 and includes a
self-restoring backdoor.

Rank Math says any site that installed and activated that build should be
treated as compromised. Admins should check update logs against that
window, rotate credentials, and scan for backdoors if affected.
