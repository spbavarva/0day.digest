American Express introduced a framework intended to ensure AI shopping
agents only make purchases that users have explicitly authorized.

The move is a response to the growing risk of agentic-commerce tools
acting on stale or misinterpreted instructions and generating unauthorized
charges or disputes. Amex has not published technical specifics on how
enforcement will work across merchants.
