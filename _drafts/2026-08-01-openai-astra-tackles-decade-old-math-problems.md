OpenAI tasked an internal version of Astra — described as the company's next
major model — with finding solutions to ten unsolved problems in mathematics
and theoretical computer science, each having seen no progress on its main
result for at least a decade. The company says the effort used less than
$2,000 in inference costs, running on GPT-5.6 Sol.

The exercise follows a similar move by Anthropic, which recently used its
Mythos Preview model to search for cryptographic weaknesses, spending
roughly $100,000 on tokens.
