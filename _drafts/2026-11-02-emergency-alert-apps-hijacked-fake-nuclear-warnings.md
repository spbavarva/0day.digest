A popular Ukrainian air raid alert system was hacked over the weekend and
used to push fake nuclear attack warnings to its users.

In apparent retaliation, a cyber group then breached a similar alert
system in Russia. The incident shows how wartime emergency-notification
infrastructure can be hijacked to cause public panic.
