New York is preparing to enforce its AI safety law, which requires major
AI developers including OpenAI and Anthropic to register with the state,
disclose their safety practices, and report critical incidents.
Companies that fail to comply face fines. This puts New York among the
small set of jurisdictions with binding disclosure and incident-reporting
requirements for frontier AI developers, rather than voluntary
commitments.
