Japan announced an emergency cybersecurity package after dozens of
companies and government agencies were hit by unauthorized access and
client data leaks. The package follows a wave of intrusions that
prompted broad unease across the country.

Details of specific funding or technical mandates in the package were
not included in initial coverage.
