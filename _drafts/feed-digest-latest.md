### 4. Brotli Decoder Flaw Behind Node.js RCE Also Hits Bun, Python, Envoy
- **Source:** The Hacker News — https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/brotli-decoder-flaw-behind-nodejs-rce.html
- **Severity:** high
- **Tags:** `rce`, `vulnerability`, `cve`
- **Summary:** CVE-2026-111497, a memory-corruption flaw in Google's Brotli decoder, affects any software unpacking Brotli-compressed data, including Bun, Python's brotli/brotlicffi packages, and Envoy proxies. Crafted compressed data can overwrite buffer memory, with exposure extending well beyond the Node.js case that first surfaced it.

- **Severity:** medium
- **Tags:** `malware`, `vulnerability`
