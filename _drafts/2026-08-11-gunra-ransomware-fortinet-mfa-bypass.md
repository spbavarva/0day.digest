The Gunra ransomware-as-a-service operation is finding success against
critical infrastructure targets by combining leaked Conti source code
with older, unpatched flaws in Fortinet firewalls and VPN appliances.

The combination allows the group to bypass MFA protections on affected
devices.

Organizations running Fortinet firewalls/VPNs should confirm they are
fully patched and review MFA enforcement on remote access paths.
