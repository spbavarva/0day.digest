Anthropic rolled out Claude Mythos 5.2 to selected partners and Fable 5.2 to
regular users, with the usual coding and reasoning improvements.
BleepingComputer also noted security-relevant behavior changes: Fable 5.2
blocks fewer security-related tasks than its predecessor, while Mythos 5.2
shows improved bug-finding ability.

The shift reflects a broader trend of frontier models becoming more capable,
and more permissive, for security research use cases.
