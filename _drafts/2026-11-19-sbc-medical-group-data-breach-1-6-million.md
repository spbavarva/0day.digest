SBC Medical Group disclosed in an SEC filing that two separate cybersecurity
incidents exposed personal data belonging to 1.6 million customers and
employees. The filing does not break out which data types were taken in
each incident. No ransomware group or threat actor has been publicly
attributed yet. Further detail on the attack vector has not been released.
