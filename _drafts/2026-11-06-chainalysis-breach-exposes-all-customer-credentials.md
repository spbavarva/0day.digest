Blockchain analysis firm Chainalysis says an agentic cyber attack on its
systems exposed names, usernames, password hashes, and live tokens for all
of its users. The company pulled access to its Reactor and Data Solutions
products while it investigates.

Chainalysis says it has so far found no evidence that investigations data
itself was accessed, but the exposure of live tokens and credentials for
every customer is significant given the firm's role in crypto compliance
and law enforcement investigations. Customers should rotate credentials
and revoke any live tokens tied to Chainalysis integrations.
