Blockchain analysis firm Chainalysis revealed that an ongoing
investigation into an "agentic cyber attack" targeting its systems
found that names, usernames, password hashes, and live tokens for all
customers were exposed. Chainalysis removed access to its Reactor and
Data Solutions products while investigating.

The company says it currently has no evidence that investigations data
was accessed. Customers should treat all Chainalysis credentials and
tokens as compromised and rotate them.
