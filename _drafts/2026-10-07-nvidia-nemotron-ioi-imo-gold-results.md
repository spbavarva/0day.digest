achieving gold-level performance on both the International Olympiad in
Informatics (IOI) and the International Mathematical Olympiad (IMO)
benchmarks.

The post frames this as one model family reaching top-tier results across
both competitive programming and mathematical reasoning tasks via targeted
fine-tuning.

Further architectural or training details were not included in the
available summary.
