Tech executives have detailed the self-regulation framework agreed under the
Trump administration's AI safety deal, formally titled the "Joint Commitment
on Frontier Responsibilities." The agreement was shared by presidential
adviser David Sacks and signed by multiple industry leaders.

It relies on company self-policing rather than binding regulation, a notable
shift given the administration's prior framing of AI safety concerns.
Details on enforcement mechanisms remain thin.
