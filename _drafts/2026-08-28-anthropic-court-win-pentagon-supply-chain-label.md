A federal judge ruled that the Trump administration illegally labeled
Anthropic a supply-chain risk, handing the AI company a win in the first
of two ongoing lawsuits against the Pentagon. The ruling is limited to
this specific label dispute; a second Anthropic lawsuit against the
Pentagon continues separately in Washington.

Further detail on the court's reasoning was not included in the source
summary. The outcome is relevant to how AI vendors navigate federal
supply-chain risk designations going forward.
