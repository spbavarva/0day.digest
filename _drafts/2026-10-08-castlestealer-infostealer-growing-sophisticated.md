Flashpoint researchers detail the evolution of CastleStealer, an
infostealer that has grown more sophisticated over recent versions.

The malware now includes new techniques for bypassing browser
protections, remote command capabilities, and an exfiltration approach
designed to blend stolen data into normal network traffic, making
detection harder for defenders.
