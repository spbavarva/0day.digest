Three Ukrainians are set to stand trial for allegedly stealing access to
more than 610,000 Roblox accounts and selling that access to buyers in
Russia, authorities said.

Details on the technique used to compromise the accounts were not
disclosed in the available reporting; the case is being handled as a
criminal prosecution rather than an active, ongoing threat.
