South Korean government agencies warn that cyberattack tools and
infrastructure used by North Korea's Lazarus Group have been shared with
ransomware criminals targeting South Korean organizations.

The warning points to deepening ties between Pyongyang-backed state hackers
and the broader ransomware ecosystem, per The Record. No specific tool names
or victim organizations were disclosed in the advisory summary.
