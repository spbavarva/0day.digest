A newly identified UEFI bootkit, BlackX, was deployed in an espionage
campaign in early 2026. It exploits the previously known "Baton Drop"
vulnerability to bypass Secure Boot and load a malicious driver.

The driver is designed to interfere with security products from
Kaspersky and Avast, suppressing detection on compromised hosts.
