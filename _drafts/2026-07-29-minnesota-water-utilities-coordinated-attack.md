A coordinated cyberattack hit more than 30 community water systems across
Minnesota on July 26-27, prompting Minnesota IT Services to activate
statewide incident response. Braham, Plymouth, South St. Paul, and Maple
Plain publicly reported impacts, including a plant outage in Braham and
communications or automated-control failures elsewhere. Residents in
Braham were asked to minimize water use while the plant was offline. The
attack targeted operational technology rather than IT systems. No
attribution or entry-vector details have been published yet.
