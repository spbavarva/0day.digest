A newly attributed China-nexus espionage group, TA419, is running
credential-phishing campaigns against AI policy experts at U.S. think
tanks, universities, and legal organizations, using adversary-in-the-middle
(AitM) techniques against Microsoft accounts.

The campaigns impersonate prominent economists and AI policymakers, as well
as a named Anthropic employee, to target specific AI policy experts.
Organizations employing AI policy staff should review MFA and
conditional-access hardening against AitM phishing kits.
