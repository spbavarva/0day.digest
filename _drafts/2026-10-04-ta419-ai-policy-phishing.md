A newly attributed China-nexus espionage group, TA419, is running
adversary-in-the-middle credential phishing campaigns against artificial
intelligence policy experts at U.S. think tanks, universities, and legal
sector organizations.

The lures impersonate prominent economists and AI policymakers, as well
as a named Anthropic employee, to single out specific AI policy experts.
AitM phishing kits can intercept session tokens and bypass MFA, so
targeted individuals should verify sign-in prompts closely and review
account activity for unrecognized sessions.
