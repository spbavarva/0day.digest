Researchers from George Washington University have published a paper
examining whether the timing and cause of an AI system "going rogue" can
be predicted mathematically, according to SecurityWeek.

The work aims to give a quantitative basis for anticipating misalignment
events in deployed chatbots rather than relying solely on after-the-fact
incident review.

Further detail on the formula's inputs, validation data, or whether it's
been tested against real-world incidents wasn't available in initial
reporting.
