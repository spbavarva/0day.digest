A threat actor tracked as "FrostyNeighbor" has launched a destructive
attack in Ukraine, according to Dark Reading, and researchers see it as a
sign of Belarus stepping up direct involvement in Russia-aligned cyber
operations.

The group's activity suggests deeper coordination between Belarusian and
Russian state cyber objectives as the conflict continues.

Specific technical details on FrostyNeighbor's tooling, initial access
methods, or the scope of the destructive impact were not included in
current reporting.

Defenders tracking Russia-aligned APT activity in the region should treat
Belarus-linked infrastructure as an emerging, not just secondary, threat
vector.
