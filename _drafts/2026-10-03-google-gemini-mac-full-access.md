Google is reportedly testing a Gemini capability for macOS that would let
the assistant read any file on the device, open apps, browse the web, and
take actions without a per-action permission prompt.

Removing per-action confirmation widens the blast radius of prompt
injection or a compromised session, since the assistant would already
hold broad standing access to local files and apps. The feed summary
doesn't specify rollout timing or what controls, if any, will gate the
access.
