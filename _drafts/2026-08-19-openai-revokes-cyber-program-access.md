Security researchers say OpenAI revoked their access to Trusted Access
for Cyber, a program designed to give vetted defenders access to better
models so they could find and report vulnerabilities faster.

The program's stated goal was accelerating patch timelines for bugs
discovered with AI assistance. Details on why access was revoked, and for
how many researchers, remain limited.

Practitioners relying on the program for vulnerability research should
confirm their current access status with OpenAI.
