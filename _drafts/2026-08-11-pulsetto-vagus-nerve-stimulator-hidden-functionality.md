CISA disclosed a hidden-functionality vulnerability (CVE-2026-18844, CVSS
8.1) in the Pulsetto Vagus Nerve Stimulator affecting all versions.
Successful exploitation could let an attacker use hidden commands to
disable the device's electrical safety mechanisms or modify stimulation
output settings, posing a physical safety risk to users. Pulsetto is
headquartered in Lithuania; devices are deployed worldwide.
