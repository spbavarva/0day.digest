CISA published results from two simultaneous red team assessments
against critical infrastructure organizations using similar tradecraft.
Both organizations were fully compromised at the domain level, but the
two had sharply different defensive outcomes — one detected nothing
during the engagement.

The report underscores that similar attack surface and adversary
tradecraft don't guarantee similar detection outcomes; monitoring and
response capability made the difference.
