Anthropic is working on a watermarking approach for text generated by Claude,
aimed at making it easier to identify AI-generated content online. The
coverage frames this as part of a broader push toward provenance and
detection tooling for AI output.

Technical details of the watermarking mechanism itself were not disclosed in
available reporting. Worth tracking as AI-generated-content detection becomes
more relevant to trust and safety workflows.
