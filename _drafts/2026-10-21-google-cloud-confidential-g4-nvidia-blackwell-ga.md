Google Cloud announced general availability of Confidential Computing on its
G4 instances, powered by NVIDIA RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell Server edition GPUs.
The feature integrates NVIDIA Confidential Computing into Google Cloud's AI
Hypercomputer stack.

It's aimed at protecting proprietary model weights, user prompts, and
generated outputs while they're actively being computed — relevant for
enterprises running agents that process sensitive data.
