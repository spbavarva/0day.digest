ESET reports that Russian state-sponsored group Sandworm worked with
another threat actor, UAC-0099 (aka Earth Sirrush), to carry out a
destructive wiping attack against a Ukrainian logistics company.

Initial access was gained on July 16, 2025 via a spear-phishing email
carrying a malware-laced attachment, which led to deployment of a new
mesh-network backdoor. The attack was destructive, consistent with
Sandworm's established pattern of targeting Ukrainian infrastructure and
logistics operators.
