A joint law enforcement advisory warns that the North Korean hacking group WaterPlum compromised at least 30,000 devices worldwide between December 2025 and July 2026.

The group transferred more than $10.7 million in stolen cryptocurrency back to North Korea over the course of the campaign. No specific technical indicators from the advisory were included in the source summary.
