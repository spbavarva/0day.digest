Russia's federal statistics agency, Rosstat, confirmed a cyberattack
claimed by an alliance of Belarusian and pro-Ukrainian hacker groups.
The attackers say they disabled the agency's systems and exfiltrated
large volumes of sensitive government data.

No technical details on the intrusion method have been published
yet. The incident is part of an ongoing wave of hacktivist operations
targeting Russian state infrastructure.
