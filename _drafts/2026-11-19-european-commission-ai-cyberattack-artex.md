The European Commission confirmed a cyberattack in which intruders used
ARTEX, an open-source AI-assisted hacking platform. CERT-EU identified the
tool's use after POLITICO reported it; the FBI warned just last week that
ARTEX is being used by both criminal and state-sponsored groups. This is a
concrete instance of AI tooling lowering the barrier to attacking a
high-value government target, rather than a theoretical risk. Details on
what was accessed within Commission systems have not been published.
