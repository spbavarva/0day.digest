Attackers are exploiting three vulnerabilities in JFrog Artifactory to
bypass authentication and elevate privileges to administrator, then use
that access to deploy backdoors.

Patch status and CVE identifiers were not included in the source
reporting. Organizations running Artifactory should review the vendor
advisory for affected versions.
