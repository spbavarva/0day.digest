OpenAI has unveiled GPT-5.6-Cyber, a cybersecurity-focused model built on
GPT-5.6 Sol and aimed at vulnerability research, penetration testing, and
incident response. OpenAI says the model is trained to improve
performance on tasks like finding zero-day vulnerabilities and developing
exploit chains, with reduced refusals for certain higher-risk security use
cases. The model is being rolled out alongside an expansion of OpenAI's
Daybreak platform.
