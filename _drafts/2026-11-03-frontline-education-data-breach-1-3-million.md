Frontline Education disclosed a data breach affecting more than 1.3
million people after hackers exploited a flaw in third-party software
to steal personal information belonging to school district staff and
students.

Specific technical details of the exploited flaw were not included in
the available summary. Affected school districts should follow
Frontline Education's breach notification guidance.
