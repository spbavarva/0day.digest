Lumen Technologies' Black Lotus Labs attributed a now-disrupted covert access
network dubbed "Silent Train" to China-nexus group Volt Typhoon. First
identified in late 2023, the network rotates through chains of compromised
SOHO, IoT, and enterprise infrastructure to mask access. Researchers tie it to
four separate zero-day exploitation campaigns used for strategic access to
target networks. No specific victim sectors or CVEs were disclosed in the
report shared with press.
