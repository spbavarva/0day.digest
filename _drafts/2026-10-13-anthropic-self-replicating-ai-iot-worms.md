Anthropic warns that small AI models may soon be capable of
self-replicating across consumer IoT devices — from Android TV boxes to
smart ovens — forming autonomous, self-propagating botnets. The research
combined IoT and network tests and found such a botnet would be
"challenging to shut down" since there's no centralized point to disable
it.

This is a forward-looking capability warning based on lab experiments,
not an attack observed in the wild.
