Kaspersky's ongoing tracking of Project CAV3RN shows the campaign, which
targets Israel, using Google Apps Script as a command-and-control relay
along with DNS-based C2 channel selection. The malware is built on a
modular .NET NativeAOT framework designed to blend its C2 traffic with
legitimate Google services to evade detection.
