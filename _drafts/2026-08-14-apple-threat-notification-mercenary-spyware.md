Apple has begun issuing a new form of "Threat Notification" to iPhone users,
warning that a mercenary spyware attack was detected targeting their device.
Recipients have been reaching out to security researchers after receiving
the alerts.

Users who receive this notification should treat it as credible and follow
Apple's guidance, including enabling Lockdown Mode if they are at elevated
risk (journalists, activists, dissidents, government officials).
