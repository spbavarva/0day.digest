Proofpoint says generative AI has eliminated many of the behavioral tells
that email filters have relied on to catch phishing — clumsy wording,
generic pretexts, reused templates. AI-generated phishing messages now arrive
fluent, personalized, and unique per recipient.

Proofpoint shared examples of the shift at its Protect 2026 conference,
noting this weakens detection systems that rely on behavioral signals rather
than content or infrastructure indicators.
