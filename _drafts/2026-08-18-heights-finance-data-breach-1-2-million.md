Heights Finance disclosed a data breach affecting at least 1.2 million
individuals. Attackers stole names, addresses, phone numbers, Social
Security numbers, and financial information from a third-party platform
used by the company.

The exposure of SSNs and financial data at this scale creates significant
identity theft and fraud risk for affected individuals.
