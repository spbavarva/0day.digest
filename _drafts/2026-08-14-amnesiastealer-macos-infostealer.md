A new Rust-based macOS infostealer dubbed AmnesiaStealer has been identified
in the wild. The malware harvests users' saved passwords, macOS Keychain
data, Chromium-based browser data, and Safari cookies, and can control
active browser sessions.

The Rust codebase and cross-browser targeting suggest the malware is under
active development.
