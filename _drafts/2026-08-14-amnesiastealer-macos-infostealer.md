A Rust-based macOS infostealer dubbed AmnesiaStealer harvests saved
passwords, Keychain data, and Chromium-based browser data, and also
extracts Safari cookies. The malware additionally gives operators control
over active browser sessions.

Mac users, particularly those handling credentials or session tokens for
sensitive services, should watch for unexpected browser session activity
and keep endpoint protection current.
