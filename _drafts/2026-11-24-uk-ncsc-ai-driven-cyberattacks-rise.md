Britain's National Cyber Security Centre said it has observed a "marked
rise" since September in cyberattacks assisted by artificial intelligence,
warning that the technology is increasing attackers' speed, scale, and
persistence.

The agency said the attacks largely succeed by exploiting long-standing,
basic security weaknesses rather than novel techniques, and reiterated that
strong security fundamentals matter more than ever.
