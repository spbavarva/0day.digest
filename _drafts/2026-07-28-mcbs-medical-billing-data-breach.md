Medical Computer Business Services (MCBS), a healthcare billing company,
disclosed that a 2025 network breach exposed sensitive information
belonging to more than 1.26 million people. The disclosure did not specify
the intrusion vector or the remediation steps that followed. Organizations
that use MCBS for billing services should watch for breach notification
details as they become available.
