MITRE released ATT&CK v20, adding 15 new detection strategies — six for
Enterprise and nine for ICS — backed by 38 new analytics, according to
SecurityWeek.

New coverage areas include AI abuse techniques, MFA bypass methods, and
attacks against water utility systems, giving defenders updated reference
material for threat modeling and detection engineering.
