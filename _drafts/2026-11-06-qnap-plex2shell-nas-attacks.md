QNAP warned that hackers are actively breaching its NAS devices by
exploiting the "Plex2Shell" vulnerability chain in Plex Media Server.
QNAP is urging users to update Plex Media Server to the latest version
available in the QNAP App Center.

NAS admins running Plex on QNAP devices should patch immediately and
restrict external access to the management interface in the meantime.
