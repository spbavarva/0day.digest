Coinkite, maker of the Coldcard bitcoin hardware wallet, destroyed part of
its unsold inventory after attackers exploited a firmware vulnerability to
steal more than $88 million from customers.

The exact exploitation mechanism and affected firmware versions were not
detailed in initial coverage. Coldcard owners should watch for vendor
guidance and apply any firmware updates promptly.
