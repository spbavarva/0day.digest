Researchers reported what they describe as the first purported
"near-autonomous" attack carried out by a nation-state-linked actor. A
Chinese-language operator used a complex AI framework to target and
compromise government agencies, likely in Taiwan.

The report frames the incident as an early example of AI systems taking on a
larger share of attack execution with reduced human operator involvement,
rather than AI being used solely as a productivity aid for human attackers.
