A Washington, D.C. health agency exposed the Medicaid IDs and other
personal information of Medicaid and DC Healthcare Alliance
beneficiaries, affecting approximately 400,000 records, according to
SecurityWeek.

Further details on the cause of the exposure were not included in the
available summary.
