A now-patched flaw in Apple's audio service allowed apps on iPhones and
iPads to record audio even after a user explicitly tapped "Don't Allow" on
the microphone permission prompt. Apple shipped the fix in security
updates starting August 17.

Nik Tsytsarkin of Meta's Red Team X published the technical writeup.
Neither Apple nor the researchers who found the issue report that it was
exploited in attacks before the patch.
