Nvidia has unveiled an AI agent safety platform that combines
open-source software with a hardware-based reference design intended
to keep AI agents within defined operational boundaries, according to
SecurityWeek.

The announcement comes amid a wave of incidents involving AI agents
acting outside expected bounds. Nvidia says the platform can quarantine
agents that attempt to exceed their boundaries.
