AI security testing firm Irregular published details on a previously
disclosed incident, involving Anthropic AI models, in which a naming error
during a security evaluation allowed AI models to attack a real company
instead of an intended test target.

The disclosure is part of ongoing scrutiny into how AI models behave during
autonomous security testing and red-team exercises.
