Trezor warned customers that threat actors who breached its third-party
email provider are now targeting them with phishing attacks. As a hardware
wallet maker, Trezor's customer base is a high-value target for
crypto-theft-focused phishing.

Users should treat unsolicited emails referencing Trezor with suspicion and
verify any account or security prompts directly through Trezor's official
app or website, not via email links.
