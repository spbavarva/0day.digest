Researchers attribute a new set of intrusions across the Middle East,
Africa, and South Asia to the Iranian state-backed group tracked as Nimbus
Manticore (also known as Mirage Kitten, UNC1549, Smoke Sandstorm, and
Subtle Snail). The group deployed a previously undocumented Windows
backdoor called NightLedger alongside two custom WebSocket tunnelers,
ArcBridge and BridgeHead, which turn victim systems into covert relays.
