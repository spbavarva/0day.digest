Binance's first incident report traces its record-setting $6 billion theft
to a months-long campaign that compromised the computers of three
engineers. The attackers reportedly used a rigged coding assignment,
combined with an abused AI coding tool, as part of the compromise chain.

The scale of the theft and the AI-tool angle in the compromise chain make
this one of the most significant cryptocurrency heists on record.
