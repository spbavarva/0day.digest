CISA issued an advisory for Hitachi Energy TRO600 series products (versions
≤9.2.1.6, ≤9.1.1.0, 9.2.2.1, and 9.1.2.0), affected by multiple
vulnerabilities (CVE-2026-10756, CVE-2026-106087) that can be exploited for
remote code execution and disclosure of sensitive information. Operators
should apply Hitachi Energy's recommended mitigations.
