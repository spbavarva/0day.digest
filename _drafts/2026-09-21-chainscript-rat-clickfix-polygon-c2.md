Threat actors are using ClickFix-style lures to deliver a previously
undocumented remote access trojan (RAT) called ChainScript.

The malware has appeared under multiple build names, including
ComponentTask33, UpdateDigital, HostShared, and OrchidViolet66, while
posing as Spotify, Zoom Workplace, and Microsoft Teams installers.

Notably, ChainScript uses the Polygon blockchain to rotate its
command-and-control infrastructure, per Blackpoint Adversary Pursuit
Group (APG).
