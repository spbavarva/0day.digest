Threat actors are compromising Android-based car head units through a
legitimate device-update app, enlisting affected devices into a proxy
botnet. The compromised units are also being used for ad fraud.

This is a supply-chain-style compromise of the update mechanism rather than
a direct exploit of the head unit software itself. The source summary did
not disclose the specific vendor, app name, or number of affected devices.
