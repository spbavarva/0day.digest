Microsoft published a "Humanist AI Code of Conduct" that draws a
line between defensive cyber research and operational attack
capability for AI systems.

The policy reportedly sets boundaries around cyberattack-related AI
use, establishes a chain of command, and defines safety constraints.
The source summary did not include specifics on enforcement
mechanisms or how the policy applies to Microsoft's own products
versus third-party use.
