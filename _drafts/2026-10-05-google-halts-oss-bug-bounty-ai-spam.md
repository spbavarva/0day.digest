Google has temporarily suspended submissions to its Open Source
Software Vulnerability Rewards Program (OSS VRP) after being flooded
with invalid, AI-generated vulnerability reports. The move highlights a
growing operational burden bug bounty programs face as AI
report-generation tools proliferate. No timeline for reopening the
program was given. Other bounty programs may face similar pressure
going forward.
