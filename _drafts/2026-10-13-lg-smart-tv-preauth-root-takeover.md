Researchers chained three vulnerabilities in LG's webOS that let an
attacker on the same local network take root control of certain smart TVs
without a PIN or any user interaction. The chain was demonstrated against
the 32LQ636BPSA and 27TQ600SW models running webOS 7.5.7-25.

LG has shipped a fix, per an SSD Secure Disclosure advisory published
October 12. Owners of affected models should update firmware.
