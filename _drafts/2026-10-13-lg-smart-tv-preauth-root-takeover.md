Three chained vulnerabilities in LG's webOS can give an attacker on the
same local network root access to certain smart TVs without a PIN or any
owner interaction, according to an SSD Secure Disclosure advisory. The
chain was demonstrated on two models (32LQ636BPSA and 27TQ600SW) running
webOS 7.5.7-25.

LG has issued a fix. Owners of affected models should install the
available firmware update.
