A customer reported that a Trezor Safe 5 hardware wallet purchased
from reseller CryptoBilis contains an extra circuit board that
transmits the wallet's recovery phrase over a mobile network.

Trezor has not yet examined the device itself, but is telling
everyone who bought a Trezor from CryptoBilis since March 2026 to
move their funds to a new wallet with a freshly generated recovery
phrase.

This looks like a hardware supply chain tampering case affecting one
specific reseller rather than Trezor's own manufacturing. Anyone who
bought hardware wallets through unofficial or third-party resellers
should verify authenticity before trusting a device with funds.
