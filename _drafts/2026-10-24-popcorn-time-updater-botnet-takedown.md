Dutch security firm Eye Security pushed more than 2 million updates, between
November 2025 and March 2026, to computers it did not own in order to
disable a background updater it deemed dangerous.

The updater belonged to Time4Popcorn, an abandoned fork of Popcorn Time, the
pirated-media streaming app. Eye Security said the updater could have let an
outsider take over affected machines, potentially fueling a large botnet.
