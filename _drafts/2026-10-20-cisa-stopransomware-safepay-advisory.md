CISA and partner agencies published a joint advisory on SafePay ransomware,
active since August 2024 and targeting businesses and critical
infrastructure across North America, South America, Europe, and Australia.

SafePay actors typically gain initial access via stolen or guessed
passwords, exploitation of vulnerabilities in security systems, and
phishing. Organizations should review the full advisory for IOCs and apply
the recommended mitigations, particularly around credential hygiene and
perimeter security system patching.
