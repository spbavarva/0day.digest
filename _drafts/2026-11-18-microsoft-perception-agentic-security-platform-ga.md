Microsoft has moved its "Perception" agentic security platform out of
preview, making four blue-team agents and a Recon Agent generally available.
Additional integrations and vulnerability shielding capabilities remain in
preview.

This is part of the broader trend of AI agents being embedded directly into
SOC and vulnerability management workflows.
