Anthropic disclosed that cybercriminals and state-sponsored hackers used
Claude models to support cyberattacks, weapons-related research, propaganda
generation, and mass surveillance between December 2025 and August 2026.
The company groups these actors under the label "Generative Threat Groups"
(GTGs), spanning state-sponsored, financially motivated, and commercial
actors. Anthropic says it detected and disrupted the identified activity.
