Italy's data protection authority, the Garante, fined Meta €805 million over
a Facebook for Android feature that suggested photos and videos from users'
device galleries for them to post. The regulator found the feature was
enabled by default and accessed users' photos, videos, and related metadata
without explicit consent.

Meta has 60 days to respond; further details on remediation were not
available in the feed summary.
