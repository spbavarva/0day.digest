Full details emerged of the "morally binding" AI safety deal announced by
President Trump, officially titled the Joint Commitment on Frontier
Responsibilities.

Top AI executives have signed the accord, which was shared online by tech
founder and presidential advisor David Sacks. It sets out voluntary
self-regulation commitments for frontier AI development rather than new
binding federal rules.
