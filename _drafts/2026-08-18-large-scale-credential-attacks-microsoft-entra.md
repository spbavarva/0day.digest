Unit 42 published updated guidance on mitigating large-scale credential
attacks after a threat actor calling itself "TheHatman" claimed to have
stolen a large volume of credentials from multiple organizations' Microsoft
Entra tenants.

Organizations using Microsoft Entra should review the threat brief for
practical mitigation steps and treat exposed credentials as compromised
pending rotation and monitoring for anomalous sign-ins.
