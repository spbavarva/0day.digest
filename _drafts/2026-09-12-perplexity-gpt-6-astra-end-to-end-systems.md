OpenAI says Perplexity is using its GPT-6 Astra model to write
communications, modify software, and monitor production systems, checking
in with humans much less frequently than it did with earlier models.

No benchmark results or specific reliability metrics were included in the
source summary. The post frames this as evidence of growing enterprise
trust in autonomous agent workflows built on Astra.
