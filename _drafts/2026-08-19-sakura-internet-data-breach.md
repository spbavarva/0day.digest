Japanese cloud and data center provider Sakura Internet disclosed that
hackers accessed its sales management system, which stores customer
contract and membership information.

Up to 1.36 million accounts may be affected.

Affected customers should watch for phishing attempts referencing their
Sakura Internet account details and follow any rotation guidance the
company issues.
