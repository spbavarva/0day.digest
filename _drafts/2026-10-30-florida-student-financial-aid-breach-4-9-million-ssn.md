Florida's Department of Education (FLDOE) disclosed a data breach affecting
the Florida Student Financial Aid website, discovered on October 19.

The state says approximately 4.9 million people had their Social Security
numbers exposed in the incident.

No details on the attack vector, how long the exposure lasted, or whether
the data has been used fraudulently were included in the disclosure.

Affected individuals should watch for identity-theft attempts and consider
credit monitoring or a credit freeze.
