Cisco Talos describes how malware command-and-control infrastructure is
shifting away from fragile, centralized servers and hardcoded domains toward
decentralized storage on public blockchains, which eliminates single points
of failure for takedown efforts.

Because blockchain data can't be removed once written, this "bulletproof
dead drop" approach makes conventional domain and IP-based C2 disruption
much harder for defenders.
