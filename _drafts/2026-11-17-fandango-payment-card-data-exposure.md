Fandango is notifying customers that payment card numbers, CVVs,
expiration dates, and email addresses may have been exposed during a
roughly four-day window in October when buyers checked out through the
ticketing platform. The exposure covers active card data rather than just
account metadata, raising the risk of direct fraudulent use.

No total number of affected customers was disclosed. Anyone who purchased
tickets through Fandango during that window should monitor card statements
and consider reissuing affected cards.
