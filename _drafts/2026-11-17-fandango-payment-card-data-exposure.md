Fandango is notifying ticket buyers that card numbers, CVVs, expiration
dates, and email addresses used at its ticketing checkout may have been
exposed over a four-day window in October. The inclusion of CVVs makes
this a notable payment-card incident, since CVV data isn't supposed to be
retained after authorization under PCI DSS.

Affected customers should watch for fraudulent charges and consider
replacing the card used during the exposure window.
