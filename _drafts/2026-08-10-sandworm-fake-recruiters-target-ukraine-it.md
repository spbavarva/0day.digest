Ukraine's CERT-UA said a campaign linked to the Sandworm hacking unit,
associated with Russia's GRU, has been posing as recruiters to target
Ukrainian IT workers since at least May 2026, per The Record. The activity
uses fake recruiting outreach as a lure, a known Sandworm-linked technique
for initial access. No specific malware families or IOCs were disclosed in
the report. IT workers in Ukraine, and organizations that employ them,
should treat unsolicited recruiting contact with added scrutiny.
