Intruders used a single set of compromised credentials to access an ETS
SFTP server and copy records belonging to millions of California students
dating back to 2006, according to SecurityWeek.

The incident underscores the outsized impact a single leaked credential can
have against systems lacking MFA or tighter access controls on bulk data
transfer endpoints.
