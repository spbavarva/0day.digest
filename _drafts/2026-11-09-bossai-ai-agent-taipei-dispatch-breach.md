A single attacker using BOSSAI AI agents pulled emergency dispatch
records covering seven Taipei districts, according to researchers at
Acronis's Threat Research Unit.

The same agents also found a Taipei fire department platform exposing
patient data from 2018, including ECG readings and ambulance video
footage, with no login required.

Researchers are highly confident the attacker is a single person,
illustrating how AI agents can let one individual carry out
reconnaissance and data theft at a scale previously requiring a team.
