Eclypsium's November InfraTrust report looked at infrastructure security
advisories covering exploited vulnerabilities over the past month.

Most of those advisories involve flaws in third-party software bundled into
network appliances, rather than code written by the appliance vendors
themselves.

The finding underscores that vendor risk assessments need to account for
bundled third-party components, not just vendor-authored code.
