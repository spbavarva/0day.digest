Cryptocurrency exchange Bitget confirmed that the attackers who stole $387.5
million from it last week gained access by exploiting a zero-day
vulnerability in third-party security products, rather than a flaw in
Bitget's own code.

This continues a pattern of large crypto-platform breaches originating in
the security supply chain rather than the primary target's own
infrastructure. The affected third-party vendor was not named in the
statements referenced here.
