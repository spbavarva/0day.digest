CISA disclosed multiple vulnerabilities (CVE-2026-9852, CVE-2026-9853,
CVE-2026-9854), rated up to CVSS v3 7.8, in Hitachi Energy's MicroSCADA X
SYS600 product, affecting versions up to 10.7 and 10.8.

The flaws can be exploited to carry out various attacks against the SCADA
platform. MicroSCADA is widely deployed in energy-sector grid management;
affected operators should apply Hitachi Energy's recommended mitigations.
