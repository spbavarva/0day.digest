Cisco Talos's Q3 2026 incident response trends report found phishing
remained the leading initial-access technique, while the share of ransomware
and pre-ransomware cases increased quarter over quarter. Attackers
increasingly impersonated IT help desks in vishing (voice phishing) attacks.
Finance and public administration tied as the most-targeted industries.
