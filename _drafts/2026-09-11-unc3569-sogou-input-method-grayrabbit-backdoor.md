A China-linked group tracked as UNC3569 exploited a flaw in Sogou Input
Method, one of the most widely used Chinese-character typing tools on
Windows, to deploy a backdoor called GRAYRABBIT. Gen Digital said the
attack chain started with a crafted link and gave the attacker the same
level of access as the logged-in victim. Tencent, which owns Sogou, was
named in the research. The campaign highlights risk in widely deployed
input-method software that rarely gets security scrutiny.
