Anthropic released Claude Haiku 5.5, its new fast, low-cost model,
succeeding Haiku 4.5.

The new model matches the pricing of OpenAI's GPT-6 Luna at $0.10/$0.50 per
million input/output tokens up to 100,000 tokens, after which the price
increases 5x to $0.50/$2.50. Haiku 4.5 had been priced at $1/$5 per million
tokens, roughly 10x GPT-6 Luna's launch price.
