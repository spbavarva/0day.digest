Three suspected Russian-linked cyber espionage clusters — tracked as
UNC6293, UNC7005, and UNC5976 — have been observed abusing legitimate
Google OAuth authentication flows and WhatsApp device-linking features to
hijack accounts. The campaigns single out individuals in academia, aerospace
and defense, government, and think tanks across Europe and the US.
Researchers describe the clusters as persistent and adaptive. Because the
technique abuses legitimate auth flows rather than exploiting a software
bug, standard malware detection may not catch it; targeted sectors should
review OAuth grants and linked devices for anomalies.
