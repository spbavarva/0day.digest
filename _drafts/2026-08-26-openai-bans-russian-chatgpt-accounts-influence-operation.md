OpenAI said it banned a cluster of Russian ChatGPT accounts that used
VPNs to bypass access restrictions and run an influence operation. The
accounts used ChatGPT to generate social media posts and comments
promoting the International Burke Institute, shared across Substack,
Telegram, X, Facebook, and LinkedIn.

The takedown is another example of AI-generated content being used at
scale for influence operations, and of a lab detecting and disrupting
misuse of its own platform.
