The FBI is warning that threat actors are using an AI-powered
penetration-testing framework called ARTEX to automate cyberattacks against
organizations worldwide. The advisory ties ARTEX to a recent supply-chain
breach at ConnectWise that compromised Microsoft 365 customer environments.

Organizations running ConnectWise tooling or Microsoft 365 integrations
should review their exposure to the referenced breach. The feed summary did
not include specific IOCs or detection guidance.
