The Greatness phishing-as-a-service (PhaaS) platform has expanded
beyond credential phishing into adversary-in-the-middle (AiTM) attacks
and device-code phishing.

Its latest campaign spoofs RingCentral to target and steal Microsoft
365 account credentials and session tokens.

Organizations should watch for unusual device-code sign-in flows and
educate users on verifying RingCentral-branded prompts before entering
credentials.
