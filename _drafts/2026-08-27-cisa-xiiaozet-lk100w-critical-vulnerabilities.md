CISA issued an advisory for the Xiiaozet LK100W (versions below
2.1.240), citing OS command injection, missing authentication for a
critical function, and authentication bypass via an alternate path. The
flaws carry a CVSS v3 score of 9.8.

Successful exploitation could let an attacker take full control of the
device. The advisory lists the affected sector as Information
Technology.
