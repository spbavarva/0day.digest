DentaQuest disclosed a data breach that potentially impacts over 23 million
people. Hackers stole personal and dental health information from
DentaQuest's computer network in May 2026. The scale of the breach, affecting
tens of millions of individuals' health data, makes it one of the larger
healthcare-sector breaches disclosed this year. Affected individuals should
watch for phishing attempts referencing dental or health insurance details
and monitor for identity theft.
