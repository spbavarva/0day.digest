DentaQuest disclosed a data breach that may affect more than 23 million
people. According to SecurityWeek, hackers accessed DentaQuest's computer
network in May 2026 and stole personal and dental health information.
DentaQuest is a dental benefits administrator serving Medicaid and other
insurance plans, making this one of the larger healthcare-sector breaches
disclosed this year. No details on the attack vector or threat actor were
included in the source summary. Affected individuals should watch for breach
notification letters and monitor for identity theft or insurance fraud.
