WhatsApp's help pages contained hidden instructions directing its support
chatbot not to recommend a data protection authority or regulator to users
who could no longer delete their accounts, even though WhatsApp's European
privacy policy tells users they can file complaints with one.

The instructions sat in the page code, never shown on screen, until they
were removed around October 18. The incident raises questions about
undisclosed system prompts shaping chatbot behavior around user rights.
