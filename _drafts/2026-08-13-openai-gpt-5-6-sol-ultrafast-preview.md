OpenAI previewed "Ultrafast," a new API service tier that runs GPT-5.6 Sol
up to 14x faster than standard serving.

The tier is powered by Cerebras hardware and delivers up to 750 output
tokens per second, aimed at latency-sensitive applications like real-time
agents and voice interfaces.
