Dave Chismon, the NCSC's chief technology officer for architecture, said in
a blog post that the current imbalance in AI capability favors attackers.

He argued that AI-accelerated cyberattacks are likely to grow faster than
automated defenses can keep pace, widening the gap between offense and
defense.
