The ShinyHunters extortion group has published data stolen from nearly
13 million Carhartt accounts, according to breach notification service
Have I Been Pwned. The breach reportedly occurred earlier this month.

No technical detail on the intrusion vector was available at time of
writing. Carhartt customers should treat any associated credentials as
compromised and watch for phishing attempts referencing the breach.
