The FCC added foreign-produced mobile robots and networked power inverters
to its Covered List on July 28, generally preventing new models from
receiving the equipment authorization required for import, marketing, or
sale in the US.

Previously authorized models can still be sold, and devices already in use
are unaffected. Federal purchase and use restrictions were also part of the
action, per The Hacker News.
