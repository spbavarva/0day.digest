A Chrome extension called "Poper Blocker," marketed as an ad blocker
and downloaded by millions of users, exfiltrates sensitive user
information despite being listed in the official Chrome Web Store,
according to Dark Reading.

The extension has retained its store listing and Google's approval
despite prior researcher warnings.
