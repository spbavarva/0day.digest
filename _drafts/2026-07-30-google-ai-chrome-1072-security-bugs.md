Google says it is expanding its use of AI to find and fix security
vulnerabilities in Chrome, crediting the approach with more than 1,000 bug
fixes across the browser's two most recent releases.

The company frames this as evidence that AI-assisted vulnerability discovery
is scaling faster than traditional manual review. No breakdown of bug
severity or CVE identifiers was provided in the announcement.
