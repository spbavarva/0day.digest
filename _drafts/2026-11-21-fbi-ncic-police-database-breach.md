FBI systems have reportedly been breached for the second time in two
months, this time targeting the National Crime Information Center (NCIC).
NCIC is used to share criminal justice information between local, state,
and federal law enforcement agencies nationwide.

The scope of data accessed and the intrusion method have not yet been
detailed. A second reported compromise of FBI infrastructure in a short
window raises questions about whether earlier fixes addressed the root
cause.
