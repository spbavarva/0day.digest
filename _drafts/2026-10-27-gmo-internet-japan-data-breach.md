Japan's GMO Internet is verifying customer data that suspected hackers
posted online following a breach of a cloud service used by the company.
The scope of the exposed data and number of affected customers had not
been confirmed at the time of reporting.

GMO Internet operates a wide range of internet infrastructure and
financial services in Japan, meaning any confirmed breach could have
broad downstream impact. Further details are expected as GMO's
investigation continues.
