Amazon released TorchNeuron, a developer tool aimed at making it easier to
run PyTorch workloads on non-Nvidia chips, including its own Trainium and
Inferentia silicon. PyTorch is the dominant open-source framework used to
build most AI applications, and the broader ecosystem is heavily tuned for
Nvidia GPUs today.

The move is part of a wider push by cloud providers to reduce dependence on
Nvidia hardware for AI training and inference. No security-specific details
were disclosed.
