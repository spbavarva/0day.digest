OpenAI has added a "Computer History" feature to ChatGPT's macOS desktop
app that turns a user's on-screen activity into training data, per The
Verge.

The feature builds a timeline of clicks and keystrokes that ChatGPT and
Codex can reference to learn work patterns, suggest automations, and pick
up half-finished tasks. Continuous local activity capture on the desktop
broadens the data an attacker or curious insider could target if the
history store is compromised or mishandled.
