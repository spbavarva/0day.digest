Google DeepMind announced Genie 4, a new generation of its world model
for real-time interactive simulation. The model adds audio generation,
more realistic worlds, and real-time natural-language steering of
generated scenes.

Genie 4 can maintain a consistent scene for up to two minutes, double the
limit of Genie 3, with improved physics and more humanlike characters.
The model reportedly uses Gemini 3.8 Flash under the hood.
