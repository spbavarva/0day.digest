Healthcare technology company Veradigm disclosed a patient data breach after
a ransomware gang claimed an attack tied to a third-party vendor incident.
Veradigm said access was limited to a specific interface and did not affect
its broader environment — networks, servers, or databases — and caused no
operational disruption.
