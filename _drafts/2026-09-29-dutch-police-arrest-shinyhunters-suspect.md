Dutch police arrested Pepijn van der Stap in connection with an
investigation into the ShinyHunters hacking group. Van der Stap was
previously convicted in 2023 for hacking multiple organizations, stealing
their data, and extorting victims.

ShinyHunters has been linked to a number of high-profile data theft and
extortion campaigns.
