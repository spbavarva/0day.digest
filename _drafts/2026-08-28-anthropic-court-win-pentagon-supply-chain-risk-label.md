A federal judge ruled that the Trump administration illegally labeled
Anthropic a supply-chain risk, handing the AI company its first court win
in a dispute with the Pentagon. Anthropic's second lawsuit against the
Pentagon is still ongoing in Washington. The ruling is a notable data point
for AI labs navigating U.S. government procurement and national-security
risk designations.
