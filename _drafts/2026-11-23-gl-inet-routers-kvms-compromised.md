Attackers compromised GL.iNet routers and KVM devices by exploiting a
flaw in GoodCloud, the remote management service used to administer them.

The flaw allowed attackers to push malware directly to affected devices.
Remediation requires flashing new firmware rather than a standard malware
removal process.
