Ireland's Data Protection Commission has received more than 40 breach
notifications from credit unions that used a Belfast-based company as a
data processor. The breach went unnoticed for six weeks before it was
disclosed.

The incident highlights the risk of concentrated third-party data
processing relationships: a single compromised supplier can cascade
breach notifications across dozens of otherwise-unrelated institutions.
