The Silver Fox threat actor is distributing the ValleyRAT backdoor
disguised as a signed Chinese adware application built around QN Wallpaper,
a legitimate desktop-wallpaper tool. Running under a trusted, signed
process lets the malware slip past users who have added the adware to
their antivirus exclusion lists. Kaspersky independently confirmed the
same ValleyRAT-as-adware campaign. Admins should avoid broad antivirus
exclusions for unverified signed adware.
