N-able disclosed that unknown threat actors gained unauthorized access to
a server in its Mail Assure and Spam Experts Hosted Cloud, forcing the
company to disable management interfaces and APIs across three regions.
N-able's tools are widely used by managed service providers to run email
security for downstream customers, so disruption at the vendor level has
knock-on effects for everyone relying on it. The investigation is ongoing
and N-able hasn't yet detailed what data, if any, was accessed.
