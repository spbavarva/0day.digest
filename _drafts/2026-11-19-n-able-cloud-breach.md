N-able disclosed that unknown threat actors gained unauthorized access to
a server in its Mail Assure and Spam Experts Hosted Cloud, disabling
management interfaces and APIs across three regions. The company says it
has taken steps to mitigate the threat and is hardening its services to
reduce the risk of recurrence. The investigation is ongoing, and N-able
has not yet detailed the scope of data accessed. N-able's products are
widely used by MSPs to manage customer email security.
