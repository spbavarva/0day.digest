Fortinet patched nearly 100 vulnerabilities in a single release, which
SecurityWeek attributes to the company integrating frontier AI models
into its product security testing. The available summary doesn't break
down severity or exploitation status for individual CVEs in the batch.

Fortinet customers should review the advisory and prioritize patching
based on their specific product exposure.
