Researchers report that suspected Kazakhstan-linked hackers used an AI
agent to help spy on Central Asian governments, steal sensitive documents,
and infiltrate critical infrastructure networks.

The AI agent reportedly assisted with reconnaissance and intrusion steps
rather than acting purely as a coding aid, echoing a broader trend of
state-linked actors folding autonomous AI tooling into espionage
operations against government and critical-infrastructure targets.
