Attackers used Hermes, an autonomous open-source AI agent tool, running in
unrestricted "YOLO mode" to conduct an espionage operation against
Thailand's Ministry of Finance. The incident is an example of AI agents
being used to drive real offensive operations rather than just assist a
human operator.
