Iran-linked hackers have expanded a campaign against US water utility
systems beyond the previously reported Minnesota incident, with Michigan,
South Dakota, and Georgia reportedly also targeted. The attacks target
public water system control infrastructure, continuing a pattern of
nation-state actors probing under-secured operational technology in the
water sector. Water utilities should verify remote access to ICS/SCADA
systems is not exposed to the internet and enforce MFA on remote management
interfaces.
