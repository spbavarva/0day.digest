TP-Link patched 15 vulnerabilities in the zero-touch provisioning (ZTP)
mechanism of its Omada network devices.

The flaws could be chained together with previously disclosed issues
to achieve remote code execution and breach affected networks.

Administrators running Omada ZTP-managed devices should apply the
available firmware updates as soon as possible.
