Recorded Future's Insikt Group has flagged a campaign, linked to Russian
state actor APT28, targeting government and diplomatic organizations in
Romania, Spain, and Türkiye between late September 2025 and early April
2026. The campaign deployed a previously undocumented backdoor dubbed
HOOKEDGE, a lightweight Windows batch script. It underscores continued
Russian state-linked targeting of European government and diplomatic
entities.
