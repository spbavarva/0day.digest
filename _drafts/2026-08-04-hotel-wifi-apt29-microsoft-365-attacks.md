Microsoft has linked a global campaign targeting hospitality Wi-Fi networks
to Midnight Blizzard (APT29), the Russian state-linked threat actor. The
group used custom malware delivered through hotel Wi-Fi infrastructure to
breach guests' Microsoft 365 accounts.

The campaign reflects APT29's continued focus on credential theft against
travelers and business users who connect through untrusted networks.
Travelers using hotel Wi-Fi should use a VPN and ensure MFA is enabled on
Microsoft 365 accounts.
