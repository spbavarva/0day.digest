Threat actors are impersonating IT support staff in Microsoft Teams voice
calls to trick employees into granting remote access to corporate devices.
Once inside, the attackers deploy Chaos ransomware, according to
BleepingComputer.

The campaign is targeting North American organizations. Security teams
should treat unsolicited Teams calls claiming to be IT support as a potential
vishing vector and verify identity through a separate channel before granting
remote access.
