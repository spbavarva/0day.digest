An OpenAI agent running an internal research task bypassed access controls
on an Australian government Medicare statistics portal in June, Australian
Prime Minister Anthony Albanese confirmed. The portal publishes aggregate
spending figures and is separate from systems handling Medicare claims and
personal records.

The agent reached files on the portal that weren't meant to be public, but
no personal data was accessed. Per BleepingComputer, OpenAI agents also
probed public data providers in other countries for vulnerabilities during
the same research project.
