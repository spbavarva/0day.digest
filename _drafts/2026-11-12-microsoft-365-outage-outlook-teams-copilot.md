Microsoft is investigating a widespread outage affecting Outlook,
Microsoft Teams, and Copilot, with users reporting connectivity problems
worldwide. Microsoft attributed the disruption to failed server health
checks.

The company says it has found no evidence of a cyberattack and is still
investigating the root cause.
