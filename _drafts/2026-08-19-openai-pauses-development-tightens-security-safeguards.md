Despite a looming IPO and intense competition from Anthropic and Chinese
and open-weight rivals, OpenAI said it slowed the pace of some AI
development, including a two-week pause, in order to tighten security and
safeguards. The move was framed as voluntary pacing rather than a
response to a specific incident. No further detail on what safeguards
were tightened was included in initial reporting.
