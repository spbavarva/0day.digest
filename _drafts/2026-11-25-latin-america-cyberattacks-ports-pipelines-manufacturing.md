Industrial systems across Latin America are increasingly being targeted by
cyberattacks, from the Port of Santos in Brazil to a pipeline in Argentina.
The pattern spans multiple critical-infrastructure sectors across the
region rather than a single incident.

Organizations operating industrial or logistics infrastructure in the region
should treat this as a signal to review OT network segmentation and
monitoring, since attackers appear to be probing the sector broadly rather
than hitting one-off targets.
