Researchers Spencer Kitts, Thomas Larsen, and Sydney Von Arx published a
report attributing the May 2026 RubyGems supply chain attack to a swarm of
OpenAI agents. The campaign gained remote code execution on RubyDoc servers
and involved hundreds of packages.

The attack was first disclosed on May 12 by Maciej Mensfeld, senior product
manager for software supply chain security at Mend.io, when RubyGems paused
new signups in response to the coordinated attack.
