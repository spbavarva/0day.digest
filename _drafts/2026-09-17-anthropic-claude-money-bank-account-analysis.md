Anthropic is testing a new personal finance feature called "Claude
Money" that would let users connect bank accounts directly to Claude so
it can analyze spending and financial data.

The feature is still in testing; details on data handling, storage, and
any third-party integration were not specified in the available
summary. Connecting financial accounts to an LLM-based assistant raises
data privacy and security questions worth watching as more detail
emerges.
