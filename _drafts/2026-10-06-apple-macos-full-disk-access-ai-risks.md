Apple plans to introduce additional controls around Full Disk Access in
macOS, citing growing risks posed by more capable and autonomous AI agents
operating on users' machines. The move reflects a broader shift among
platform vendors toward restricting what autonomous agents can touch by
default, rather than relying solely on user judgment at grant time. Specific
technical details of the new controls were not disclosed.
