Inherent, a British AI lab founded by DeepMind alumni, released an AI agent
called Faraday built to replicate published scientific papers. The company
says Faraday outperformed agents built on Anthropic and OpenAI models at this
task. The claim comes from Inherent itself, and the feed summary does not
include independent benchmark details or methodology. Worth watching as a
data point on agentic research-replication capability, but treat the
comparison claim as unverified until third-party evaluation surfaces.
