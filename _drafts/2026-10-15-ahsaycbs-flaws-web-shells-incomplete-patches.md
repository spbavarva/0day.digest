Ahsay Backup released updated fixes for two vulnerabilities in AhsayCBS,
CVE-2026-105133 and CVE-2026-105134, after its earlier patches proved
incomplete. The flaws can be chained to bypass authentication and execute
arbitrary commands, and have already been exploited in the wild to drop web
shells and XMRig cryptocurrency miners.

Organizations running AhsayCBS should apply the new fixes rather than rely
on the original patch set.
