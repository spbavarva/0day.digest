The China-linked Warlock ransomware group exploited SharePoint
vulnerabilities to gain initial access in attacks against a water
utility, a telecom provider, a regional government body, and a
university.

Organizations running on-prem SharePoint should confirm they are current
on available patches and review for signs of exploitation.
