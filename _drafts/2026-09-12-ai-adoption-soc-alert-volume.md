The Hacker News describes a fast-growing class of SOC alerts triggered
not by attacks on AI systems, but by the ordinary footprint of an
organization using AI: developers running coding agents and staff signing
up for consumer AI tools with corporate accounts.

The piece reports this alert category is growing faster than any other in
enterprise security operations centers, though no specific volume figures
were given in the summary.
