Google is rolling out Gemini 4 Carbon to the general public after
initially limiting access to a group of "cybersecurity defenders" two
weeks ago. The release includes a new agent "swarm" feature, and Google
is positioning Carbon as its strongest model yet, claiming it can compete
with the best systems from Anthropic and OpenAI.
