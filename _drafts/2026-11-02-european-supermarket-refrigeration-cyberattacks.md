Multiple European supermarket chains had refrigeration systems disrupted
after attackers remotely changed temperature settings on freezers and
cooling units, this week's incident marking the second such wave in recent
weeks.

The earlier incident also manipulated temperature controls and caused
stock to spoil. The intrusion method and the party responsible have not
been disclosed.
