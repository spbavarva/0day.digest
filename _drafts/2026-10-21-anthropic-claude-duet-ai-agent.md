Anthropic launched Claude Duet, a new AI agent positioned as a competitor to
Meta's Muse and OpenAI's Dots. The agent targets professionals and small
business owners and is launching as a research preview.

Claude Duet is available as a standalone app on iOS and Android, as well as
through Claude on the web.
