Anthropic proposed federal legislation that would allow a model's
release to be blocked if it could plausibly be used to kill 50 or more
people. As drafted, the bill would require a federal agency to obtain a
judge's permission before blocking a model's release on those grounds,
rather than letting an agency act unilaterally.

Critics note this structure could make it harder, not easier, for
agencies to intervene quickly despite the bill's safety framing. The
proposal has not been introduced as formal legislation; it remains a
policy pitch from Anthropic.
