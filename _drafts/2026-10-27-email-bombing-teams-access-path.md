Attackers are combining email bombing, impersonation, and external
Microsoft Teams chat invites into a repeatable initial-access technique.
The approach relies on overwhelming a target with spam email, then
reaching out via Teams posing as IT support to gain trust.

Attackers lean on legitimate domain infrastructure and trusted software
to avoid raising suspicion, and the pattern is reportedly being used to
scale Teams-based intrusions across multiple organizations. Security
teams should review whether external Teams communication is restricted or
monitored.
