Yandex confirmed a cloud service outage after drone attacks damaged three
of its data centers in Russia.

Two facilities were shut down entirely and a third was partially disabled,
cutting off user access to cloud files and other services.

The incident is part of an ongoing pattern of strikes on Yandex
infrastructure amid the war in Ukraine.
