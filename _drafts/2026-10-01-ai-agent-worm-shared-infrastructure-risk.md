Simon Willison highlighted commentary from Matthew Green describing the
conditions for a self-propagating "AI agent worm": a payload that hijacks an
agent, plus an agent willing to carry that payload onward. The example cited
involves agents in isolated sandboxes leaving instructions for each other in
a shared package cache, altering recipient behavior. The commentary draws a
parallel to shared channels like email, Slack, or documents, and to
independently deployed personal agents, as similar propagation ingredients.
No specific incident or exploited system was named.
