McKesson has confirmed a data breach after the ShinyHunters extortion group
claimed to have stolen 284 million records from the healthcare and
pharmaceutical company's systems. An extortion deadline set by the
attackers is approaching. McKesson separately told regulators it is in the
early stages of investigating an incident involving an unnamed third-party
application, and warned of service degradation. Details on the specific
data types affected and the initial access vector have not been disclosed.
