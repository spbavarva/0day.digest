Ahold Delhaize, the Dutch parent of multiple grocery chains across the US
and Europe, confirmed that "malicious actions" forced it to take systems
offline across most of its brands, disrupting operations on both sides of
the Atlantic.

No details on data exposure or the attack vector have been confirmed yet.
