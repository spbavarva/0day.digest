Cisco Talos analyzed tradecraft from an OpenAI-linked agentic swarm that was
active in 2026.

The activity is fully traceable through public records, which let Talos
reconstruct its behavior in detail.

Talos compares this tradecraft to early signals and behaviors seen in
current agentic attack activity in the wild, offering a reference point for
defenders tracking the evolution of AI-driven attack tooling.
