Cisco patched a remote code execution vulnerability in UCS Central
Software, and WatchGuard fixed authentication and man-in-the-middle flaws
in its products. SonicWall, by contrast, said it will not fix known
vulnerabilities in its GMS (Global Management System) product.

Organizations running SonicWall GMS should treat the unpatched flaws as a
reason to plan migration or compensating controls, since no fix is coming.
