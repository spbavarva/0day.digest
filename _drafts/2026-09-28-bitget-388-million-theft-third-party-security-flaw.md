Cryptocurrency exchange Bitget says an attacker stole approximately
$388 million by exploiting a vulnerability in a third-party security
product the exchange used, rather than a flaw in Bitget's own systems.

The attacker used the flaw to obtain high-level internal credentials,
then used them on September 24 to send fraudulent withdrawal commands to
Bitget's wallet system. Bitget has since resumed Bitcoin withdrawals.
