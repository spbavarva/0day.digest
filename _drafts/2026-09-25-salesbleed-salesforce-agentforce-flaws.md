Researchers disclosed three vulnerabilities in Salesforce Agentforce, dubbed
"SalesBleed," that let attackers hijack trusted AI agents for zero-click
data exfiltration.

The flaws allowed hackers to steal data and launch phishing attacks by
abusing the trust placed in Agentforce's automated agents, without requiring
any user interaction.

The source summary does not specify whether the flaws were exploited in the
wild before disclosure or Salesforce's remediation timeline.
