DeepSeek made its V4 Pro 0813 model available through OpenRouter, though the
company has not published an official announcement page for the release.
Open weights have not yet been confirmed, but both the April DeepSeek-V4-Pro
and July DeepSeek-V4-Flash-0731 releases eventually shipped open weights,
making a similar release plausible for V4 Pro 0813.

Early testing noted noticeably different outputs across the model's low,
medium, and high reasoning levels.
