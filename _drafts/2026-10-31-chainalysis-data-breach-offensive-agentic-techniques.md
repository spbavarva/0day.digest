Blockchain analytics firm Chainalysis disclosed that an external actor used
"offensive agentic techniques" to access internal tools this month, exposing
customer names, email addresses, user IDs, and organizational roles.

The company says the breach did not expose investigation content,
authentication credentials, or session tokens. No detail on the specific
agentic tooling or number of affected customers has been released.
