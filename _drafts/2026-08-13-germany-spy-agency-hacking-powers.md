Germany's cabinet approved legislation that would let its intelligence
agencies hack foreign systems, sabotage adversaries' supply chains, and
feed false information to extremists inside Germany.

The bill represents the biggest overhaul of the country's spy laws since
the postwar era, expanding offensive cyber authorities that were
previously far more restricted under German law.
