The Tor Project released an emergency security update for its relay
software to strengthen defenses against an ongoing distributed
denial-of-service attack. The project says the attack has overwhelmed
relays and affected most operators on the network.

No attribution or technical breakdown of the attack technique was
available at time of writing. Relay operators should apply the update
promptly given the attack is still active.
