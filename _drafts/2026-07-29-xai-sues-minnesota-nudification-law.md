xAI is suing Minnesota Attorney General Keith Ellison over a state law targeting "nudification" apps, arguing its punitive provisions violate the First Amendment. The company says the law leaves it "no practical choice" but to restrict image-editing features in Grok Imagine. The law was passed in May 2026 and broadly targets apps that can generate non-consensual explicit imagery. This is an ongoing legal dispute with no ruling yet reported.
