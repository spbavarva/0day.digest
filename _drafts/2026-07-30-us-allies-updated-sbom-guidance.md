The US and allied nations have published an updated Software Bill of Materials (SBOM) guidance document, five years after the original release. The refresh introduces new elements, removes outdated ones, and updates terminology to reflect how SBOM practices have matured. This is a compliance and tooling reference update rather than a response to a specific incident; organizations with SBOM programs should review the changes against their current processes.
