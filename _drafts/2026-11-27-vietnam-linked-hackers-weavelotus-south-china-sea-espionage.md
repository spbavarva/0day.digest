SOCRadar has attributed a previously undocumented espionage campaign,
tracked as WeaveLotus, to a Vietnam-linked threat actor. The group
targeted military, government, and legislative organizations across
Southeast Asia, seeking intelligence on South China Sea defense and
military agreements.

Researchers assessed at least five intrusions, including activity in
Thailand. No specific malware families or technical indicators were
disclosed in the initial report.
