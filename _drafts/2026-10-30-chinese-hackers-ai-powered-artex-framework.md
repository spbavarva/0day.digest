Chinese state-sponsored threat actors have been using a Go-based,
AI-powered penetration-testing framework called ARTEX in intrusions
against more than 200 organizations worldwide.

CrowdStrike attributes the activity to a China-based targeted-intrusion
group it tracks as Vapor Panda (formerly VexatiousTemper).

ARTEX reportedly automates parts of the reconnaissance and exploitation
workflow using AI, letting the group run simultaneous operations against
many high-value targets.

Further technical details on ARTEX's specific capabilities and the initial
access vector were not included in current reporting.
