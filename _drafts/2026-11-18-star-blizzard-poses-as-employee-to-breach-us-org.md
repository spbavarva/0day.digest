Russian state-backed group Star Blizzard weaponized data and identities
stolen from a prior victim in late 2025 to phish and compromise a separate US
organization, posing as an employee of the already-hacked firm.

The campaign shows the same stolen-identity chaining pattern seen in other
recent Russian APT activity: data from one breach is reused to add
credibility to attacks on new targets.
