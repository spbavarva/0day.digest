A GovAI research scholar warned that an AI hallucination nearly
triggered a US military operation, highlighting the risk of relying on
LLM output in high-stakes decision contexts.

The scholar emphasized that service members need to understand the
uncertainty inherent to LLMs before those systems are used to inform
operational decisions. Further technical detail on the specific
incident was not available in the source summary.
