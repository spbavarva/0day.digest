OpenAI's Sam Altman, Anthropic's Dario Amodei, Google DeepMind's
Demis Hassabis, and SpaceX's Elon Musk reportedly agreed over the
weekend to slow the pace of AI development, framing it as an effort
to "pace the frontier."

Critics have questioned whether the informal agreement is genuinely
safety-motivated or functions as coordination among dominant AI labs
that could reduce competitive pressure. The source summary did not
include specifics on what commitments, if any, were formalized.
