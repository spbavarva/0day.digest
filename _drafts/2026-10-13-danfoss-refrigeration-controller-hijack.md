Attackers took remote control of refrigeration controllers made by Danish
company Danfoss, warming fridges and freezers to defrost at hundreds of
French supermarkets on the night of October 11, forcing retailers to
discard food. The controllers are normally managed remotely by
third-party maintenance firms, and that remote-management access appears
to be how attackers reached them.

Retail chains affected include stores run by Les Mousquetaires. The
incident illustrates the physical-world impact of OT device compromise in
retail supply chains.
