CISA added two actively exploited flaws impacting WSO2 and Adobe Commerce /
Magento to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities (KEV) catalog, citing evidence
of active exploitation.

One is CVE-2026-5430 (CVSS 9.8), a path traversal vulnerability in the WSO2
API Control Plane. Details on the second flaw, affecting Adobe Commerce and
Magento, were not fully available in the source summary.

Federal agencies must remediate KEV-listed flaws by CISA's mandated deadline;
organizations running WSO2 or Adobe Commerce/Magento should prioritize
patching given confirmed in-the-wild exploitation.
