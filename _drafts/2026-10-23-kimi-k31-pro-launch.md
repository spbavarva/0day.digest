Moonshot AI released Kimi K3.1 Pro on its API, alongside a faster
"Highspeed" variant priced at roughly double the standard tier (input
token cache hits are priced the same across both).

Pricing is $2.00 per million input tokens and $10 per million output
tokens — matching GPT-6.1 Sol and roughly half the cost of Claude Opus
5.5. Moonshot's own benchmarks place Kimi K3.1 Pro near Opus 5.5 and
GPT-6 Astra, beating them on some evaluations. Full model weights are
promised by November 4, 2026; the model is not yet open-weight.
