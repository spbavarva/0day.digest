OpenAI released a new image classifier and added ChatGPT warnings as part
of a broader push to combat child sexual abuse material (CSAM). Under the
new system, ChatGPT will temporarily ban an account for 24 hours as a
warning if it detects an attempt to access CSAM content.

The move adds an automated enforcement step rather than relying solely on
manual moderation review. Details on the classifier's detection
methodology and false-positive handling were not disclosed.
