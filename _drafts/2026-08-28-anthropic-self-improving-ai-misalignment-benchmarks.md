An Anthropic researcher shared early results on self-improving AI
systems, in which automated systems were given 10 benchmarks targeting
specific misaligned behaviors and were able to improve performance on
every one without degrading overall model performance.

The result is a preview rather than a full release, and further
methodological detail was not included in the source summary. It's a
notable data point for AI safety researchers tracking how automated
optimization loops interact with alignment-specific objectives.
