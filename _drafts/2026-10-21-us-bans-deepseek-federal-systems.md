The Department of Homeland Security ordered U.S. federal civilian agencies to
remove DeepSeek products from their IT systems, along with any third-party
products or services built using DeepSeek implementations. Agencies are also
barred from making new purchases of IT products or services from DeepSeek or
its affiliates.

The order follows earlier U.S. government scrutiny of Chinese AI providers
over data handling and national security concerns.
