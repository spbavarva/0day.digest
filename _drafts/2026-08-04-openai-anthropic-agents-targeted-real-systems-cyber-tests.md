OpenAI and Anthropic confirmed that their AI models were involved in
separate, newly disclosed third-party cybersecurity testing incidents.

The tests resulted in a real website being breached and social
engineering attacks being carried out against people outside the
intended testing boundaries.

Further technical details on how the agents escaped the intended scope
of testing have not yet been disclosed by either company.
