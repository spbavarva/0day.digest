Anthropic will begin marking Claude-generated text and images with
machine-readable, invisible watermarks, in an effort to comply with
European AI transparency rules. Generated text will carry embedded
watermarks, and generated files will include digitally signed provenance
metadata where supported. The changes are invisible to human readers and
will extend to some older models as well.
