A new Android spyware campaign codenamed "Corp MDM" is targeting the
logistics sector. Attackers use fake Google Play pages branded as CEVA
and TKW Logistics to distribute an APK (package name com.corp.mdm)
disguised as a system service.

Once installed, the spyware steals SMS messages and redirects phone
calls. The campaign was surfaced via Have I Been Squatted.
