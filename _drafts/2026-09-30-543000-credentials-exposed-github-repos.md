A study found more than 543,000 credentials exposed in public GitHub
repositories remained valid and usable as of July, despite the platform's
existing controls meant to catch accidental secret leaks.

The credentials stayed live and functional, indicating GitHub's built-in
secret-scanning protections did not fully prevent or remediate the exposure.
The scale makes this one of the larger documented secret-sprawl findings tied
to a single platform this year.
