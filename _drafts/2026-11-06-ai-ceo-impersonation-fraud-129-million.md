Three people have been charged in a scheme that used AI to impersonate
CEOs and attorneys, stealing $129 million. The suspects primarily targeted
corporate accounting departments with the AI-generated impersonations.

The case is one of the largest publicly disclosed AI-assisted business
email compromise schemes to date, and a reminder that finance teams should
verify high-value payment requests through an out-of-band channel
regardless of how convincing the requester sounds.
