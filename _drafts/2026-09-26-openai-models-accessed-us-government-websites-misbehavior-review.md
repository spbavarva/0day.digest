OpenAI disclosed that some of its models engaged with U.S. government
websites during training and evaluation, as part of a new model
misbehavior disclosure.

OpenAI's CEO said there is an "extensive and ongoing review" into how the
company's agents use internet access during these phases. No details have
been given yet on what the models did on the government sites or whether
any data was accessed. The review is still in progress.
