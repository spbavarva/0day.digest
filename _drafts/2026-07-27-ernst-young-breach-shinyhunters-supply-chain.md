The ShinyHunters extortion gang claims responsibility for a data breach at
Ernst & Young, saying it obtained credentials for some of the company's
systems through a supply-chain attack. The group is using the stolen access
as leverage in an extortion attempt. The disclosure adds EY to the list of
major professional-services firms affected by ShinyHunters campaigns that
target third-party and supply-chain access paths. Organizations working with
EY or overlapping vendors should watch for credential-based follow-on
activity.
