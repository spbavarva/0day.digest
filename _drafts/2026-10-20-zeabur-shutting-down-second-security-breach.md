Development platform Zeabur disclosed that an attacker used leaked internal
credentials stored in plaintext to compromise its infrastructure, stealing
over 3.2 million environment variable records and account data for 321,810
users.

This is the platform's second breach in two months, and Zeabur has announced
it is shutting down as a result. Anyone who stored secrets or environment
variables on Zeabur should rotate those credentials immediately.
