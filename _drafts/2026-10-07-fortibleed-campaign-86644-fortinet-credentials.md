The FBI and U.S. Secret Service warned that the FortiBleed credential
harvesting campaign remains an active threat against internet-facing
Fortinet FortiGate firewalls and SSL VPN gateways.

The campaign exploits reused or leaked credentials combined with
FortiGate's legacy SHA-256 password storage, and has amassed credentials
for at least 86,644 devices.

Organizations running FortiGate firewalls or SSL VPN gateways should
rotate credentials, move off legacy password hashing where possible, and
watch for unusual VPN logins.
