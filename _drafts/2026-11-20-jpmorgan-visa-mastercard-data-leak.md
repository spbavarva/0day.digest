JPMorgan, Visa, and Mastercard have each pointed to third-party providers
after the N0n extortion group leaked data allegedly tied to their
customers.

None of the three has confirmed a direct breach of their own systems; all
three frame the leaked data as originating from a compromised third-party
vendor.
