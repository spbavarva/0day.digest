Apple patched an iMessage flaw that could let a remote attacker join a
group chat and receive its messages, plus a related bug that let newly
added group members read messages sent before they joined. The same
round of updates fixes separate Bluetooth pairing vulnerabilities.

The fixes also extended to older devices, including the iPhone XS, XS
Max, XR, and 7th-generation iPad. Users should update to the latest
iOS/iPadOS release.
