CISA added two vulnerabilities to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities
catalog based on evidence of active exploitation: CVE-2026-41709, a Broadcom
VMware ESX insufficient logging flaw, and CVE-2026-46300, a Linux kernel bug
involving missing initialization of a variable. Federal civilian executive
branch agencies must remediate per Binding Operational Directive 26-04;
other organizations running these products should prioritize patching.
