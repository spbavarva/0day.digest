Microsoft says the Russian state-sponsored group Midnight Blizzard has been
compromising hotel Wi-Fi networks worldwide to intercept and steal
travelers' Microsoft account credentials and deploy espionage malware on
connected devices. The campaign targets guests at hospitality venues, using
compromised gateway infrastructure to sit between travelers and the
internet. Travelers connecting to hotel Wi-Fi should use a VPN and treat MFA
prompts on unfamiliar networks with extra scrutiny.
