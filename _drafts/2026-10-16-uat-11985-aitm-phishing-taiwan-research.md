A spear-phishing campaign tracked as UAT-11985 has targeted individuals
affiliated with Taiwan research organizations using fake event invitations.

Victims are directed to an adversary-in-the-middle (AitM) phishing
framework designed to harvest Google account credentials and multi-factor
authentication codes.

The AitM technique lets attackers capture session tokens even when MFA is
enabled, bypassing that protection.
