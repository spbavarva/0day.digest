A preview of Anthropic's IPO prospectus reportedly discloses that the
company's AI development plans "could further increase the risk that our
models cause harm." The filing also reportedly details mounting losses and
leadership proposals to retain control post-IPO. Anthropic is said to be
targeting a valuation around $2 trillion. The disclosures are notable for
coming directly from a leading AI safety-focused lab as part of its own
public offering paperwork.
