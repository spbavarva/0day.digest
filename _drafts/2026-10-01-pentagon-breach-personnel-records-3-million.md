The Pentagon's Defense Manpower Data Center (DMDC) is notifying millions of
military service members that hackers stole their personal data.

The breach stems from an intrusion into the Pentagon's human resources
management system that occurred in October 2025.

Nearly 3 million people are affected, making this one of the largest
breaches of military personnel data on record.
