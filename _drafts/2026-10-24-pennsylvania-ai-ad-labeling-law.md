Pennsylvania Governor Josh Shapiro signed the Artificial Intelligence
Deceptive Advertising Act into law. Any ad — still image, video, audio,
or text — that has been significantly modified or generated by AI must
now carry a disclosure label in a specified format.

The law targets deceptive use of AI in advertising rather than AI tools
generally. It adds Pennsylvania to a growing list of states legislating
AI content disclosure requirements.
