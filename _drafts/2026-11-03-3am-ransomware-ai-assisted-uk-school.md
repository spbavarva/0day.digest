A 3AM ransomware affiliate breached a UK school estate from an
unmanaged host and successfully encrypted roughly 37 of nearly 50
reachable machines. Researchers reviewing the intrusion found evidence
suggesting a model may have generated and revised attack commands
during the breach.

Researchers stopped short of claiming the attack was fully automated.
If confirmed, it would be an early example of AI-assisted command
generation during a live ransomware intrusion.
