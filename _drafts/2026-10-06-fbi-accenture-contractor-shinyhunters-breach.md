The FBI has removed an Accenture contractor after attributing a breach that
exposed personal information of thousands of bureau employees to a missed
software patch. The breach has been linked to the ShinyHunters threat group.
The incident highlights third-party patch management as the root cause of a
breach affecting federal law enforcement personnel data, rather than a novel
exploitation technique.
