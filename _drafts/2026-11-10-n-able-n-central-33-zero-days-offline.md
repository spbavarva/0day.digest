A researcher publicly disclosed more than 30 vulnerabilities — collectively
tracked as Able2Shell — affecting N-able's N-central remote monitoring and
management platform, along with working proof-of-concept exploits. The
flaws could enable remote code execution, credential theft, and creation
of administrator accounts.

N-able took its N-central Hosted Environments offline as a precaution and
is urging on-premises customers to shut down their servers until further
notice. N-central is widely used by MSPs, so exposure extends to their
downstream clients.
