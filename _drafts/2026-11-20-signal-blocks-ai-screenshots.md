Signal is restricting screenshot capability on Android and iOS to prevent
AI agents built into mobile operating systems from reading users'
messages and on-screen data. Signal's president said agentic AI features
in Gemini and Siri that analyze on-screen content are "undermining
Signal's ability to independently guarantee privacy."

The move is a response to OS-level AI agents gaining the ability to read
screen content across apps, not a vulnerability in Signal itself.
