Researcher Simon Willison documented how Executive Order 14434, aimed
at "Super Intelligence," has disrupted the U.S. Census Bureau's
ability to report certain AI-adoption statistics it previously
published. He found the issue while trying to update an analysis of
corporate AI adoption trends using Census Bureau data.

The post raises concerns about AI-focused policy actions having
unintended effects on unrelated government data transparency.
