The XWorm remote access trojan has expanded its plugin ecosystem to
function as a full surveillance platform. Reported new capabilities
include location tracking, live audio streaming, and interaction with a
victim's authenticated browser sessions.

No indicators of compromise or distribution vector were included in the
available summary.
