The EU is standing up a new team in Brussels focused on cracking down on
AI-generated deepfakes, illicit imagery, and AI-assisted hacking. Once the
EU AI Act comes fully into force, AI companies will be required to clearly
label chatbot and AI-generated imagery outputs with disclosures or digital
watermarks.

No enforcement timeline or specific penalties were detailed in the
available summary. The move adds to the EU's broader AI Act compliance
push for companies operating in the bloc.
