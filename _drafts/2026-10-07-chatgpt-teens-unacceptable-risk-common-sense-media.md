Common Sense Media, a nonprofit that reviews apps and services for youth
safety, said OpenAI's ChatGPT for Teens — introduced in August — poses an
"unacceptable risk" to minors.

ChatGPT for Teens includes guardrails intended to support students, but the
nonprofit's review concluded those guardrails are insufficient.

Specific findings behind the "unacceptable risk" rating were not detailed
in the available summary.
