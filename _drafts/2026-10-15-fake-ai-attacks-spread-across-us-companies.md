ANY.RUN linked 1,258 public sandbox analyses to campaigns impersonating
popular AI products between April and October 2026, using fake tools to
distribute malware, steal browser sessions, and compromise business
accounts.

US organizations accounted for 16% of country-attributed targets, raising
operational pressure on MSSPs serving American businesses.

The campaigns rely on the popularity of AI branding rather than a novel
technical exploit, so standard anti-phishing and download-vetting controls
apply.
