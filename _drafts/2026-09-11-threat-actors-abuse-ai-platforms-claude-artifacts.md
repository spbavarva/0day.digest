Huntress research describes threat actors abusing legitimate AI platforms
to host malicious content and trick users into installing malware.
Observed techniques include weaponized Claude Artifacts, shared AI
conversation links, sponsored search placements, and ClickFix-style lures.
Because the hosting infrastructure sits on a trusted AI platform domain,
the content is more likely to bypass reputation-based filtering. Defenders
should treat unsolicited AI-platform share links with the same scrutiny as
unknown external links.
