French energy giant ENGIE confirmed that attackers stole customer data
after breaching a marketing platform used by its business partners. ENGIE
is one of the world's largest power and natural gas providers, meaning
the breach could affect a substantial customer base, though exact scope
wasn't confirmed in initial reporting.

The breach originated through third-party infrastructure rather than
ENGIE's core systems directly, another example of customer data exposure
stemming from third-party vendor platforms.
