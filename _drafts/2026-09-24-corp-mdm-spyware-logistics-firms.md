A new campaign is distributing Android spyware dubbed Corp MDM to
employees in the logistics sector, according to Have I Been Squatted.

The campaign uses fake Google Play pages branded to look like CEVA and
TKW Logistics to distribute an APK disguised as a system service, using
the package name com.corp.mdm.

Once installed, the spyware steals SMS messages and redirects phone
calls. Logistics-sector staff should avoid installing apps from links
outside the official Google Play Store.
