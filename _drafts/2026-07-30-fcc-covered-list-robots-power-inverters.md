The FCC added foreign-produced mobile robots and networked power inverters to its Covered List on July 28, citing cyber risk. The action generally blocks new models from receiving the equipment authorization required for import, marketing, or sale in the US. Previously authorized models can still be sold, and devices people already own are unaffected. The order reflects continued US regulatory scrutiny of network-connected hardware supply chains from adversarial nations.
