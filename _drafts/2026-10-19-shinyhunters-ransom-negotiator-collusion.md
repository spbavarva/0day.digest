U.S. federal investigators pursuing the ShinyHunters hacking group — which
recently stole highly sensitive data from the FBI — have identified
executives at two ransomware negotiation firms who maintained unusually
close relations with the extortion group.

Sources close to the investigation say one executive in the United States
helped ShinyHunters operate a website used to pressure victims into paying
a ransom, raising conflict-of-interest concerns within the ransomware
negotiation industry.
