Hitachi Energy disclosed that its APM Edge product, versions up to 6.10, is
affected by the "Dirty Frag" vulnerabilities (CVE-2026-43284,
CVE-2026-43500), rated 8.8 on CVSSv3.

Successful exploitation could impact the confidentiality, integrity, and
availability of the product. Hitachi has published recommended immediate
mitigation actions pending a full remediation.
