U.S. cybersecurity and intelligence agencies say six Chinese AI companies
have conducted industrial-scale distillation attacks against American
frontier AI models since at least late 2024, extracting outputs and
reasoning processes to train competing models. The activity is described as
"systematic extraction" that agencies say now forms the core of these
firms' AI development strategy.
