MITRE's ATT&CK v20 release adds 15 new detection strategies — six for
Enterprise and nine for ICS — backed by 38 analytics, with new coverage
spanning AI abuse, MFA bypass, and water utility attacks.

Defenders using ATT&CK-mapped detections should review the new analytics
for applicability to AI-enabled attack paths in their environment.
