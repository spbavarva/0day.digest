US authorities charged three suspects, now facing extradition from
Europe, in a CEO-impersonation fraud scheme that used AI to help steal
more than $100 million. The case highlights the growing use of AI
tooling to make business email compromise and executive-spoofing scams
more convincing.

Organizations should reinforce out-of-band verification for high-value
payment requests that claim to come from executives.
