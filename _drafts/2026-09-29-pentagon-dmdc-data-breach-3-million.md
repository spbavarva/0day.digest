A data breach at the Defense Manpower Data Center (DMDC), which maintains
personnel records for the U.S. Department of Defense, has affected roughly 3
million people. The DMDC handles sensitive personnel data across the
military and DoD-affiliated organizations, making the exposure notable for
scale and sensitivity. Further technical details on the breach vector were
not included in initial reporting. Affected individuals should watch for
follow-on phishing or identity-theft attempts referencing DoD affiliation.
