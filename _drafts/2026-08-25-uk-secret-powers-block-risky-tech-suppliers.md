The UK government is seeking new legal powers to ban specific technology
vendors from supplying companies in critical national sectors, with the
ability to do so in secret. The proposal is aimed at reducing supply chain
risk from suppliers considered a national security concern. Details on which
sectors, what threshold triggers a ban, and how affected companies would be
notified have not been finalized. The secrecy provision is likely to draw
scrutiny given its impact on vendor transparency and due process.
