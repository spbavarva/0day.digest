Threat actors are impersonating popular AI products to distribute malware,
steal browser sessions, and compromise business accounts, according to
ANY.RUN. The firm linked 1,258 public sandbox analyses to fake-AI campaigns
between April and October 2026, with US businesses accounting for 16% of
country-attributed targets.

The trend adds operational pressure on MSSPs, who need to distinguish
legitimate AI-tool traffic from lookalike malware droppers riding the same
branding.
