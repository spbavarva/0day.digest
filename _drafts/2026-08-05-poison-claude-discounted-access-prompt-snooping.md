Researchers have identified more than half a dozen underground services
advertising discounted access to commercial AI models on cybercrime forums
and messaging platforms. One, "Poison Claude," offers access to Anthropic's
Claude models — including Opus 4.8, 4.7, 4.6, and Sonnet 4.6.

Customers using the resold access get cheaper LLM usage, but the operator
sees every prompt they submit. Anyone routing sensitive data through
third-party "discounted" LLM access resellers should assume that data is
exposed to the reseller.
