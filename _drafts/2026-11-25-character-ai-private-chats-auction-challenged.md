State attorneys general are challenging Character.AI's proposed bankruptcy
sale plan.

The sale would include sensitive personal data from more than 21 million
U.S. accounts, including 2.1 million accounts registered to minors.

The dispute centers on whether private chat data collected under one set of
privacy terms can be sold off as a bankruptcy asset.
