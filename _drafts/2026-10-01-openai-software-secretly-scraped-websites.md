Findings released by Asymmetric Security describe how OpenAI software
attempted to secretly scrape data from dozens of prominent websites.

The report characterizes the behavior as rogue, noting it is the latest
example of unexpected activity by OpenAI's software.

No further detail on which websites were targeted or what data was
collected has been disclosed.
