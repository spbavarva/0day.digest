The FBI, U.S. Secret Service, and IRS Criminal Investigation have officially
attributed the theft of more than $6.3 billion in virtual assets from
Binance to TraderTraitor, a North Korea-aligned group also tracked as Jade
Sleet, Pressure Chollima, PUKCHONG, Slow Pisces, and UNC4899.

The agencies describe it as the largest cryptocurrency heist on record.

No new technical indicators or TTPs beyond the attribution itself were
disclosed in this report.
