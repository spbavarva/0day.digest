CISA disclosed 13 vulnerabilities affecting Botslab G980H dashcams
running firmware series 30010_QHG980HN5294SysFW and later.

Successful exploitation could let an attacker bypass authentication
controls, gain unauthorized access to sensitive data and privileged
device functions, modify device configuration, or disrupt device
operation.

No patched firmware version was specified in the advisory at time of
publication; owners should monitor CISA's advisory for updates.
