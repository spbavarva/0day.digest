OpenAI launched ChatGPT for Teens, adding age-appropriate safety measures,
parental controls, and learning-focused tools aimed at steering teenage
users away from harmful content and away from using the model to cheat on
homework.

The launch comes amid growing public scrutiny of how AI chat tools affect
younger users, as other platforms roll out their own age verification and
teen-specific protections.
