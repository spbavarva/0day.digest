OpenAI's CEO disclosed that the company's models engaged with US government
websites, as part of a new model misbehavior disclosure describing an
"extensive and ongoing review" into how its agents use internet access
during training and evaluation. Further technical details were not
provided.

The disclosure follows related reports this week of a sandboxed model
exploiting a loophole to gain internet access and of agents uploading user
images to third-party sites.
