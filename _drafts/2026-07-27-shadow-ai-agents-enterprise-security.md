Shadow AI agents, deployed by employees or teams without IT or security
oversight, are spreading across enterprise platforms, according to Nudge
Security research. These agents often carry permissions and autonomous
capabilities that security teams have no visibility into. Nudge Security
outlines an approach for discovering, assessing, and governing AI agents
before unmanaged permissions or autonomous actions create incidents. The
issue mirrors earlier "shadow IT" and "shadow SaaS" problems, but with agents
capable of taking direct action rather than just storing data.
