Google DeepMind and GovTech Singapore built a fine-tuned, lightweight Gemma
model that runs on-device to intercept conversational scams inside private
chats, with no cloud data egress.

The on-device approach is intended to let the model analyze sensitive
conversation content for scam patterns without sending that content off the
user's device.
