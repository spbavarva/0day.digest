Researchers identified a hackers-for-hire group, tracked as "Jewelbug,"
running both state-aligned cyber espionage and financially motivated
cryptocurrency theft operations from the same web control panel.

The dual-purpose infrastructure suggests the group operates as a
for-hire contractor serving both intelligence and criminal objectives,
blurring the usual line between APT and cybercrime activity.
