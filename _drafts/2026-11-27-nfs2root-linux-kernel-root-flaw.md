A 12-year-old critical vulnerability in the Linux kernel's NFS server,
tracked as CVE-2026-99362, allows remote attackers to gain root
privileges, per BleepingComputer. The flaw has reportedly gone unnoticed
for over a decade despite affecting a widely used kernel subsystem.

Patches should be applied to affected kernels as soon as they're
available. No confirmation of active exploitation was included in
initial reporting.
