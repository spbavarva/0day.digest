Dutch authorities confirmed the arrest of a 24-year-old man from Amsterdam in
connection with the ShinyHunters extortion group, which has been linked to
several high-profile data theft campaigns. The individual is expected to
appear before a Dutch court. Following the arrest, the FBI separately urged
remaining ShinyHunters members to turn themselves in. No new technical
indicators or victim details were disclosed alongside the arrest.
