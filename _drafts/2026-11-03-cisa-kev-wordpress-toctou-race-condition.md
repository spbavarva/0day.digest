CISA added CVE-2026-119977, a WordPress Core time-of-check
time-of-use (TOCTOU) race condition vulnerability, to its Known
Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog based on evidence of active
exploitation.

Federal civilian agencies are required to remediate under Binding
Operational Directive 26-04. WordPress site operators should check
their core version and apply the available fix given confirmed active
exploitation.
