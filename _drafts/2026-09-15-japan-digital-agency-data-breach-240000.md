Japan's Digital Agency disclosed a data breach affecting roughly
240,000 people after attackers exploited a vulnerability in a VPN
product to steal personal information.

The source summary did not specify the VPN vendor, the vulnerability
exploited, or the exact categories of data taken. No details on
remediation or notification timelines were included.
