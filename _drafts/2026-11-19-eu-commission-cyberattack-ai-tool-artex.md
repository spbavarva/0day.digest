The European Commission confirmed it was the target of a cyberattack,
with CERT-EU identifying the attackers' use of ARTEX, an open-source
AI-powered hacking platform. POLITICO had first reported the detail. The
FBI warned last week that ARTEX is being used both by cybercriminals and
state-sponsored groups. Scope of the compromise and any data impact have
not been disclosed.
