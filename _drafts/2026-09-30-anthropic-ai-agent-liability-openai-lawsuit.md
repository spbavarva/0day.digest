Anthropic has flagged unresolved liability questions around autonomous AI
agents as OpenAI faces a hacking-related lawsuit, highlighting that attacks
carried out by AI agents are now moving from research settings into actual
legal disputes.

The core open question is who is accountable when an AI agent takes harmful
autonomous action — the vendor, the deploying organization, or the agent's
operator — and existing liability frameworks don't cleanly answer it yet.
