A large-scale, industrialized campaign dubbed Trigram is impersonating
roughly 50 brands across the AI and blockchain sectors to trick developers
into running infostealer malware themselves, according to a technical
report from Stormshield. The campaign targets the AI, blockchain, and
logistics sectors across Europe and the U.S.

Developers who download tools, SDKs, or packages claiming to be from AI or
blockchain brands should verify sources carefully before execution, since
the campaign relies on developers self-executing malicious payloads rather
than exploiting a software vulnerability.
