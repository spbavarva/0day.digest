Infostealer logs have exposed AI account credentials and sessions tied
to more than 80,000 corporate domains, according to research from
SOCRadar covered by BleepingComputer.

The exposure spans shadow-AI usage within organizations to
"LLMjacking" — attackers using stolen credentials to access paid AI
services at the victim's expense. Risks include exposure of stored
conversations and unauthorized usage billed to legitimate accounts.
