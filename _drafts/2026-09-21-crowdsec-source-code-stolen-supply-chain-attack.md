Cybersecurity firm CrowdSec has confirmed that its source code was
stolen in a data breach.

CrowdSec attributes the incident to the May 2026 TanStack supply chain
attack, meaning the exposure traces back to a compromise further up
the dependency chain rather than a direct breach of CrowdSec's own
infrastructure.

Further detail on the scope of the stolen code and any downstream
impact was not included in the source summary.
