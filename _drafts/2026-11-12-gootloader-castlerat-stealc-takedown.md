Europol and law enforcement partners from nine countries seized hundreds of
servers and domains used to run the Gootloader, CastleRAT, and StealC
malware operations.

All three families have been widely used as initial-access tools, with
Gootloader known for SEO-poisoning campaigns that led to ransomware
deployment. The takedown disrupts shared infrastructure rather than
guaranteeing the operators are out of business, so a resurgence on new
infrastructure is possible.
