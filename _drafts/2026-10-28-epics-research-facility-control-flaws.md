Four long-hidden vulnerabilities in EPICS Base, the control software widely
used at particle accelerators and fusion research facilities, have been
patched.

The flaws, found by an autonomous vulnerability-discovery system, could let
an attacker crash control processes.

EPICS underpins instrumentation and control systems at research facilities
worldwide. Facility operators running EPICS Base should apply the patched
release.
