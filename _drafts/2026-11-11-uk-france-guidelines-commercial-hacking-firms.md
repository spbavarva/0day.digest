The UK and France published their first joint guidelines for commercial
cyber intrusion capability (CCIC) providers — firms that develop, sell, or
finance offensive hacking tools.

The guidelines call on these businesses to vet their customers, establish
technical safeguards against misuse, and maintain systems for monitoring
and reporting abuse.

This marks an early step toward formal government oversight of the
commercial offensive-security tool market.
