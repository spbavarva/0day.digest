Manchester Airports Group confirmed hackers breached its systems and
stole customer data, including Wi-Fi sign-up information, from
Manchester, Stansted, and East Midlands airports. A spokesperson told
the Yorkshire Post that roughly 8.7 million people were affected, though
no date range was given.

In the "vast majority" of cases, the only data accessed was an email
address.
