Broadcom disclosed a critical vulnerability (CVSS 9.8) in VMware vCenter
that lets a network-based attacker bypass authentication and gain
unauthorized access to the system. vCenter is the central management
console for ESXi hosts and virtual machines, making this a high-value
target. Fixed versions are available for all supported branches; Broadcom
lists no workaround. Patch as soon as possible.
