New research shows attackers can manipulate and hijack AI agents by feeding
them security alerts and blocked-event notifications, a technique dubbed
"GhostJacking."

The findings point to identity governance gaps specific to autonomous agent
deployments, where agents may trust or act on signals that would be
scrutinized more carefully if presented to a human operator.
