A phishing campaign tracked as Operation CloudFire used Google Calendar
invites, trusted redirect chains, and fake login pages to hijack corporate
Google sessions and bypass MFA protections. Material Security says it
blocked malicious messages and helped take down nearly 900 domains used by
the attackers.

The campaign relied on session hijacking rather than credential phishing
alone, which is why MFA didn't stop it.
