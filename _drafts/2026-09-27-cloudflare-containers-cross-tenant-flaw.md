Cloudflare has fixed a vulnerability in its Containers and Sandboxes
product that allowed customers on the Workers Paid plan to recover
residual data left behind by other customers' containers running on the
same physical host.

The flaw has been patched. Details on scope of exposure or whether it
was exploited before the fix are not yet available.
