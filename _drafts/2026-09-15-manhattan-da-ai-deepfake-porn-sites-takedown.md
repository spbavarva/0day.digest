Manhattan District Attorney Alvin Bragg announced the takedown of 12
websites that hosted AI-generated deepfake pornography using the faces and
bodies of real people, affecting more than 1,200 individuals. The sites
allowed users to generate illegal, non-consensual explicit content.

No arrests or additional enforcement details were included in the source
summary.
