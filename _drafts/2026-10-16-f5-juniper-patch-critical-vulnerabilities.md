F5 and Juniper Networks released patches for critical vulnerabilities in
their networking products this week.

Successful exploitation of some of the flaws could lead to remote code
execution and full device takeover, according to the vendors' advisories.

No active exploitation has been reported. Administrators running affected
F5 or Juniper Networks devices should apply the available patches as soon
as possible.
