The FBI, along with the U.S. Secret Service and IRS Criminal
Investigation, has formally attributed the theft of more than $6.43
billion in virtual assets from Binance to TraderTraitor, a North
Korea-aligned hacking group also tracked as Jade Sleet, Pressure
Chollima, PUKCHONG, Slow Pisces, and UNC4899.

The attribution marks one of the largest cryptocurrency heists on
record and underscores the group's continued focus on exchange
compromises to fund the North Korean regime. No new technical IOCs
were included in this disclosure.
