Southern Company is notifying customers that utility account information for
roughly 400,000 Georgia Power and Alabama Power accounts was accessed by
hackers.

The disclosure did not specify the attack vector or the exact categories of
data exposed beyond account information.

Affected customers should watch for phishing attempts referencing their
utility accounts and monitor for unauthorized account changes.
