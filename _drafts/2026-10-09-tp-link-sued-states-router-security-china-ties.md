Four more US states — Florida, Iowa, Montana, and Nebraska — sued router
maker TP-Link Systems on October 6, joining Texas, which filed suit in
February. That brings the total to five states suing the company.

The states allege TP-Link misled buyers about how secure its routers are and
how separate the company is from China. TP-Link denies the claims and says
it will fight them in court.
