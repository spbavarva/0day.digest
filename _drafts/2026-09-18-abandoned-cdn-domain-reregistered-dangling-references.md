A domain that once belonged to a now-defunct content delivery network was
allowed to expire and was re-registered by a new owner in July 2025.
Thousands of websites, code repositories, and documentation pages still
carry hard-coded references to hostnames beneath that domain, meaning the
new owner can potentially serve content to any client that still trusts
those old references.
