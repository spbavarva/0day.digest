CISA added two vulnerabilities to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities
catalog based on evidence of active exploitation: CVE-2026-48356, an
unrestricted file upload flaw in Adobe Commerce and Magento, and
CVE-2026-111497, an unchecked return value vulnerability in Google
Brotli.

Federal civilian agencies must remediate under Binding Operational
Directive 26-04. Brotli's broad use across web infrastructure means the
second flaw may have wide-reaching exposure. Organizations running Adobe
Commerce/Magento or software bundling Brotli should check for patches.
