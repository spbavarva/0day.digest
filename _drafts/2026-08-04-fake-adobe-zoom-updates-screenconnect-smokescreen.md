An active, multi-wave campaign tracked by Securonix Threat Labs as
SMOKE#SCREEN uses social engineering lures themed around Adobe and Zoom
software updates, business document reviews, and system maintenance
utilities.

The lures stealthily deploy Remote Monitoring and Management (RMM)
tools such as ConnectWise ScreenConnect to establish persistent remote
access. Defenders should watch for unsanctioned RMM installs and flag
update-themed phishing lures impersonating Adobe or Zoom.
