The US and Australia jointly released guidance on isolating operational
technology and supporting systems at critical infrastructure organizations,
according to SecurityWeek. The guidance outlines steps for isolating OT
networks and for operating in an isolated state for an extended period if
needed. It's aimed at critical infrastructure operators looking to limit
the blast radius of an IT-side compromise reaching OT environments. No
specific new vulnerability or incident prompted the release.
