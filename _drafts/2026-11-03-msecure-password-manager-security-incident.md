mSeven Software, maker of the mSecure password manager, emailed
customers on November 3 warning of suspicious activity linked to the
app and urging them to change passwords, reset two-factor
authentication, and remove stored passkeys.

The root cause of the suspicious activity and whether vault contents
were accessed were not specified in the available summary. mSecure
users should follow the vendor's guidance and rotate credentials
stored in the app as a precaution.
