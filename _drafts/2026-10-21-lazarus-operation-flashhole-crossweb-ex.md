Kaspersky's GReAT team documented Operation FlashHole, a complex campaign
attributed to the Lazarus group that combines a watering-hole technique
with abuse of CrossWeb EX, a financial security software product. The
operation deploys a sophisticated malware set against its targets.

Organizations using CrossWeb EX should review Kaspersky's full technical
writeup for IOCs and detection guidance.
