CISA issued a warning about a critical vulnerability in Danfoss AK-SM 8xxA
Series controllers, which food retailers use to manage commercial
refrigeration, HVAC, and lighting systems. No patch is currently available.

Operators running affected Danfoss controllers should review CISA's advisory
for mitigations and network segmentation guidance while a fix is pending,
given the equipment's role in food safety and building operations.
