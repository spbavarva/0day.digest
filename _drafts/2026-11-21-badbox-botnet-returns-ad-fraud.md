The BADBOX malware, found across a broad range of generic Android TV boxes,
has returned to large-scale ad-fraud operations following last year's
disruption efforts.

The botnet is now generating a record 40 billion ad requests per day,
according to reporting.

The resurgence suggests the earlier takedown did not fully dismantle the
infrastructure behind the operation.
