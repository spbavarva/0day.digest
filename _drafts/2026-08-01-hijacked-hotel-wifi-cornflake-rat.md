Microsoft reports that a fake browser update served over hijacked hotel
Wi-Fi has been used to deliver CornFlake, a remote access trojan capable of
capturing webcam images, microphone audio, and keystrokes.

The operation, tracked as CaptiveCrunch, is attributed to Storm-2945, which
Microsoft assesses as an operational sub-cluster of Midnight Blizzard.
Travelers connecting to hotel Wi-Fi should treat unexpected browser update
prompts as suspicious and verify updates through official channels.
