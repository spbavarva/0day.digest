Cisco Talos reports ransomware incidents in Japan rose 4.7% year-over-year
in the first half of 2026. The Gentlemen was the most active group, with
leak-site listings more than doubling between January and July, while
Qilin ranked second and showed evidence of using AI tooling in its
operations.

Small and mid-sized enterprises with capital under JPY 1 billion
accounted for roughly 80% of victims. Talos also investigated The
Gentlemen's infrastructure as part of the report.
