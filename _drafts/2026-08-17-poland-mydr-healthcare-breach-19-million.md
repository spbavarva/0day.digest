MyDr, a privately-owned Polish company that supplies software to doctors,
clinics, and other healthcare providers, disclosed a security incident
potentially affecting up to 19 million people. The company said it has
identified and removed the cause of the incident and introduced additional
security measures. Polish authorities are investigating.

No details on the attack vector, data types exposed, or a confirmed final
victim count have been disclosed.
