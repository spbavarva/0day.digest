Anthropic launched Claude Meetings, a feature that turns workplace
discussions into plans and tasks in real time. As participants talk,
Claude can draft the plan being discussed, map out a timeline on a shared
whiteboard, answer questions in the meeting chat, and pull information
from connected work apps, such as prior-year figures. The launch is part
of a broader push by Anthropic into AI-powered office and productivity
tooling, building on its existing enterprise and coding tool footprint.
