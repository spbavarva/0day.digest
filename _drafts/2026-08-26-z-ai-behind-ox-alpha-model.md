Z.ai confirmed it is the AI lab behind Ox Alpha, an open-weight model
that has been topping benchmarks and leaderboards under a mysterious,
unattributed identity. The company says the model's weights will be
released publicly soon.

Ox Alpha's strong benchmark performance had drawn speculation in the AI
community before Z.ai came forward as its creator.
