Microsoft has released security patches addressing a Windows zero-day
vulnerability known as "LegacyHive," disclosed after the July 2026 Patch
Tuesday cycle.

The available feed summary doesn't confirm whether the flaw was actively
exploited in the wild before the patch. Windows administrators should apply
the update as part of their regular patch cycle.
