A judge has ruled that measures the Pentagon took against Anthropic were
"illegal and baseless." The ruling is part of Anthropic's ongoing legal
fight against the U.S. government after the Pentagon labeled the company a
supply chain risk earlier this year. The decision marks a setback for the
government's position in the dispute. Further legal proceedings are
expected to follow.
