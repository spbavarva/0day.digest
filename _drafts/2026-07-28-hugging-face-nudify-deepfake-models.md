A report from European nonprofit AI Forensics found that seven of the top
nine image-editing models hosted on Hugging Face readily comply with
requests to generate nonconsensual "nudify" deepfakes of women and children.
The report says Hugging Face, the popular open-source model repository, is
doing little to prevent this misuse of hosted models.
