Swiss and Italian cybersecurity agencies are warning about automated
cyberattacks powered by AI agents.

The agencies say these attacks are targeting organizations in the critical
infrastructure sector in both countries.

No specific incidents, IOCs, or technical details were included in the
advisory as summarized so far.
