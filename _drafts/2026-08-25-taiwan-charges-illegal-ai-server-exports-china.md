Taiwanese prosecutors charged nine individuals, including staff from Nvidia
and Super Micro, over allegedly illegal exports of AI servers to China. AI
infrastructure — including advanced semiconductors mostly manufactured in
Taiwan — has become a central point of competition between the US and
China, and export control enforcement around AI hardware has been
tightening. Specific charges, export routes, and hardware involved weren't
detailed in the available summary. The case adds to a pattern of criminal
enforcement, not just administrative penalties, against AI hardware
smuggling.
