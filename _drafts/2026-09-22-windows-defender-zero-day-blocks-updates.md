Security researcher Abdelhamid Naceri (also known as Nightmare Eclipse)
released a proof-of-concept for a new Microsoft Defender zero-day that
blocks antivirus signature and engine updates.

With updates blocked, protected systems can fall out of date against
current threats without an obvious indicator to the user. No patch was
available at the time of disclosure.
