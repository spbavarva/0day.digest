MetaMask disclosed an ongoing security incident affecting part of its
infrastructure, saying it is working with external partners and security
advisors to remediate the issue. The company states it has identified no
immediate threat to user wallets. Some Ethereum validators associated with
MetaMask's infrastructure exited their positions as a precaution while the
incident is investigated. No technical detail on the nature, cause, or scope
of the incident has been disclosed.
