Arizona's court system disclosed that hackers stole personal information on
more than 1.3 million people.

The investigation found the attackers breached the Fines/Fees and
Restitution Enforcement (FARE) Program, a statewide system that helps courts
collect outstanding debts tied to traffic and criminal violations.

Given the scale and sensitivity of court-linked financial and legal data,
affected individuals should watch for follow-on phishing or identity-theft
attempts referencing court debts or fines.
