Cisco has patched a dozen critical vulnerabilities across its product
line. The flaws could allow unauthorized access, information
disclosure, privilege escalation, denial-of-service, and remote code
execution.

Administrators should prioritize patching Cisco devices given the range
and severity of the issues disclosed.
