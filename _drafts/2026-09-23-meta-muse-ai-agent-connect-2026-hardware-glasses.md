At Meta Connect 2026, Meta announced Muse agents will get their own email
addresses for completing tasks and gain video chat capability.

Meta also unveiled Muse Charm, a standalone wearable device dedicated to
the AI agent that resembles a chunky smartwatch without a strap. Muse is
additionally coming to Meta's smart glasses, letting users activate it by
voice for tasks like workout guidance.
