China's spy chief cited Anthropic's Claude Mythos and OpenAI's
GPT-5.5-Cyber by name as evidence of what he called a "disruptive upgrade"
in cyber capabilities, warning that these models increase the speed and
potential weaponization of vulnerability discovery and malware
development.

The statement is part of a broader geopolitical narrative rather than a
disclosure of a specific incident.
