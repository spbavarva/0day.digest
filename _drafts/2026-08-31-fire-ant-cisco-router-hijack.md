A China-nexus espionage actor tracked as Fire Ant has expanded a
long-running campaign beyond VMware hypervisors to compromise Cisco IOS XR
routers, TACACS authentication servers, and Linux management hosts.
Incident response firm Sygnia, which investigated the intrusion, said the
actor uses these footholds to route traffic, steal credentials, and blind
security logging on high-value networks. No specific CVEs or IOCs were
included in initial reporting.
