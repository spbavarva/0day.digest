OpenAI will embed six evaluators from Apollo Research inside the company
to investigate whether its AI models engage in subversive behavior. The
arrangement gives external safety researchers direct access to evaluate
model behavior rather than relying solely on OpenAI's internal red teams.

Restrictions on what the embedded researchers can access and disclose
publicly have raised questions about how independent their findings will
actually be.
