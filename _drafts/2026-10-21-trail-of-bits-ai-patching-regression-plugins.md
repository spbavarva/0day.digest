Trail of Bits published two new AI agent plugins, patching-bugs and
regression-hunt, built through its Patch the Planet initiative. The
patching-bugs plugin reproduces reported bugs, develops fixes, and tests
them; regression-hunt checks whether a change breaks previously working,
supported behavior.

Engineers direct the agents' work and review results, and maintainers can
supply their own security assumptions and patching requirements so the
agents operate within a project's specific rules.
