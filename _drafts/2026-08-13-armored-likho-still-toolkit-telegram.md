Kaspersky researchers detailed a new campaign by the Armored Likho threat
group, which poses as fundraising outreach to lure victims into running
malware. The campaign delivers an updated version of the group's Still
Toolkit, built to steal data from Telegram and eavesdrop on victims.

The fundraising lure suggests targeting of individuals or organizations
likely to engage with charitable appeals. The report does not disclose
victim scale or specific targeted sectors.
