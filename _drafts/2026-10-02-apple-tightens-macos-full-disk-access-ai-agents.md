Apple says it will add new controls around macOS's Full Disk Access
permission, warning that increasingly capable AI agents make broad
access to a user's files, messages, mail, and browsing history riskier.

The change is aimed at ensuring users who want to grant an app that
level of access can do so more deliberately.
