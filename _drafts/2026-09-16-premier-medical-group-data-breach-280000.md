Premier Medical Group disclosed a data breach affecting 280,000 people
after hackers accessed files in June 2026 containing patients' names,
contact information, diagnosis details, and health insurance
information.

The exposure of detailed medical and insurance data at this scale
creates meaningful identity-theft and medical-fraud risk for affected
patients.
