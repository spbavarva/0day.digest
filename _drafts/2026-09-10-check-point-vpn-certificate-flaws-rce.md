Check Point has patched two critical vulnerabilities, both rated CVSS 9.8,
in how its firewall and management products handle VPN certificates. One
affects Check Point Security Gateways; the other affects those gateways
along with associated management infrastructure.

The company says both flaws could allow an unauthenticated remote attacker
to execute code, but only "under specific conditions" it has not detailed.
No active exploitation has been reported. Affected customers should apply
the available patches.
