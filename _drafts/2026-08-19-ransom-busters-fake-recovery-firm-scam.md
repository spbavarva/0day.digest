A suspected ransomware affiliate is running a scam under the name
"Ransom Busters," posing as a legitimate ransomware recovery service.

The actor contacts victims before an attack becomes public, claiming it
can provide decryption keys and delete stolen data for a fee.

Because the outreach happens pre-disclosure, victims have limited ability
to verify legitimacy. Organizations hit by ransomware should independently
verify any recovery-service outreach through law enforcement or known
incident-response firms before paying.
