CISA added five security flaws to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities (KEV)
catalog after confirming abuse by Flax Typhoon, a China-linked threat
actor, and set an October 11 deadline for federal agencies to patch.

One of the five is CVE-2015-3306, a decade-old improper access control
vulnerability in ProFTPD (CVSS 10.0) that allows unauthenticated attackers
to read and write arbitrary files.

The inclusion of an 11-year-old flaw shows Flax Typhoon continues to find
value in unpatched legacy software on exposed infrastructure. Agencies
must remediate by the deadline under CISA's directive; other organizations
running affected software should prioritize patching too.
