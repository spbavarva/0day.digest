Researchers at Dream Research Labs disclosed a cyber espionage campaign run
by an Iran state-aligned threat actor, dubbed DarkBlinders, targeting
Israel and the Kurdistan Region of Iraq between August and October 2026.

The campaign compromised a high-profile individual in Israel along with a
government cloud environment belonging to the Kurdish regional government.
Further technical detail on the intrusion methods has not yet been
disclosed.
