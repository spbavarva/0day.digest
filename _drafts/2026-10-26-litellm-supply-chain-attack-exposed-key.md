LiteLLM, an open-source gateway widely used by AI developers, says an API
key exposed in May went unrevoked and was behind a supply-chain attack.

The attack is believed to have targeted tens of thousands of LiteLLM users
over the past week. Developers who use LiteLLM should rotate any
credentials tied to the exposed key and review logs for anomalous API
activity dating back to May.
