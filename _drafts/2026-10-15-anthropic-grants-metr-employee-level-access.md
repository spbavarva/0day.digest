Anthropic is giving researchers at METR employee-level access to its internal
operations, allowing METR to publish independent assessments of Anthropic's
risks, incidents, and safety practices.

Anthropic has reportedly given up the right to block METR's findings before
publication, making this one of the more significant external-oversight
commitments made by a frontier AI lab to date.
