Unit 42 detailed a campaign dubbed "Spring Ring" that combines voice
phishing (vishing) with abuse of Microsoft Teams to deploy malware inside
enterprise networks. The campaign specifically targets domain controllers
after gaining an initial foothold. The research outlines the
social-engineering pretext and delivery chain used to gain access.
Enterprises using Teams should review call/chat-based social engineering
defenses and monitor for anomalous domain controller access.
