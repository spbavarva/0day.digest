Researchers discovered critical vulnerabilities in Belgian eID software used
by roughly 2 million people, according to SecurityWeek. The affected
software is used by eight of Belgium's ten largest banks and more than 60
government agencies. No CVE identifiers, exploitation status, or patch
availability were included in the available summary. Given the software's
use in banking and government authentication, affected organizations should
prioritize confirming patch status.
