Critical vulnerabilities were discovered in Belgium's eID software, used by
roughly 2 million people. The affected software is deployed across eight of
Belgium's ten largest banks and more than 60 government agencies. No CVE
identifiers, exploitation details, or patch status were included in the
disclosure. Organizations relying on Belgian eID integrations should watch
for an official advisory and apply vendor guidance once available.
