India's CERT-In warned that threat actors are carrying out
AI-assisted cyberattacks against ICT assets in the country, resulting
in unauthorized financial transactions at banking and fintech firms
as well as technology service providers.

The advisory doesn't specify the AI techniques used or provide IOCs
beyond describing the activity as AI-assisted. It adds to a growing
pattern of AI tooling being used operationally in financial-fraud
campaigns rather than purely in research demonstrations.
