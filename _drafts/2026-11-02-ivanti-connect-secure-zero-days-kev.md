Ivanti released patches for two more Connect Secure remote access gateway
vulnerabilities, tracked as CVE-2026-111357 and CVE-2026-118527, that have
been chained together in zero-day attacks since at least October 1.

CISA has added both flaws to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog.
Organizations running Ivanti Connect Secure should apply the patches
immediately given the confirmed active exploitation.
