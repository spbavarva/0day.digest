Attackers built custom versions of ChatGPT (Custom GPTs) that impersonate
legitimate products to lure victims into a ClickFix-style attack. Users are
tricked into copying and executing PowerShell commands, a pattern that
typically leads to malware installation.

The abuse of a legitimate, trusted OpenAI feature to host convincing lures
lowers the bar for social-engineering campaigns that rely on victims running
attacker-supplied commands themselves.
