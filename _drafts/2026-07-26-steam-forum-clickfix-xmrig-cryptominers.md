Threat actors are abusing Steam discussion forums to run ClickFix attacks, posting fake "fixes" for game and computer problems that instead trick users into infecting their own devices. Victims who follow the instructions end up running commands that deploy the XMRig cryptominer. The campaign relies on social engineering rather than a software vulnerability, targeting gamers seeking troubleshooting help on official Steam forums.
