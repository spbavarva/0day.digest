Transport for London has disclosed that roughly 265,000 Oyster photocard
customers may have had account details exposed in a data breach. TfL has
not specified which data types were included beyond "account details," nor
named an attacker. This follows TfL's much larger breach disclosed in
2024, making this the second incident affecting the agency's customer data
in recent memory. Affected users should watch for phishing referencing
their TfL account.
