Texas has sued OpenAI under the state's deceptive trade practices law,
alleging the company deceived consumers about the safety of its products.

In an unusual move, the suit seeks to hold founder and CEO Sam Altman
personally liable, and asks for sweeping reforms and fines against OpenAI.
