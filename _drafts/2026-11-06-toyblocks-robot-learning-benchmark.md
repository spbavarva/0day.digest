Allen AI has released ToyBlocks, a controlled simulation environment built
for training and evaluating robot learning agents.

The environment is aimed at giving researchers a consistent, repeatable
setting to benchmark robot learning approaches rather than relying on
ad-hoc physical or simulated setups.
