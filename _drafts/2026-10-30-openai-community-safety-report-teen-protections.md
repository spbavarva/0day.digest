OpenAI published a community safety report alongside new protections aimed
at teen users of ChatGPT.

The update expands violence-prevention safeguards, adds earlier
intervention points when concerning behavior is detected, and commits
OpenAI to public safety reporting going forward.

The announcement doesn't include specific metrics on how many incidents
triggered interventions or how detection accuracy was measured.

This is a policy and safety-tooling update rather than a response to a
single disclosed incident.
