Bad actors are misusing Google Play's Early Access program, intended for
developers to solicit feedback on apps before general release, to
distribute thousands of deceptive Android apps that falsely promise money,
rewards, casino winnings, or premium content.

Because Early Access apps sidestep the standard listing review process, they
reach users with less scrutiny than fully published apps. Users should be
wary of Early Access apps promising financial rewards.
