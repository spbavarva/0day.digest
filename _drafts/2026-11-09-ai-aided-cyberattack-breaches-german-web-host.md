Hackers allegedly used AI assistance to breach German web hosting
company Mittwald through a tool designed to let customers connect AI
assistants to their accounts.

Mittwald disclosed the incident itself. Details on the exact exploitation
method and scope of customer impact have not been fully confirmed.

The case is a concrete example of an AI-assistant integration point
being abused as an attack vector, rather than a theoretical prompt
injection scenario — worth watching for providers building similar
assistant-to-account integrations.
