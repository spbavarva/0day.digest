US investigators raided 40 locations across Texas and seized 187 laptops as
part of a crackdown on North Korean IT workers using "laptop farms" to
disguise their true location while working remote jobs at US companies.

Two people were arrested in connection with the scheme. These operations
typically involve US-based facilitators running company-issued laptops so
North Korean workers can appear to be logging in domestically, funneling
salary income back to the regime while potentially providing insider access
to employer networks.
