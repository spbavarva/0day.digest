Attackers hijacked Microsoft's official X account, which has over 13
million followers, and used it to promote a Clippy-themed cryptocurrency
token in an apparent pump-and-dump scheme.

The incident highlights the continued value of large corporate social
accounts as a distribution channel for crypto scams.
