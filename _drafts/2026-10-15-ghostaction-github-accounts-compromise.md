The GhostAction credential-theft campaign has ramped up, targeting more than
30,000 repositories across roughly 3,000 GitHub users and organizations
after compromising around 1,900 GitHub accounts. GitGuardian observed a
spike in activity on October 8–9, 2026, tied to an attempt to extract 3,174
secrets from public repositories' CI configurations.

The campaign is a reminder that CI secrets committed or exposed in public
repos remain a high-value, frequently-hit target.
