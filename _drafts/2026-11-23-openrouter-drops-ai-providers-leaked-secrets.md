OpenRouter removed three AI providers from its platform after discovering
that they had violated the platform's data-handling policies and exposed
sensitive data, including prompts and secrets routed through their
endpoints.

The incident highlights the risk of trusting third-party AI inference
providers, since customers of AI aggregators may not control or audit how
downstream providers actually handle that traffic.
