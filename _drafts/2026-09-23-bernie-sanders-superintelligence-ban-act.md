Sen. Bernie Sanders (I-VT) and Rep. Greg Casar (D-TX) introduced the
Ban Artificial Superintelligence Act, which would ban development of
artificial superintelligence — defined in the bill as AI capable of
the "destruction or disempowerment of humanity," including
overthrowing government. Violators would face up to 20 years in
prison under the proposal.

The bill is a legislative proposal, not law, and its prospects are
unclear. It reflects growing congressional attention to AI safety and
catastrophic-risk framing in U.S. policy debates.
