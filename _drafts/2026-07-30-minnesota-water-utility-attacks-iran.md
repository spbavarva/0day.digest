A likely Iran-backed threat actor targeted more than 30 community water
systems in Minnesota, exploiting internet-exposed programmable logic
controllers (PLCs). It's part of a broader pattern of nation-state actors
probing under-resourced US water utilities that often run OT equipment
without basic network segmentation or authentication.

CISA has separately urged the water sector to lock down internet-facing
controllers in response. No disruption to water treatment or delivery has
been reported, but the incident underscores how exposed OT assets remain a
soft target for state-linked actors.
