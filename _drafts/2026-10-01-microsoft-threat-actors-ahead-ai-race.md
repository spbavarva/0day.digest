Microsoft says cyberattackers are currently benefiting from artificial
intelligence faster than defenders are.

The company reports that AI is helping threat actors speed up
vulnerability discovery, malware development, and post-compromise
activity, while security teams struggle to keep pace.

The report underscores a widening gap between offensive and defensive AI
adoption across the industry.
