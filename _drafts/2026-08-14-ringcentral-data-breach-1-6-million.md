The ShinyHunters extortion group stole personal information belonging to 1.6
million RingCentral accounts after breaching the company in July, according
to the breach notification service Have I Been Pwned. Exposed data reportedly
includes names, addresses, email addresses, and phone numbers.

RingCentral customers should watch for phishing attempts referencing their
account details and enable additional account protections where available.
