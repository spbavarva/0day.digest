A newly identified Android trojan, dubbed RatHat, relies on AI for
real-time device navigation and control. Instead of scripted automation,
the malware uses AI to interpret what's on screen and act accordingly.

This increases the trojan's adaptability across different apps and
device configurations, and makes its behavior harder to fingerprint
for evasion purposes.

Further technical detail on distribution and capabilities was not
included in the source summary.
