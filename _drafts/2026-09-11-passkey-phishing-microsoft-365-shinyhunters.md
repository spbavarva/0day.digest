Microsoft says threat actors linked to ShinyHunters, Helix, and other
extortion groups are running passkey- and SSO-themed social engineering
attacks against corporate Microsoft accounts. The lures impersonate
passkey enrollment or single sign-on prompts to trick users into handing
over credentials. Successful attacks have led to data theft from
Microsoft 365 services. Organizations should treat unexpected passkey/SSO
setup prompts as a phishing indicator and reinforce user awareness of the
technique.
