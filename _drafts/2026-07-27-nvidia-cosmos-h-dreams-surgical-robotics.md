NVIDIA has released Cosmos-H-Dreams, a generative simulation model aimed at
real-time simulation for surgical robotics. The model is part of NVIDIA's
Cosmos family focused on physical-world simulation and generative world
models. Limited technical detail beyond the surgical robotics use case was
available in the source summary. The release extends NVIDIA's generative
simulation work into a safety-critical robotics domain.
