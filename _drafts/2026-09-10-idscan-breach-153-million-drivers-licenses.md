Identity verification company IDScan has confirmed that hackers accessed
customer data stored in its cloud platform, days after reports linked the
company to a database containing more than 153 million driver's license
scans. IDScan has not yet detailed the exact number of individuals affected
or the root cause of the exposure.

Organizations relying on IDScan for identity verification should watch for
further disclosure from the company and treat any driver's license data
processed through its platform as potentially exposed.
