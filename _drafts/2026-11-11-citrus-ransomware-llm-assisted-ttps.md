Kaspersky's GReAT team breaks down the Citrus ransomware group's TTPs
and tooling, including use of the VLESS protocol for traffic tunneling
and a tool called Sn00py for encryption. The group also reportedly uses
LLMs to assist with aspects of its operations, and researchers link
Citrus to the Toy Ghouls threat cluster.

The research provides tooling detail useful for detection engineering
against this ransomware family.
