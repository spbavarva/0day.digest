Kaspersky's GReAT team detailed a ransomware operation branded
AuditTeam that frames its extortion as a "forced audit" consulting
engagement rather than a traditional ransom demand, using an implant
called OpsAgent that the group has rewritten in Rust.

Instead of a standard ransom note, the extortion model demands victims
pay a "consulting fee" in exchange for deletion of stolen data,
repackaging double-extortion tactics in business-sounding language.

The Rust rewrite of OpsAgent suggests the group is actively investing
in evading detection and improving operational resilience rather than
relying on an off-the-shelf toolkit.
