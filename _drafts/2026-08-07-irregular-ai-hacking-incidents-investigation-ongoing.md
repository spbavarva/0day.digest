Irregular, the AI security firm investigating recent AI hacking incidents
tied to Anthropic, OpenAI, and Meta's models, said its investigation is
ongoing. A spokesperson declined to confirm whether additional incidents
beyond those already reported have occurred, citing the ongoing nature of
the probe. No new technical details about the original incidents were
disclosed.
