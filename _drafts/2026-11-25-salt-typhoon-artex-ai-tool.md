Microsoft says an AI tool called Artex, already implicated in several
high-profile breaches, is being rapidly integrated into the hacking suites
of state-sponsored groups.

China's Salt Typhoon and North Korean groups are among those adopting the
tool, according to Microsoft.

This fits a broader trend of AI tooling being folded into established
nation-state hacking workflows rather than replacing them outright.
