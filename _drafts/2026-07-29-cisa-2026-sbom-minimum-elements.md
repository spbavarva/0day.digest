CISA, the NSA, FBI, and international partners released updated 2026
guidance on the Minimum Elements for a Software Bill of Materials,
replacing the framework NTIA published in 2021. The update incorporates
feedback from a 2025 public comment period and reflects current SBOM
tooling and practitioner needs while keeping the original document's core
principles. An SBOM serves as an inventory of a software product's
components, intended to help organizations track and respond to
vulnerabilities in their dependencies.
