Russian cybersecurity firm Solar reported that a Belarus-aligned
hacktivist group, tracked as Partisan Zmiy (aka Cyber Partisans),
maintained access inside a Russian healthcare organization's network for
nearly two years. The group concealed a backdoor, dubbed Vasilek, inside
VMware Tools to maintain persistence while evading detection.

Partisan Zmiy has been active since at least 2020 and has previously
targeted industrial organizations. Using legitimate VMware tooling as a
backdoor host is a reminder to monitor trusted infrastructure components
for tampering, not just unfamiliar binaries.
