Google warned that threat actors are increasingly relying on agentic AI
workflows to automate vulnerability discovery and exploitation. These
AI-based offensive frameworks are accelerating attacks toward machine speed,
compressing the time between disclosure and exploitation.

The warning follows separate reports of similar agentic frameworks, such as
Artex, already being adopted by state-linked threat actors.
