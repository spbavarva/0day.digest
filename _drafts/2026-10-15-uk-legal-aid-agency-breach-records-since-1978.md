The UK Legal Aid Agency, which manages the Ministry of Justice's Public
Defender Service, confirmed attackers who breached its IT systems in April
2025 stole records dating back to 1978. A later forensic review found the
stolen data was both older and broader than originally disclosed, covering
legal aid applications, claims, and payments submitted mostly between 2007
and 2025.

The agency has not disclosed a total affected-individual count.
