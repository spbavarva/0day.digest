OpenAI unveiled GPT-6 Cyber, a model it says discovered 81 previously
unknown high-severity Firefox vulnerabilities and developed working
exploits for 948 distinct Linux kernel flaws. OpenAI rates the model
Critical for cybersecurity capability and says it beats the prior
GPT-5.6 Cyber at exploitation while staying in scope more reliably.

The release underscores the growing use of frontier models for both
vulnerability discovery and exploit development.
