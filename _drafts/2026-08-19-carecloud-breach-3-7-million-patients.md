Healthcare software firm CareCloud filed documents with the U.S.
Department of Health and Human Services confirming that 3,756,469
individuals had information exposed in a breach the company says occurred
earlier this year.

A hacker reportedly spent roughly eight hours inside one of CareCloud's
electronic health record environments during the intrusion.

Affected individuals should watch for official HHS-mandated breach
notification letters and be alert to phishing attempts referencing their
healthcare provider or CareCloud.
