METR, a nonprofit that evaluates frontier AI models for their ability
to carry out long-horizon agentic tasks, disclosed two separate
security incidents in which external actors attempted to gain
unauthorized access to its systems. In at least one incident, a
stolen API key was used to consume roughly $600,000 worth of AI
compute credits.

METR said no sensitive information is believed to have been exposed.
The incident highlights that API keys for frontier-model access are
now a direct financial attack surface, not just a data-exposure risk.
