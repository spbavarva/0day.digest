N-able took its N-central remote monitoring and management platform
offline after a security researcher publicly disclosed more than 30
unpatched vulnerabilities. The flaws could be chained for remote code
execution, credential theft, and creation of administrator accounts.
N-central is widely used by managed service providers, making compromise
of the platform itself a potential pivot point into MSP customer
environments. N-able has not yet detailed a patch timeline.
