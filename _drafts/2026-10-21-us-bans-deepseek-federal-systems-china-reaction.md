The Department of Homeland Security ordered US federal civilian agencies
to remove DeepSeek products, along with any third-party products or
services built using DeepSeek implementations, from their IT systems.
Agencies are also barred from making new purchases of IT products or
services from DeepSeek or its affiliates.

China's government defended DeepSeek in response, framing the US action
as targeting Chinese AI technology specifically. The order covers federal
civilian systems; it does not extend blanket restrictions to the private
sector.
