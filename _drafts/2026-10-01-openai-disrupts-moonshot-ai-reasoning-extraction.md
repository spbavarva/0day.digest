OpenAI said it identified and disrupted a coordinated distillation campaign
attempting to extract protected reasoning output from its AI models. The
company traced a core cluster of the activity back to early July and
attributed it to individuals associated with Moonshot AI, a Beijing-based AI
company, without naming specific individuals or detailing further evidence.
The disclosure adds to a pattern of AI labs flagging attempts to extract or
distill proprietary model behavior via API access.
