The Wikimedia Foundation is staking out a middle position in a group
of landmark AI copyright cases, including litigation involving OpenAI
and The New York Times.

Its argument: courts should legally protect large language models
training on copyrighted work, but should not extend that same
protection to chatbots that regurgitate that copyrighted work back to
users verbatim.

Wikimedia warns that an overly black-and-white ruling in either
direction could either deprive Wikipedia-style projects of useful
AI-based tools, or make it easier for AI products to launder
copyrighted content without consequence.
