Fortinet FortiGuard Labs has disclosed a long-standing supply chain attack
on QuickFox, a VPN and network acceleration tool designed for overseas
Chinese users. The attack has reportedly been ongoing since at least August
2025.

A trojanized version of the QuickFox Windows installer delivers a backdoor
codenamed FDMTP. Users of QuickFox should verify installer integrity and
treat any existing installation as potentially compromised.
