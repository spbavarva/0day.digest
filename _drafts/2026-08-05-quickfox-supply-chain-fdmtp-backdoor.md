Researchers disclosed a long-standing supply chain attack on QuickFox,
a VPN and network acceleration tool aimed at overseas Chinese users.

Fortinet FortiGuard Labs reports the campaign has trojanized the
Windows installer to deliver the FDMTP backdoor, and has been ongoing
since at least August 2025.

Users of QuickFox should verify installer integrity and treat any
existing installation as potentially compromised pending further
guidance from the vendor.
