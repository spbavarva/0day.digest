OpenAI says it disrupted a coordinated campaign attempting to extract its
models' protected reasoning through adversarial distillation, and is
strengthening defenses against similar extraction attempts going forward.

The company did not name the actors behind the campaign or provide technical
indicators in this post, framing it primarily as an IP-protection and
platform-integrity measure.
