Researchers identified 319 Chrome extensions, with a combined 13.4 million
reported installs, redirecting users' searches through tracking servers to
generate advertising revenue from Bing and Yahoo. The campaign shows
continued abuse of the Chrome Web Store's trust model at significant scale.

Users running affected extensions should remove them and review browser
extension permissions more broadly, since the hijacking behavior was not
disclosed at install time.
