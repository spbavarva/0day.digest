Unit 42 found attackers using Cairn, an open-source AI agent framework, to
autonomously carry out intrusions against targets in the semiconductor,
defense, and civil society sectors.

Rather than using AI as a coding assistant, the operators let the agent
plan and execute steps of the attack chain with limited human direction.
This marks a shift toward AI-driven autonomous intrusion against
high-value, security-sensitive sectors and is worth watching for defenders
building detections around agentic behavior.
