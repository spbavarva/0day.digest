Independent researcher Ben Zimmermann scanned 7.25 million public
repositories on Gitee, a Chinese code-hosting service, and found 26,400
credentials that were still valid when tested, reported October 28. Most
were keys for cloud and AI services. Anyone who copied an exposed key from
public code could have used it, underscoring the ongoing risk of secrets
committed to public repos.
