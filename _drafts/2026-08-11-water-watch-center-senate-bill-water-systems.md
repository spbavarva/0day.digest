A new Water Watch Center, launched at DEF CON, aims to help
under-resourced water utilities defend against cyberattacks. The
initiative coincides with a new Senate bill intended to boost
cybersecurity funding and support for U.S. water systems, a sector
regulators have flagged as under-protected against nation-state and
criminal threats.
