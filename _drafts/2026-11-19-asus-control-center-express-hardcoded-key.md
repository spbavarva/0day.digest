ASUS released Control Center Express v1.7.29 to patch three critical
vulnerabilities, including a hard-coded cryptographic key that could let a
remote attacker gain administrative control of the management console and
the devices it manages. Control Center Express is used by IT admins to
manage fleets of ASUS business PCs and servers, so compromise of the
console is a path to broader network access. No workaround exists —
affected organizations should update immediately. ASUS has not said
whether the flaws are being actively exploited.
