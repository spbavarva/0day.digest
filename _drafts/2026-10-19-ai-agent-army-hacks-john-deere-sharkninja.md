AI security firm Norforge found that a hacker broke into more than 100
websites using a largely autonomous army of AI agents. Norforge did not
name the targets, but Reuters reported the victims included John Deere,
SharkNinja, and Cummins.

The incident adds to growing concern that AI agents can meaningfully lower
the skill and effort required to run breaches at scale, with most of the
operation reportedly requiring limited human intervention.
