Accenture reports that attackers exploited zero-day flaws in Mitel
MiCollab before Mitel issued its advisory, in what the firm suspects was
an AI-assisted extortion campaign.

The suspected AI assistance reportedly helped attackers identify or
weaponize the flaws faster than typical manual exploit development.

Exact AI involvement and specific CVEs weren't detailed in this report;
related MiCollab vulnerabilities were separately added to CISA's KEV
catalog this week. Organizations running MiCollab should apply Mitel's
advisory guidance and check for signs of pre-disclosure compromise.
