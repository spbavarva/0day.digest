The US Department of Homeland Security is ordering federal civilian
agencies to stop using DeepSeek products and remove them from government
systems, citing national security concerns.

The order extends a broader federal push against Chinese AI tools. Agencies
and contractors with federal ties should audit for DeepSeek usage, including
instances embedded via third-party tools.
