Since mid-August 2026, a phishing operation has been stealing live Google
sessions from corporate users using calendar invites paired with live
proxy infrastructure designed to pass standard link-reputation checks.

Material Security's Threat Research team, which calls the effort Operation
CloudFire, has taken down hundreds of attacker domains tied to the
campaign. The technique is notable for evading the checks defenders
typically rely on to judge whether a link is malicious.
