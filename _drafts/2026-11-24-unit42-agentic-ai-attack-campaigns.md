Palo Alto Networks' Unit 42 tracked campaigns in which attackers deployed
autonomous AI agents to automate multi-platform intrusions.

The agents relied on standard, off-the-shelf tooling but left distinct
operational traces that researchers say defenders can use to detect
agent-driven activity.
