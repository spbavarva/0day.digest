Threat actors breached TeamUpdraft's website and gained unauthorized access
to customer connection keys for UpdraftCentral, UpdraftVault, and
UpdraftClone, then used those keys to plant backdoors on WordPress sites.
TeamUpdraft develops UpdraftPlus, a WordPress backup plugin with more than
four million active installations.

Sites managed through UpdraftCentral should rotate connection keys and check
for unauthorized plugin or file changes.
