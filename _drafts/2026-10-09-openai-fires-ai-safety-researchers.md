OpenAI is defending its decision to fire three safety researchers —
Jasmine Wang, Tomek Korbak, and Mikita Balesni — after an internal
investigation found they committed what the company called "a significant
breach of trust."

In a post on X, OpenAI said the researchers were dismissed for violating
clear policies on handling sensitive information, without detailing what
information was involved or how it was mishandled.

The episode adds to ongoing friction between OpenAI and parts of its
safety research community, though the company hasn't disclosed further
specifics publicly.
