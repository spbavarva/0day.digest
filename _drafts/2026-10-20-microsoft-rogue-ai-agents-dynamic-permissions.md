Microsoft said companies should treat AI agents as insiders, and is rolling
out controls that grant agents access per task, tightening permissions as
an agent's calculated risk score rises.

The approach aims to contain the blast radius of agents that go rogue or
get hijacked mid-task, extending the broader industry push toward treating
autonomous agents as a distinct identity and access management category.
