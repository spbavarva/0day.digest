Huntress observed threat actors abusing OpenAI's Custom GPTs feature,
disguising malicious GPTs as legitimate product offerings that funnel victims
to fake sites using ClickFix-style lures to deliver a remote access trojan.

This is the latest case of attackers weaponizing a trusted AI platform's own
feature set to add credibility to social-engineering lures, following prior
abuse of other shared-content mechanisms on AI platforms.
