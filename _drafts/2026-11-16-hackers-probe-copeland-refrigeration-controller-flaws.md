VulnCheck disclosed 14 vulnerabilities affecting Copeland XWEB EVO
refrigeration controllers, and threat actors have already begun probing
two of them shortly after disclosure.

The rapid attacker turnaround underscores how quickly newly published ICS
vulnerability details get weaponized, especially for internet-exposed
industrial controllers. Operators running XWEB EVO controllers should
check internet exposure and prioritize patching or network isolation for
the two actively probed flaws.
