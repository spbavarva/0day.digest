A new infostealer dubbed "Aokigahara" targets developer keyrings alongside
AI coding assistants. The malware's focus on AI tooling extends the
traditional infostealer playbook, pulling AI assistant session data,
sensitive business data, and developer infrastructure credentials from a
single compromised endpoint.

This expands the blast radius of an infostealer infection beyond browser
credentials into active AI agent sessions and developer secrets.
Developers using AI coding assistants should treat endpoint compromise as
a risk to connected AI session tokens, not just traditional credentials.
