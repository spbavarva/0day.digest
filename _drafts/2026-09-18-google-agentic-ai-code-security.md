Google described new AI-native agentic methods it has built to embed
high-precision, pervasive vulnerability scanning and patching directly
into its software development lifecycle. The approach continuously scans
every code change across hundreds of millions of lines of Google's
codebase, aiming to keep pace with AI-accelerated development while
managing emerging AI-based vulnerability classes.
