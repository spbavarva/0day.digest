Two separate cybersecurity reports detail China-linked hacking operations
targeting AI companies and Asian governments.

One campaign used phishing emails that impersonated Western experts to
deliver a backdoor against Taiwanese targets.

The reports add to a growing body of evidence that AI firms are becoming
priority targets for nation-state espionage.
