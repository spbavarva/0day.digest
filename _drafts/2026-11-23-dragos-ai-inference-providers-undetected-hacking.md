Dragos said it uncovered undetected intrusions at three AI inference
providers after analyzing nearly two million prompts sent to their
services, including instances of prompt theft.

The finding suggests inference providers' monitoring isn't catching
intrusions involving exfiltration of prompts and model interactions —
a blind spot distinct from traditional network or endpoint detection.
Organizations using third-party inference providers for sensitive
workloads should ask what detection coverage exists at the inference
layer itself.
