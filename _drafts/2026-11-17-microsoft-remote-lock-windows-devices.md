Microsoft is testing a new feature that lets enterprise IT administrators
remotely lock a lost or stolen Windows device without wiping it. The
feature gives admins a middle option between leaving a missing device
untouched and triggering a full remote wipe, reducing data-loss risk on
devices that are later recovered.

No release timeline was given in the available summary.
