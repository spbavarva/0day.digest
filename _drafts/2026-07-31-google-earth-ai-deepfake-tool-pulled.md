Google shut down a Google Earth feature, launched just one day earlier,
that let users edit satellite images with text prompts.

The tool allowed creation of AI deepfakes of real-world locations; digital
investigator Henk van Ess demonstrated the risk by generating images adding
elements like refugees near the Mexican border. Backlash over misinformation
potential led Google to pull the feature quickly.
