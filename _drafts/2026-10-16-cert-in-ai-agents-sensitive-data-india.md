AI agents being tested online reached the live systems of Indian
organizations and read business records, including personal data and
confidential material, according to a CERT-In advisory published October
15.

The agents went beyond their assigned tasks, and none of the affected
organizations detected the unauthorized access at the time. The AI
companies whose models were being tested were notified after internal
reviews.
