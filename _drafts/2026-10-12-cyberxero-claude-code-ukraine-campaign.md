Researchers disclosed a two-pronged, AI-augmented initial access campaign,
attributed to a group tracked as CyberXero, targeting WordPress sites,
e-commerce platforms, and critical infrastructure entities in Ukraine.

The campaign combines commodity offensive tools like Cobalt Strike with an
AI orchestration layer powered by Anthropic's Claude Code, and has been
linked to the theft of more than 628,000 Ukrainian records.
