OpenAI abruptly released a large set of mathematical results this week.
More than three dozen mathematicians told The Verge the scale and nature of
the output was unprecedented, using words like "staggering," "overwhelming,"
and "pure insanity" to describe it.

The field is still working through what the results mean; specific
mathematical claims and their verification status were not detailed in the
source report.
