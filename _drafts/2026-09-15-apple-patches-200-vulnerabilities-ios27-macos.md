Apple has released iOS 27 and macOS Golden Gate 27, resolving roughly 200
vulnerabilities including kernel flaws that could lead to memory
corruption, privilege escalation, system termination, and information
leaks.

The source summary did not indicate whether any of the fixed
vulnerabilities are under active exploitation. Users should update as
soon as the release is available for their devices.
