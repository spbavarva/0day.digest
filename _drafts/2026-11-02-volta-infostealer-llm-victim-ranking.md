Researchers discovered a new Windows infostealer, VOLTA-AUTODOXER (VOLTA),
that queries large language models including Claude, Gemini, and Kimi to
rank stolen victim data by monetization value.

Like other stealer families, VOLTA harvests passwords and cookies from
Chromium-based browsers along with data from Outlook profiles and Slack
workspaces.
