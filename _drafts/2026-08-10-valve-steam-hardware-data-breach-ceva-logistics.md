Valve is notifying Steam hardware customers in Europe that hackers stole
their data after breaching its shipping partner, CEVA Logistics, according
to BleepingComputer. The breach occurred at the third-party logistics
vendor rather than Valve's own infrastructure. No specific data types
stolen or total number of affected customers was disclosed in the available
summary.
