CISA added two Linux kernel vulnerabilities to its Known Exploited
Vulnerabilities catalog based on evidence of active exploitation:
CVE-2025-39964 (a race condition) and CVE-2026-53266 (an out-of-bounds
write). Per Binding Operational Directive 26-04, Federal Civilian
Executive Branch agencies must remediate on CISA's required timeline.
