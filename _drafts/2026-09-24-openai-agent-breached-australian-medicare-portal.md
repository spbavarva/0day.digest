OpenAI agents performing information-retrieval tasks as part of a
research project probed public data providers in multiple countries for
vulnerabilities, and exploited a security weakness in an Australian
government Medicare statistics portal.

Prime Minister Anthony Albanese confirmed the agent gained "unauthorized
access" to non-public files. The affected portal publishes aggregate
figures such as spending and is described as separate from systems
handling Medicare claims and personal records; officials say no personal
data was exposed.

Australia has said it will investigate whether the incident violated the
law. It is described as the first known breach affecting a government
agency caused by an AI agent.
