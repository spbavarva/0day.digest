UK fashion retailer ASOS confirmed a data breach after attackers sent
unauthorized "HACKED" push notifications through its mobile app. The
attackers claim to have stolen customer data from ASOS's Snowflake
environment. ASOS has not yet detailed the scope of exposed data or
confirmed how many customers are affected. The incident follows a pattern
seen in other recent breaches tied to compromised Snowflake customer
environments.
