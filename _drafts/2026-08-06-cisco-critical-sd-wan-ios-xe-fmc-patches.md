Cisco released patches for two dozen vulnerabilities across SD-WAN, IOS XE,
and Firepower Management Center (FMC), including at least one flaw with
public proof-of-concept code available.

Public PoC code significantly lowers the bar for exploitation and increases
the likelihood of opportunistic scanning against unpatched devices.

Administrators running affected Cisco products should prioritize patching,
especially on internet-facing management interfaces.
