Microsoft launched MAI-Cyber-1-Flash, its first cybersecurity-specific AI
model, integrated into MDASH, its multi-model vulnerability identification
and remediation harness. Microsoft says the MAI-Cyber-1-Flash plus GPT-5.4
configuration scores 95.95% on the CyberGym benchmark at roughly half the
cost of its prior best MDASH configuration (GPT-5.4, GPT-5.4 mini, and
GPT-5.3 Codex). Access to the new model is currently limited to approved
partners.
