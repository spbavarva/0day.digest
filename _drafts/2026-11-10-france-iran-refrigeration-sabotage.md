The French government attributed an October cyberattack on refrigeration
controllers at roughly 700 French retail sites to a cyber unit of Iran's
Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps. Attackers broke into the controllers
on the night of October 11 and altered defrost settings to run at high
temperatures, causing food losses and, in at least 15 cases, fires. The
incident is a rare confirmed case of nation-state OT sabotage producing
physical consequences at commercial retail scale.
