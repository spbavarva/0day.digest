The US Senate passed a bipartisan bill aimed at strengthening cybersecurity
in the healthcare sector. The legislation follows a year in which more than
730 cyber breaches affected over 270 million Americans, at an average cost
of $10 million per breach.

Specific provisions of the bill were not detailed in the available summary.
