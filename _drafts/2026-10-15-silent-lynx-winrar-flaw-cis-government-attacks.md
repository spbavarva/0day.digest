A threat actor tracked as Silent Lynx has targeted government organizations
across the Commonwealth of Independent States in a campaign running from
June 2025 through August 2026. The group uses four distinct infection chains
involving macro-enabled documents, PS2EXE downloaders, GitHub-hosted
PowerShell payloads, and crafted WinRAR archives exploiting a WinRAR flaw to
deploy reverse shells.

The multi-chain approach suggests an actor with resources to maintain
several parallel TTP sets against the same target set.
