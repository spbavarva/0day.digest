CISA and the FBI say the SafePay ransomware gang has breached more than 600
critical infrastructure organizations worldwide since 2024. The joint
advisory flags SafePay as one of the more prolific ransomware operations
currently targeting critical infrastructure sectors.

Organizations in critical infrastructure should review the joint advisory for
SafePay-associated indicators and prioritize patching and segmentation
controls accordingly.
