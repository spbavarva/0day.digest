Attackers breached AI inference provider Fireworks AI and accessed chat
transcripts belonging to Notion customers who used Notion's AI features
built on Fireworks' infrastructure.

SecurityWeek reports the exposure affected fewer than 1% of Notion
workspaces, and most of the exposed transcripts could not be attributed to
specific users. This is Fireworks' second disclosed breach in as many
months, underscoring the data-exposure risk of relying on third-party
inference providers for AI features that touch customer content.
