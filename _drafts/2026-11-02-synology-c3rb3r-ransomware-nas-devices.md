Synology is warning customers of a surge in C3RB3R ransomware attacks
targeting its network-attached storage devices and is urging
immediate updates. The advisory does not specify the initial access
vector or exploited vulnerability in the available summary.

NAS devices are a frequent ransomware target because they're often
internet-exposed and hold bulk backup data. Synology NAS
administrators should apply the latest firmware and review internet
exposure of management interfaces.
