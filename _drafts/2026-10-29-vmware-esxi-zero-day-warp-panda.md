CISA added CVE-2026-41709, a Broadcom VMware ESX logging flaw, to its
Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog.

CrowdStrike revealed that the China-nexus group "Warp Panda" weaponized
the flaw in attacks targeting the U.S. legal sector. The vulnerability
lets an attacker with administrative privileges perform operations
without being logged, reducing audit trail visibility. Federal agencies
are required to patch per CISA's KEV deadline; other VMware ESX
operators should prioritize patching too.
