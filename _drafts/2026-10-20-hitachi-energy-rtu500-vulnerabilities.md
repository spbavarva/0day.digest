CISA published an advisory on vulnerabilities affecting Hitachi Energy's
RTU500 series remote terminal units, used in energy-sector control systems.

The flaws can impact confidentiality, integrity, and availability of
affected devices across multiple firmware version ranges. Asset owners
running RTU500 series equipment should consult the advisory for affected
firmware versions and apply Hitachi Energy's remediation guidance.
