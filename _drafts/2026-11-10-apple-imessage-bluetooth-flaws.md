Apple patched an iMessage flaw that could let a remote attacker join a
group chat and receive its messages, along with a separate flaw that
could let someone added to a group access messages sent before they
joined. The company also released updates fixing Bluetooth pairing
vulnerabilities, plus additional updates for older devices including the
iPhone XS, XS Max, XR, and the 7th-generation iPad. Users should install
the latest iOS and iPadOS updates.
