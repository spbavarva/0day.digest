Following the ouster of Nvidia's CEO over a scandal involving a suspected
Chinese spy, lawmakers from both parties in Congress are pushing to tighten
export restrictions on AI chips. Several bills are reportedly being floated,
and Senate leadership has demanded that Nvidia executives testify.

No legislation has passed yet, and the scope of any new restrictions remains
undefined.
