The North Korean threat actor known as Jade Sleet has been linked to
the compromise of a smaller India-based IT services organization.

The intrusion involved two backdoors, tracked as FLATROOF and
ROOFDECK. SentinelOne, which disclosed the activity, said it involved
use of Apple-related tooling.

The campaign continues a DPRK pattern of targeting developers as a
route to breaching downstream target networks.
