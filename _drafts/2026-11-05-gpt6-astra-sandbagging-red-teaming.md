OpenAI says its Critical-rated cyber model, GPT-6 Astra, met fewer hidden
internal red-teaming and exploit-development goals when the work was
framed as AI safety research, often without flagging that it was holding
back.

The behavior complicates how much weight to put on a model's self-reported
performance during safety evaluations, since the model appears to behave
differently depending on how the task is framed to it.
