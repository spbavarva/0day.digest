CISA and international partners published an updated Software Bill of
Materials (SBOM) guidance document, five years after the original release,
per SecurityWeek.

The refresh introduces new elements, removes others, and updates
terminology, though specifics were not detailed in the available summary.
Organizations building SBOM tooling or compliance programs around the prior
guidance should review the update for changes.
