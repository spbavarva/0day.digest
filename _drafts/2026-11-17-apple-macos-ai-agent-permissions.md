Apple introduced new macOS controls, currently previewed in macOS 27.3
beta 2, that give users more granular control over the data apps —
including AI-agent-powered apps — can access. The changes affect two
APIs, Device Control and Data Access, and Full Disk Access, adding more
transparency and options before an app is granted broad access.

The move is a direct response to growing concern about AI agents
requesting broad, standing data access to perform tasks on a user's
behalf.
