Cisco Talos' Q2 2026 incident response trends report found a significant
increase in phishing as an initial access vector, alongside growing
weaponization of legitimate remote management tools during attack chains.
The findings are based on Talos IR engagements over the quarter. Defenders
should tighten monitoring around both phishing delivery and unauthorized
use of RMM software in their environments.
