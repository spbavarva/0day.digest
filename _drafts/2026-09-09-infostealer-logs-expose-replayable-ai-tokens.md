Cybercriminals are hijacking AI accounts via information stealer logs,
turning harvested credentials and session tokens into "stolen keys" that
grant illicit access to tools from model providers including Google and
Anthropic. Infostealers such as Lumma Stealer and Vidar harvest credentials,
session tokens, and API keys from compromised systems, and replaying these
tokens can bypass MFA protections on the underlying accounts.
