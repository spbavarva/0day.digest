Revolut disclosed that attackers had access to customer information for
roughly five months, targeting 680 high-profile accounts. The attackers
reportedly impersonated an Italian government agency to extract the data
and later demanded a $3 million ransom.

The report does not specify the initial access method or whether the
ransom was paid. Given the high-profile nature of the targeted accounts,
affected users should watch for follow-on social-engineering attempts
referencing the leaked data.
