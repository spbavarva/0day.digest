Safe Superintelligence (SSI), the AI lab founded by former OpenAI chief
scientist Ilya Sutskever, has announced a long-term partnership with Nvidia
to scale its research after roughly two years operating in stealth. The
announcement marks SSI's first major public disclosure of its infrastructure
strategy since founding. No model release, benchmark, or safety research
details were shared alongside it. The deal signals SSI moving from pure
research stealth toward large-scale compute deployment.
