Researchers linked the long-running Help TDS traffic distribution system to
Hotejka, a Russian-language affiliate program believed to be a rebrand of the
ROI777 traffic-monetization operation active since at least 2017. The
operation offers PHP code templates that partners install on compromised
websites to redirect visitors into scam funnels.

More than 20,000 Cloudflare Pages domains were found hosting tech support
scam pages tied to the network, illustrating how free hosting tiers on major
platforms get abused at scale.
