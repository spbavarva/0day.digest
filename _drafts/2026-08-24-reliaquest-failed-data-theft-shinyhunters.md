Cybersecurity company ReliaQuest confirmed that one of its employees was
targeted in a social engineering attack, with the attackers impersonating
a member of ReliaQuest's own security team.

The attempted data theft failed. The incident is notable mainly for the
tactic: attackers going after a cybersecurity vendor itself by posing as
its internal security staff.

Security teams should reinforce verification procedures for internal
security-team requests, especially ones involving credentials or account
access.
