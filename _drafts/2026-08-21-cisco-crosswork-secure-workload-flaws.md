Cisco published another round of security updates for its Crosswork and
Secure Workload platforms as part of an ongoing internal security review,
patching nine vulnerabilities — five of which score a maximum CVSS 10.0.
Four of the flaws affect Crosswork Data Gateway, Crosswork Network
Controller, and Crosswork Planning regardless of device configuration.
Admins running these platforms should prioritize patching over routine
maintenance cycles given the severity scores involved.
