Fortinet patched critical, unauthenticated bugs in FortiMonitorOnSight and
its Chrome extension that allow attackers to bypass authentication and proxy
a user's browser traffic. No active exploitation was reported. Organizations
using FortiMonitorOnSight or its browser extension should update to the
patched versions.
