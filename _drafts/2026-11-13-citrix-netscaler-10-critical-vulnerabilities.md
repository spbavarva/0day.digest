Citrix released patches for 10 critical vulnerabilities in NetScaler, a
product line that has repeatedly been targeted by attackers in the past
(including CitrixBleed). Available detail indicates successful exploitation
could lead to unexpected behavior or denial-of-service conditions.

Given NetScaler's exploitation history, administrators should apply these
patches promptly rather than waiting for confirmed in-the-wild exploitation.
