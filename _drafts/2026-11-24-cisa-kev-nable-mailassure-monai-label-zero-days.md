CISA added two vulnerabilities to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog
after confirming active exploitation: CVE-2026-134624, a CVSS 9.3
server-side request forgery flaw in N-able MailAssure's LDAP integration,
and a zero-day in MONAI Label that is reportedly being exploited by AI
agents.

Federal agencies must remediate both flaws per CISA's KEV deadlines. Any
organization running N-able MailAssure or MONAI Label should prioritize
patching and check for signs of compromise.
