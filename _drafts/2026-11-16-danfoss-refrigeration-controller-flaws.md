Dutch security consultancy Midnight Blue disclosed six unpatched
vulnerabilities in Danfoss AK-SM800A refrigeration controllers that could let
a remote, unauthenticated attacker fully take over a device. The newest
available firmware, version 5.4.0, does not fix the flaws, and no patch is
currently available.

Government cybersecurity agencies in Belgium, Ireland, and Germany have told
operators to take action. Refrigeration controllers are widely used in
commercial and industrial cold-chain settings, making this a notable OT
exposure.
