Cisco Talos uncovered a cluster of activity it tracks as UAT-11587,
targeting government and policy organizations across Asia, including
Taiwan, India, the Philippines, and Cambodia.

The group delivers a previously undocumented backdoor Talos calls
"Antino," a name drawn from a string found in the malware's developer
artifacts.
