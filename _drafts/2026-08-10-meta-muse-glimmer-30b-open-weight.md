Meta released Muse Glimmer, a 30B-parameter open-weight model licensed under
Apache 2.0 — a shift from the more restrictive Llama license terms used for
prior releases.

The model is positioned for agentic task completion and tool use, with
reported results on full-task benchmarks including DeepSearch QA,
MCP-Atlas, τ-Bench, and SWE-Bench.
