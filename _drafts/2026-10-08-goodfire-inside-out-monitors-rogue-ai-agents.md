AI safety startup Goodfire has launched "inside-out" monitors designed
to catch rogue AI agents at a fraction of the cost of existing
approaches.

Rather than using a second AI model to review everything an agent does,
the monitors inspect the model's internals while it works and only
escalate to a backup reviewer when something looks suspicious.
