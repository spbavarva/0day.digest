The threat actor known as Blind Eagle has resurfaced with a campaign
targeting organizations in Latin America using judicial and
administrative-themed phishing lures, including fake court notifications,
fiscalía notices, and tutela proceedings written in Portuguese and
Spanish. The lures deliver a remote access trojan that shares lineage
with AsyncRAT and DCRat. Organizations in the region, particularly those
handling legal or government correspondence, should be alert to
unsolicited court-themed email attachments.
