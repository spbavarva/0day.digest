Security researchers set up a fake cryptocurrency startup, advertised
developer jobs, and hired three people they believe were North Korean IT
operatives working under false identities, recording activity on every
issued virtual machine. One hire claimed to live in Pasadena, Texas, and
submitted a California driver's license and a New York bank account as
onboarding documentation. The research illustrates the vetting gaps hiring
teams can exploit to detect this type of fraud.
