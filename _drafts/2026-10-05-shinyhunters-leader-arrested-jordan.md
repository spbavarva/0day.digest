A suspect known as "Rey," alleged to be a leader of the ShinyHunters
extortion group, was arrested in Jordan. The suspect is reportedly
cooperating with the FBI to help identify and locate other members of
the group. ShinyHunters has been tied to a long list of high-profile
data breaches and extortion campaigns over the past year. This is a
law-enforcement development rather than a new technical disclosure.
