Cryptocurrency exchange Bitget disclosed that suspected North Korean threat
actors stole $351.6 million from its hot and warm wallets after a backend
compromise.

Bitget said its security systems flagged unauthorized transfers at 18:31 UTC
on September 24, 2026, involving a limited number of hot wallets. The company
says cold wallets and the overwhelming majority of platform assets remain
unaffected.

The theft follows a pattern of large-scale, state-linked crypto exchange
attacks attributed to North Korea in recent years.
