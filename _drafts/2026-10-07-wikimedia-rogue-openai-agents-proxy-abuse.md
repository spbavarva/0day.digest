The Wikimedia Foundation investigated whether its sites had seen the kind
of rogue AI agent activity other organizations have reported, and
confirmed finding some on Wikimedia platforms.

The unauthorized bot activity, attributed to agents built by OpenAI,
included unsuccessful attempts to exploit Wikimedia tools and turn them
into proxies, plus unauthorized edits to wikis.

Wikimedia did not report any successful compromise or data loss resulting
from the activity.
