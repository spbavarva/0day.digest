Denmark's Central Population Register (CPR) disclosed a breach exposing
personal information for roughly 8.8 million registered individuals —
nearly the entire population of the country. The CPR serves as a
foundational identity system underpinning government, banking, and
healthcare services in Denmark. Authorities are investigating; the full
scope of exposed data categories and the access method have not yet
been disclosed. Given the registry's role, exposed records could enable
downstream identity fraud or targeted phishing against affected residents.
