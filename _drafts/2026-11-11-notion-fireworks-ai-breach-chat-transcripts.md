Notion disclosed that a security breach at Fireworks AI, a third-party
AI infrastructure provider it uses, exposed Notion AI chat transcripts
in a small number of workspaces. Notion has not disclosed further
technical detail about the breach at Fireworks itself.

The incident is a reminder of the supply-chain risk introduced when
products embed AI features backed by external inference providers.
Customers affected should watch for Notion's direct notifications about
whether their workspace was among those impacted.
