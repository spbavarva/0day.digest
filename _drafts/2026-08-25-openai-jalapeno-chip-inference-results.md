OpenAI published benchmark results for Jalapeño, its custom inference chip,
claiming faster and more power-efficient AI inference with higher throughput
and lower latency compared to currently available state-of-the-art hardware.
The results were reportedly tested on SemiAnalysis' InferenceX benchmark,
and OpenAI hardware VP Richard Ho said the chip delivers both lower latency
and higher throughput rather than forcing a tradeoff between them. This is
OpenAI's first public benchmark disclosure for in-house inference silicon,
signaling a move toward vertical integration of compute for serving its
models. No availability timeline or deployment scale was included in the
available summary.
