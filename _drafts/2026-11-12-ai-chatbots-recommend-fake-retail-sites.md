A study by IP protection firm Podqi, shared with The Verge, found that
shoppers using AI chatbots to research products or find places to buy
items can be directed to imposter sites and other risky websites. The
researchers tested ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity with shopping-related
prompts.

The findings highlight a growing risk as more consumers rely on AI
chatbots instead of search engines for purchasing decisions.
