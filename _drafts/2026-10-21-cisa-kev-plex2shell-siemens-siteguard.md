CISA added four actively exploited vulnerabilities to its Known Exploited
Vulnerabilities catalog, including a set of Plex Media Server flaws
collectively tracked as Plex2Shell. One of the disclosed CVEs,
CVE-2026-96652 (CVSS 5.3), is a server-side request forgery (SSRF) in
Plex Media Server. The addition also covers vulnerabilities in Siemens
and SiteGuard products.

Federal civilian agencies must patch KEV-listed flaws by CISA's mandated
deadline; any organization running Plex Media Server, Siemens, or
SiteGuard products should prioritize patching given confirmed in-the-wild
exploitation.
