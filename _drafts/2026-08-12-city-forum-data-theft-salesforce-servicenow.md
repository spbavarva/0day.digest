An ongoing data-theft campaign, tracked as "City-Forum," uses custom tooling
to steal data exposed to anonymous users through Salesforce Experience Cloud
and ServiceNow customer portals. The campaign has reportedly been active
since at least March 2025 and has hit organizations across multiple sectors.

The activity underscores the risk of anonymous-access misconfigurations in
widely used SaaS customer portal products.
