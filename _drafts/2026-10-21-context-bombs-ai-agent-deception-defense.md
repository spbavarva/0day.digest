Researchers describe "context bombs" — deceptive prompts that lure AI agents
into decoys and dead ends — as a defensive technique that cut simulated AI
attack success rates by up to 91% in tests, while raising the cost of a
successful attack 23-fold.

The approach targets autonomous or semi-autonomous AI agents conducting
reconnaissance or attacks, feeding them misleading signals rather than trying
to block them outright.
