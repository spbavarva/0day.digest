A wave of attacks on refrigeration and cold-chain monitoring systems at
European supermarkets and blood banks has spread unusually far and fast.
Dark Reading's analysis frames it as a case study in how security issues
in one industry's shared equipment and vendors can cascade across an
entire supply chain.

The pattern echoes other OT/ICS incidents where a narrow initial
compromise ripples outward because affected organizations share
controllers, vendors, or monitoring platforms.
