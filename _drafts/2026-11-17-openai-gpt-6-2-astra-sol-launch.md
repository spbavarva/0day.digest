OpenAI introduced two new frontier models: GPT-6.2 Astra, positioned for
its hardest workloads, and GPT-6.2 Sol, a more efficient model for everyday
use. Both are available in the API as gpt-6.2-astra and gpt-6.2-sol, with
improvements to professional outputs, coding, and computer use across
ChatGPT Work, Codex, and the API.

Astra is priced at $8/MTok input and $40/MTok output — about 80% of GPT-6
Astra's price — with cached input at $0.40/million, a bigger discount than
the previous generation's $1/million cached rate.
