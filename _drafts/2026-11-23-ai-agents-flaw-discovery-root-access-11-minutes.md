A China-nexus threat actor used the AI-driven penetration testing
tool "Cairn" to go from flaw discovery to root access in 11 minutes,
in attacks against East Asian universities. The campaign exploited a
zero-day in a medical imaging application for initial access.

The speed shows AI agents compressing exploit development and
chaining timelines in real-world intrusions, not just research
benchmarks. Organizations running medical imaging software should
prioritize patching and watch for anomalous access given active
exploitation.
