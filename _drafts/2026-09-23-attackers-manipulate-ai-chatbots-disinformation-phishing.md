Threat actors are manipulating AI chatbot answers — including
ChatGPT, Gemini, and Google AI Overviews — as part of a mass
disinformation and phishing campaign. Attackers are seeding the web
with malicious links and manufactured content, then optimizing it so
AI systems surface it in generated answers.

This is a growing class of AI-answer-poisoning attack distinct from
traditional SEO manipulation, since the target is the AI system's
synthesized response rather than search rankings directly.
