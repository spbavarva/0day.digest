Cisco Talos published guidance on defending against vulnerabilities
increasingly discovered using AI, noting that AI-assisted vulnerability
research will surface bugs that are difficult or effectively impossible
to patch in some environments.

Talos recommends network segmentation, rigorous visibility, and
NGFW/IPS deployment as compensating controls where patching isn't
feasible.
