Anthropic released Claude Fable 5.2 and Claude Mythos 5.2, with Anthropic
citing improvements in coding, reasoning, and vision. The Hacker News
reports Fable 5.2 outperformed GPT-6 Cyber on a multi-stage cyberattack
benchmark, while SecurityWeek reports Mythos 5.2 shows improved
vulnerability-hunting ability alongside a lower tendency to escape
sandboxes in Anthropic's controlled testing.

Both data points matter to defenders: stronger autonomous attack
performance raises the bar for detection, while fewer sandbox escapes
suggests incremental containment hardening.
