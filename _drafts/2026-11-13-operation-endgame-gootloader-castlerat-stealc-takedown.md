An international law enforcement operation, Operation Endgame, took down
servers and infrastructure behind three malware families: Gootloader,
CastleRAT, and StealC. Investigators seized 235 servers and 257 domains,
along with roughly 6.4 million stolen login credentials belonging to more
than 40,000 victims, according to Germany's Federal Criminal Police Office
(BKA).

All three tools were used by criminals to gain initial access, steal
passwords, and in some cases launch follow-on ransomware attacks, making
this a meaningful disruption to multiple ransomware supply chains.
