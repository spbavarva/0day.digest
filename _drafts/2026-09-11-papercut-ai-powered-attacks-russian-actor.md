A Russian threat actor used AI tooling to build, test, and deploy exploits
against PaperCut vulnerabilities, targeting hundreds of organizations
worldwide. The campaign is part of a broader pattern of AI-assisted
exploitation disclosed this week by Anthropic and security researchers.
Organizations running PaperCut should confirm they're on a patched release
given the active targeting.
