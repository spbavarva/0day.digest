Cloud security firm Sysdig documented an incident where a human
threat actor exploited a remote code execution vulnerability in a
Marimo notebook and pivoted to an SSH bastion host within eight
seconds of gaining initial access.

The findings show that skilled human operators, not just AI-assisted
tooling, can move through a compromised environment at speeds that
challenge conventional detection and response timelines. Sysdig
frames this alongside a broader trend of AI shrinking the gap between
vulnerability discovery and exploitation. Further technical detail on
the exploitation chain was not included in the source summary.
