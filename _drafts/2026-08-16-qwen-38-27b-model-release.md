Alibaba's Qwen research lab released Qwen 3.8 27B, an Apache 2.0-licensed,
vision-capable 27B parameter model. Simon Willison notes the size is well
suited to running on a reasonably specced laptop, and that Qwen's
self-reported benchmarks show gains over both its predecessor, Qwen 3.6 27B,
and the closed-weight Qwen 3.7-Plus. He also flagged that the model defaults
to wildly overthinking problems, producing excessive reasoning before
answering. Independent benchmarks confirming Qwen's reported numbers have
not yet been published.
