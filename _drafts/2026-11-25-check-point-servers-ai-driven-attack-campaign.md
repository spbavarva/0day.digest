An unknown threat actor used AI-driven offensive tooling to compromise 24
Check Point Security Management servers across 22 organizations worldwide.
GreyNoise is tracking the intrusion activity under the moniker GT13.

Targets span financial services, energy, and government sectors. Check
Point customers should review management server exposure and GreyNoise's
indicators for this campaign.
