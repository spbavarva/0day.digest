Apple's threat notification system flagged spyware targets in 99
countries, and recipients reportedly included German lawmakers and
foreign policy staff among the country's political elite.

No specific spyware vendor or technique was identified in the
available summary. Apple's notifications are reserved for users it
believes were individually targeted by state-sponsored spyware.
