Binance, the world's largest cryptocurrency exchange, has linked the
theft of roughly $6.3 billion in crypto assets to a compromised laptop
belonging to one of its DevOps engineers.

The root-cause points to endpoint/credential compromise as the entry
point for one of the largest crypto heists on record, rather than a
smart-contract or protocol-level exploit.

Organizations handling high-value digital assets should treat
engineer endpoints with production access as a top-tier target and
review laptop hardening, session controls, and credential scoping
accordingly.
