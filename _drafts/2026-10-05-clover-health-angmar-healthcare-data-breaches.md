Hackers stole patient information from Clover Health Investments and
AngMar Management Services in breaches that occurred in July, affecting
roughly 250,000 people combined. No technical details on the intrusion
method or specific data types were included in the available summary.
Affected individuals should watch for breach notification letters and
consider credit monitoring if offered. This adds to a steady stream of
mid-sized healthcare data breaches this year.
