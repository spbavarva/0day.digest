An anonymous hacking group claims to have broken into computer systems
connected to Russia's election infrastructure, days before the country
begins voting for a new parliament.

The claim has not been independently verified, and no technical
evidence, IOCs, or specifics about which systems were accessed have been
published. Treat this as an unverified claim pending confirmation or
technical disclosure from the group or independent researchers.
