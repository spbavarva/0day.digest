Eight Cisco Talos researchers shared practical techniques for making an
adversary's next move slower and riskier.

The approaches span deception, behavioral detection, breaking attack
dependencies, and resisting attacker-manufactured urgency.

The piece is aimed at defenders looking to raise the operational cost of
intrusion rather than relying solely on prevention.
