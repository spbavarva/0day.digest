Spain's Data Protection Agency (AEPD) received its first reported case
of a data breach allegedly caused by an AI agent. Regulators say an
autonomous AI agent, built on a known large language model, chained
together a successful login, vulnerability discovery, and access to
personal data.

The case is described as a potential milestone for autonomous,
AI-driven cyberattacks. No details on the affected organization, the
LLM involved, or the number of records exposed have been disclosed.
