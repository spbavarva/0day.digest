Infostealer logs have exposed AI account credentials and active sessions
tied to more than 80,000 corporate domains, according to research from
SOCRadar. The exposure spans shadow AI usage (employees using unsanctioned
AI tools) and stolen logins traded for LLMjacking — unauthorized use of
victims' paid AI accounts.

Risks include exposure of stored conversations, exfiltration of data
shared with AI assistants, and abuse of compromised accounts to run up
usage costs. Organizations should audit which AI services employees use
and monitor infostealer marketplaces for exposed credentials tied to their
domains.
