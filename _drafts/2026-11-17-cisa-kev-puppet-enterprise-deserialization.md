CISA added CVE-2026-115731, a deserialization-of-untrusted-data
vulnerability in Perforce Puppet Enterprise, to its Known Exploited
Vulnerabilities catalog based on evidence of active exploitation.
Deserialization flaws are a frequent vector for remote code execution.

Under Binding Operational Directive 26-04, federal civilian agencies must
prioritize patching KEV-listed vulnerabilities on CISA's required
timeline; other organizations running Puppet Enterprise should treat this
as confirmed active-exploitation risk and patch accordingly.
