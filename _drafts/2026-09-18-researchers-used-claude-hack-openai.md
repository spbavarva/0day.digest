A team of three independent researchers at Hacktron say it took under 72
hours to compromise OpenAI employee accounts using Anthropic's Claude
Opus 4.8 and 5, per the Wall Street Journal. Using an AI-built exploit
combined with a sign-in flaw, they gained access to OpenAI's internal
"Monorepo" code repository. The researchers reported the flaws and earned
a bug bounty rather than exploiting the access maliciously.
