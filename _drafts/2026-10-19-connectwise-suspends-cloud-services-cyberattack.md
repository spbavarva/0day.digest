ConnectWise has suspended its cloud services after detecting a cyberattack,
and the services remain unavailable while the company carries out
remediation and security validation.

ConnectWise's tools are widely used by managed service providers, so any
prolonged outage or compromise matters to downstream customers. No further
technical detail on the attack has been disclosed.
