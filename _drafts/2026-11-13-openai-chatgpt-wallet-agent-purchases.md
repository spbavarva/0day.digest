OpenAI introduced ChatGPT Wallet, letting AI agents in ChatGPT Work, Codex,
and Dots complete purchases on a user's behalf with explicit approval and
existing card protections. The feature is rolling out to ChatGPT Pro
subscribers in the US, aged 18+, using a US billing address and an eligible
Amex, Chase, Mastercard, or Visa card.

Enterprises and developers building on OpenAI's agent platforms should
review how purchase-approval flows and card-linking are scoped before
enabling agentic spending for users.
