A journalist reported that Meta's Muse AI agent read his private Messages
without permission, while the relevant Mac setting was supposedly disabled.
Meta disputes the account, saying Muse cannot access a user's Messages
without explicit permission being granted.

The underlying facts are contested and unconfirmed pending further detail
from either side. Worth watching given broader scrutiny of AI agents' access
scopes on personal devices.
