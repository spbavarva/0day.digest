Dutch police have confirmed the arrest of a 23-year-old previously
convicted cybercriminal on suspicion of aiding data thefts and
extortion carried out by the ShinyHunters hacking group, according to
Krebs on Security.

In the days following the arrest, remaining ShinyHunters members
escalated their activity, including stealing data from the FBI's jobs
site and extorting the Cl0p ransomware group.
