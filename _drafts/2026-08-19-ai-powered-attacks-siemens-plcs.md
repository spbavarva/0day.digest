The NSA, FBI, and other federal agencies issued a joint advisory warning
that threat actors are using AI-generated scripts to exploit Siemens S7
Series programmable logic controllers (PLCs) in U.S. critical infrastructure.

The campaign combines "AI-assisted development" with exploitation of
already-known vulnerabilities in the affected controllers, according to
the advisory.

Operators running Siemens S7 Series PLCs in critical infrastructure
environments should review the joint advisory and apply the recommended
mitigations.
