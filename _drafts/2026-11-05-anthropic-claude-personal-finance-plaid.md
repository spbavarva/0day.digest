Anthropic has launched Personal Finance in Claude, a feature that connects
the chatbot to users' bank and card accounts via the Plaid bank-bridging
platform. Users can ask Claude questions like "where did my money go this
month" and get a spending overview generated from linked account data.

Users enabling this feature should review what account data Claude and
Plaid can access and how long that data is retained.
