Binance has linked the theft of approximately $6.3 billion in
cryptocurrency to a compromised laptop belonging to one of its DevOps
engineers.

Blockchain analysts cited by SecurityWeek say the theft bears the
hallmarks of North Korea's TraderTraitor group, a threat actor with a long
history of targeting crypto exchanges.

Binance is still investigating how the laptop was compromised and hasn't
confirmed the exact mechanism attackers used to pivot from the device to
production systems.

The incident is among the largest crypto thefts on record and underscores
how a single compromised employee endpoint can cascade into exchange-wide
losses.
