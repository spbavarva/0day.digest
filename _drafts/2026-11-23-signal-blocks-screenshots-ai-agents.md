Signal is blocking screenshot capture by OS-level AI assistants such as
Apple's Siri and Google's Gemini Intelligence, saying these agents are
making the environment "increasingly hostile" to user privacy by
default.

The move targets screen-reading AI features that could capture message
contents without an explicit user action. Users on affected devices
should check whether their OS now blocks screenshots inside Signal and
adjust assistant permissions accordingly.
