Signal is rolling out screenshot blocking on Apple and Google operating
systems to limit data exposure to AI agents built into those platforms.

Signal said OS-level AI assistants such as Google's Gemini Intelligence
and Apple's Siri are changing the environment in ways that make it more
hostile to privacy, since those assistants can read on-screen content
across apps.
