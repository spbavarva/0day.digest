Attackers remotely altered settings on internet-exposed refrigeration
controllers at supermarkets across Belgium, Sweden, Estonia, and other
European countries overnight into November 2, warming fridges and
freezers, per The Hacker News. No chain reported more than several dozen
affected stores, but some had to discard or pull chilled and frozen
food. Danfoss, a Danish maker of the affected refrigeration controls, is
named in the report.
