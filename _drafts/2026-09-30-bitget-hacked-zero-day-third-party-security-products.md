Cryptocurrency exchange Bitget confirmed that the attackers who stole
$387.5 million last week breached its systems by exploiting a zero-day
flaw in third-party security products protecting its infrastructure.

The disclosure follows initial reporting of the theft and identifies the
initial access vector used in the breach.
