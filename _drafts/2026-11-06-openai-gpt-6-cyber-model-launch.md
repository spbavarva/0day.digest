OpenAI has released a new GPT-6 Cyber model, which it rates Critical for
cybersecurity capability. The company says the model beats its
predecessor, GPT-5.6 Cyber, at finding and exploiting vulnerabilities
while staying within scope boundaries more reliably.

The release underscores the growing capability of frontier models to
autonomously discover and exploit flaws, which practitioners on both
offense and defense should factor into their threat models.
