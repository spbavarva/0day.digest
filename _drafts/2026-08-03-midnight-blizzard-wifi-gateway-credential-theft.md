Russian state-sponsored group Midnight Blizzard has been linked to a campaign
stealing Microsoft account credentials via compromised public Wi-Fi networks
at hospitality organizations. Guests connecting to affected hotel and venue
Wi-Fi gateways have had their Microsoft credentials intercepted. The technique
targets travelers broadly rather than a specific organization's network,
making it harder to defend against with standard enterprise controls. Users
authenticating to Microsoft accounts over hotel or venue Wi-Fi should treat
the network as untrusted and prefer a VPN or cellular connection.
