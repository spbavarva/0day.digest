Microsoft has launched a cybersecurity-specific model, MAI-Cyber-1-Flash,
inside MDASH, its multi-model vulnerability identification and remediation
harness. Paired with GPT-5.4, the new configuration scored 95.95% on the
CyberGym benchmark, which Microsoft says costs 50% less than its prior best
MDASH combination (GPT-5.4, GPT-5.4 mini, GPT-5.3 Codex). Access is
currently limited to approved customers.
