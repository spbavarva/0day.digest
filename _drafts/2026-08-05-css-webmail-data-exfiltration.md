Researchers demonstrated that CSS, once considered purely a design tool,
can be powerful enough to exfiltrate data from webmail clients, and that
some vendors aren't prepared to defend against it.

The technique doesn't require JavaScript, which lets it bypass defenses
that focus on script-based attacks in email clients. Webmail providers and
enterprises should review CSS-rendering behavior in their mail clients as
part of their email security posture.
