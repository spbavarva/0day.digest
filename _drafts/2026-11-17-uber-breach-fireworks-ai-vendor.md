Uber is notifying nearly 150,000 drivers that attackers stole their data
after breaching Fireworks, a third-party AI infrastructure provider used by
Uber. Uber was notified by the vendor on October 9 that internal
credentials had been stolen and used to access Uber-related data held by
Fireworks.

The incident is a reminder that AI infrastructure vendors now sit in the
same sensitive-data blast radius as traditional SaaS and cloud providers.
Organizations using third-party AI infrastructure providers should confirm
what customer data those vendors retain and how vendor credential
compromise would be detected.
