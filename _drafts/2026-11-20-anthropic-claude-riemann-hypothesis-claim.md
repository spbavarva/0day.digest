Anthropic mathematician Levent Alpöge says he has, working with Claude,
discovered a proof of the Riemann Hypothesis, one of mathematics's most
famous unsolved problems, and believes the method extends to the
Generalized and Extended Riemann Hypotheses as well.

The claim has not yet been independently verified; Alpöge has opted to put
out a current draft for community review rather than wait for a fully
vetted paper.

Separately, mathematician Alessandro Masullo has put forward an
independent, fully human proof of the Generalized Riemann Hypothesis that
Stanford mathematician Brian Conrad has been reviewing for months. If
either claim holds up, it would be a major result regardless of which
came first.
