The China-aligned threat actor known as Webworm is suspected of using
a modified version of the open-source SoftEther VPN Bridge tool to
maintain covert remote access on compromised networks.

The modified variant strips recognizable detection fingerprints and
disguises itself as network-monitoring software to evade security
tools. It's another example of a China-nexus actor customizing
legitimate open-source tooling for stealthy persistence rather than
building malware from scratch.
