The China-aligned threat actor Webworm is suspected of using a modified
version of the open-source SoftEther VPN Bridge tool to establish covert
remote access on compromised networks.

The modified variant disguises itself as network-monitoring software and
implements tricks to hide from security tools and remove recognizable
detection fingerprints, making the backdoor harder to spot during incident
response.
