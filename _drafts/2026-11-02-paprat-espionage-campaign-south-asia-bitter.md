Dark Reading detailed a cyber-espionage campaign dubbed PapRAT, linked
to the India-aligned threat group Bitter, targeting organizations
across South Asia. The campaign uses three distinct attack chains that
lean on legitimate Windows functionality to blend in with normal
system activity.

No specific IOCs were included in the available summary. Defenders in
the region should review logging for abuse of legitimate OS utilities
consistent with living-off-the-land techniques.
