Trail of Bits examined Binance's October 2026 security incident, where
a compromised DevOps laptop allowed an attacker to access a service
container and submit unauthorized withdrawals. The post argues
organizations holding user funds should ask whether their security
controls can detect a valid signature granting an attacker wallet
authority, not just monitor transfers and ownership changes.

Organizations monitoring only fund movement, not authority changes,
may miss the shift in control until assets actually move.
