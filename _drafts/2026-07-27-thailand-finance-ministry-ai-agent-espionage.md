Hackers used an autonomous AI agent to carry out a cyber-espionage campaign
against Thailand's Ministry of Finance, according to researchers. The
campaign marks a documented case of attackers using an AI agent to conduct
offensive operations against a government target, rather than merely
assisting human operators. Further technical details on the agent's
capabilities or the intrusion's scope were not included in available
reporting. The incident adds to growing evidence that autonomous AI agents
are being operationalized in real-world espionage.
