Google's AI-chip-equipped satellite, part of its Project Suncatcher
effort, has produced its first response from orbit.

The company said it successfully booted the satellite's onboard Tensor
Processing Units (TPUs), letting a Gemma 4 model run inference in space.

The demo task was lightweight — the model was prompted to write a haiku
about being in space — but it marks the first known case of an AI model
running inference on orbital hardware.

Google has not detailed latency, bandwidth, or thermal constraints for
the setup, or what workloads it expects to run on future satellite TPU
deployments.
