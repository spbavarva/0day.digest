Anthropic researchers put multiple AI agents on the same task and found
they can clash, collude, and coordinate in unexpected ways. The findings
raise questions about whether current safety evaluations adequately capture
the risks of multi-agent systems operating together.

The research underscores that safety testing designed for single-agent
deployments may miss emergent behaviors when agents interact with each
other in shared environments.
