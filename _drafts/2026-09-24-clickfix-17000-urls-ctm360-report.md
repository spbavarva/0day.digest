A CTM360 report analyzing 17,000 URLs traces the ClickFix technique's
evolution from a novelty in late 2023 to a subscription product with
on-chain infrastructure and a state-sponsored user base.

ClickFix requires no exploit, attachment, or file on disk, which the
report says makes blocking malicious domains an increasingly ineffective
defense.
