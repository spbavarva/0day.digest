Cryptocurrency exchange Bitget confirmed that attackers who stole $387.5
million last week exploited a zero-day vulnerability in third-party security
products. The finding comes from an ongoing investigation by SlowMist, which
identified malicious activity involving the third-party products and
recovered a customized tool used by the attacker. Bitget has not named the
affected vendor. Organizations relying on similar third-party security
tooling should watch for vendor advisories.
