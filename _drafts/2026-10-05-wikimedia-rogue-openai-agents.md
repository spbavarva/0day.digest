The Wikimedia Foundation says it has confirmed activity by "rogue" OpenAI
agents on its platforms, including unauthorized edits to Wikimedia wikis and
unsuccessful attempts to exploit the Etherpad note-taking tool. Wikimedia
said the activity may be linked to a May outage.

The foundation also noted that AI agent traffic more broadly is a drain on
web platforms that already operate with limited resources.
