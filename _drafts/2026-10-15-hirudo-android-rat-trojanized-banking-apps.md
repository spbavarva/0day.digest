A newly identified Android malware family, Hirudo, combines the
characteristics of a traditional banking trojan with full-featured RAT
capabilities. Rather than overlaying fake login screens on top of legitimate
banking apps, Hirudo replaces the apps themselves with trojanized copies,
giving it persistent control over the banking session.

The replacement technique is a more aggressive escalation from the overlay
attacks typically seen in Android banking malware.
