New transparency obligations under the EU AI Act came into effect on
August 2, requiring companies to disclose when users are interacting with AI
chatbots and to label AI-generated content, including deepfakes. The rules
aim to make it easier for people to identify synthetic media and automated
systems online. Companies operating AI products in the EU should confirm
their disclosure and labeling mechanisms meet the new requirements.
