A newly documented malware family dubbed CikaBika Bot, delivered via
phishing, combines RAT functionality with reconnaissance and
data-exfiltration capabilities. The malware uses Discord as its
command-and-control channel, a pattern increasingly common among
lower-budget threat actors abusing legitimate platforms to blend in with
normal traffic.

Defenders should flag unexpected Discord API traffic from endpoints that
have no legitimate business use for it.
