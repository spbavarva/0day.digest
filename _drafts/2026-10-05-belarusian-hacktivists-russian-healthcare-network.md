Researchers attributed a two-year espionage campaign inside a Russian
healthcare network to the Belarusian Cyber Partisans, a hacktivist group
typically known for disruptive, public attacks on governments and
infrastructure rather than quiet long-term intrusions. The extended
dwell time suggests a shift toward stealthier tradecraft for this actor.
No specific IOCs or technical TTPs were included in the available
summary. Defenders tracking hacktivist groups should note this move
toward nation-state-like persistence.
