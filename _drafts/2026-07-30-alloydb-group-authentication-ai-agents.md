Google Cloud announced group-based Identity and Access Management
authentication for AlloyDB, aimed at reducing reliance on static database
passwords.

The feature targets enterprises managing access for both employees and AI
agents at scale, an area Google frames as an operational and security burden
today. Google positions the change as a step toward passwordless, centrally
audited database access.
