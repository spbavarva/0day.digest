Ukrainian authorities, working with the FBI, arrested four suspected members
of an international ransomware organization linked to more than $40.5 million
in ransom payments from at least 28 companies worldwide.

The arrests represent a law enforcement disruption of an active ransomware
group rather than a new technical disclosure. No new TTPs or IOCs were
published alongside the announcement.
