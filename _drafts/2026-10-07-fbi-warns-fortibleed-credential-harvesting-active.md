The FBI and Secret Service warned that the FortiBleed credential-harvesting
campaign remains an active threat against internet-facing Fortinet FortiGate
firewalls and SSL VPN gateways.

The campaign exploits reused or leaked credentials along with legacy SHA-256
password storage on affected devices, and has amassed 86,644 Fortinet device
credentials to date.

Administrators of FortiGate firewalls and SSL VPN gateways should rotate
credentials, move off legacy password storage where possible, and treat any
exposed management interface as potentially compromised.
