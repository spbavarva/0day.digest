Google launched a new website that lets anyone check whether a piece of
media — an image, video, or audio clip — was generated using AI.

The tool checks for SynthID, Google's invisible watermarking technology
embedded in AI-generated content from its own models, and is aimed at
helping the public verify the provenance of media they encounter online.
