Apple released security updates fixing a CoreGraphics zero-day vulnerability
that was exploited in what Apple described as "extremely sophisticated"
targeted attacks against iOS devices. The flaw's characteristics and targeted
use pattern are consistent with spyware-style operations against a small
number of high-value individuals. iOS users, especially those at elevated
risk (journalists, activists, executives), should update immediately.
