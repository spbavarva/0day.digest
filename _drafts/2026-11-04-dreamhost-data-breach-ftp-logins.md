DreamHost disclosed that attackers had access to its File Manager platform
for roughly two months before the intrusion was discovered. During that
window, the attackers stole customers' FTP/SFTP usernames and passwords
along with webmail session data.

Affected customers should rotate FTP/SFTP credentials and review webmail
account activity for signs of unauthorized access.
