The India-linked threat group BITTER is running a cyber espionage campaign
against organizations in South Asia using malware dubbed PapRAT.

The campaign uses three distinct attack chains that abuse legitimate
Windows functionality to blend in with normal activity. No specific victim
organizations have been named.
