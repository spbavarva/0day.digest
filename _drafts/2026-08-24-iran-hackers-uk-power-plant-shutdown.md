An Iran-linked threat actor caused a four-day shutdown at a UK power
plant, resulting in real-world operational disruption rather than a
purely IT-side incident.

The attack has raised concerns about the resilience of Britain's
distributed energy infrastructure and the potential for the technique to
be repeated against similar targets.
