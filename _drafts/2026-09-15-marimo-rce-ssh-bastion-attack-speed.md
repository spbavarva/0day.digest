Cloud security firm Sysdig documented an intrusion where a human attacker
exploited a remote code execution vulnerability in a Marimo notebook and
pivoted to an SSH bastion host within eight seconds of initial access.

The finding shows that skilled human operators, not just AI-assisted
tooling, can move through a compromised environment nearly as fast as
automated attacks once they have a foothold.
