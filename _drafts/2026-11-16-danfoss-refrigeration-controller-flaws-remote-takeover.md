Dutch security consultancy Midnight Blue disclosed six unpatched
vulnerabilities in Danfoss AK-SM800A refrigeration controllers that could
let a remote attacker take over a device without authenticating. No patch
is available — the newest firmware, version 5.4.0, does not fix the flaws.

Government cybersecurity agencies in Belgium, Ireland, and Germany have
already issued guidance to operators this week in response. Organizations
running AK-SM800A controllers should isolate management interfaces from
the internet until a fix ships.
