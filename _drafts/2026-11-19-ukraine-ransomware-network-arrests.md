Ukrainian authorities arrested four suspected members of an
international ransomware network accused of extorting more than $40
million from companies worldwide, including US oil and healthcare firms.
The operation reflects continued law-enforcement pressure on ransomware
crews operating out of Eastern Europe. No further detail on the group's
name, TTPs, or specific victims has been released yet.
