Hardware wallet maker SafePal disclosed that an authorization flaw in an
order-tracking plugin exposed the names, email addresses, shipping
addresses, phone numbers, and purchase details of approximately 39,798
customers. Affected customers were notified individually by email on
August 16 from security@safepal.com.

The exposed data did not include wallet keys or funds, but the contact
information could be used for targeted phishing against crypto holders.
