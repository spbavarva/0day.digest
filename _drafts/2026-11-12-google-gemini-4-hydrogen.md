Google introduced Gemini 4 Hydrogen, a new model the company says is
better at understanding complicated questions — including multi-part
questions, typo-heavy queries, and highly specific requests. The model
breaks down questions and addresses each part in its responses.

Gemini 4 Hydrogen is rolling out starting today in Google Search's AI
Mode, the Gemini app, and across all Google Search tiers.
