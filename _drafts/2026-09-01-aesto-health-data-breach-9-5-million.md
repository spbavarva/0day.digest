Healthcare technology company Aesto Health disclosed that hackers
stole personal and health information belonging to 9.5 million
individuals. The stolen data was taken from the company's AWS
infrastructure.

Details on the initial access vector or the nature of the AWS
compromise were not included in the source summary. Given the scale
of the breach, affected individuals and downstream healthcare
providers should watch for official notifications and be alert to
follow-on phishing or identity-theft attempts.
