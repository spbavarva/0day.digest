Developers Peter James and Jonny L. Saunders say they independently
coaxed Meta's new Muse AI agent into zipping up and sharing the entire
contents of its root filesystem with minimal prompting.

The leaked contents reportedly include Ubuntu system files, app
templates, and internal documentation — not just user-facing data.

The finding raises questions about sandboxing and prompt-injection
resistance in Meta's newly launched consumer AI agent.
