Bitsight found that budget Android TV boxes shipped with apps that rewrite
the device's hardware identity to impersonate Samsung, Huawei, Xiaomi, or
Vivo phones, then use that spoofed identity to click ads on
operator-controlled websites. Researchers named the operation Fuyao and
attributed it to Zhejiang Fengwo IoT Technology Co., a mainland China
company founded in 2019.

The same apps reportedly have a second function turning the device into a
residential proxy, though full details on that capability were not included
in the available summary. Owners of low-cost Android TV/streaming boxes
should review installed apps and outbound network activity.
