Capcom disclosed that hackers stole roughly 11 million email addresses
associated with Capcom ID accounts. The attackers also changed login
passwords for 196 accounts.

The breach appears to be primarily an exposure of email addresses rather
than a broader credential compromise. Affected users should watch for
phishing attempts targeting the leaked addresses and verify their Capcom
ID account access.
