Capcom disclosed that hackers stole 11 million email addresses tied to
Capcom ID accounts and changed login passwords on 196 accounts.

No further detail on the attack vector was given in the disclosure.
Affected users should treat their Capcom ID credentials as exposed and
watch for follow-on phishing targeting the leaked email addresses.
