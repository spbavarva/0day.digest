Kaspersky's GReAT team published a detailed breakdown of how
misconfigurations across different layers of virtualization
infrastructure contributed to real-world ransomware cases on ESXi and
Proxmox environments.

The research maps common misconfiguration patterns to the attack layers
they expose, giving defenders running virtualized infrastructure a
reference for where to prioritize hardening efforts.
