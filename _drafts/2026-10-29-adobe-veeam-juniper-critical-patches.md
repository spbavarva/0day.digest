Adobe, Veeam, and Juniper Networks shipped patches for critical
vulnerabilities that could lead to arbitrary code execution, authorization
bypasses, and privilege escalation. No public reports of active exploitation
yet, but given the severity, affected organizations should apply updates
promptly.
