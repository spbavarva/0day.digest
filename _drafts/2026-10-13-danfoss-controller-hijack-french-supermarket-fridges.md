Attackers took remote control of refrigeration and freezer units at
hundreds of French supermarkets on the night of October 11, raising
temperatures and forcing stores to discard spoiled food. The attack
reached the appliances through a Danfoss controller used to remotely
manage store refrigeration, affecting chains including Les Mousquetaires.

This is an operational technology (OT) attack with direct physical
consequences rather than a pure data breach. No CVE or patch status was
mentioned in the available coverage.
