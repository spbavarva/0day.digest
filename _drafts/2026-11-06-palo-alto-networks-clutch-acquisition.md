Palo Alto Networks is reportedly in talks to acquire Clutch, an AI agent
security startup, for $500 million. Clutch is backed by Lightspeed and
SignalFire and was last valued at $85 million.

The deal, if it closes, would be a notable markup and signals that major
security vendors see AI agent security as a category worth consolidating
around now rather than building in-house.
