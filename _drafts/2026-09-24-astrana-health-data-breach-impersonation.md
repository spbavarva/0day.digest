Healthcare technology firm Astrana Health disclosed a data breach in
which attackers impersonated company personnel and contacted employees
to gain access to its servers.

The company reported to regulators, including in an SEC filing, that
the attackers accessed confidential and private information as a result.

The incident is the latest in a string of healthcare-sector breaches
relying on impersonation and social engineering rather than a technical
exploit to gain initial access.
