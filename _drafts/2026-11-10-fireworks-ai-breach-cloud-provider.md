AI infrastructure startup Fireworks AI disclosed a new security breach in
which attackers accessed its development machines after first breaching a
third-party cloud provider Fireworks relies on.

BleepingComputer did not report further detail on what data or code was
accessed. Customers of Fireworks' hosted inference platform should watch
for official guidance on whether any credentials or models were exposed.
