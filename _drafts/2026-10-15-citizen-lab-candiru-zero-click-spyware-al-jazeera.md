Researchers at Citizen Lab say Candiru, an Israeli spyware vendor that sells
to governments, infected devices at Al Jazeera using zero-click attacks
delivered through ordinary online ads. The malicious code executed the
moment an ad loaded, in some cases while the ad was off-screen, requiring no
click or other action from the target.

The campaign highlights advertising networks as a largely unmonitored
infection vector for nation-state-grade spyware.
