SailPoint's "Horizons of Identity Security" report finds that enterprises
are deploying autonomous AI agents at AI speed while continuing to rely on
identity and access controls built for human-speed operations. The report
calls this gap a "velocity paradox" between business ambition for AI and the
readiness of identity security programs.

The findings add to a growing body of reporting on AI agents operating with
excess standing access relative to the controls meant to govern them.
