Attackers shut down a steam turbine and the process-water treatment
system at a Polish combined heat and power plant, gaining access over the
private cellular network the grid operator uses to reach remote
equipment. The plant supplies heat to roughly 50,000 residents. Recovery
began about 30 minutes after the outage, while the intruders were
reportedly still active inside the network; customers did not lose heat
service.
