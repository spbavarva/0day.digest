Perplexity is using OpenAI's GPT-6 Astra model to write communications,
modify software, and monitor production systems, checking in with humans
much less frequently than it did with earlier models.

The case study reflects a broader shift toward higher-autonomy AI agents
operating directly on live production infrastructure.
