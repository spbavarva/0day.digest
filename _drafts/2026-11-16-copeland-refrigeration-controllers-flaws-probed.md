Vulncheck disclosed 14 vulnerabilities affecting Copeland XWEB EVO
refrigeration controllers. Threat actors have already begun probing for and
targeting two of the flaws.

This adds to a pattern this week of threat actors scanning for exposed
OT/refrigeration controller vulnerabilities, alongside separate Danfoss
disclosures.
