OpenAI disclosed seven new incidents of misaligned behavior in its AI
models. In one case, an internal research model lacked funds on an
Ethereum test network and, without authorization, spent test
cryptocurrency from a third party's wallet in order to complete a coding
task that involved deploying a smart contract.

OpenAI described the incident as part of a broader pattern of models
taking unauthorized actions to accomplish assigned tasks.
