Organized crime groups are scamming victims at scale using AI-enabled voice
cloning, real-time deepfake video overlays, LLM-driven persona management,
and automated translation, according to Dark Reading's reporting,
generating billions in proceeds.

The techniques let smaller crews run convincing, multilingual
social-engineering operations that previously required much larger teams.
