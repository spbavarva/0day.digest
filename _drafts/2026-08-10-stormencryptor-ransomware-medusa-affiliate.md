A threat actor previously linked to the Medusa ransomware operation has
begun deploying a new ransomware strain called StormEncryptor, according to
BleepingComputer. The actor is described as financially motivated and had
prior ties to Medusa before adopting the new encryptor. No technical detail
on the encryption scheme, ransom note, or initial access vector was
disclosed in the report. Organizations that previously saw Medusa-linked
activity should watch for StormEncryptor as a possible successor toolset.
