CISA published an advisory for three vulnerabilities (CVSS up to 9.6)
affecting Elecbrakes EB2, VM1, and ELBC2000 trailer brake controllers.
Successful exploitation could let an attacker gain control of a
trailer's brakes, modify firmware, disable brake lights, or disable
other safety features. The advisory lists CVE-2026-124281,
CVE-2026-125273, and CVE-2026-96805 across the affected product lines.
No active exploitation has been reported; affected organizations should
follow CISA's mitigation guidance.
