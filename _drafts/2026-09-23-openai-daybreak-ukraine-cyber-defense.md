OpenAI is extending access to its Daybreak program to the Government
of Ukraine to support the cyber defense of civilian infrastructure.

The program gives participating governments access to OpenAI's
models for defensive cybersecurity work. OpenAI did not disclose
further technical details on scope or specific capabilities.
