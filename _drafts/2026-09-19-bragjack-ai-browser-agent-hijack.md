Security researcher Gal Weizman of Forever Security disclosed BragJack, a proof-of-concept attack that hijacks AI assistants built into Chrome, Edge, Opera Neon, Perplexity Comet, and Claude in Chrome using a single malicious browser extension.

The technique, dubbed "Prompt Forcing," has earned Weizman over $20,000 in bug bounty payouts and resulted in two CVEs so far. Users of AI-integrated browsers should treat unfamiliar extensions as a direct path to hijacking their AI agent's actions.
