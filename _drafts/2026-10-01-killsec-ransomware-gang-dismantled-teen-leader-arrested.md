An international law enforcement operation dubbed "Operation KillSwitch"
has dismantled the KillSec ransomware-as-a-service group, which stole data
from victim organizations and threatened to leak it unless paid.

Police arrested a 16-year-old suspected of running the group as part of
three arrests made on September 30, and seized KillSec's data leak site and
servers.

SecurityWeek reports police secured at least 110 terabytes of data stolen
from victims during the raids.
