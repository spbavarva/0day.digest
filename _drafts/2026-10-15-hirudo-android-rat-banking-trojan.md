Hirudo, a newly identified Android banking trojan, combines traditional
banking-malware data theft with full RAT capabilities.

The malware replaces legitimate banking apps on infected devices with
trojanized copies, giving attackers persistent access to victims' financial
accounts.

Mobile users should avoid installing APKs from outside official app stores
and verify banking app integrity if device compromise is suspected.
