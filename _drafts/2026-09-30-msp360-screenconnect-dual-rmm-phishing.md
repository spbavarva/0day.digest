Microsoft warned of phishing campaigns distributing a trojanized MSP360
Remote Monitoring and Management (RMM) installer, disguised as meeting
invitations, PDF-themed lures, and fake software-update prompts.

Once run, the installer deploys legitimate MSP360 software under a deceptive
file name, giving attackers remote management access — a dual-RMM technique
that layers a second remote-access tool on top of the initial foothold.
