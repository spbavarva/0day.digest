California Attorney General Rob Bonta announced a $6 million settlement with
23andMe's successor company, resolving allegations that it failed to
properly protect customer data ahead of the 2023 breach.

This isn't a new incident, but it's a notable regulatory precedent: it shows
state AGs are willing to pursue direct financial penalties for breach-related
security failures involving sensitive genetic/health data.
