Kaspersky's GReAT team detailed the Citrus Ransomware group's TTPs,
including use of the VLESS protocol for traffic tunneling and a tool
called Sn00py for encryption. Researchers also found evidence the group
used LLMs as part of their tooling.

The group has links to the Toy Ghouls cluster, according to the research.

This is another example of ransomware operators incorporating LLM
assistance into their workflow, a trend worth tracking for both detection
engineering and threat-intel teams.
