N-able released hotfixes for zero-day vulnerabilities affecting its
N-central remote monitoring and management (RMM) platform. The bugs were
publicly disclosed over the weekend and forced N-able to take vulnerable
hosted environments offline.

RMM platforms like N-central are attractive targets because a single
compromise can provide access to many downstream customer networks, making
this a priority patch for MSPs and their clients.

Administrators running N-central, hosted or self-managed, should apply the
hotfixes immediately and review logs for signs of prior compromise.
