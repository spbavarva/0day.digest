A breach of South Korea's government-backed startup platform exposed
encrypted personal data after an encryption key was found included in an
API response.

Penta Security pointed to the incident as a textbook example of why
encryption keys must be stored and managed separately from the data they
protect — encryption alone doesn't help if the key ships alongside the
ciphertext.
