Apple detailed the architecture behind Secure Exclaves, which isolates
ambient sensor data — including camera and microphone feeds — from the main
OS on HomeView and new iPhones. The design keeps sensor processing in an
isolated enclave separate from the primary operating system.

The disclosure gives practitioners and researchers insight into how Apple
segments trust boundaries for sensitive sensor data on its latest hardware.
