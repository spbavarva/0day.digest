Researchers detailed a technique dubbed "AdFection," in which spyware
vendors exploit real-time ad bidding infrastructure to identify and track
specific individuals and organizations. By targeting ad impressions served
to a victim's device, attackers can use the ad ecosystem itself as a
delivery and surveillance channel without requiring any click from the
target.

The technique highlights how legitimate ad-tech infrastructure can be
repurposed for targeted surveillance, a growing concern given minimal
oversight of real-time bidding data flows.
