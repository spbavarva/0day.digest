Snowflake is ending password-based authentication for legacy service
accounts, pushing organizations toward passwordless methods. According
to Token Security, the harder part of the migration isn't the technical
switch itself but figuring out what actually uses each account, who
owns it, and how much access it still needs.

Teams with long-lived Snowflake service accounts should start an
inventory now rather than waiting for the deprecation deadline to force
a rushed migration.
