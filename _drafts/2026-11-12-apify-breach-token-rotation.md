Web-scraping and automation platform Apify rotated critical internal
secrets and revoked some third-party OAuth connections after detecting
unauthorized access to its internal systems.

The company is also prompting customers to reset two-factor authentication.
Apify customers embed API tokens in automation pipelines and integrations,
so anyone using the platform should review and rotate any credentials
shared with or generated by Apify as a precaution.
