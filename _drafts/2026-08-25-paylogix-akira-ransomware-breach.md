Benefits management firm Paylogix told regulators that hackers stole
sensitive financial and health data on tens of thousands of people from its
systems. The attack has been attributed to the Akira ransomware group.

Paylogix processes employee benefits data for other companies, so the
exposure likely extends beyond its own direct customer base to employees at
firms that use its platform.
