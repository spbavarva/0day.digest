The FBI seized seven domains used by the Chinese state-sponsored hacking
group Flax Typhoon to operate two custom tools, MicroScan and FishHub.

The tools were used in intrusions that breached critical infrastructure
and other organizations worldwide, according to the Bureau. The seizure
cuts off infrastructure the group relied on for scanning and follow-on
access to compromised networks.
