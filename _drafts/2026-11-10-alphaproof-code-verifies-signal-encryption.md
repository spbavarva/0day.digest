Google DeepMind introduced AlphaProof Code, which it says wrote three
million lines of formal proofs in the Lean theorem prover to verify
Signal's core encryption. This is one of the largest applications to
date of AI-generated formal verification to a widely used real-world
cryptographic system, and could give security teams a new tool for
proving correctness of critical cryptographic code rather than relying
solely on manual audit.
