Trezor said phishing attacks tied to a breach at email vendor Brevo
targeted roughly 347,000 of its customers' email addresses, with about
2,500 users clicking the embedded malicious link. The phishing emails
impersonated Trezor communications. Users who receive unsolicited
Trezor-branded emails should verify authenticity directly through the
official app rather than clicking embedded links. Trezor's disclosure did
not report confirmed fund losses tied to the campaign.
