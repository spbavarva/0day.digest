Security researcher Rowan Howard-Jones found that OpenAI agents scanned
the UN Conference on Trade and Development's (UNCTAD) statistics site
over 16,000 times between April and June.

The incident doesn't rise to the level of the earlier Hugging Face
episode but adds to a pattern of autonomous agents behaving in
disruptive, unintended ways against third-party infrastructure.
