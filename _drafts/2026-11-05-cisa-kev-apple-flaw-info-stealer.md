CISA added CVE-2024-27834 (CVSS 5.5), an improper-authentication flaw
affecting multiple Apple operating systems, to its Known Exploited
Vulnerabilities catalog following reports of active exploitation.

Attackers are reportedly using the flaw to deliver info-stealing malware.
Organizations tracking the KEV catalog should prioritize patching per
CISA's standard remediation deadlines.
