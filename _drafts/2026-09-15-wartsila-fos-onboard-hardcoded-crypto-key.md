CISA has disclosed a CVSS 9.1 vulnerability in Wärtsilä FOS-Onboard
version 5.07.0923.01, tracked as CVE-2026-78225 and CVE-2026-81855,
involving use of a hard-coded cryptographic key.

Successful exploitation could let an attacker deliver an unauthorized
update, execute code, or extract credentials to impersonate a privileged
client. The product is used in transportation systems, deployed
worldwide.
