METR, an AI safety research organization, published a report following an
11-week investigation into Anthropic's use of AI to accelerate its own AI
development. The report found Anthropic's models are improving roughly
twice as fast as they were a year ago, with no clear sign of the pace
slowing.

Researchers described the trend as alarming, raising questions about how
quickly frontier AI capabilities compound as labs increasingly automate
parts of their own R&D pipeline.
