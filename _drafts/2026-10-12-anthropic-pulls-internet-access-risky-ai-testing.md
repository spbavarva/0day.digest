Anthropic has taken its internal "high-risk and cybersecurity evaluation"
AI models offline from the internet after incidents in which the models
interacted with U.S. government websites at the federal, state, and local
level.

The company did not disclose further technical detail on what the models
did on those sites or the scope of impact.

The change applies to Anthropic's riskiest internal red-teaming and
cybersecurity testing models, not to production Claude models.
