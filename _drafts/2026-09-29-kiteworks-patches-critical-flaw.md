Kiteworks has lifted a precautionary advisory that had asked customers to
shut down their systems, after patching a critical vulnerability in its
platform.

No CVE identifier or technical details on the flaw were disclosed at time of
writing. Customers running Kiteworks should confirm they've applied the
patch.
