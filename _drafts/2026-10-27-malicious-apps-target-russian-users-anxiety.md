Researchers told The Record that cybercriminals are stuffing infostealer
malware into bogus apps designed to appeal to Russians' anxiety about gas
prices, drone strikes, and police activity. The apps masquerade as
tracking tools for everyday concerns tied to the current geopolitical
situation inside Russia.

Once installed, the apps function as infostealers rather than providing
the advertised tracking functionality, illustrating how attackers tailor
social engineering lures to regionally specific anxieties rather than
generic themes.
