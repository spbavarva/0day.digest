Coinbase says it blocked $4.7 million in cryptocurrency transfers tied to a
scam promoting a fake giveaway that used an AI-generated deepfake of Elon
Musk during a Tesla livestream watched by more than 500,000 viewers.

The incident is another example of AI-generated video being used at scale to
run financial fraud against crypto holders, relying on the deepfake's
realism to bypass normal skepticism about celebrity giveaways.
