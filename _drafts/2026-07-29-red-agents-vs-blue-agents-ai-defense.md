Security researchers are using AI red-team agents to train blue-team defensive agents, addressing a perceived imbalance where agentic AI capabilities have favored offense over defense. The approach pits automated attacker agents against defender agents in a training loop intended to improve automated detection and response. Dark Reading frames this as an emerging methodology rather than a specific product release.
