CISA added four actively exploited vulnerabilities to its Known Exploited
Vulnerabilities catalog: an incorrect-authorization flaw in GPUStack
(CVE-2026-123218, CVSS 9.8), plus flaws in SignalWire FreeSwitch, OpenBSD
OpenSSH, and IBM Aspera Faspex. Federal agencies have three days to patch
under CISA's directive.

All four are confirmed under active exploitation. Organizations running
these products, inside or outside the federal space, should prioritize
patching immediately.
