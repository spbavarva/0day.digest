Attackers exploited a Fortinet vulnerability to deploy a persistent,
stealthy implant on FortiGate devices belonging to a telecommunications
organization, according to SecurityWeek.

The implant's stealth and persistence suggest a capable actor specifically
targeting telecom infrastructure. Organizations running FortiGate devices
should confirm patch levels and check for related indicators of compromise.
