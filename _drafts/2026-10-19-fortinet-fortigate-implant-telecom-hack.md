Researchers linked a previously exploited Fortinet vulnerability to a
persistent, stealthy implant discovered on FortiGate devices at a
telecommunications organization.

The implant's persistence suggests a well-resourced actor specifically
targeting telecom infrastructure. FortiGate operators should check for the
indicators described in the underlying research and confirm the relevant
Fortinet patches are applied.
