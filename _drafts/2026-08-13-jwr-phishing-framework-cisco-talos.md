Cisco Talos identified an undocumented phishing framework, internally
branded "JWR" by its developer, built to convincingly impersonate checkout
and login pages across major payment and shopping platforms.

The framework's polish and targeting of payment flows specifically suggest
it's built for financially motivated phishing-as-a-service operations
rather than opportunistic credential harvesting.
