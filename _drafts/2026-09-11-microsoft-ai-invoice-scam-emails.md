Microsoft researchers analyzed a wave of fraudulent business invoice emails
and found threat actors layering multiple tactics to make messages appear
more legitimate, including the use of AI tools. The added techniques make
the scam emails harder to distinguish from real vendor invoices at a
glance. No specific IOCs or affected organizations were named in the
summary.
