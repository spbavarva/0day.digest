Fintech company Revolut has disclosed a data breach after sharing
customer financial information and passport data with a threat actor
impersonating a government agency.

According to The Record, the fraudsters obtained the data by
submitting an "emergency data request" from a compromised, legitimate
government email account, bypassing Revolut's normal verification for
law-enforcement requests. Revolut has not disclosed the number of
customers affected.
