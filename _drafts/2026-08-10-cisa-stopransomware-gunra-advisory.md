CISA published a #StopRansomware advisory on Gunra, a ransomware-as-a-service
operation used by affiliates against government, critical infrastructure,
and other organizations. Gunra first appeared in 2025 and expanded to a RaaS
model in 2026. The group uses a double-extortion model, encrypting data and
threatening to publish it on a dedicated leak site if the ransom isn't paid.
The advisory includes technical detail and detection guidance for
defenders; organizations should review CISA's full advisory for IOCs and
mitigations.
