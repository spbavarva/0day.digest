A financially motivated threat actor is using open-source AI agent
frameworks to attack online retailers at scale, stealing more than
600,000 credit card records and infecting more than 100 sites with
skimmers.

The use of AI agent frameworks to automate skimmer deployment across
many targets simultaneously marks a shift toward AI-assisted attack
scaling in card-skimming operations. Affected retailers and payment
processors should review for skimmer indicators and unusual
automated traffic consistent with agent-driven reconnaissance.
