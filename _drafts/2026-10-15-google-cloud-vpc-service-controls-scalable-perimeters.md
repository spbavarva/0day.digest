Google Cloud introduced new scalable perimeter options for VPC Service
Controls, aimed at securing non-human identities such as autonomous agents
that act on behalf of users and request data across projects at high speed.

Google frames traditional, manually-operated network perimeters as poorly
suited to agent-driven workloads — overly permissive perimeters raise
data-theft risk, while overly strict ones block legitimate automation.
