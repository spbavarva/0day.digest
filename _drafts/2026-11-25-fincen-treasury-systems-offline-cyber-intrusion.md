The Treasury Department's Financial Crimes Enforcement Network (FinCEN) has
taken some systems offline after a cybersecurity incident resulted in
unauthorized access to a limited subset of its systems.

FinCEN collects suspicious activity reports and other Bank Secrecy Act
filings from banks and other financial institutions, making the agency a
high-value target. The agency has extended filing deadlines for affected
filers while it investigates.
