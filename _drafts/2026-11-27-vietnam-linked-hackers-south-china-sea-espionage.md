Researchers at SOCRadar have attributed a previously undocumented espionage
campaign, tracked as WeaveLotus, to a suspected Vietnam-sponsored hacking
group. The campaign targeted military, government, and legislative
organizations across Southeast Asia to gather intelligence on South China
Sea defense and military agreements.

The activity reportedly comprised at least five intrusions, including
targets in Thailand. Organizations tied to regional maritime defense policy
should be alert to targeted espionage activity from this cluster.
