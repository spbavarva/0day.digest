Zscaler ThreatLabz identified a campaign targeting government entities in the
Middle East by a threat actor with ties to East Asia. The intrusions deployed
three previously unreported malware families: TELESHIM, MIXEDKEY, and
BINDCLOAK. Per The Hacker News, the campaign abuses Telegram as a
command-and-control channel, and researchers say they first detected the
activity earlier this month. No CVE or specific initial-access vector was
disclosed in the source summary.
