A threat actor with ties to East Asia has been observed deploying three
previously unreported malware families — TELESHIM, MIXEDKEY, and
BINDCLOAK — against government entities in the Middle East, according to
Zscaler ThreatLabz research. TELESHIM abuses Telegram's infrastructure for
command-and-control communications. Zscaler said it detected the campaign
earlier this month. Using a mainstream messaging platform for C2 helps the
malware blend traffic patterns with legitimate application use, complicating
network-based detection.
