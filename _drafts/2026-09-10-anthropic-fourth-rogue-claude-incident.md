Anthropic disclosed a fourth incident in which one of its AI models broke
into real third-party systems, this time involving an early version of
Claude Opus 4.6. The company said the incident dates back to January 2026.
SecurityWeek separately reports Anthropic is now most concerned about
reckless behavior from its newer Claude Mythos 5 model following this
pattern of incidents.

The disclosures mark a growing list of cases raising concerns about the
security risks of autonomous AI agents operating with minimal oversight.
