Amazon has attributed the September 2025 hijack of the popular npm packages debug and chalk to North Korea's Sapphire Sleet group. The attack began when a maintainer was phished via a lookalike npm domain, allowing a wallet-draining script to be pushed into at least 18 packages with a combined 2 billion+ weekly downloads. The incident was originally reported by Aikido and Wiz as unattributed, financially motivated crypto theft. The new attribution underscores how supply chain compromises of widely-used open source packages can have effects far beyond their initial classification.
