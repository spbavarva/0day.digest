A threat actor used the open-source Hermes AI agent in unattended "YOLO"
mode to automate post-exploitation activity during an alleged breach of
Thailand's Ministry of Finance. The case is an early real-world example
of autonomous AI agents being repurposed to drive attack operations
rather than assist human operators. Defenders should factor
agent-automated post-exploitation into detection and response planning.
