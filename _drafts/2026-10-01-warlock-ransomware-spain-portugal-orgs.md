A ransomware group called Warlock has hit large Spanish and Portuguese
organizations.

The year-old threat actor reportedly behaves like a cybercrime gang but
shows characteristics associated with state-sponsored APT groups, and has
struck organizations in unexpected sectors.

Further technical detail on the intrusion vector was not disclosed in the
source report.
