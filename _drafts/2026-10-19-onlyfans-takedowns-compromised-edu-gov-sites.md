Copyright takedown requests tied to OnlyFans and other adult content
creators are surfacing a broader pattern: attackers exploit vulnerable
university and government websites to host content that manipulates search
engine rankings.

Security teams at .edu and .gov domains should treat unexpected takedown
notices or oddly indexed content as a possible signal of compromise, not
just a legal nuisance.
