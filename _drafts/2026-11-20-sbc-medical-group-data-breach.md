Threat actors accessed databases containing customer information
associated with SBC Medical Group clinics in Japan, impacting more than 2
million individuals.

No further technical detail on the attack vector has been disclosed.
Affected individuals should watch for official breach notifications and
monitor for follow-on phishing or fraud attempts using exposed data.
