OpenAI highlighted Perplexity as an early example of enterprise use of
GPT-6 Astra with reduced human oversight. Per OpenAI, Perplexity uses Astra
to write internal communications, modify software, and monitor production
systems, checking in with humans far less often than it did with earlier
models.

OpenAI did not disclose specific guardrails or incident-response mechanisms
for when Astra acts autonomously in production. The example is a useful
data point for security and AI-safety teams on how much autonomy
enterprises are now granting AI agents in live systems.
