A Russia-linked threat group, tracked as "Cold Relic," is using data stolen
from one compromised organization's cloud accounts to craft convincing
impersonations of trusted contacts and attack a second set of targets,
according to research cited by Google.

The group reuses legitimate stolen correspondence and account data to reach
people in the first victim's professional network, effectively chaining one
cloud breach into the next. Because the outreach comes from genuinely
compromised but trusted accounts, it raises the bar for defenders relying on
sender reputation alone.
