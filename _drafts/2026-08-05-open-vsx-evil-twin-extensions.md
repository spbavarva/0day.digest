A cluster of 77 extensions on the Open VSX marketplace was found
impersonating legitimate developer tools — "evil twins" — while
transmitting information about the systems and development environments
they were installed on.

The extensions were uploaded between July 26 and August 1, 2026, and were
identified by Manifold Security. They have since been removed from Open
VSX. Developers should verify publisher identity before installing
extensions from open marketplaces.
