Cisco Talos is tracking a China-nexus threat actor deploying a
previously undocumented backdoor codenamed Antino, which uses Outlook
and OneDrive for command-and-control.

The campaign has targeted government and policy organizations in
Taiwan, India, the Philippines, Cambodia, Pakistan, Thailand, and
Myanmar.
