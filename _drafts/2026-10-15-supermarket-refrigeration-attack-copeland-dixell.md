A wave of cyberattacks against roughly 700 French supermarkets'
refrigeration systems has expanded to affect Copeland and Dixell industrial
controllers, beyond the vendors initially implicated. No group has claimed
responsibility for the attacks.

The incident underscores how outdated OT/ICS firmware in commercial
refrigeration — often managed by third-party contractors rather than
in-house IT — remains an under-monitored attack surface.
