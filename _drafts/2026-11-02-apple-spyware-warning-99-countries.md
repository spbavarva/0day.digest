Apple issued threat notifications to users in 99 countries warning
they may have been targeted by mercenary spyware. As is its practice,
Apple did not attribute the activity to a specific actor or region,
citing the cost and sophistication of such operations.

No technical indicators were included in the available summary.
Recipients of Apple threat notifications should follow Apple's
guidance, including enabling Lockdown Mode, and seek support from
digital-security organizations.
