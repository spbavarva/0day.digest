US authorities disrupted MicroScan and FishHub, two scanning and hacking
tools used by Flax Typhoon and other China-linked advanced persistent threat
groups to scan and hack US and foreign critical infrastructure.

The disruption follows separate reporting that Flax Typhoon has been
actively exploiting known vulnerabilities now added to CISA's KEV catalog.
