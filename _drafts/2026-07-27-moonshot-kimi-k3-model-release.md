Moonshot AI has released the weights for Kimi K3, a 2.8 trillion parameter
model, totaling roughly 1.56TB on Hugging Face. The model ships under
Moonshot's modified MIT license (carried over from K2), which adds an
attribution requirement for commercial users with more than 100 million
monthly active users or over 20 million USD in revenue.
