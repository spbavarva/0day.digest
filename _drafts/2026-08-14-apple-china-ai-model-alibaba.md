Apple reportedly trained a custom large language model for the China market
in partnership with Alibaba, according to Reuters sources. The partnership
is described as a rare cross-border collaboration given rising tensions
between Beijing and Washington over AI technology.

Neither company has publicly confirmed the details of the arrangement.
