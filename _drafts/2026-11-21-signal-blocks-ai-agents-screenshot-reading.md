Signal announced it will restrict screenshots by default on Android and
iOS to stop AI agents from reading private chats.

The feature targets agentic systems built into iOS 27 and Android versions
where the encrypted messaging platform can detect an AI assistant
attempting to read Signal content on screen.

Signal conceded that the protection is "incomplete" and does not fully
close the gap between on-device AI assistants and sensitive message
content.
