FBI systems have reportedly been breached for the second time in two
months, this time targeting the agency's National Crime Information Center
(NCIC), which is used to share information between local, state, and
federal law enforcement.

Available reporting does not specify what data, if any, was accessed, and
there is no official FBI confirmation yet.

This follows an earlier reported breach of FBI systems within the same
two-month window.
