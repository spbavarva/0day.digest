A new White House memo directs the National Coordination Center to
establish a program letting private security firms apply for approval to
conduct offensive "hack-back" operations against foreign cybercrime
organizations.

Contracts may require a $1 million bond, which would be forfeited if a
company fails to comply with operational requirements — an attempt to
limit collateral damage from sanctioned private-sector hacking.
