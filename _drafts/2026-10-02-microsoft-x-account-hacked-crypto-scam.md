Attackers hijacked Microsoft's official account on X, which has over 13
million followers, to promote a Clippy-themed cryptocurrency token in an
apparent pump-and-dump scheme.

The incident highlights the continued targeting of high-follower
corporate social media accounts for crypto scams.
