Medical Computer Business Services (MCBS), a healthcare billing company, has
disclosed a 2025 network breach that exposed sensitive information belonging
to more than 1.2 million people. The disclosure covers a broad set of
patients whose billing data passed through MCBS systems. No details on the
attack vector or exploited method were provided in the source summary.
